# B03.CH10.P08 — Exception and definition capture: the silent channel

This paragraph specifies **exception capture** and **definition capture** as the silent channel through which OBA-compatible systems regress while
keeping the label. Unlike overt policy reversal, this channel works by altering *what counts* and *when rules apply*: small shifts in definitions,
thresholds, netting rules, and exception scopes can neutralise caps, disable ΔM discipline, and reintroduce toll positions without an obvious political
fight.

OBA therefore treats definition sets and exception regimes as **high-security governance objects**: they require version pinning, changelog discipline,
restatement duties, exception registries, selectivity tests, and hard-stop remedies when drift indicators rise.

## Core principles

* **Control the definitions, control the outcome**: measurement and enforceability collapse when semantics drift.
* **Exceptions are policy in disguise**: enough exceptions equal a new regime; “temporary” is the standard bypass word.
* **Thresholds are attack surfaces**: small parameter changes can move large cashflows outside rails.
* **Remedies must be fail-closed**: when definitional integrity is lost, scope narrows to auditable subset and re-authorisation is required.
* **Accountability must be contestable**: public rationale and judicial pathways prevent semantic laundering.

## B03.CH10.P08.S01 — Definition-shift detection: changelog anomalies, restatement gaps

Definition capture is detected by anomalies in how definitions change and how corrections propagate. The key is not that definitions never change, but
that changes are **traceable, justified, and restated** across all dependent artefacts.

### Definition-shift detection signals (minimum)

* **Changelog anomalies**: frequent micro-edits; vague rationales; changes concentrated in high-impact terms (caps base, control tests, consolidation, eligibility).
* **Backwards incompatibility without migration**: definition changes that break comparability without bridging notes.
* **Selective edits**: changes that reduce measured extraction or broaden carve-outs while preserving or increasing real-world tolls.
* **Restatement gaps**: corrected definitions not propagated to dashboards, prior classifications, or legal interpretations; “correction” without recomputation.
* **Method drift opacity**: definition changes embedded in tooling updates without explicit semantic versioning.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Definition sets **MUST** be versioned with semantic changelogs and bridging/migration notes; undocumented changes are prohibited.
* Any definitional change affecting caps, ΔM, auditability, or exceptions **MUST** trigger a restatement plan and publication of impacted metrics.
* If restatement gaps persist, the system **MUST** downgrade claims based on affected metrics and fail-closed to auditable definitions.

## B03.CH10.P08.S02 — Exception stacking detection: exception ratio, renewals, selectivity

Exception capture is detected not by the existence of exceptions but by their **accumulation**, **renewal pattern**, and **selective application**.
OBA uses exception ratios and renewal behaviour as drift diagnostics.

### Exception stacking signals (minimum)

* **Exception ratio↑**: rising share of cases/contracts/entities operating under exemptions, waivers, special regimes, or bespoke terms.
* **Renewals↑**: repeated renewal of “temporary” exceptions; renewals without updated evidence.
* **Selectivity**: exceptions clustered among insiders, dominant sectors, or politically connected entities; different treatment for similar cases.
* **Scope creep**: exceptions expand from narrow edge-cases to broad categories.
* **Sanction asymmetry**: sanctions fall or become selective precisely where exceptions rise (soft enforcement).

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* An exception regime **MUST** maintain a public exception registry with: scope, rationale, legal basis, sunset date, renewal history, and affected volumes.
* Exceptions **MUST** be time-bounded and renewal requires affirmative re-authorisation with evidence; auto-renewal is prohibited.
* Rising exception ratio or selective clustering **MUST** trigger escalation and independent audit; otherwise exceptions become covert policy rewrite.

## B03.CH10.P08.S03 — Capture via thresholds: threshold shifts and netting tricks

Threshold capture is the technique of moving regulated quantities outside rails by adjusting thresholds or by changing netting/consolidation rules so
that liabilities, profits, or fee-stacks “disappear” on paper.

### Threshold/netting capture techniques (canonical)

* **Threshold shifts**: raising eligibility thresholds, reporting thresholds, cap applicability thresholds, or enforcement trigger thresholds.
* **Bunching incentives**: designing thresholds that encourage entity splitting, contract fragmentation, or classification games.
* **Netting tricks**: allowing offsets that neutralise caps or extraction measurements (e.g., netting across entities, time windows, or instrument classes).
* **Consolidation dilution**: weakening control/benefit/block tests so that structures remain outside group reporting.
* **Time-window games**: redefining measurement windows to hide spikes (end-of-period smoothing, deferral).

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Any threshold change **MUST** include anti-gaming analysis (bunching/splitting tests) and publish expected distributional impacts.
* Netting and consolidation rules **MUST** be treated as constitutive rails in high-impact domains; changes require special procedure or independent review.
* If threshold games are detected (splitting↑, bunching↑, netting anomalies↑), the system **MUST** tighten rules (equivalence doctrines) and restate metrics.

## B03.CH10.P08.S04 — Remedy: semantic freeze, hard stops, re-authorisation

When definitional integrity is compromised, the remedy cannot be “more transparency.” OBA requires **fail-closed remedies** that stop drift and force a
return to governable semantics.

### Remedy ladder (minimum)

* **Semantic freeze**: temporarily freeze definition changes in affected domains; allow only bug-fix corrections under strict restatement discipline.
* **Hard stops**: suspend the use of contested metrics for decisions; narrow scope to auditable subset; halt exception renewals pending review.
* **Re-authorisation**: require renewed mandate approval for material definitional shifts or sustained exception expansion.
* **Independent reconstruction**: commission parallel re-computation (shadow computation) and independent audit of lineage and methods.
* **Rollback**: revert to last-known-good definition set and reissue classifications/decisions where feasible.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* If changelog anomalies and restatement gaps co-occur, semantic freeze **MUST** be triggered.
* If exception ratio exceeds thresholds or renewals become routine, hard stops and re-authorisation **MUST** activate.
* During remedy, governance must operate only on auditable definitions; discretionary continuation is prohibited.

## B03.CH10.P08.S05 — Accountability: public rationale + judicial contestability

Because definitions and exceptions determine rights and obligations, they must be contestable. Accountability means decisions can be challenged on the
record: who changed what, why, and with what authority, and whether the change violates rails.

### Accountability requirements (minimum)

* **Public rationale**: each definitional change or exception must publish mechanism and axis impacts (E/RB/caps/ΔG/ΔM/auditability), not only intent.
* **Authority citation**: legal/mandate basis and procedure used (constitutive vs parametric vs emergency).
* **Contestability path**: standing rules for who may challenge; timelines; evidence disclosure; remedy powers.
* **Judicial review compatibility**: records and logs must be sufficient for courts/tribunals to assess arbitrariness, selectivity, and rail breach.
* **Nullification and restatement**: ability to void unlawfully issued exceptions/definitions and restate dependent decisions.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Definition and exception decisions **MUST** be published with rationale, authority, and contestability routes.
* If judicial review is functionally blocked (missing records, sealed methods), the regime is non-compatible with order-democracy claims.
* Where accountability finds rail breach, nullification/restatement **MUST** follow; “we will do better next time” is not a remedy.
