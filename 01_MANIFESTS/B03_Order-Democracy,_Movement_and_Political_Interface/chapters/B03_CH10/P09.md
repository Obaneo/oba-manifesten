# B03.CH10.P09 — Detection: minimum set of capture indicators and signalling

This paragraph defines a **minimum detection layer** for capture: a compact indicator suite that makes drift visible early enough to intervene. In OBA,
anti-capture is not a belief; it is an operational property: the system must continuously answer (i) **is capture pressure rising?**, (ii) **where is it
entering?**, and (iii) **which escalation and correction protocol is triggered?** Detection therefore spans four domains: institutional performance,
economic outcomes, governance/tooling integrity, and narrative/epistemic hygiene. Signals must be published, versioned, and linked to specific
interventions, not merely observed.

## Core principles

* **Detection is a rail**: without measurable signals, capture becomes deniable and uncorrectable.
* **Use leading indicators**: outcome metrics alone detect too late; process and integrity metrics detect earlier.
* **Triangulate**: no single metric is sufficient; combine institutional, economic, governance, and narrative signals.
* **Signals must trigger action**: monitoring without thresholds and escalation is symbolic compliance.
* **Fail-closed on unknowns**: if measurement fails, scope narrows to auditable subset until restored.

## B03.CH10.P09.S01 — Institutional signals: sanction rate↓, enforcement latency↑, staff churn patterns

Institutional signals detect capture at the enforcement layer: when institutions stop acting, act slowly, or lose integrity capacity.

### Minimum institutional indicator set

* **Sanction rate↓**: sanctions per verified breach decline, especially when breach detection is stable or rising.
* **Enforcement latency↑**: time from complaint/detection to action increases; backlogs grow.
* **Selectivity patterns**: sanctions cluster on low-power targets while high-power categories show softness.
* **Audit scope shrinkage**: fewer deep audits, fewer re-performances, reduced access to underlying records/tooling.
* **Staff churn anomalies**: sudden exits in audit/enforcement/procurement roles; repeated leadership turnover; reliance on contractors increases.
* **Whistleblower channel signals**: report volume spikes with low follow-through; retaliation incidents rise.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Institutions **MUST** publish sanction and latency metrics with baselines and uncertainty; absence is an auditability breach.
* Churn and contractor reliance in high-risk roles **MUST** be treated as capture risk and trigger independent review.
* If sanction rate falls while exceptions rise or E stabilises/↑, escalation is mandatory (capture presumption).

## B03.CH10.P09.S02 — Economic signals: E stabilisation/↑, fee-stacks↑, concentration↑

Economic signals detect whether the system is actually reducing extraction or merely relabeling. Capture tends to preserve or reconstitute extraction via
fees, market power, and scarcity rents.

### Minimum economic indicator set

* **E stabilisation/↑**: extraction proxies stop declining or rise (toll take-rates, claim rents, fee spreads, monopoly margins).
* **Fee-stacks↑**: additional layers of intermediation fees, “service charges,” mandatory add-ons, platform take-rate increases.
* **Concentration↑**: market concentration metrics rise in high-impact sectors; entry barriers increase; merger intensity increases.
* **Pass-through asymmetry**: cost shocks passed to consumers/workers faster than benefits; indicates power asymmetry.
* **R/B stress**: lower-tail restruimte deteriorates despite claimed reforms; essentials access degrades.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Economic signals **MUST** be tied to definitional versions; measurement drift invalidates trend claims.
* If E does not decline while governance claims OBA compliance, a mislabeling/capture investigation **MUST** trigger (function-over-label).
* Fee-stack indicators **MUST** be tracked at channel level (ΔM/market flows) to prevent hidden toll rebuilding.

## B03.CH10.P09.S03 — Governance signals: vendor concentration↑, reproducibility↓, exceptions↑

Governance signals detect capture in the machinery: tooling, definitions, procurement, and exception regimes. These are often the earliest and most
diagnostic indicators.

### Minimum governance indicator set

* **Vendor concentration↑**: fewer suppliers in critical rails; bundled contracts; non-exitable dependencies.
* **Reproducibility↓**: inability to re-run computations; missing artefacts; undocumented parameter/model changes; replication failures.
* **Exceptions↑**: exception ratio increases; renewals become routine; exceptions cluster among insiders; sunsets fail.
* **Definition drift anomalies**: frequent micro-changes in high-impact terms; weak rationales; restatement gaps.
* **Audit obstruction**: vendors or agencies refuse access; “commercial confidentiality” used to block verification.
* **Procurement anomalies**: repeated single-bid awards; revolving consultancy patterns; spec tailoring.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Vendor and reproducibility metrics **MUST** be published; black-box operation in core rails is non-compatible unless fully contestable.
* Exceptions and definition changes **MUST** be registered with renewal history and restatement status; otherwise drift cannot be governed.
* If reproducibility falls or vendor concentration rises beyond thresholds, escalation must trigger exitability remediation and shadow computation.

## B03.CH10.P09.S04 — Narrative signals: uniform talking points, source poverty, definition shift in media

Narrative signals detect capture in the epistemic layer: when discourse converges into scripted frames that suppress definitional clarity and substitute
metrics or incidents for mechanism analysis.

### Minimum narrative indicator set

* **Uniform talking points**: cross-outlet repetition of identical frames, slogans, and “expert conclusions” without disclosed lineage.
* **Source poverty**: claims circulate without primary data, methods, or uncertainty; “studies say” without auditable references.
* **Definition shift in media**: key terms (cap, commons, audit, extraction) subtly redefined; OBA language used for OOA functions (washing).
* **Incident amplification**: rare cases dominate; exceptions justified repeatedly; structural drift indicators disappear from coverage.
* **Correction failure**: errors persist without restatements; quiet edits; no propagation to downstream summaries.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Public institutions **MUST** apply source-duty in official communications; citation-free claims are downgraded in legitimacy.
* Definition shifts used in policy justification **MUST** be flagged and countered with pinned definition sets and clarification notes.
* If narrative signals coincide with governance drift (exceptions↑, reproducibility↓), escalation must treat it as coordinated capture risk.

## B03.CH10.P09.S05 — Integration: linkage to the early-warning set and escalation protocols

Signals matter only if integrated into action. This section defines how the indicator suite links to early-warning systems and to the escalation and
correction ladder.

### Integration requirements (minimum)

* **Early-warning dashboard**: a single published bundle containing the minimum indicator suite, with trend lines pinned to definition versions.
* **Thresholds and triggers**: explicit thresholds (absolute and rate-of-change) that activate escalation; include uncertainty handling.
* **Authority chain**: who must act when triggers fire (oversight, auditors, ombuds, procurement authority).
* **Timelines**: maximum time to acknowledge, investigate, publish interim findings, and decide interventions.
* **Intervention mapping**: each trigger maps to a predefined intervention set (audit escalation, rotation, procurement freeze, semantic freeze, rollback).

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Every indicator **MUST** have: definition, source, update cadence, threshold, and linked intervention; otherwise it is decorative.
* Trigger events **MUST** be logged and publicly visible; silent threshold breaches are non-compliant.
* When measurement fails (missing data, reproducibility failure), the system **MUST** fail-closed to auditable scope and commission reconstruction.
