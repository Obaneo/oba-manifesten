# B03.CH08.P07 — Conflict mechanics in the political arena: neutralisation, capture, and regression

This paragraph specifies the **mechanics by which OBA reforms are neutralised** in real political arenas. The interface assumes adversarial pressure:
actors benefiting from OOA mechanisms will attempt to preserve extraction while adopting OBA language, diluting caps through exceptions, relocating
allocation into non-contestable black boxes, and exploiting crises to re-legitimate claim support. The purpose of this paragraph is to define these
patterns as **classifiable failure modes** with **binding response rules** (downgrades, washing flags, hard stops).

## Core principles

* **Politics is adversarial**: order claims are attacked through semantics, exceptions, opacity, and shock opportunism.
* **Neutralisation beats rejection**: the dominant failure mode is not open opposition but symbolic adoption with functional preservation of OOA.
* **Exception and opacity are structural weapons**: they create insider lanes and destroy comparability over time.
* **Crisis accelerates regression**: shocks are used to entrench toll positions and justify asset-support reflexes.

## B03.CH08.P07.S01 — OBA-washing: symbolic adoption without enforcement

**OBA-washing** is the strategic adoption of OBA vocabulary, branding, and institutional forms while leaving extraction mechanisms intact. The core move
is to migrate the debate from mechanisms to labels: “we are OBA” replaces “here are the binding rails and the audit trails.”

### Functional profile (washing)

* **Label-over-function**: OBA terms appear in speeches and preambles but not in enforceable clauses, standards, or logs.
* **Non-binding caps**: caps are announced but contain loopholes (entity splitting, exemptions, valuation games, offshore vehicles).
* **E avoidance**: extraction is discussed as “profit” or “growth” without P/E/C decomposition, cost-basis discipline, or toll neutralisation.
* **Audit theatre**: audits exist as attestations without replicable computation, open definitions, or restatement obligations.
* **Compliance by narrative**: success is declared through selected KPIs while axis deltas (E, R/B distribution, caps, ΔG, ΔM discipline) remain unknown.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Any actor/proposal using OBA language **MUST** be evaluated on enforceable mechanisms; rhetorical alignment is **non-evidence**.
* If binding rails (caps, auditability, ΔM channel discipline) are absent or bypassable, classification **MUST** be **OBA-washing** (not “partial”).
* Where audits are sealed/proprietary or non-replicable, classification **MUST** be downgraded and washing risk flagged.
* The interface **MUST** require a minimum evidence bundle for any “OBA-compatible” claim: rail deltas + logging plan + audit plan + correction triggers.

## B03.CH08.P07.S02 — Exception stacking: cap erosion via “temporary” holes

**Exception stacking** is the primary technique for eroding binding caps and constraints while preserving the appearance of compliance. The political
pattern is to introduce “temporary” carve-outs for emergencies, strategic sectors, or transitional hardship, then layer additional exceptions until
the baseline is functionally void.

### Functional profile (exception stacking)

* **Temporary becomes permanent**: sunsets are extended, renewed automatically, or replaced by successor programs.
* **Threshold tricks**: exemptions are engineered via thresholds that incentivize entity splitting, reclassification, or contractual restructuring.
* **Discretion points proliferate**: waivers, negotiated compliance, “case-by-case” hardship clauses create insider corridors.
* **Complexity-as-shield**: administrative complexity increases, reducing auditability and creating plausible deniability.
* **Comparability breaks**: the system can no longer be measured consistently across time/jurisdictions because rules are no longer uniform.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Any exception **MUST** include: explicit scope, objective eligibility criteria, a hard sunset date, and **rollback triggers**.
* Stacking exceptions without tightening auditability **MUST** trigger reclassification to **OOA-consistent** or **OBA-washing** (depending on rhetoric usage).
* Threshold-based exemptions **MUST** include anti-gaming tests (bunching, splitting, form arbitrage); absence is a downgrade condition.
* Where exceptions affect caps or ΔM channels, the interface **MUST** treat them as high-risk and require independent audit and public publication of take-up and leakage.

## B03.CH08.P07.S03 — Black-box politics: technocratic allocation without contestability

**Black-box politics** is the relocation of distribution, allocation, or enforcement into systems that cannot be contested: proprietary models, sealed
expert panels, vendor scoring, or opaque procurement chains. The political advantage is obvious: conflict is defused by declaring decisions “technical,”
while extraction is preserved through non-observable channels.

### Functional profile (black-box regime)

* **Technocratic authority replaces rules**: decisions are justified by expertise rather than by public definitions and replicable computation.
* **Vendor lock-in**: measurement and enforcement are outsourced to proprietary systems; audit becomes permissioned or impossible.
* **Non-restatable outcomes**: when errors or biases are found, there is no mandated restatement mechanism that propagates corrections.
* **Non-contestable allocation**: affected parties cannot challenge classifications because the input data, rules, or weights are hidden.
* **Accountability inversion**: officials can disclaim responsibility (“the model decided”), while vendors disclaim public duty (“trade secrets”).

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Any allocation or enforcement that affects E, caps, ΔM channels, or commons access **MUST** be contestable and replicable; otherwise classification **MUST** be downgraded.
* Proprietary metrics or sealed audits **MUST NOT** qualify as OBA-compatible evidence.
* The interface **MUST** require: public definition sets, open logging schemas, independent replication rights, and mandatory restatements on material error.
* If black-box mechanisms are introduced as a “pragmatic” shortcut, the proposal **MUST** be classified as **OOA-susceptible** unless an audited open substitute exists.

## B03.CH08.P07.S04 — Crisis extraction: toll profiteering and the asset-support reflex during shocks

Shocks (war, energy crunch, pandemics, financial stress, migration surges, climate events) compress decision time and expand discretion. Under OOA,
crises are routinely used to (i) expand toll claims (“scarcity pricing”), and (ii) legitimise the **asset-support reflex**: guarantees, liquidity,
and bailouts that preserve claim values and enable extraction rebound.

### Functional profile (crisis regression)

* **Toll profiteering**: scarcity is converted into durable claims; emergency pricing becomes the new baseline.
* **Claim preservation over order restoration**: relief is routed to asset holders and incumbents rather than to lower-bound R/B stability and toll neutralisation.
* **Emergency exceptions**: caps, procurement rules, and audit standards are suspended “temporarily,” creating long-tail leakage.
* **Opacity expansion**: emergency subcontracting and expedited procurement reduce traceability; corruption and capture risk rises.
* **Narrative locking**: “we had no choice” becomes the shield against later rollback and restatement.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Any crisis package claiming OBA compatibility **MUST** prioritise: (i) essentials continuity, (ii) R/B lower bounds, and (iii) toll neutralisation over claim preservation.
* Crisis measures **MUST** include explicit sunsets and post-crisis restatement/audit requirements; absence triggers downgrade.
* If ΔM expansion is used, channels **MUST** be auditable and bounded; asset/claim support without compensating toll neutralisation **MUST** be classified as **OOA-consistent**.
* The interface **MUST** treat “emergency” as a high-risk context: default classification is conservative until evidence and audit trails exist.
