# B03.CH08.P05 — Policy packaging: how proposals are formulated as “OBA-compatible”

This paragraph specifies how policy proposals must be **packaged** to be intelligible and admissible under the OBA interface. Packaging is not PR.
It is **semantic discipline**: a proposal must be expressed in the order grammar (flows, rails, tests) such that it can be projected onto axes,
audited, challenged, and corrected without relying on ideological trust. The goal is to prevent two systemic failure modes: (i) *label politics*
(“this is OBA because we say so”), and (ii) *mechanism drift* (the policy’s real functioning diverges from its stated purpose through carve-outs,
opacity, or reclassification).

## Core principles

* **Flows over flags**: speak in mechanisms and measurable flow decompositions, not in identity labels.
* **Test-ready by default**: a policy claim is incomplete until it includes measurement, audit, and correction commitments.
* **Function-over-label**: legal and public language must bind functionally to prevent semantic laundering.
* **Explicit boundaries**: state non-goals and prohibited routes to close predictable bypass channels.

## B03.CH08.P05.S01 — Language discipline: speak in flows and rails, not labels

OBA-compatible packaging requires a controlled vocabulary. The interface treats “OBA”, “fair”, “free”, “pro-market”, “pro-worker”, “green”, and
similar labels as **non-evidence**. Acceptable language describes what happens to **flows** and **rails**: where extraction is located, which toll
positions are neutralized, how caps bind, how commons substitute dependency, and how ΔM is channelled.

### Required language primitives

* **Flow primitives**: P/E/C decomposition of relevant payments; where claims are booked; where fixed burdens L arise; expected R/B distribution shift.
* **Rail primitives**: explicit deltas for E, caps (W-MAX/I-MAX or equivalents), commons ΔG, ΔM channel discipline, auditability.
* **Mechanism primitives**: lock-in removal, interoperability mandates, cost-base caps, anti-carve-out constraints, consolidation rules, sanction triggers.

### Forbidden language patterns (packaging-level)

* **Pure label claims**: “OBA-compatible” without mechanism and metrics.
* **Moral substitution**: “just/solidaristic/freedom” as stand-ins for rail deltas.
* **Ideological camouflage**: left/right framing used to replace mechanism explanation.
* **Model-only assertions**: “the model says it works” without open rules/logs.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Every proposal claiming OBA compatibility **MUST** be written primarily in flow and rail language (mechanisms + deltas).
* Labels **MAY** be used only as summaries and **MUST** be backed by explicit rail deltas and evidence hooks.
* Ambiguous terms (“fair price”, “reasonable profit”, “market rate”) **MUST** be operationalized or treated as non-compliant packaging.

## B03.CH08.P05.S02 — Minimal claim structure: problem → mechanism → measurement → audit → correction

To be admissible, a proposal must follow a minimal claim chain that makes it testable under adversarial conditions. The chain is:

1. **Problem statement** (order-relevant, not ideological)
2. **Mechanism** (how the proposal changes rails)
3. **Measurement** (what will be measured, how, with what data definitions)
4. **Audit** (who verifies, how often, with what replicability guarantees)
5. **Correction** (what happens when it fails: rollback, tightening, restatement)

### Minimal claim template (required fields)

* **Problem**: locate extraction + blocking surface; identify affected essentials and R/B impact distribution.
* **Mechanism**: which toll positions are neutralized; how caps bind; whether commons substitution occurs; ΔM channel path.
* **Measurement**: axis deltas and KPIs tied to definitions; baseline; horizon; uncertainty bands; “unknown” handling.
* **Audit**: audit scope, independence, publication; logging granularity; replicable computation; restatement obligations.
* **Correction**: automatic triggers (E not falling, bypass rate rising, audit failure); sunset clauses; rollback plan; escalation path.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* A proposal **MUST** include all five elements; omission of any element **MUST** downgrade classification to **Unknown/Untestable**.
* “Audit” **MUST** be externalizable and replicable; internal review alone is insufficient.
* “Correction” **MUST** include at least one automatic trigger; purely discretionary correction is non-compliant packaging.

## B03.CH08.P05.S03 — Anti-relabeling: function-over-label in communication and legal text

OOA systems persist by **relabeling**: extraction is renamed as innovation, rent as return, monopoly as efficiency, and opacity as expertise.
OBA-compatible packaging must therefore bind functionally: the text must constrain behavior even when actors attempt semantic laundering.

### Anti-relabeling rules (functional binding)

* **Define by mechanism**: rights and obligations refer to measurable behaviors (pricing above cost bands under scarcity, lock-in terms, refusal of portability),
  not to identity categories (“good actors”, “strategic sectors”).
* **No status laundering**: exemptions cannot be granted by naming (“green”, “national champion”, “critical infrastructure”) without test criteria.
* **Equivalence doctrine**: legal forms that are functionally equivalent to restricted claims are treated identically (anti-vehikel, anti-synthetic exposure).
* **Cost-base discipline**: where “reasonable cost” is used, cost categories and audit access are specified; otherwise it becomes a blank cheque.

### Communication discipline

Public communication must mirror the legal functional binding: it should not sell the policy with labels while hiding the functional constraints,
because that creates political incentives to later remove constraints “quietly.”

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Legal text **MUST** define obligations using functional criteria tied to measurable triggers.
* Any exemption category **MUST** include objective criteria, a sunset date, and rollback triggers; purely declarative exemptions are forbidden.
* Where equivalence is possible (vehicles, derivatives, SPVs, outsourcing opacity), the proposal **MUST** state an explicit equivalence rule or be downgraded.

## B03.CH08.P05.S04 — Boundary statements: explicit “non-goals” and prohibited routes

OBA-compatible packaging requires explicit **boundary statements**: what the policy does *not* aim to do, and which implementation routes are prohibited.
This is not rhetorical modesty. It is a defensive measure against scope creep, capture, and laundering.

### Required boundary statement categories

* **Non-goals**: outcomes the proposal is not designed to guarantee (e.g., “does not target GDP growth”, “does not preserve existing asset values”).
* **Prohibited routes**: implementation paths that would recreate OOA mechanisms (asset/claim support reflex, opaque ΔM channels, black-box scoring, open-ended exceptions).
* **No-trade clauses**: rails that cannot be sacrificed as “pragmatic compromise” (auditability, binding caps, anti-bypass consolidation).
* **Domain boundaries**: where commons substitution is intended and where market provision remains acceptable under contestability conditions.

### Typical prohibited routes (non-exhaustive)

* **Asset-support reflex**: stabilizing rents/asset prices via guarantees while claiming “social protection.”
* **Opacity outsourcing**: delegating measurement/enforcement to proprietary vendors or sealed audits.
* **Exception inflation**: indefinite carve-outs, negotiated compliance, threshold tricks.
* **Shadow claims**: off-balance liabilities or contingent claims that recreate extraction while bypassing caps.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Every proposal claiming OBA compatibility **MUST** include boundary statements covering non-goals and prohibited routes.
* If a prohibited route is used, the proposal **MUST** be reclassified as **OBA-washing** unless the route is explicitly controlled, sunsetted, and audited.
* Boundary statements **MUST** be enforceable (legal/contractual), not aspirational.
