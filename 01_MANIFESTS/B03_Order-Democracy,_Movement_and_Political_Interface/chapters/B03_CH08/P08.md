# B03.CH08.P08 — Media and narrative struggle: frames as an order instrument

This paragraph treats **media and narrative** as an **order instrument**: frames determine what the public is able to see and therefore what can be
politically enforced. In OBA terms, the narrative struggle is not “messaging.” It is the contest over whether extraction (E), toll positions, bypass
routes, and auditability failures remain *invisible* or become *common knowledge with evidence*. OOA stability depends on keeping the mechanism layer
opaque; OBA stability depends on rendering it legible through **concrete flows**, **dashboards**, and **audit-log based correction**.

## Core principles

* **Frames are allocation tools**: they shift scrutiny away from mechanisms and toward identities or moral emotions.
* **Mechanism visibility is power**: what cannot be named, measured, or shown cannot be capped or neutralised.
* **Anti-confusion discipline**: the interface must treat definitional drift and metric gaming as adversarial tactics.
* **Epistemic hygiene**: source duty, replication, and restatements are not academic norms; they are political enforcement infrastructure.

## B03.CH08.P08.S01 — Counter-frames: “unworkable”, “capital flight”, “state surveillance”, “anti-innovation”

OOA defenders rarely argue “we want extraction.” They deploy **counter-frames** that re-label OBA rails as threats. The frames are predictable because
they target the fault-line objects (caps, auditability, ΔM discipline, commons).

### Canonical counter-frames (non-exhaustive)

* **“Unworkable / too complex”**: used to pre-empt auditability by claiming that rigorous logging and rule-binding cannot be done at scale.
* **“Capital flight / investment collapse”**: used to defend non-binding caps by framing bounded accumulation as self-harm.
* **“State surveillance”**: used to delegitimise audit trails and measurement, often by conflating public auditability with intrusive personal monitoring.
* **“Anti-innovation / anti-entrepreneurship”**: used to protect toll positions by presenting rents as rewards for innovation.

### Interpretation rule

These frames are not refuted by moral reassurance. They are refuted by **mechanism separation**: show what is being capped (toll claims and dominance),
what remains free (productive activity), what is logged (flows relevant to extraction), and what privacy boundaries exist.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* The interface **MUST** classify these counter-frames as *claims* requiring mechanism-level grounding (what exactly becomes unworkable; which capital moves; what surveillance is implied; what innovation is blocked).
* Responses **MUST** use the fault-line objects: clarify caps scope, audit boundaries, ΔM channels, and commons substitution—rather than arguing ideology.
* Any proposal defended primarily by counter-frames while refusing measurement commitments **MUST** be flagged as **OOA-susceptible**.

## B03.CH08.P08.S02 — Confusion techniques: definition shifting, metric gaming, spotlighting edge cases

Narrative conflict is often won by **confusion** rather than persuasion. OOA systems persist by making mechanism questions hard to ask and hard to
answer. This section defines the main confusion techniques as auditable failure patterns.

### Confusion techniques (canonical)

* **Definition shifting**: changing what “extraction,” “competition,” “reasonable return,” “cap,” or “commons” means mid-debate.
* **Metric gaming**: cherry-picking KPIs (GDP, average wages, headline inflation) while ignoring R/B distribution, fixed-burden L, and toll sector margins.
* **Spotlighting edge cases**: amplifying rare or extreme scenarios to discredit the general rule (“what about the widow’s pension fund?”) while
  refusing to discuss the dominant flows and distributions.
* **Unit-of-analysis tricks**: using aggregated averages to hide tail harm; redefining households/entities to blur consolidation.
* **Complexity fog**: producing over-technical narratives to exhaust scrutiny, often paired with claims that only insiders can understand the system.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Any public classification or debate output **MUST** pin definitions to canonical IDs (versioned) and must flag attempts to shift them.
* Metrics presented in support of an order claim **MUST** include distributional R/B and at least one extraction-proxy; omission triggers downgrade.
* Edge cases **MAY** be addressed only after the main flow decomposition is shown; using edge cases to avoid main flows **MUST** be flagged as confusion.
* The interface **MUST** treat “complexity fog” as an anti-audit signal and demand simpler, test-ready statements.

## B03.CH08.P08.S03 — Counter-framing: dashboards, audit logs, concrete flows, exception ratio

OBA counter-framing replaces debate-by-label with **visibility-by-artefact**. The primary weapons are not slogans but instruments that let outsiders
see and verify: dashboards, public audit logs, flow decompositions, and exception analytics.

### Required counter-framing artefacts

* **Flow dashboards**: show P/E/C decomposition and where E concentrates (by sector, channel, income decile, region) with uncertainty bands.
* **R/B distribution panels**: p10/p25/median and share below α; include L decomposition (housing, energy, mobility, health, education, platform fees).
* **Caps compliance panels**: consolidation status, avoidance signals (entity splitting, valuation discontinuities), enforcement actions and outcomes.
* **ΔM channel ledger**: where new capacity went (commons, lower-bound stabilisation, asset/claim support), with traceable channel objects.
* **Exception ratio**: exceptions per rule, take-up, leakage estimates, renewal frequency; high exception ratio is treated as capture risk.

### Counter-framing rule

Do not argue “trust us.” Publish the artefact that allows opponents and neutrals to test the claim. The narrative function of the artefact is to
invert the burden of proof: “show your logs or retract your claim.”

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Any actor claiming OBA compliance **MUST** provide dashboard-level outputs sufficient for independent scrutiny (subject to privacy-safe aggregation).
* The interface **MUST** treat high exception ratio, low sanction rate, or declining reproducibility as prima facie evidence of regression.
* If audit logs cannot be published, the output **MUST** specify exactly what is withheld, why, and what compensating verification exists; otherwise downgrade.

## B03.CH08.P08.S04 — Epistemic hygiene: source duty, replication, restatements when correcting

OBA politics requires **epistemic hygiene** because order enforcement depends on shared reality. The interface adopts a strict correction ethic:
claims must be sourced, computations replicable, and errors restated publicly. This is not “academic virtue.” It is an anti-capture mechanism: if
corrections do not propagate, falsehood becomes durable infrastructure.

### Epistemic hygiene duties (normative)

* **Source duty**: any public claim about rails (E, R/B, caps, ΔG, ΔM) must cite the dataset, definition version, and computation rule.
* **Replication duty**: computations must be reproducible by independent parties; “confidential methodology” is non-compliant for order claims.
* **Restatement duty**: when material errors are found, corrected outputs must be issued, and downstream summaries must be updated (not merely appended).
* **Version pinning**: claims must reference the definition set version; changes require changelogs and backward comparability notes.
* **Unknown discipline**: where evidence is missing, mark “unknown” rather than fill with narrative.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Media outputs produced under this interface **MUST** include source/version metadata and a replication pathway.
* Corrections **MUST** be issued as restatements (with propagation) when errors affect classification labels or rail deltas.
* Actors who refuse replication or restatement obligations **MUST** be treated as washing-risk and their claims downgraded to **Unknown/Untestable**.
* It is **FORBIDDEN** to present composite indices or “scores” without publishing their component definitions and weights.
