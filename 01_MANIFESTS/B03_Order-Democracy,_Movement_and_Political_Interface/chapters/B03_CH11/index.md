# B03.CH11 — Conflict dynamics within OBA: internal schisms, splits, and recovery procedures

This chapter specifies how **internal conflict** functions as a systemic risk in **OBA-compatible order-democracy**, and how it is contained without
breaking the rails. It provides a typology of conflict sources and modes, formal criteria for when conflict becomes a **fork**, and binding escalation
and recovery procedures (rollback, re-authorisation, reconstitution, restatements). The goal is to keep disagreement **governable**: evidence-bound,
contestable, and reversible, rather than degenerating into sabotage, authoritarian drift, or regression to OOA reflexes.
