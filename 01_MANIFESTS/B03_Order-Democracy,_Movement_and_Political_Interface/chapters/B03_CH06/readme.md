# B03.CH06 — Movement and support structures: coalitions, subject positions, and organisational forms

This chapter specifies the **movement layer** required to make OBA durable in democratic politics: the coalition logic, actor
positions, organisational forms, and support infrastructure that allow order institutions to survive **sequencing conflicts**,
**capture pressure**, and **crisis politics**. It treats the movement as an **implementation interface** (mandate discipline,
monitoring, escalation, and protection), not as an ideology or a policy platform.

The chapter delivers **machine-readable building blocks** for coalition design, organisational architecture, funding and media
rails, and operational procedures that keep the movement aligned with OBA’s test axes (**E / caps / commons-ΔG / R/B / ΔM**) and
prevent regression into rhetoric-only adoption.

## How to use this chapter

- If you are **building an OBA movement or support coalition**: use the subject-position map (P02), coalition logic (P03), and
  organisational-form menu (P04) to design a structure that survives shocks, infiltration attempts, and swing dynamics.
- If you are **translating political victory into implementation**: use P06–P07 to convert mandates into **testable assignments**
  and to run monitoring/escalation loops (scorecards, sanction rate, exception ratio, rollback triggers).
- If you are **designing resilience against counter-moves**: use P08 for threat models and protection mechanisms (legal defence,
  redundancy, open tooling, funding hygiene).
- If you are **governing internal disagreements**: use P09 as the internal conflict/fork discipline to prevent drift and sham unity.

## Structure

P01 — Purpose and delimitation: movement as an implementation interface, not an ideology  
P02 — Subject positions: structural actors, exposures, and incentive profiles  
P03 — Coalition logic: stable core, swing groups, fault lines, and erosion patterns  
P04 — Organisational forms: party/union/commons/network/dual-structure architectures  
P05 — Support structures: knowledge, funding, operations, media channels, living-proof commons  
P06 — Mobilisation strategies: framing, targeting, escalation, and infiltration resistance  
P07 — Interaction with institutions: mandate translation, staffing, monitoring, correction, commons interface  
P08 — Counter-moves and repression: resistance profiles and protection mechanisms  
P09 — Internal conflicts: schisms, orthodoxy drift, and internal fork protocol  
P10 — Evaluation: metrics, incident reviews, version control, and handover protocols (→ B03.CH12 inputs)

## Status

This chapter is **normative** for movement-facing claims of OBA implementation: it defines the minimal governance and operational
discipline required to keep institutions enforceable and contestable under political pressure. Updates must preserve the core
principle that movements are evaluated by **observable behaviour** (mandate discipline, monitoring, escalation, anti-capture),
not by ideological self-description.
