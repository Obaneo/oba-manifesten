# B03.CH12.P07 — Fork and compatibility outcome: when it formally deviates

This paragraph defines the **formal compatibility outcome** of the democratic consistency test: how a governance regime is classified as **compatible**,
**partially compatible**, or **incompatible**, and when deviation becomes a **fork event** that requires naming separation under the fork/compatibility
rules (B03.CH07). The purpose is to prevent the most common failure mode of reform movements: the label survives while the rails are replaced by
exceptions, black-box allocation, captured enforcement, or emergency normalisation. In OBA, compatibility is not self-declared; it is a measurable
status derived from non-negotiables and drift indicators.

The compatibility outcome is the final step in the response ladder: it binds public communication, legal text, and registry entries. When a regime is
incompatible, continuing to present it as “OBA” is misrepresentation and must trigger relabeling and fork registry updates.

## Core principles

* **Compatibility is functional**: measured against non-negotiables, auditability, and rail-binding—not ideology or intent.
* **Partial compatibility is strictly temporary**: allowed only under emergency deviation with hard sunsets, rollback, and evidence discipline.
* **Incompatibility requires name separation**: semantic blending is washing and a governance breach.
* **Forking is a governance act**: once rails diverge structurally, the system must either return via recovery or be registered as a fork.

## B03.CH12.P07.S01 — Compatible: all non-negotiables intact; variation purely parametric

A regime is **compatible** when it satisfies all democratic non-negotiables (P02) and drift indicators remain below thresholds, such that variation is
only parametric: choices about rates, sequencing, priorities, and local implementation within the rails.

### Compatibility conditions (minimum)

* **Non-negotiables intact**: separation of functions, contestability, transparency minimum, exception discipline, anti-overreach rails.
* **Auditability intact**: reproducibility pass for critical outputs; audit coverage above minimum.
* **Caps binding**: no structural carve-outs; exceptions within ratio bands and enforced sunsets.
* **ΔM contestable**: allocation is explainable, reproducible, and challengeable; no black-box routing.
* **Commons discipline**: ΔG maintenance and boundary rules are enforced with published registers and appeal routes.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Compatible regimes may still be politically contested; compatibility is not an endorsement, only a structural status.
* Compatible regimes must publish periodic consistency reports and maintain versioned artefacts; “silent compliance” is not acceptable.
* If compatibility depends on unpublished or proprietary components, it is not compatibility (auditability fails).

## B03.CH12.P07.S02 — Partially compatible: temporary emergency deviation with hard sunsets/rollback and evidence discipline

A regime is **partially compatible** when a temporary emergency deviation exists, but the deviation is bounded by strict democratic discipline and does
not become structural. Partial compatibility is not a “middle way”; it is a **time-bounded exceptional state** under heightened scrutiny.

### Partial compatibility conditions (minimum)

* **Emergency justification**: clearly defined shock condition; scope limited to what is necessary for continuity.
* **Hard sunsets**: deviation auto-expires; renewal requires re-authorisation logic and published evidence.
* **Rollback readiness**: technical and contractual exitability exists; baseline can be restored.
* **Evidence discipline**: measurement continuity maintained; uncertainty bands disclosed; ex post audits scheduled.
* **No overreach**: prohibited linkages and coercive expansions remain blocked; contestability routes remain operable.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Partial compatibility must include a published recovery plan with deadlines and verification criteria.
* Repeated renewals without rollback convert partial compatibility into incompatibility (exception normalisation).
* If measurement blackouts occur, partial compatibility cannot be claimed; fail-closed or incompatible classification applies.

## B03.CH12.P07.S03 — Incompatible: structural cap erosion, black-box governance, or permanent exception explosion

A regime is **incompatible** when it structurally violates non-negotiables or when drift becomes persistent such that governance is no longer
contestable/auditable in OBA terms. Incompatibility can exist even if sector outcomes appear acceptable; the failure is in democratic form.

### Incompatibility conditions (minimum)

* **Structural cap erosion**: exceptions function as baseline; sunset failure; carve-outs for insiders; threshold tricks/netting to evade caps.
* **Black-box governance**: unreplicable allocation or enforcement; proprietary models without audit; repeated audit refusals; monopoly measurement.
* **Permanent exception explosion**: exception ratio persistently above thresholds; stacking creates parallel regime.
* **Contestability failure**: evidence access blocked; appeal routes ineffective; procedural stacking creates de facto vetoes.
* **Authoritarian drift**: surveillance/linkage expansion, selective enforcement, emergency normalisation beyond sunsets.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Incompatibility triggers mandatory naming separation (S04) and fork registry procedures.
* Incompatible regimes must either enter recovery (rollback/reconstitution/restatements) or be treated as formal forks with disclosed rails deltas.
* Claims of OBA compliance without restoring non-negotiables are prohibited.

## B03.CH12.P07.S04 — Label and naming rule: mandatory relabeling upon incompatibility

Naming is not branding; it is semantic integrity. If a regime is incompatible, continuing to use “OBA” (or “OBA-compatible”) is **washing** and destroys
the shared language required for contestation.

### Naming rules (minimum)

* **OBA-compatible**: only if compatible status is met and evidenced.
* **Partially compatible**: only if emergency deviation is time-bounded with sunsets/rollback and evidence discipline; must include end date.
* **Incompatible**: must be labeled as such; must disclose which non-negotiables and rails are violated.
* **OBA-inspired**: allowed for rhetorical affinity without compatibility; must not imply compliance.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Relabeling must be public and propagated to legal texts, dashboards, registries, and communications.
* Mislabeling triggers sanctions in governance systems that recognise OBA naming rules (see B03.CH07 name usage discipline).
* Semantic blending (keeping “OBA” while breaking rails) is treated as an integrity incident requiring independent audit review.

## B03.CH12.P07.S05 — Handover: inputs to Book 2/4 for transition and cognitive consistency tests

The compatibility outcome is not the end of evaluation. It produces inputs for adjacent capstone tests and operational regimes, especially under
incompatibility or partial compatibility where shocks and information-order failures are implicated.

### Handover outputs (minimum)

* **To Book 2 (transition and shock regimes)**:

  * classification of emergency deviations, rollback feasibility, and sequencing constraints;
  * crisis rent signals and buffer needs (R/B stabilisation) to prevent backlash-driven rail breaks.
* **To Book 4 (information order and cognitive consistency)**:

  * reproducibility failures, black-box drift incidents, model/rule churn, and source-duty violations;
  * requirements for replication tooling, model cards, and anti-monopoly information infrastructure.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

* Compatibility outcomes must include a structured handover bundle: definition IDs, breached thresholds, incident IDs, and recovery status.
* Where incompatibility is driven by information-order failures (closed computation), Book 4 constraints become mandatory prerequisites for recovery.
* Where incompatibility is driven by shock regimes (emergency normalisation), Book 2 re-authorisation and sunset discipline becomes the primary recovery path.
