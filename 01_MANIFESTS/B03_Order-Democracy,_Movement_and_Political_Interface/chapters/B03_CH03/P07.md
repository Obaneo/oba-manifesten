# B03.CH03.P07 — Mandate protection against capture and semantic drift

Mandates fail in practice when they are **captured** (implementation bent toward insiders) or **eroded semantically** (the label
remains while the operative rules shift). OBA therefore treats mandate protection as part of the mandate itself: an electoral
instruction is incomplete unless it includes enforceable rails for disclosure, auditability, anti-relabeling, and correction.
This paragraph defines the minimum protection set that prevents “implementation by laundering” and keeps mandate execution
contestable over time.

---

## B03.CH03.P07.S01 — Lobbying and funding disclosure as a mandate condition

Capture often enters through hidden inputs: who wrote the text, who financed the coalition, and which actors obtained privileged
access. OBA makes disclosure a *condition of legitimacy*, not an optional transparency ideal.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Any mandate that claims OBA-conform status MUST include enforceable disclosure rules for:
  - lobbying actors and meetings,
  - consultation inputs and text lineage,
  - political and movement funding (thresholds + ultimate beneficial control where applicable).
- Undisclosed material influence (hidden funding, off-register drafting, concealed intermediaries) MUST trigger escalation and
  can invalidate the affected mandate components pending investigation.
- Disclosure obligations MUST be paired with sanctioning capacity (fines/bans/nullification) and must not be satisfied by
  “voluntary reporting.”

---

## B03.CH03.P07.S02 — Vendor lock-in prevention: open standards and auditability

Implementation is frequently captured by tooling: proprietary systems, unverifiable scoring, and “vendor-defined metrics” become a
shadow constitution. OBA treats auditability and exitability as mandatory procurement properties.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Any system used to execute, allocate, classify, or measure under the mandate MUST meet:
  - open interfaces/data export,
  - version pinning + changelog discipline,
  - logging that supports independent audit and replication.
- Procurement MUST include exitability clauses (portability, escrow where needed, second-source feasibility, shadow computation
  options) sufficient to prevent vendor veto power.
- If a vendor refuses audit access or reproducibility, the affected function MUST be hard-stopped or re-routed to a compliant
  implementation.

---

## B03.CH03.P07.S03 — Anti-relabeling: function-over-label as legal core

A common erosion pattern is formal compliance with words while changing the function: “fees” become “subscriptions,” toll gates
become “service tiers,” caps become “temporary relief,” and extraction is renamed “innovation.” OBA blocks this with a legal
anti-relabeling rule.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Mandate text MUST encode function-over-label: classification and enforcement follow the operative economic function, not the
  contractual or rhetorical label.
- Look-through rules MUST apply to chains, intermediaries, and layered contracts (to prevent fee-stacks and disguised tolls).
- Any definitional change that weakens enforcement or measurability MUST be treated as a constitutive change and routed through
  version control + higher-threshold review (no silent redefinitions).

---

## B03.CH03.P07.S04 — Audit of mandate execution: independent review and sanction rate

A mandate is only real if execution can be verified and corrected. OBA therefore requires independent auditing of both outcomes and
the enforcement process itself, using stable metrics that reveal drift.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Mandate execution MUST be subject to independent audit with:
  - access to logs, registries, and decision rationales,
  - reproducible calculation rules,
  - publication of findings and restatements when corrections occur.
- Minimum drift indicators MUST be tracked and published, including at least:
  - sanction rate trends,
  - enforcement latency,
  - exception ratio/renewals,
  - reproducibility and audit coverage signals.
- Audit scope MUST expand automatically under early-warning signals (capture/drift triggers), and audits MUST not be deferrable by
  the implementing body.

---

## B03.CH03.P07.S05 — Sanctions for mandate breach: rollback, re-authorisation, institutional correction

Without credible consequences, mandates degrade into symbolism. OBA requires a correction ladder that neutralises extraction,
reverses incompatible changes, and, where needed, resets institutions.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- When mandate breach is detected (capture, semantic drift, black-box allocation, cap erosion), the response MUST include one or
  more of:
  - rollback of the violating rule/contract/tooling change,
  - requalification (function-over-label) with corrective enforcement,
  - disgorgement/clawback where extraction occurred,
  - procurement reset / vendor exclusion,
  - governance reset (rotation/reconstitution) where institutional capture is present.
- If breach affects constitutive rails or persists through repeated correction attempts, the system MUST trigger
  re-authorisation (new mandate) and/or fork classification with correct public labeling.
- Non-suspensive defaults SHOULD apply where delay creates regressions (i.e., objections cannot be used to freeze enforcement),
  with explicit, narrow exceptions only.
