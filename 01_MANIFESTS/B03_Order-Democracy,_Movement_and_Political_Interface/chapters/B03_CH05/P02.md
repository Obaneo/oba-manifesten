# B03.CH05.P02 — Separation of functions: execution, audit, oversight, and judicial review

This paragraph defines the **separation-of-functions architecture** required for OBA-compatible governance. The purpose is not
bureaucratic complexity but **anti-capture** and **anti-drift**: the same actor (or tightly coupled stack) must not be able to
(i) make allocative/enforcement decisions, (ii) validate those decisions, (iii) define the exception regime that excuses them, and
(iv) control the ultimate dispute resolution. Separation is treated as a **constitutive rail**: without it, auditability and
contestability collapse into self-reporting, and exceptions become a quiet channel for semantic erosion.

---

## B03.CH05.P02.S01 — Execution: registration, allocation, enforcement, and operational decisions

**Execution** is the operational arm that applies the rules to cases and runs the systems that produce outcomes.

### Scope of execution (typical)
- **Registration**: intake, classification, and assignment under pinned definition sets.
- **Allocation**: applying ΔM and other allocation rules to decide routing, priority, and eligibility.
- **Enforcement**: detection, remediation actions, sanctions initiation under defined ladders.
- **Operations**: service delivery, maintenance actions, and day-to-day decisions within budget/rail constraints.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Execution MUST produce **complete decision logs**: inputs, definition-set version, method IDs, rationale, and outputs.
- Execution MUST NOT unilaterally redefine terms, change calculation rules, or introduce new exception classes.

---

## B03.CH05.P02.S02 — Independent audit: verification, forensic access, meta-audit

**Independent audit** exists to verify that execution and oversight remain within rails, and to detect misclassification, drift,
capture, and black-box behaviour.

### Audit functions
- **Verification**: reproduce decisions from logs and check compliance against pinned definitions and rules.
- **Forensic access**: obtain sufficient system/contract/tooling evidence to detect side letters, hidden routing, or vendor black boxes.
- **Meta-audit**: audit of auditors—rotation, second-source replication, and challenge of audit methodologies.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Audit MUST be institutionally independent from execution (budgetary, staffing, and governance separation).
- Audit MUST have **forensic access** sufficient to evaluate computation, data lineage, and exceptions (subject to privacy rails).
- Refusal or obstruction of audit access MUST trigger escalation (enhanced audit mode / hard stops where applicable).

---

## B03.CH05.P02.S03 — Oversight: policy frames, exception management, escalation decisions

**Oversight** defines the governance frame within which execution operates. It does not execute cases and does not audit itself.
Its role is to manage mandates, exception regimes, and system-level escalation.

### Oversight responsibilities
- **Policy framing**: translate mandate into bounded operational objectives and test-axis commitments.
- **Exception governance**: define, approve, register, and sunset exceptions; enforce non-stackability and rollback readiness.
- **Escalation decisions**: trigger enhanced audits, impose hard stops, mandate rollbacks, and initiate reconstitution when drift is detected.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Oversight MUST run an **exceptions register** with scope, duration, impact, rationale, sunset/rollback conditions, and renewal thresholds.
- Oversight MUST NOT be allowed to suppress audit findings or “negotiate” definitions outside the canonical source hierarchy.

---

## B03.CH05.P02.S04 — Judicial review: objection/appeal, proportionality, evidentiary standards

**Judicial review** is the contestability endpoint: a neutral forum that can rule on disputes, proportionality, and evidence
standards without being captured by the operational stack.

### Core functions
- **Objection/appeal**: ensure decisions can be challenged with access to relevant evidence.
- **Proportionality**: prevent overreach and protect rights within the order rails (including prohibited linkages).
- **Evidentiary standards**: require traceable inputs and reproducible methods; penalise black-box claims.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Parties MUST have a workable path to challenge decisions, including access to the evidence necessary to contest classification and routing.
- Courts/tribunals MUST be able to order **disclosure and correction** (including restatements) when drift or misclassification is proven.
- Where immediate regression risk exists, appeals SHOULD be non-suspensive by default, with explicit, logged exceptions only.

---

## B03.CH05.P02.S05 — Separation rails: incompatible roles, cooling-off, conflict-of-interest rules

Separation is enforced through **rails**: explicit incompatibilities, cooling-off periods, and conflict-of-interest regimes that
prevent “stacked control” across layers.

### Separation rails (minimum)
- **Incompatible roles**: no concurrent control over execution + audit; audit + vendor delivery; oversight + regulated entity governance.
- **Cooling-off**: time barriers for movement between regulated actors, vendors, auditors, and oversight roles.
- **Conflict-of-interest**: public disclosure, recusal rules, ownership/beneficial-control restrictions, and enforcement with sanctions.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Role incompatibilities MUST be codified and enforced; “informal” separation is non-conform.
- Cooling-off and disclosure MUST cover consultancy pathways and shadow influence (advisory boards, subcontracting, intermediaries).
- Violations MUST have consequences (nullification where appropriate, sanctions, and mandatory reconstitution/rotation triggers).
