# B03.CH01.P03 — Minimum set of political criteria: what “OBA-conform policy” means

This paragraph defines the **minimum political criteria** for calling a policy **OBA-conform**. “OBA-conform” is not a rhetorical
signal; it is a **testable status claim** that must be supported by **measurable outcomes**, **auditable procedures**, and explicit
**definition-set references**. The minimum set exists to prevent: (i) symbolic adoption without enforcement, (ii) semantic drift,
(iii) exception-driven hollowing-out, and (iv) uncontrolled forks presented as “still OBA”.

---

## B03.CH01.P03.S01 — Political test questions per pillar (E, caps, commons/ΔG, R/B, ΔM)

OBA-conform policy must be expressible in a small set of **political test questions** aligned to the pillars. These questions are
designed to be answerable using evidence, not ideology.

### Core test questions (minimum)
- **E (extraction)**: *Where is extraction/toll taking reduced, by which mechanism, and how is it measured?*
- **Caps**: *Which claim/power concentrations are capped, how is bindingness enforced, and what is the anti-avoidance design?*
- **Commons / ΔG**: *What commons substitute is expanded or protected, what are the boundary rules, and what is the maintenance plan?*
- **R/B**: *Which lower bounds are guaranteed, for whom, and how does the distribution profile improve (not just the average)?*
- **ΔM**: *How is allocative space routed across channels, and how can affected parties contest decisions?*

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Any OBA-conform claim MUST answer all pillars **explicitly** (silence is non-conform).
- If a pillar is declared “not applicable”, the policy MUST provide a **formal justification** and show why no indirect regressions
  are introduced elsewhere (look-through requirement).

---

## B03.CH01.P03.S02 — Required evidence: measurability, auditability, public definition set

Political legitimacy under OBA depends on **evidence-bounded governance**: definitions must be public, measurements reproducible,
and audits operational.

### Evidence requirements (minimum)
- **Measurability**: metrics exist with defined inputs, uncertainty handling, and baseline.
- **Auditability**: independent verification is possible (data access, logs, methods).
- **Public definition set**: every key term used in claims is pinned to a canonical definition-set ID/version.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- OBA-conform policy MUST publish: (a) the **definition-set references**, (b) a **measurement plan**, and (c) an **audit plan**.
- If evidence is missing or non-reproducible, the claim MUST be treated as **non-conform** (fail-closed).

---

## B03.CH01.P03.S03 — Anti-avoidance as a democratic problem: function-over-label and look-through

Avoidance is treated as a democratic problem because it enables **formal compliance with substantive regression**. OBA therefore
requires anti-avoidance discipline at the political level, not only in enforcement practice.

### Core principles
- **Function-over-label**: classify by economic/control function, not naming, packaging, or narrative.
- **Look-through**: trace through intermediaries, chains, and stacked contracts to the real control/benefit/block structure.
- **Anti-laundering**: prevent “policy laundering” where extraction re-enters via delegated/outsourced layers.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Any OBA-conform policy MUST define anti-avoidance rules sufficient to detect: relabeling, threshold gaming, exception stacking,
  and chain-based evasion.
- If look-through is operationally impossible (opacity), the policy MUST treat the relevant channel as **prohibited** or impose a
  hard stop until auditability is restored.

---

## B03.CH01.P03.S04 — Exception discipline: sunsets/rollback as democratic self-binding

Exceptions are permitted only as **explicit, bounded deviations** with automatic expiry and reversible implementation. Without this,
exceptions become the primary vehicle for capture and semantic drift.

### Core principles
- **Sunsets**: every exception expires automatically unless reauthorised under stricter conditions.
- **Rollback**: reversibility is engineered (legal, technical, contractual).
- **Non-stackability**: exceptions cannot accumulate into a parallel regime.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Every exception MUST have: scope, rationale, duration, measurable impact, audit triggers, and rollback requirements.
- Exception renewals MUST be treated as escalation events and require **higher evidentiary and procedural thresholds**.

---

## B03.CH01.P03.S05 — Compatibility/forks: name usage and the boundary of “still OBA”

Name usage is governed because labels are the primary vector of semantic drift. “OBA-conform” requires a defined boundary between
**compatible variation** and **fork-level deviation**.

### Core principles
- **Compatibility is a status**: a policy is compatible, partially compatible, or incompatible based on tests.
- **Fork honesty**: constitutive deviations must be declared as forks and cannot be marketed as “OBA” by default.
- **Version discipline**: policies must pin their claims to definition-set versions and changelog their deltas.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Any public use of “OBA-conform” or “OBA-compatible” MUST include the relevant **compatibility label** and definition-set version.
- If a policy alters non-negotiables, measurability, auditability, or contestability, it MUST be classified as a **fork** and MUST
  NOT be presented as “still OBA” without explicit fork labeling.
