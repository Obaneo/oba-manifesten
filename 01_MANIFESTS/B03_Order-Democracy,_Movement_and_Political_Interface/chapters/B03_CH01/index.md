# B03.CH01 — Index (English)

## Chapter title
**B03.CH01 — Kernel: OBA core in one page (reference to Kernel Spec)**

## Purpose of this chapter
CH01 is the **one-page political interface** to the OBA core: it states what the political layer may rely on, what is non-negotiable, what “OBA-conform policy” minimally means, which democratic risks Book 3 addresses, and how to read Book 3 without drift.
