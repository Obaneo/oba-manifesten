# B03.CH09 — Representation and participation: the role of citizens, workers, commons members, and the state

This chapter defines **representation and participation** as **order-critical interfaces**: mechanisms that translate lived experience, role-based
authority, and institutional constraints into **governable order criteria** (E-reduction, caps integrity, R/B floors, commons ΔG, ΔM channel discipline,
auditability). It separates *who participates* from *what they are authorised to decide*, and treats participation channels as **capture surfaces**
unless roles, authority, evidence, and appeal rails are explicit.
