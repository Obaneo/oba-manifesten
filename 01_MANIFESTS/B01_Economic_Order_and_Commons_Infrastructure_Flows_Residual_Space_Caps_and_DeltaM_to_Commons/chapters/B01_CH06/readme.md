# B01.CH06 — Commons as an economic base layer: ownership, access, maintenance, and rights

This chapter specifies **commons** as an economic base layer within OBA: not “public property” and not “private property”, but a **regime of
access, maintenance, and governance** designed to reduce burdens (L), stabilize residual space (R/B), and structurally substitute for markets
that regenerate parasitic tolls and lock-in (E).

Commons is treated as a first-class order object: it has a rights layer, governance minima, financing and maintenance discipline, measurement
requirements (ΔG + quality), and boundary rules that prevent re-privatization and commons-washing.

---

## What this chapter is

- A **definition and delimitation** of commons as an order regime (access + maintenance + governance).
- A **rights specification**: how access/use/contribution/exclusion/re-entry are defined without turning commons into gatekeeping.
- A **governance minimum**: decision structure, transparency, conflict resolution, rotation, and anti-capture requirements.
- A **maintenance-first financing model**: capex/opex discipline and quality norms as constitutive requirements.
- A **substitution doctrine**: when commons replaces parasitic markets, and how transition is executed without continuity loss.
- A **measurement interface**: ΔG(t) plus quality indicators, with auditability and versioning.

---

## What this chapter is not

- Not a romantic “community” narrative; commons is specified as a controllable infrastructure layer.
- Not identical to “the state”: state ownership or public service delivery may exist, but commons is defined by its regime, not by the owner.
- Not a license for surveillance or behavioral scoring; access and contribution must remain compatible with privacy minimalism and dignity.

---

## Binding constraints

- **Maintenance-first:** a commons without enforceable maintenance and quality norms is not a commons; it is a degradation pathway.
- **Non-exclusivity by default:** exclusion is allowed only under explicit conditions (capacity, abuse, contribution) with proportionality and
  contestability.
- **Anti-privatization:** legally inalienable elements and boundary rules prevent re-sale, concession drift, and toll layering.
- **Anti-capture:** governance must prevent vendor dominance, elite gatekeeping, and exception culture.
- **Measurability:** commons claims require ΔG(t) and quality evidence; “we built a program” is not proof.

---

## Chapter structure

- **P01 — Definition:** commons as a regime; delimitation; conditions for exclusion; anti-privatization; commons-washing detection.
- **P02 — Rights layer:** access/use/contribution/exclusion/re-entry; portability across relocation/job change/regime change.
- **P03 — Governance minimum:** decision structure, conflict resolution, transparency, rotation, fork compatibility.
- **P04 — Maintenance & financing:** capex/opex discipline, stewardship roles, quality norms, financing mix (incl. ΔM→commons link).
- **P05 — Substitution:** when commons replaces parasitic markets; substitution patterns; transition and continuity; interaction with caps/E.
- **P06 — Measurement:** ΔG(t) and quality; equity without identity categories; auditability and version control.
- **P07 — Interfaces:** commons–state–market contracts; procurement and open standards; data/interoperability; legal protection; escalation/repair.

## Outputs expected from this chapter

When implemented (via Codex artifacts), this chapter should enable:

- A **commons registry**: commons_id, scope, access rules, governance body, maintenance budgets, service levels, incident logs.
- Standard **rights templates**: access criteria, fair-use bands, contribution definitions, exclusion and re-entry procedures, portability rules.
- **Governance templates**: quorum rules, decision types, rotation/incompatibility rules, dispute escalation ladders, audit-of-the-auditor hooks.
- **Maintenance packs**: capex/opex plans, backlog thresholds, quality metrics, procurement requirements (open standards).
- **Boundary rule packs**: prohibitions on toll layering, exclusivity, lock-in around commons access.
- **ΔG + quality dashboards**: publishable aggregates with method_id and revision policy.

## Interfaces

- **CH02 (P/E/C decomposition):** commons contributions (C) and commons substitution reduce E and restructure flows.
- **CH03 (R/B):** commons must reduce mandatory burdens (L) and stabilize tails (share_below_alpha).
- **CH04 (caps):** commons must not become an accumulation bypass via concessions, privileged claims, or governance capture.
- **CH05 (E phase-down):** commons is a structural instrument for substituting toll markets and collapsing lock-in.
- **CH07 (ΔM routing):** ΔM→commons requires governance minima, traceability, and maintenance-first budgeting.
- **CH10 (audit):** measurement rules, versioning, and public reporting discipline ensure commons claims remain falsifiable.
