## Paragraph detail

### B01.CH06.P01 — Definition of commons in OBA: not “public property” and not “private”, but an order of use and maintenance
- B01.CH06.P01.S01 — Definition: commons as a regime of access + maintenance + governance (not merely an ownership form)
- B01.CH06.P01.S02 — Delimitation: commons versus public service, state ownership, cooperative, and market good
- B01.CH06.P01.S03 — Non-exclusivity and conditions: when exclusion is permitted (capacity, abuse, contribution)
- B01.CH06.P01.S04 — Anti-privatization rule: which elements must be legally inalienable
- B01.CH06.P01.S05 — “Commons-washing” detection: signals that something is called commons but functions as a private toll

### B01.CH06.P02 — Rights layer: access, use, contribution, exclusion, re-entry
- B01.CH06.P02.S01 — Access rights: baseline access, priority rights, conditional access (criteria)
- B01.CH06.P02.S02 — Use rights: bands, fair use, quotas, and scarcity protocols
- B01.CH06.P02.S03 — Contribution duties: maintenance, time, money, data — what counts as contribution and how it is weighted
- B01.CH06.P02.S04 — Exclusion: grounds, proportionality, term, and objection/appeal
- B01.CH06.P02.S05 — Re-entry: repair paths, amnesty criteria, and anti-stigmatization rules
- B01.CH06.P02.S06 — Rights portability: what happens under relocation, job change, or regime change

### B01.CH06.P03 — Governance minimum: decision-making, conflict resolution, transparency, rotation
- B01.CH06.P03.S01 — Minimum structure: body, mandate, quorum, and basic decision types
- B01.CH06.P03.S02 — Decision-making: consensus/majority/lot — selection criteria per decision type
- B01.CH06.P03.S03 — Conflict resolution: escalation ladder, independent arbitration, and sanction paths
- B01.CH06.P03.S04 — Transparency: open books, audit log, public reporting (without privacy breach)
- B01.CH06.P03.S05 — Rotation and anti-capture: terms, incompatibilities, and conflicts of interest
- B01.CH06.P03.S06 — Fork compatibility: how governance variants are allowed without core loss

### B01.CH06.P04 — Maintenance and financing: capex/opex, stewardship, quality norms
- B01.CH06.P04.S01 — Capex: investment logic, prioritization, and depreciation within a commons regime
- B01.CH06.P04.S02 — Opex: structural operations, maintenance cycles, and minimum service levels
- B01.CH06.P04.S03 — Stewardship: roles (steward/manager, supervisor, user), responsibilities, liability
- B01.CH06.P04.S04 — Quality norms: output norms, safety, accessibility, and continuity
- B01.CH06.P04.S05 — Financing mix: contributions, tariffs, public funds, ΔM→commons (link to CH07)
- B01.CH06.P04.S06 — Perverse incentives: preventing underinvestment, overuse, and rent-seeking within commons

### B01.CH06.P05 — Commons as a substitute for parasitic markets (where and when)
- B01.CH06.P05.S01 — Selection criterion: where markets structurally produce toll/lock-in (essentials, networks, natural monopolies)
- B01.CH06.P05.S02 — Substitution patterns: full replacement, hybrid layer, or a public option next to the market
- B01.CH06.P05.S03 — Transition: user migration, contract-break risk, and continuity guarantees
- B01.CH06.P05.S04 — Interaction with caps/E reduction: how commons substitution lowers E and raises R/B

### B01.CH06.P06 — Measurement: ΔG(t) and quality indicators (not only quantity)
- B01.CH06.P06.S01 — ΔG(t): definition of commons growth (capacity, reach, coverage)
- B01.CH06.P06.S02 — Quality: availability, reliability, accessibility, and user satisfaction (measurable)
- B01.CH06.P06.S03 — Equity metrics: who benefits (distribution), without identity categories
- B01.CH06.P06.S04 — Auditability: measurement rules, revisions, and version control (anti-drift)

### B01.CH06.P07 — Relation commons–state–market: interface contracts and boundary rules
- B01.CH06.P07.S01 — Boundary rules: what markets may not do around commons (tolls, exclusive access, lock-in)
- B01.CH06.P07.S02 — Interface contracts: procurement, supply contracts, SLAs, and open standards
- B01.CH06.P07.S03 — Data and interoperability: preventing vendor lock-in and platform capture
- B01.CH06.P07.S04 — Legal protection: inalienability, public review, and anti-privatization clauses
- B01.CH06.P07.S05 — Escalation and repair: what happens under mismanagement, capture, or degradation of commons
