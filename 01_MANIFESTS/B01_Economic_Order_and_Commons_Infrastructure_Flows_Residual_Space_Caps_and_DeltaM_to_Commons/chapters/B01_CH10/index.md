## Paragraph detail

### B01.CH10.P01 — Minimum dataset: what must be measured to keep E, R/B, caps, and commons in order
- B01.CH10.P01.S01 — Data minimum per pillar: (i) E measurement, (ii) R/B profiles, (iii) cap effectiveness, (iv) ΔG/commons quality
- B01.CH10.P01.S02 — Transaction flows: price, volume, contract type, fee structure, term
- B01.CH10.P01.S03 — Cost and margin data: minimum cost categories for E detection (fee-stacks, markups)
- B01.CH10.P01.S04 — Claims and ownership data: beneficial control, exposure, collateral, rights/concessions
- B01.CH10.P01.S05 — Commons data: capacity, coverage, quality, maintenance cycles, access
- B01.CH10.P01.S06 — Uncertainty: error margins, imputation, and explicit “unknown” categories (no silent filling)

### B01.CH10.P02 — Consolidation: through holdings/chains (ultimate beneficial control and economic reality)
- B01.CH10.P02.S01 — UBC/UBO principle: who controls and who benefits (control test + benefit test)
- B01.CH10.P02.S02 — Look-through valuation: trusts, foundations, holdings, SPVs, and nominee structures
- B01.CH10.P02.S03 — Economic exposure: derivatives, synthetic positions, revenue sharing, and side letters
- B01.CH10.P02.S04 — Chains and platforms: attribution through multi-actor stacks (platform–supplier–financier)
- B01.CH10.P02.S05 — Cross-border: treaty shopping, transfer pricing, IP location, re-domiciliation
- B01.CH10.P02.S06 — Consolidation conflicts: objection procedures, evidence standards, and burden-shifting (where appropriate)

### B01.CH10.P03 — Audit architecture: independent functions, powers, logging, sampling vs full coverage
- B01.CH10.P03.S01 — Roles: execution, audit, oversight, and judicial review (separation of functions)
- B01.CH10.P03.S02 — Powers: access, compulsion, forensic access, and international cooperation
- B01.CH10.P03.S03 — Logging and traceability: end-to-end log of flows, definitions, and decisions (no black boxes)
- B01.CH10.P03.S04 — Sampling versus full coverage: when sampling is sufficient and when full coverage is required
- B01.CH10.P03.S05 — Trigger-based audits: thresholds, anomalies, and red flags (fee-stacks, concentration, margin spikes)
- B01.CH10.P03.S06 — Audit of auditors: rotation, independent review, and anti-capture measures

### B01.CH10.P04 — Public reporting: indicators, definitions, revisions, versioning (against semantic drift)
- B01.CH10.P04.S01 — Dashboard minimum: E indicators, R/B profiles, cap exceedances, ΔG/commons quality, ΔM channel distribution
- B01.CH10.P04.S02 — Definition publication: concept set, exclusions, and reference budgets (L)
- B01.CH10.P04.S03 — Revisions: correction mechanism, restatements, and transparency on changes
- B01.CH10.P04.S04 — Version control: changelog, rationale, and backward compatibility (where possible)
- B01.CH10.P04.S05 — Open calculation rules: replicability by external parties (without forcing privacy loss)

### B01.CH10.P05 — Privacy and proportionality: measuring without total surveillance (principles + boundaries)
- B01.CH10.P05.S01 — Data minimalism: collect only what is necessary for the four pillars (E, R/B, caps, commons)
- B01.CH10.P05.S02 — Aggregation and pseudonymization: standards for public reporting and internal audit
- B01.CH10.P05.S03 — Access control: who may see what, under which conditions, with logging
- B01.CH10.P05.S04 — Forbidden linkages: where profiling/control becomes order-incompatible surveillance
- B01.CH10.P05.S05 — Emergency regime: what is temporarily allowed in crisis, for how long, and how rollback is enforced
