# B01.CH08.P02 — Fiscality: shifting away from labour/consumption toward extraction/accumulation (OBA logic)

This paragraph specifies fiscality as an **order instrument** rather than a revenue-only instrument. In OBA, fiscal design is evaluated by its
effects on (i) **E reduction**, (ii) **R/B stabilization** (especially tails), and (iii) **commons build-up and maintenance**—while avoiding the
creation of new toll layers through compliance ecosystems, mandatory intermediaries, or opaque exemptions.

The central design move is **incidence-first**: fiscality must be defined by who actually bears the burden and how it reshapes incentives,
rather than by legal labels. OBA therefore prioritizes a structural shift away from labour/consumption bases toward extraction and accumulation
bases, with explicit anti-arbitrage and consolidation discipline.

---

## B01.CH08.P02.S01 — Purpose of fiscality in OBA: press down E, stabilize R/B, fund commons without creating new tolls

### Purposes (binding)
Fiscality must serve three purposes simultaneously:

1. **Press down E:** reduce parasitic margins, fee-stacks, scarcity rents, and monopoly tolls by raising their cost and removing their advantage.
2. **Stabilize R/B tails:** prevent sub-α clustering and reduce volatility driven by essentials burdens and debt cascades.
3. **Fund commons (ΔG + maintenance):** finance baseline access and maintenance-first continuity without producing privatized toll perimeters.

### Non-permitted purposes (binding)
- Fiscality may not be used to **preserve asset values** or privileged claim structures as a primary goal.
- Fiscality may not be implemented via systems that function as an **administrative toll gate** (excess proof burden, complex carve-outs).

---

## B01.CH08.P02.S02 — Tax base: labour/consumption versus extraction/accumulation (definition criteria)

OBA requires explicit base definitions that match the P/E/C and caps logic.

### Base families (minimum; binding)
- **Labour/consumption bases:** wages, payroll-like bases, broad consumption taxes.
- **Extraction bases:** bases linked to E mechanisms (fee-stacks, monopoly rents, scarcity rents, abusive take-rates, junk fees).
- **Accumulation bases:** bases linked to W and I concentration (wealth holdings, capital gains, claim stacks, high-end income flows).

### Definition criteria (binding)
- **Substance over form:** classify based on economic reality (control/benefit/exposure), not contractual label.
- **Attribution and consolidation:** apply look-through rules through holdings, funds, nominees, and synthetic exposures.
- **Incidence-first:** publish and test who bears the burden; legal payer is not sufficient.

---

## B01.CH08.P02.S03 — Levies on E: design principles (attribution, sectors, fee-stacks, market power)

Levies on E are designed to collapse the payoff of toll mechanisms and to force redesign toward P/commons-compatible structures.

### Design principles (minimum; binding)
- **Attribution to the gate:** levy the entity/position that controls the toll interface (platform gate, monopoly concession, billing stack),
  not only the visible downstream seller.
- **Fee-stack decomposition:** levy based on the presence and depth of fee-stacks (intermediary layers, junk fee shares, take-rate stacking).
- **Market power linkage:** where concentration and structural scarcity exist, levies must target excess margins above functional bands.
- **Anti-pass-through checks:** detect and constrain pass-through that simply raises essentials burdens (L) for households.

### Conformance rule (binding)
- An “E levy” that is easy to pass through into essentials prices without controls becomes a burdens tax and fails the R/B objective.

---

## B01.CH08.P02.S04 — Levies on accumulation: interaction with W-MAX/I-MAX (prevent double bypass)

Accumulation levies must reinforce caps and must not create avoidance incentives that route around W-MAX/I-MAX.

### Interaction rules (minimum; binding)
- **Base alignment:** definitions of wealth and income bases must align with W-MAX and I-MAX counting rules (including look-through and in-kind).
- **No bypass creation:** avoid designs that encourage shifting income into untaxed vehicles, or shifting wealth into synthetic exposures.
- **Coordination with remedies:** where caps trigger reallocation (e.g., to commons), fiscality must not penalize compliant reallocation routes more
  than evasive routes.

### Conformance rule (binding)
- If fiscal design increases the attractiveness of reclassification (I→W, W→corporate shell, W→synthetics), it is non-conforming until
  anti-avoidance is tightened.

---

## B01.CH08.P02.S05 — Anti-arbitrage: internationalization, transfer pricing, IP boxes, and consolidation principles

Without anti-arbitrage, the tax base migrates and the system regresses into hollow rules.

### Required anti-arbitrage rails (minimum; binding)
- **Consolidation across borders:** beneficial control and economic exposure tests apply to offshore vehicles and nominee structures.
- **Transfer pricing discipline:** enforce substance requirements and publishable red-flag indicators for profit shifting.
- **IP and licensing scrutiny:** treat IP-box style structures as presumptively high-risk for E unless duration/market-power and cost evidence
  justify.
- **Exit routes and hooks:** define exit taxation / withholding / reporting hooks for re-domiciliation and control-center moves.
- **Treaty-aware enforcement:** denial-of-benefits style logic where structures exist primarily for arbitrage.

### Conformance rule (binding)
- If cross-border structures can erase incidence visibility, fiscality becomes optional for high positions and fails caps compatibility.

---

## B01.CH08.P02.S06 — Transparency: public accountability of revenues and destination (commons / GROSS≈NET)

Fiscal legitimacy requires that the public can see what is collected, from whom (in incidence terms), and what it finances.

### Required transparency surface (minimum; binding)
- **Revenue composition:** publish revenue by base family (labour/consumption vs extraction vs accumulation).
- **Incidence reporting:** publish distributional incidence estimates as a bounded, revisable method (method_id, assumptions, uncertainty).
- **Destination reporting:** publish destination shares: commons capex/opex, social security stabilizers, enforcement/audit capacity.
- **Overhead disclosure:** publish execution costs and complexity indicators (administrative L).
- **Revision discipline:** no silent restatements; publish changes, rationale, and comparability impact.

### Conformance rule (binding)
- Fiscal systems that cannot publish incidence and destination in a replicable way are capture surfaces and must fail closed until repaired.
