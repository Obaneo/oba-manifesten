### B01.CH02.P02 — Definitions of P (productive), E (extractive/parasitic component), C (commons contribution)
- B01.CH02.P02.S01 — P: criteria for productive value creation (output, service, maintenance, reproduction)
- B01.CH02.P02.S02 — E: criteria for extraction (toll/claim above functional cost; lock-in; scarcity construction)
- B01.CH02.P02.S03 — C: criteria for commons contribution (build/maintain/access; non-exclusive availability)
- B01.CH02.P02.S04 — Mixed forms: when a flow is split into P+E or P+C (attribution rule)
- B01.CH02.P02.S05 — Negative and zero values: when C or P can be zero/negative (degradation, depletion, damage)

### B01.CH02.P03 — Basic accounting: attribution of flows to actors and positions
- B01.CH02.P03.S01 — Actor versus position: owner, manager, executor, platform, financier as distinct roles
- B01.CH02.P03.S02 — Attribution principle: “who receives what” and “who controls/can block” (ultimate control)
- B01.CH02.P03.S03 — Consolidation: through holdings/vehicles (economic reality over legal form)
- B01.CH02.P03.S04 — Chain attribution: distributing P/E/C across multiple links (supply chain, platform stack)
- B01.CH02.P03.S05 — Prevent double counting: rules for gross/net flows and internal pass-through charging

### B01.CH02.P04 — Node types (household, firm, financial entity, state, commons institution)
- B01.CH02.P04.S01 — Household: income mix, burdens, access to commons
- B01.CH02.P04.S02 — Firm: production, pricing, market power, surplus distribution
- B01.CH02.P04.S03 — Financial entity: claims, interest, securitization, collateral circuits
- B01.CH02.P04.S04 — State: fiscality, regulation, order enforcement, public allocation
- B01.CH02.P04.S05 — Commons institution: governance, access, maintenance, non-exclusive provisions

### B01.CH02.P05 — Edge cases: mixed flows and multi-actor chains
- B01.CH02.P05.S01 — Innovation and IP: when temporary surplus is P versus when it becomes E (duration/market-power criterion)
- B01.CH02.P05.S02 — Interest and credit: productive credit versus structural debt extraction (function/alternatives criterion)
- B01.CH02.P05.S03 — Rent and land: cost-covering stewardship versus land rent as E (scarcity/claim criterion)
- B01.CH02.P05.S04 — Platforms: service (P) versus toll/lock-in (E), and data-commons (C)
- B01.CH02.P05.S05 — Public/private hybrids: concessions, PPP, quasi-public bodies — split rules for P/E/C

### B01.CH02.P06 — Measurement strategy: the minimum you must observe to distinguish P/E/C

- B01.CH02.P06.S04 — Publishable indicators: E share per sector, P/C build-up, concentration and lock-in metrics
- B01.CH02.P06.S05 — Version control of definitions: how changes in P/E/C rules remain traceable (anti-drift)
