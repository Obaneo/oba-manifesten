# B01.CH07 — ΔM to commons: allocation channels, governance, and anti-capture

This chapter specifies how **ΔM (monetary space/expansion)** is treated as an order-critical power and how it must be routed to **commons**
rather than to asset support and claim inflation. ΔM is not a technical footnote: it is a constitutive allocation lever that determines
whether the order builds capacity (ΔG), stabilizes residual space (R/B), and accelerates E phase-down—or whether it recreates accumulation and
extraction via the asset channel.

The chapter defines:
- the meaning of ΔM in the economic layer,
- the admissible allocation channels and forbidden/risk channels,
- an allocation priority matrix (commons continuity → R/B floor → E reduction),
- governance and contestability requirements,
- anti-capture rails (auditability, plurality, fail-closed discipline),
- and a public dashboard surface for verification.

---

## What this chapter is

- A **routing doctrine**: how monetary space is allowed to enter the economy without becoming hidden asset support.
- A **governance spec**: role separation, checks & balances, and contestable procedures for ΔM decisions.
- A **capture-prevention module**: structural constraints against vendor/sector capture and exception drift.
- A **measurement interface**: required dashboards that make ΔM claims falsifiable and reproducible.

---

## What this chapter is not

- Not a macroeconomic theory treatise; it does not debate schools. It specifies order constraints and verification surfaces.
- Not a full banking regulation code; detailed instruments/templates belong in the Codex (Book X).
- Not a replacement for caps: ΔM discipline and caps (CH04) must be co-designed; neither substitutes for the other.

---

## Binding constraints

- **No black boxes:** every ΔM flow must be traceable from creation → channel → recipient → final destination.
- **No asset-support relapse:** channels whose primary effect is asset inflation and W accumulation are forbidden/risk channels.
- **Commons-first priority:** ΔM is prioritized for commons continuity/build-up (ΔG + quality), then R/B floor stabilization, then accelerated E
  reduction—subject to cap compatibility.
- **Fail-closed:** under uncertainty or missing evidence, release is paused; “trust us” is not admissible.
- **Contestability:** objections/appeals are part of the design; legitimacy is procedural and auditable, not rhetorical.

---

## Chapter structure

- **P01 — Definition & power:** what ΔM is and why allocation is constitutive.
- **P02 — Channels:** state, bank/credit, market/asset, direct commons channels; forbidden/risk channels; ex-ante channel selection rule.
- **P03 — Criteria:** priority matrix and minimal conditions (no bypass of caps, no new tolls, measurability).
- **P04 — Governance:** decision bodies, mandates, checks, transparency, objection/appeal without system paralysis.
- **P05 — Anti-capture:** auditability, plurality, transparent rules, fail-closed, anti-lobby rails, red-teaming.
- **P06 — Finance interface:** how banks/credit/collateral/risk models can reintroduce accumulation; constraints and exits.
- **P07 — Accountability:** public dashboards, distribution impacts, E impacts, revisions, and independent verification.

## Outputs expected from this chapter

When implemented (via Codex artifacts), this chapter should enable:

- A **ΔM ledger**: standardized records linking creation events to channels and end destinations.
- A **public dashboard surface** that publishes:
  - ΔM volumes by channel,
  - recipient classes,
  - commons outputs (ΔG + quality),
  - R/B distribution impacts,
  - E-reduction impacts,
  - and revision/restatement policy.
- A **channel compliance regime**: automatic detection of leakage into asset support and accumulation.
- A **governance audit pack**: decision logs, exception registers, contact registers, red-team reports, and audit-of-auditors outputs.

---

## Interfaces

- **CH03 (R/B):** ΔM choices must show up as R/B floor stabilization and tail improvement (distribution-first).
- **CH04 (caps):** ΔM routing must not create cap bypasses via asset inflation, privileged returns, or concentration.
- **CH05 (E phase-down):** ΔM can accelerate E reduction by funding commons substitution and reducing scarcity rents.
- **CH06 (commons):** ΔM-to-commons requires governance minima, maintenance-first budgets, and measurable ΔG/quality.
- **CH10 (audit):** data minimums, consolidation rules, and public reporting discipline are required for traceability and falsifiability.
