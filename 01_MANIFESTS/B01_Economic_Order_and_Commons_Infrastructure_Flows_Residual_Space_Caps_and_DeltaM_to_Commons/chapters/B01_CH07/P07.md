# B01.CH07.P07 — Accountability: public dashboards and auditable accounting of ΔM flows

This paragraph specifies the minimum **public accountability surface** required for ΔM governance to be verifiable. ΔM claims cannot be
validated by speeches, mandates, or total spending numbers. They are validated by whether ΔM is **routed through compatible channels**, by
whether it produces **commons output (ΔG + quality)**, by whether it stabilizes **R/B tails**, and by whether it accelerates **E reduction**
without leakage into asset inflation and W accumulation.

Accountability outputs must be strictly privacy-minimal while still sufficient to detect washing, selective routing, emergency drift, and
capture.

---

## B01.CH07.P07.S01 — Dashboard minimum: ΔM volume, channel distribution, recipient classes, commons output (ΔG + quality)

A ΔM dashboard must disclose how much monetary space was created, through which channels it moved, who received it, and what commons capacity
it produced.

### Required dashboard elements (minimum; binding)

**ΔM volumes**
- total ΔM by time window (gross and, where relevant, net after unwind),
- decomposition by accounting form/category (e.g., credit facilities, guarantees, direct funds, purchases), without relying on opaque labels.

**Channel distribution**
- shares by channel family: state / bank-credit / market-asset / direct commons,
- explicit marking of any market/asset channel usage (presumptively risk; triggers justification and review).

**Recipient classes**
- allocation by recipient class: commons funds, public agencies, banks/facilities, operators/contractors (aggregated),
- concentration indicators for recipients (top-N shares) where publishable.

**Commons output**
- ΔG(t) indicators: capacity/coverage additions attributable to ΔM programs (domain-specific),
- quality indicators: availability/reliability/accessibility/maintenance readiness measures,
- maintenance funding status: opex/maintenance commitments vs realized execution (avoid “build and neglect”).

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- “Money allocated” is not evidence. Commons outputs (ΔG + quality) must be shown.
- If ΔM is high while ΔG/quality stagnate, ΔM is leaking into claims, fees, or inefficiency and must trigger audit and redesign.

## B01.CH07.P07.S02 — Distribution reporting: impact on R/B profiles (p10/p25/median; share below α)

ΔM governance must publish how routing impacts household residual space, especially tails.

### Required distribution reporting (minimum; binding)
- **R/B profiles:** p10, p25, median (and where possible p75) by time window, using pinned definition sets.
- **share_below_alpha:** share below α (by region/cohort within join-safety constraints).
- **Tail stress proxies:** arrears/disconnections/evictions (aggregated) and time-to-recovery proxies after shocks where relevant.
- **Linkage to channels:** attribution of major R/B movements to channel/program categories where feasible (without overclaiming causality).

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Average-only reporting is invalid. If tails worsen while totals look good, the system is non-conforming.
- Claims of “ΔM helps households” require visible tail stabilization evidence.

---

## B01.CH07.P07.S03 — E impact: indicators for reduction of parasitic flows per sector/flow type

Because ΔM can either dismantle or reinforce extraction, accountability must include E-related indicators.

### Required E-impact indicators (minimum; binding)
- **E share by sector/flow type:** publishable E proxies (fee-stack depth, margin band breaches, take-rate measures, lock-in proxies).
- **Essentials price/burden link:** where relevant, show whether burdens (L components) fell due to commons substitution rather than subsidies.
- **Leakage tests:** explicit reporting on detected leakage into asset inflation, privileged concessions, or new fee ecosystems.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- If ΔM usage correlates with rising essentials prices, concentration, or fee-stack growth, the routing is failing and must be requalified.
- E-impact reporting must be compatible with CH05 typology and CH02 decomposition.

## B01.CH07.P07.S04 — Revisions and errors: how corrections are made public (without reputation asymmetry)

Accountability requires correction discipline. Errors and revisions must be visible; otherwise dashboards become propaganda.

### Required revision policy (minimum; binding)
- **Method discipline:** publish method_id, definition-set IDs, and revision policy for all dashboard series.
- **Restatements:** when definitions or methods change materially, publish restated series or explicit non-restatement with rationale.
- **Error reporting:** publish identified errors, their impact, and the corrective action taken (aggregated).
- **No silent edits:** changes to past publications require versioned releases and change logs.

### Conformance rule (binding)
- If revisions are silent or selectively applied, accountability collapses and ΔM governance must fail closed until integrity is restored.

---

## B01.CH07.P07.S05 — Independent verification: external auditors, open calculation rules, public replication of results

ΔM dashboards must be verifiable by independent parties; otherwise capture and drift are undetectable.

### Required verification surface (minimum; binding)
- **External audit:** independent auditors with access to underlying logs and the ability to test leakage and concentration effects.
- **Open calculation rules:** publish the calculation logic and aggregation procedures sufficiently for replication (privacy-safe).
- **Replication support:** provide public data extracts at aggregate levels enabling third-party replication of key indicators.
- **Audit-of-auditors:** rotation and peer review of auditors; publish audit summaries and remediation tracking.

### Conformance rule (binding)
- If verification depends on proprietary tools, undisclosed datasets, or closed models, the system is non-conforming because it cannot be
  contested.
