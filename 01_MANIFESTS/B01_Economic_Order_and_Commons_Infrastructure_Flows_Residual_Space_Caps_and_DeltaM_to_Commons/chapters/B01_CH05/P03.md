# B01.CH05.P03 — Design criteria: when is a measure OBA-compatible (effects on E, R/B, commons, caps)

This paragraph defines the **compatibility gate** for any intervention that claims to reduce parasitic flows. A measure is not OBA-compatible
because it is well-intended, redistributive, or popular. It is compatible only if it satisfies a binding criteria set across the economic
pillars: **E reduction**, **R/B improvement (distribution-first)**, **commons build-up (ΔG + quality)**, and **cap compatibility**—with
robustness against avoidance and with auditable evidence.

These criteria are designed to prevent washing and to force structural correction when outcomes do not match claims.

---

## B01.CH05.P03.S01 — Effect on E: demonstrable reduction of the extractive component (not mere relabeling)

An intervention must demonstrate a real reduction in extractive components, not a change in terminology or accounting location.

### Required E-effect evidence (minimum; binding)
- **Decomposition:** show the flow split (P/E/C where applicable) before and after intervention using the same definition set.
- **Mechanism removal:** identify which extraction mechanism is being reduced (toll, lock-in, scarcity construction, fee-stack, monopoly
  margin, debt cascade) and how the design prevents reconstitution.
- **No displacement:** show that E is not simply shifted upstream/downstream or into new fee categories.

### Conformance rule (binding)
- If measured E share does not decline (or cannot be measured), the measure cannot be certified as E-reducing.

---

## B01.CH05.P03.S02 — Effect on R/B: improvement at the bottom/medians, not only on the average

E reduction must translate into improved residual space for households, especially in the tails. Average-only gains are insufficient.

### Required R/B-effect evidence (minimum; binding)
- **Distributional reporting:** at minimum p10, p25, median R/B and share_below_alpha before/after, within defined windows.
- **Tail stabilization:** demonstrate reduced tail volatility, arrears cascades, or shock-driven collapses where relevant.
- **Cohort/region visibility:** where the intervention is sectoral or regional, report effects in relevant segments (within privacy rails).

### Conformance rule (binding)
- If the share below α rises, or p10/p25 deteriorate while averages improve, the measure is non-conforming or mis-specified and requires
  redesign.

---

## B01.CH05.P03.S03 — Effect on commons: build/maintenance/access increase (ΔG and quality)

Durable E reduction typically requires substituting toll markets with commons/public options or strengthening contestable access. Therefore a
measure must show its relationship to commons capacity.

### Required commons-effect evidence (minimum; binding)
- **ΔG(t) direction:** show whether commons capacity/coverage grows (or at least does not degrade) in the impacted domain.
- **Quality and access:** publish minimum indicators for availability, reliability, accessibility, and maintenance status (not only spending).
- **No commons-washing:** if the measure claims “commons” status, governance, access rules, and anti-toll boundary rules must be present and
  auditable.

### Conformance rule (binding)
- If commons capacity degrades (negative ΔG) or access becomes functionally unreachable (waiting times, lockouts), the measure fails durability
  even if it temporarily suppresses prices.

---

## B01.CH05.P03.S04 — Compatibility with caps: the measure creates no new bypass around W-MAX/I-MAX

A measure that reduces one extraction channel but creates accumulation bypasses is non-conforming. Cap compatibility is a required constraint.

### Required cap-compatibility checks (minimum; binding)
- **No guaranteed excess returns:** interventions may not create durable high-yield concessions or protected margins that act as new claim stacks.
- **No asset-support leakage:** financing and stabilization instruments must not inflate asset prices or concentrate ownership in ways that
  undermine W-MAX/I-MAX.
- **Ownership/control discipline:** ensure that new structures do not entrench gate power via platform rights, exclusivity, or control without
  title.

### Conformance rule (binding)
- If the intervention increases concentration metrics or creates privileged accumulation channels, it fails the compatibility gate.

---

## B01.CH05.P03.S05 — Avoidance robustness: resistance to legal/technical/international arbitrage

Because E reduction changes incentives, avoidance is not optional; it is the default response. Measures must be designed against shift.

### Required robustness features (minimum; binding)
- **Shift mapping:** identify likely shift routes (legal relabeling, technical bundling, financial repackaging, international routing) specific
  to the targeted flow type.
- **Automatic requalification:** define how the system will reclassify and neutralize shifted forms using substance-over-form logic.
- **Cross-layer coherence:** ensure CH02 attribution, CH04 anti-avoidance, and CH10 audit triggers connect to the measure.

### Conformance rule (binding)
- If the measure relies on “voluntary compliance” or cannot specify detection/remedy for predictable shifts, it is non-conforming.

---

## B01.CH05.P03.S06 — Measurability and auditability: indicators, data minimum, and falsification criteria

A measure is compatible only if it can be validated and falsified with a minimal, publishable evidence surface.

### Required measurement package (minimum; binding)
- **Indicators:** publish the minimal indicator set that corresponds to the claimed mechanism (E share, fee-stack depth, lock-in proxies,
  margin bands, R/B distribution effects, ΔG/quality where relevant).
- **Data minimum:** specify the minimum observables required (prices, costs, contract terms, control/exposure, access metrics) and how missing
  data is handled.
- **Method discipline:** publish method_id, definition-set ID, revision policy, and uncertainty bands where proxies are used.
- **Falsification criteria:** explicit conditions under which the measure is considered failing (e.g., E share flat, lock-in rising, tail
  deterioration, commons degradation).

### Conformance rule (binding)
- If the evidence package is missing, vendor-locked, or non-replicable, the system must fail-closed: no compatibility claim and no scaling
  until measurability is restored.
