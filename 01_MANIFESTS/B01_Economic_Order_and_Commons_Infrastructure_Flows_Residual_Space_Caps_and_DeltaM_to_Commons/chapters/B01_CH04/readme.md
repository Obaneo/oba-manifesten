# B01.CH04 — Bounded accumulation (W-MAX / I-MAX)

This chapter specifies how **accumulation is bounded** in the economic layer: wealth (W) and income (I) caps, their design variants, what counts as a functional equivalent, how transitions are staged, and how avoidance routes are neutralized.

It is the **cap kernel** of Book 1: without cap effectiveness (or proven equivalents), extraction can regenerate through concentration, gate power, and claims stacks even if other reforms appear to work short-term.

---

## What this chapter is

- A **conceptual model** of accumulation as claims + access + power (not only legal ownership).
- A **design space** for W-MAX and I-MAX (hard cap, progressive edge, time-bound claims).
- A **criteria framework** for functional equivalents (what counts and what is forbidden).
- A **transition module** for sequencing, phase-down paths, and emergency exception discipline.
- An **anti-avoidance module** for reclassification, offshore structures, vehicles, synthetics, and tokenization.

---

## What this chapter is not

- Not a moral critique of wealth; it defines an enforceable bound on accumulation and concentration.
- Not a jurisdiction-specific tax handbook; implementation artifacts belong in Book X (Codex).
- Not a substitute for P/E/C classification (CH02) or for commons build-up (CH06).

---

## Binding and constraints

- **Kernel precedence:** meanings of W, I, caps, consolidation, and ultimate control inherit from the Kernel Spec.
- **Substance over form:** ultimate beneficial control and economic exposure dominate legal labels.
- **No carve-outs that break the cap:** exceptions, if any, must be tightly defined and abuse-resistant.
- **Fail-closed on opacity:** where ownership/control cannot be established, conservative presumptions and/or enforcement escalation apply.

---

## Chapter structure

- **P01 — Accumulation concept:** claims stack, access/control rights, use vs rent-seeking, unit of measurement (UBC).
- **P02 — W-MAX variants:** hard cap, progressive edge, time-bound claims, exceptions, reallocation upon exceedance, entrepreneurship interaction.
- **P03 — I-MAX variants:** income base, absolute vs ratio cap, variable pay mapping, carried interest/options, deferred/overseas consolidation, W/I interaction.
- **P04 — Functional equivalents:** definition, criteria set, examples, forbidden pseudo-equivalents, compatibility test against drift.
- **P05 — Transition regimes:** sequencing, existing wealth treatment, time paths, emergency exceptions without permanent holes, shock robustness, enforcement communication.
- **P06 — Anti-avoidance:** reclassification, offshore, vehicles, derivatives/synthetics, tokenization, enforcement toolkit.

## Outputs expected from this chapter

When implemented (via Codex artifacts), this chapter should enable:

- A **cap base definition** for W and I with consolidation and economic-exposure rules.
- A **cap effectiveness dashboard**: exceedance ratios, concentration measures, and avoidance-pattern indicators.
- A **standard remedy set** for exceedance (reallocation paths, phase-down windows, escrow/lockbox where needed).
- A **standard anti-avoidance toolkit** that detects and neutralizes reclassification and structure games.
