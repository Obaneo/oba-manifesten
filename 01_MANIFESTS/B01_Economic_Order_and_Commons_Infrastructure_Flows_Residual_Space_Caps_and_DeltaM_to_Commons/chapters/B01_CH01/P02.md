# B01.CH01.P02 — The minimal OBA invariants (what must not vary)

## B01.CH01.P02.S01 — Non-negotiables: pillars and their binding status

Within the economic layer, OBA is defined by a **fixed pillar set**. These pillars are **binding**: they are not optional design preferences, and they are not subject to political “trade-offs” that hollow out the order while preserving the label.

A system may only claim OBA-compatibility in the economic layer if it implements and maintains, at minimum, the following pillars:

1. **Flow decomposition (P/E/C) is operational and enforced.**  
   Economic flows are classifiable into productive value (P), extractive component (E), and commons contribution (C), including mixed flows and chain attribution. “E” must be reducible in a way that is detectable in published indicators.

2. **Residual space is the household order indicator: R/B ≥ α, distribution-first.**  
   The chain **B → T → N → L → R** is defined, measured, and used as a stability target. Reporting must be distributional (median/percentiles/tail share), not average-only.

3. **Accumulation is bounded: W-MAX and I-MAX (or proven functional equivalents).**  
   There must be a hard bounding mechanism for wealth and income accumulation, including look-through consolidation and anti-avoidance. If “equivalents” are used, they must be demonstrably cap-effective over time.

4. **Commons build-up is structural and protected (ΔG(t) + quality).**  
   Commons are treated as a base layer (access + maintenance + governance). Growth and quality are measured, and the core elements are protected against privatization-by-proxy and toll reintroduction.

5. **ΔM allocation is governed and routed to commons, not to asset/claim inflation.**  
   Monetary space (ΔM) is treated as core power. Channels, criteria, and governance must be explicit, auditable, and constrained so that ΔM demonstrably supports commons capex/opex and order stability rather than primarily supporting asset valuations and accumulation.

These pillars are **constitutive**: removing or hollowing out any pillar is not a “variant”; it is a **compatibility breach**.

## B01.CH01.P02.S02 — Fail-closed: if evidence/measurement is missing, compatibility claims are suspended

OBA compatibility claims in the economic layer are **evidence-gated**. Where measurement or auditability is missing, uncertain, or blocked, the system must **fail closed**:

- **No label under opacity:** if the required observation set for a claim is unavailable (data gaps, non-disclosure, black-box models, vendor lock-in), the claim cannot be certified as compatible.
- **Conservative treatment under uncertainty:** when classification is uncertain (P/E/C splits, L-minimum boundaries, cap exposure), the burden is to either (i) make the classification auditable, or (ii) treat the case under a conservative presumption and publish the uncertainty explicitly.
- **Stop rules over narrative continuity:** if evidence collapses (audit capacity lost, measurement blackouts, exceptions explode), scaling and expansion are blocked until the evidence chain is restored.
- **Restatements are mandatory:** if later evidence shows that a published indicator or conformance claim was wrong, a public correction (restatement) is required, with versioning and a reasoned change log.

Fail-closed is not a stylistic preference. It is the mechanism that prevents semantic drift, compatibility-washing, and “order by assertion” in the economic layer.
