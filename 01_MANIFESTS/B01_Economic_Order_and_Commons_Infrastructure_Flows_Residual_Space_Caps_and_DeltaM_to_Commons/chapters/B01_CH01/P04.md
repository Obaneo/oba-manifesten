# B01.CH01.P04 — OBA compatibility: necessary and sufficient conditions

This paragraph defines the **compatibility test** for the economic layer. It is not a rhetorical checklist; it is a gate: compatibility claims are valid only when the necessary conditions are present **and** the sufficiency conditions are met with auditable proof.

## B01.CH01.P04.S01 — Necessity: which pillars must be demonstrably present

The following are **necessary** conditions. If any condition is missing, the economic layer is **not** eligible for an OBA-compatible label.

1. **P/E/C is defined, applied, and measurable.**  
   There is an operational classification and attribution method for flows, including mixed flows and chain attribution, with publishable indicators for E share and its reduction over time.

2. **R/B ≥ α is defined as the household order indicator, with distribution reporting.**  
   The B→T→N→L→R chain is defined and measured; α is defined (including its update/indexation rule where applicable); reporting includes median/percentiles and the share below α.

3. **Bounded accumulation exists: W-MAX and I-MAX (or proven equivalents).**  
   Caps (or equivalents) exist with a defined base, consolidation rules (ultimate beneficial control / economic exposure), and anti-avoidance mechanisms that prevent relabeling and structural bypass.

4. **Commons are a first-class economic base layer with protection and measurement.**  
   Commons are specified as a regime (access + maintenance + governance), with ΔG(t) and quality indicators and legal/boundary protections against privatization-by-proxy and toll reintroduction.

5. **ΔM governance exists and ΔM is routed to commons-capable channels, not primarily to asset/claim inflation.**  
   ΔM is defined, channels are classified, allocation criteria exist ex ante, and traceability exists end-to-end from ΔM to destination. Forbidden/risk channels are defined and constrained.

6. **Auditability and fail-closed are enforced.**  
   The system publishes definitions, methods, uncertainty, revision policies, and supports independent replication. Under missing evidence, compatibility labels are suspended rather than asserted.

These conditions must be **demonstrable** (documents, indicators, audit routes, enforcement remedies), not merely declared.

## B01.CH01.P04.S02 — Sufficiency: what counts as “works” in the economic layer (with auditable proof)

Sufficiency is outcome-oriented but still strictly procedural: “works” means that the implemented system produces the required economic-layer effects **and** those effects remain auditable and robust against manipulation.

A system counts as working in the economic layer only if it can demonstrate, over the declared evaluation window:

1. **E is structurally reduced (not relabeled).**  
   Publishable E indicators show a downward trajectory in sectors where toll/lock-in/fee-stacks were material, with detection measures for shifts into new forms.

2. **Residual space improves where it matters: the tails and floor, not only the mean.**  
   R/B distribution profiles improve: the share below α declines and tail volatility stabilizes, with transparent definitions and no displacement of burdens outside L.

3. **Accumulation is actually bounded in practice.**  
   Cap effectiveness is visible in exceedance metrics, concentration measures, and exposure consolidation; avoidance patterns are detected and neutralized without creating carve-outs.

4. **Commons capacity and quality grow and are maintained (ΔG(t) + quality).**  
   Commons outputs show sustained improvements in coverage/capacity and quality/continuity, and maintenance backlogs do not systematically expand.

5. **ΔM routing is verifiably aligned with commons and order stability.**  
   ΔM channel distributions and end-to-end traceability show that monetary space predominantly supports commons capex/opex and order objectives, with leakage tests against asset/claim support.

6. **The evidence chain holds under stress (no black boxes, no exception explosion).**  
   Measurement remains reproducible; revisions/restatements occur when errors are found; exceptions do not become a shadow regime. If evidence collapses, the system fails closed rather than continuing under narrative cover.

Sufficiency therefore requires both:
- **measured order effects** (E↓, R/B tails improve, caps bite, commons ΔG+quality↑, ΔM routed), and
- **durable auditability** (replicability, traceability, anti-avoidance, disciplined revisions).

If the system meets the necessary conditions but cannot yet demonstrate these sufficiency effects, it may be described as **structurally prepared** but not **economically validated**.
