# B01.CH01.P03 — Core notation (B, T, N, L, R; P/E/C; W-MAX/I-MAX; ΔM; commons)

This paragraph fixes the **notation layer** used across Book 1. These symbols are treated as **terms of art**: they are not rhetorical, and they are not interchangeable with synonyms. Any extension must be explicit, versioned, and compatible with the Kernel Spec.

## B01.CH01.P03.S01 — Household sequence: B → T → N → L → R

Household order is expressed through a chained accounting relation:

- **B** — *Gross* (gross inflow): the defined set of household income components within a fixed time window.
- **T** — *Levies*: taxes/premiums/contributions that count as mandatory outflows under the definition set.
- **N = B − T** — *Net*: an intermediate quantity; not the order indicator.
- **L** — *Mandatory burdens*: the defined minimum basket of unavoidable fixed burdens (essentials), excluding discretionary consumption and excluded categories per definition set.
- **R = N − L** — *Residual space*: the remaining room after mandatory burdens; used as the core order variable.
- **R/B ≥ α** — the core threshold form; evaluation is distributional (median, percentiles, tails), not average-only.

All quantities are defined against:
- a **time window** (month/year),
- a **household type** (equivalisation/normalisation rules),
- and a **versioned L-minimum set** (to prevent drift).

## B01.CH01.P03.S02 — Flow decomposition: P/E/C

Economic flows are decomposed into:

- **P** — *Productive component*: the part of a flow attributable to functional production, service delivery, maintenance, or reproduction, within bounded cost and bounded legitimate margin.
- **E** — *Extractive component*: the part of a flow attributable to toll/claim above functional cost; lock-in; scarcity construction; fee-stacks; market power without functional justification.
- **C** — *Commons contribution*: the part of a flow attributable to building, maintaining, or providing access to commons capacity, under non-exclusive availability rules and governance constraints.

A single observed flow may be:
- **pure** (P only, E only, C only), or
- **mixed** (P+E, P+C, and in defined cases split across multiple links in a chain).

Attribution requires:
- identifying the **actor** and the **position** (role),
- applying **ultimate control / block power** where relevant,
- and consolidating through vehicles where needed.

## B01.CH01.P03.S03 — Caps: W-MAX and I-MAX (including functional equivalents)

Bounded accumulation is expressed with two caps:

- **W-MAX** — *Wealth cap*: a bound on accumulated wealth/claims/exposure, defined in the wealth base with look-through consolidation and economic-exposure rules.
- **I-MAX** — *Income cap*: a bound on income flows (including variable pay, capital gains, in-kind benefits, deferred compensation) with consolidation and anti-relabeling.

If a jurisdiction uses **functional equivalents** instead of explicit caps, these equivalents must meet a fixed criteria set:
- **measurability**, **avoidance robustness**, **cap effectiveness over time**, and **reallocation to commons**.

“Cap-like” measures that only slow accumulation without an enforceable bound are not considered equivalent.

## B01.CH01.P03.S04 — ΔM: monetary space and allocation channels

- **ΔM** denotes *monetary space / monetary expansion*: a change in monetary capacity within a defined time window, irrespective of legal form (central bank operations, bank credit creation, public facilities, guarantees, asset purchases, etc.).
- The core question is **channeling**: where ΔM enters, who receives it, under which conditions, and with what downstream effect on P/E/C, R/B, and accumulation.

Book 1 treats ΔM as a **constitutive lever** that must be:
- channel-classified,
- governed with explicit criteria,
- and audited end-to-end (ΔM → channel → recipient → destination).

## B01.CH01.P03.S05 — Commons: access, maintenance, governance, and non-exclusivity rules

A **commons** in Book 1 is not defined by ownership label (“public” or “private”), but by a **regime**:

- **Access rules:** baseline access, conditional access, priority rights—explicit, contestable, and auditable.
- **Maintenance duty:** maintenance-first budgeting and execution; upkeep is not optional overhead.
- **Governance minimum:** decision rules, rotation, conflict resolution, transparency, anti-capture.
- **Non-exclusivity with boundary rules:** non-exclusive availability is the default; exclusion is allowed only under explicit conditions (capacity, abuse, contribution) with due process and re-entry paths.
- **ΔG(t) + quality:** commons performance is measured as growth (capacity/coverage) and quality (availability, reliability, accessibility), with versioned indicators and audit routes.

Where this book references concrete schemas or templates for these objects, it points to the Codex (Book X) rather than duplicating implementation detail here.
