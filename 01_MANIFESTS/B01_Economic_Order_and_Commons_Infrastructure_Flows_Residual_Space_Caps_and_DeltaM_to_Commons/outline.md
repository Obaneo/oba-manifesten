## B01.CH05 — Phase-down of parasitic flows: typology, edge cases, and design criteria

  * B01.CH05.P01.S02 — Rent and land rent: payment for access to scarce essentials (housing/location)
  * B01.CH05.P01.S03 — Monopoly/oligopoly rent: pricing above competitive / functional bands
  * B01.CH05.P01.S04 — Platform tolls and lock-in: access to networks/markets as a private gate
  * B01.CH05.P01.S05 — Arbitrage and regulatory shopping: profit from rule differences rather than production
  * B01.CH05.P01.S06 — Hybrid flows: combinations and stacking (e.g., platform + finance + IP)

* B01.CH05.P02 — Edge cases: innovation rent, risk compensation, scarcity versus artificial scarcity
  * B01.CH05.P02.S01 — Innovation rent: temporary surplus versus structural toll (duration and market-power criterion)
  * B01.CH05.P02.S02 — Risk compensation: risk premium versus extraction (alternatives and proportionality criterion)
  * B01.CH05.P02.S03 — Scarcity: natural scarcity versus created scarcity (access and blockage criterion)

  * B01.CH05.P02.S05 — Boundary decision protocol: who decides, on what data, with which objection procedure

* B01.CH05.P03 — Design criteria: when is a measure OBA-compatible (effects on E, R/B, commons, caps)

  * B01.CH05.P03.S02 — Effect on R/B: improvement at the bottom/medians, not only on the average
  * B01.CH05.P03.S03 — Effect on commons: build/maintenance/access increase (ΔG and quality)
  * B01.CH05.P03.S04 — Compatibility with caps: the measure creates no new bypass around W-MAX/I-MAX
  * B01.CH05.P03.S05 — Avoidance robustness: resistance to legal/technical/international arbitrage
  * B01.CH05.P03.S06 — Measurability and auditability: indicators, data minimum, and falsification criteria

* B01.CH05.P04 — Instruments per flow type (norming, price ceilings, splitting, commons alternatives, public options)

  * B01.CH05.P04.S02 — Price ceilings and tariff regulation: where market power is structural (utilities/essentials)
  * B01.CH05.P04.S03 — Splitting and interoperability: dismantling bottlenecks (platforms, networks)
  * B01.CH05.P04.S04 — Commons alternatives: public/commons provisioning as structural substitution for toll markets

  * B01.CH05.P04.S06 — Instrument mix per sector: when to combine (and when combinations are counterproductive)
