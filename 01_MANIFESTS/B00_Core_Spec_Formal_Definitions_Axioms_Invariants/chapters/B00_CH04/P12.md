# B00.CH04.P12 — Measurement standards and minimum data layer

This paragraph defines the minimum **data layer** and measurement standards required to compute **B, T, N, L, R**
and cohort-level **R/B** profiles in an OBA-grade way.

The standard is not “statistical best effort”. It is **auditability** and **semantic closure**:
if required inputs are missing, opaque, or non-reconcilable, the derived R/B results cannot support conformance
claims and must be handled under **fail-closed** discipline.

---

## B00.CH04.P12.S01 — Minimum data for B: income components and periodicity

To measure **B (gross receipts)**, the minimum data must support:

### Component breakdown (at least)
- wages/salaries (gross),
- self-employment / operating receipts or attributed surplus (gross, boundary explicit),
- interest/dividends,
- rent received,
- transfers-in (benefits, grants, remittances) under declared boundary,
- in-kind/quasi-cash receipts where included (with valuation method).

### Periodicity / time resolution
- a declared period (monthly/quarterly/annual) and index set,
- consistency between income receipt timing and burden timing (avoid mismatched windows),
- volatility indicators or raw series where smoothing is applied.

### Boundary and consolidation fields
- unit identifier (household/person/entity) and membership mapping,
- flags for internal transfers and pass-through flows (to prevent B inflation).

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- B must be reproducible from component data; “single net income number” without traceable components is non-auditable.
- If in-kind/quasi-cash is included, valuation method and double-count controls are mandatory; otherwise B is non-conform.

---

## B00.CH04.P12.S02 — Minimum data for T: effective burden, mandatory contributions, quasi-taxes

To measure **T (mandatory transfer-out)**, the minimum data must support:

### Effective burden measurement
- amounts paid (or withheld) over the declared period,
- liable party under the boundary definition,
- gross vs net treatment (withholding that never accrues to B must be handled consistently).

### Mandatory basis metadata
- classification flags for: tax, statutory contribution, mandatory regulated contribution,
- legal/regulatory basis reference (or regime identifier) sufficient for audit,
- differentiation between truly mandatory and elective/contractual items.

### Quasi-tax identification
- regime attributes showing mandatory participation + enforceability,
- evidence fields sufficient to distinguish quasi-tax from private tolls.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- T classification MUST be supported by mandatory-basis metadata; otherwise items cannot be counted as T for OBA claims.
- If the dataset cannot distinguish mandatory from non-mandatory payments, T is non-auditable and dependent results fail closed.

---

## B00.CH04.P12.S03 — Minimum data for L: essentials basket, contractual lock, regional price indices

To measure **L (essentials / claim layers)**, the minimum data must support:

### Essentials basket definition (parameterized)
- canonical essentials categories (housing, energy, health, mobility, basic connectivity, basic food),
- baseline service level parameters (minimum adequate standard),
- household composition adjustments (equivalization parameters),
- admissible substitutes / baseline definitions.

### Observed burden measurement
- actual paid amounts where available, or
- modeled baseline burdens using declared indices and service-level parameters, with explicit method version.

### Contractual lock / exit friction fields
- tenure/contract type (rent/mortgage; utility plan type; telecom contract type),
- penalty/exit friction indicators (duration, cancellation fees, portability constraints),
- bundling indicators and de facto mandatory add-ons.

### Regional price regime indices
- regional housing/energy/food indices and their methodology,
- flags for scarcity regimes (capacity constraints) where price indices are not sufficient,
- time-index consistency (same resolution as B/T or explicitly mapped).

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- L cannot be computed OBA-grade without an explicit essentials parameter regime + regional indices; otherwise “cost of living”
  becomes narrative and non-auditable.
- Excluding lock-in burdens or compulsory fee layers from L is non-conform where these materially affect essentials access.

## B00.CH04.P12.S04 — Uncertainty and conservatism: bands; fail-closed when inputs are non-auditable

R/B outputs inherit uncertainty from B/T/L measurement. OBA requires uncertainty to be explicit and conservative.

Canonical uncertainty requirements:

- **Bands:** where baseline burdens or valuations are modeled or partially observed, report ranges (intervals) rather
  than single-point values when uncertainty is material.
- **Coverage reporting:** publish what share of flows/units are fully observed vs modeled vs missing.
- **Sensitivity:** report sensitivity to key parameters (essentials basket, regional indices, smoothing choices, α).
- **Conservatism:** where ambiguity would enlarge discretion (especially L deflation or B inflation), interpret in a
  conservative direction for conformance claims.

Fail-closed rule:
- If required inputs are missing or non-auditable such that L or B could be materially mismeasured, the affected
  R/B results cannot certify OBA claims; components must be flagged unknown/non-conform or conservatively bounded.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Reporting R/B as precise point estimates without uncertainty where modeling/assumptions are material is non-conform.
- If inputs are non-auditable, implementations MUST fail closed for OBA conformance claims and disclose limitations;
  “best effort” is not a certification basis.
