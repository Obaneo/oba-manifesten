# B00.CH04.P02 — Canonical quantities and base identities (B,T,N,L,R)

This paragraph defines the canonical quantities **B, T, N, L, R** and the base identities that connect them:

- **N = B − T**
- **R = N − L**  (therefore **R = B − T − L**)

These quantities are **flow quantities** over a declared time period/resolution and index set (actor/cohort/sector/
jurisdiction). They are binding interfaces for OBA-grade evaluation: any alternative representation must map back to
these definitions without semantic drift.

Conservative rule: where ambiguity would enlarge discretion (especially by inflating B or deflating L), evaluation
MUST fail closed until the terms are resolved canonically.

---

## B00.CH04.P02.S01 — Definition of B (gross receipts/income; pre-transfer)

**B** is **gross receipts** (gross inflows) accruing to the evaluated unit over the declared period, **before**
mandatory transfer-out (**T**) is deducted.

B includes (non-exhaustive), when received as inflow to the unit:
- wages and salaries (including bonuses and mandatory supplements),
- self-employment/operating surplus attributed to the unit (pre-transfer),
- interest, dividends, rents received,
- transfers-in that are receipts to the unit (benefits, grants, remittances), unless explicitly excluded by a
  defined profile and reconciled (see scope/index rules),
- in-kind and quasi-cash receipts where valuation inclusion is required by later rules.

B is not “headline income” by convention; it is the **auditable sum of receipts** under declared valuation and
consolidation rules. Double counting is forbidden: receipts must be consolidated where they represent internal
circulation within the evaluated boundary.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any B measurement MUST specify: boundary (who/what unit), period/resolution, included components, valuation rules,
  and consolidation rules (to prevent double counting).
- Any implementation that inflates B by including internal transfers without consolidation, or by counting gross
  flows that do not accrue as receipts to the unit, is non-conform.

---

## B00.CH04.P02.S02 — Definition of T (levies/transfer-out; tax/fee/mandatory contributions)

**T** is **mandatory transfer-out**: levies and compulsory contributions that the evaluated unit must pay as a
condition of legal compliance or access to the public order (or quasi-public regimes), over the declared period.

T includes (non-exhaustive):
- taxes (income, payroll, property-related mandatory levies where classified as transfer-out),
- social security contributions and statutory insurance contributions that are mandatory,
- mandatory fees and levies imposed by law or by regulated/quasi-public regimes where non-payment is not a real
  option.

T is defined strictly: it is not general spending, and it is not burdens that are “hard to avoid” economically
unless they are truly mandatory under binding rules. Borderline items are governed by CH04.P03 strict T rules.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any T classification MUST show why the payment is mandatory (legal/regulatory basis or binding regime) and must
  distinguish it from L and from voluntary consumption.
- Relabeling L or E-driven burdens as “fees” to shift them into T is non-conform unless the mandatory basis is
  established and auditable.

---

## B00.CH04.P02.S03 — Definition of N: N = B − T (net after transfers)

**N** is **net after mandatory transfers**:

- **N = B − T**

N is not discretionary income; it is the receipts base remaining after mandatory transfer-out. It is the input
into the essentials layer **L**, and therefore the base from which remaining space **R** is defined.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- N must be computed from the canonical identity; implementations must not compute “net” via implicit deductions or
  unreported conventions.
- Any adjustments (timing, smoothing, consolidation) must be applied transparently to B and/or T before N is
  derived, with audit trail.

---

## B00.CH04.P02.S04 — Definition of L (essentials/claim layers; burdens that are not plausibly avoidable)

**L** is the set of **essentials / claim layers**: burdens that are not plausibly avoidable for the evaluated unit
without unacceptable loss of basic function, legality, or survival-level participation in society, given the
declared jurisdiction/sector regime and the unit’s constraints.

L includes (canonical essentials basket + extensions under rules), typically:
- housing (shelter baseline),
- energy baseline,
- basic food baseline,
- basic health care access,
- basic mobility required for participation (context-specific, parameterized),
- basic connectivity (communications / digital access baseline),
and any contractual lock-in burdens that meet the “not plausibly avoidable” criterion.

L explicitly includes E-driven claim layers embedded in essentials (rent extraction, fee stacks, refinancing spreads)
because these manifest as unavoidable burdens at the life-world level.

L explicitly excludes:
- luxury/optional upgrades and discretionary consumption beyond baseline essentials,
- avoidable contract choices where credible exit exists (unless lock-in rules apply),
- narrative “needs” not parameterized and audited.

Borderline items are governed by CH04.P04 strict L rules.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any L measurement MUST specify the essentials basket and parameters (baseline definitions, regional price regime,
  service level) and must separate baseline from upgrades.
- Inflating L by including discretionary spending, or deflating L by excluding unavoidable essentials burdens, is
  non-conform and triggers failure modes under CH04.P14.

---

## B00.CH04.P02.S05 — Definition of R: R = N − L (remaining space as buffer)

**R** is **remaining space** (remainder) after mandatory transfers and essential burdens:

- **R = N − L**  (equivalently **R = B − T − L**)

R is the canonical quantity representing the feasible space for:
- buffer formation (savings, shock absorption),
- autonomy and discretionary choice,
- resilience against volatility.

R can be negative; negative R indicates structural infeasibility under current burdens/receipts and is handled under
edge-case rules (CH04.P08).

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- R MUST be computed from the canonical identity and reported with the same period/resolution as B/T/L.
- Any OBA-grade evaluation using R MUST disclose cohort profiles and tails; reporting only averages is non-conform.
