# B00.CH12.P04 — Auditability: traceability and controllable chains

This paragraph defines **auditability** as a binding requirement for OBA measurement and status claims. Auditability means that an
independent party can trace any published claim through a controllable chain of evidence and reproduce the decision logic without
relying on trust. Where auditability is broken, OBA applies fail-closed rules: dependent status claims are suspended or downgraded
until auditability is restored.

---

## B00.CH12.P04.S01 — End-to-end lineage: claim → data → transformation → metric → decision

Every compatibility-related claim must be supported by a complete, checkable lineage chain from statement to decision.

### Lineage requirement (minimum chain)
- **Claim**: the asserted statement (status label, compliance assertion, outcome claim) with scope and version references.
- **Data**: the source datasets/records used, with provenance and access route.
- **Transformation**: the processing steps applied (cleaning, joins, allocations, classifications, deflation, aggregation).
- **Metric**: the computed statistic(s) used for evaluation, including intermediate outputs where necessary.
- **Decision**: the applied rule/test, threshold, and resulting classification or enforcement action.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- For any published claim, the full lineage chain MUST be available to audit within the governance-defined access regime.
- If any link in the chain is missing or unverifiable, the claim MUST be treated as **non-conform** for the affected scope (fail-closed).
- Lineage must be expressed in a standard, machine-checkable form where specified by Codex/Standards.

---

## B00.CH12.P04.S02 — Logging and immutability: changelogs, hashes, versioned datasets/models

Auditability requires that evidence cannot be silently altered. OBA therefore requires immutability discipline for the artifacts that
feed measurements and decisions.

### Immutability requirements (minimum)
- **Changelogs**: versioned records for methods, parameters, and transformations (what changed, why, who, when).
- **Hashes**: cryptographic hashes (or equivalent integrity checks) for published datasets, snapshots, and method artifacts.
- **Versioned datasets/models**: immutable snapshots for each reporting period and each method version used.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Silent edits are forbidden. Any modification to data, transformations, or methods MUST produce a new versioned artifact and update
  the changelog.
- If integrity proofs (hashes/signatures where required) are missing for critical artifacts, dependent claims MUST be treated as
  **non-conform** until integrity is restored (fail-closed).
- Where models are used for measurement support, the model version and training data provenance MUST be recorded as part of lineage.

---

## B00.CH12.P04.S03 — Sampling and forensic audits: red flags, anomaly detection, deep dives

Auditability must handle both routine verification and adversarial behavior. OBA therefore mandates sampling and forensic capacity.

### Audit modes (minimum)
- **Routine sampling audits**: periodic sampling of records/flows to verify correctness of classification and aggregation.
- **Forensic audits**: targeted deep dives triggered by red flags and anomalies.
- **Anomaly detection**: systematic detection of discontinuities, outliers, suspicious clustering near thresholds, and channel shifts.

### Red flags (examples, non-exhaustive)
- Abrupt metric improvements without corresponding lineage changes.
- Spikes in missingness, unexplained reclassification, or sudden channel rerouting.
- Persistent threshold-hugging behavior (values clustering just below cap or compliance triggers).
- Divergence between microdata and published aggregates beyond tolerance bands.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Implementations MUST publish a minimum audit plan: sampling frame, frequency, triggers, and escalation steps.
- Failure to perform required audits or to investigate triggered anomalies invalidates the affected measurement claims and may trigger
  status suspension (fail-closed).

---

## B00.CH12.P04.S04 — Independent audit: role separation, mandates, liability

To prevent capture, audit functions must be institutionally separable from the entities whose claims they validate.

### Independence requirements (minimum)
- **Role separation**: measurement operators, implementers, auditors, and status declarers must not collapse into one opaque actor.
- **Mandates**: auditors must have legal/procedural authority to access evidence required by lineage and verify integrity.
- **Liability**: accountability for misreporting, negligence, or deliberate concealment must be enforceable.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Compatibility declarations used for name usage or access to OBA-gated programmes MUST be subject to independent audit.
- If independence cannot be established or auditors are dependent on auditees (financially or institutionally), status claims MUST be
  treated as **uncertain** and therefore **non-conform** until independence is restored (fail-closed).
