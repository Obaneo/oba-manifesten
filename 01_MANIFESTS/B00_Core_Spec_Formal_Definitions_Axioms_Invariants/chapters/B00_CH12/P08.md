# B00.CH12.P08 — Publication requirements and transparency

This paragraph defines the **minimum publication and transparency requirements** for OBA measurements and compatibility claims.
Publication is not optional: without public method disclosure and minimum statistics, claims cannot be contested, audited, or
enforced. At the same time, publication must follow **data minimalism** and privacy constraints, using open methods and controlled
access where raw data cannot be public.

---

## B00.CH12.P08.S01 — Minimum publication: methods, definition-set ID, core statistics, uncertainty

Any measurement used in compatibility evaluation MUST be accompanied by a minimum public disclosure set.

### Minimum publication set (required)
- **Definition reference**: definition-set ID + version (and relevant Kernel/Codex artifact references).
- **Method disclosure**: method ID + version; stepwise measurement procedure; transformations; parameter settings.
- **Core statistics**: the minimum observable outputs for the claim scope (including distribution profiles where required).
- **Uncertainty**: interval estimates, error margins, sensitivity results, and the assumptions register (or a public summary with audit access to full dossier).
- **Lineage pointers**: links/references to datasets/snapshots (or controlled access route), and integrity proofs where required.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- If any element of the minimum publication set is missing, dependent compatibility claims MUST be treated as **non-conform**
  until publication is completed (fail-closed).
- Publication must be stable and citable (versioned artifacts, no mutable “living doc” as sole source).

---

## B00.CH12.P08.S02 — Public registers: compatibility status, exceptions, forks, audit results

OBA requires public registers that make status and evidence discoverable and prevent laundering through fragmented communication.

### Required registers (minimum)
- **Compatibility register**: implementations and their status label, scope, definition-set references, evidence bundle references.
- **Exceptions register**: any exception or special regime with canonical basis, scope, sunset, and review outcome.
- **Fork register**: declared forks, their changelogs, invariant deltas, and bridging documents (if any).
- **Audit register**: audit results, dates, scope, material findings, remediation status, and any suspensions.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Any claim that cannot be located in the appropriate public register MUST be treated as **non-conform** until registered.
- Unpublished exceptions are forbidden and constitute a fork trigger.
- Audit findings affecting compatibility status MUST be reflected in the register without delay.

---

## B00.CH12.P08.S03 — Access for researchers/journalists: procedures and data minimalism

Public contestability requires structured access routes for scrutiny, while maintaining data minimalism and privacy boundaries.

### Access requirements (minimum)
- **Procedures**: clear application process, eligibility criteria, scope of access, timelines, and appeal path for denied access.
- **Tiered access**: public summaries by default; deeper access for qualified auditors/researchers under controlled conditions.
- **Reproducibility support**: access sufficient to reproduce published outputs under the audit regime (methods open; data access controlled where needed).
- **Minimalism mapping**: explicit mapping of requested data fields to required observables/tests to prevent data creep.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Access regimes MUST be sufficient to enable independent verification of published claims.
- Requests for data beyond what is required for order evaluation MUST be rejected or narrowed; granting excessive access without
  justification is non-conform.

---

## B00.CH12.P08.S04 — Redaction & privacy: what may be anonymised; what must be public

OBA permits privacy protection, but not at the cost of losing auditability of compatibility-critical claims.

### Redaction rules (minimum)
- **May be anonymised/redacted**:
  - direct identifiers and sensitive fields not required for order evaluation,
  - granular records where publication would enable re-identification, provided aggregate/statistical disclosure remains sufficient.
- **Must remain public (non-negotiable minimum)**:
  - method specifications (open methods),
  - definition-set references,
  - core statistics required for claim evaluation (including distribution summaries),
  - uncertainty reporting and assumptions summary,
  - register entries for status, exceptions, forks, and audits.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Redaction MUST be documented as part of method metadata (what is redacted, why, and what verification route remains).
- If redaction prevents verification of lineage, reproducibility, or required observables, the affected claim scope MUST be treated as
  **non-conform** until a privacy-compatible verification route is provided (fail-closed).
