# B00.CH12.P12 — Failure modes and hard stops

This paragraph defines the principal failure modes that degrade evidence integrity in OBA and the hard-stop responses that prevent
continued reliance on untrustworthy measurements. The enforcement principle is simple: if measurement integrity cannot be verified,
OBA curtails the decision space and suspends compatibility privileges until integrity is restored.

---

## B00.CH12.P12.S01 — Measurement-washing: pretty dashboards without traceable data (detection)

Measurement-washing occurs when organizations present polished dashboards and KPI narratives as proof while the underlying evidence
chain is missing or unverifiable.

### Detection indicators (non-exhaustive)
- **No lineage links**: published numbers cannot be traced to datasets, snapshots, or registered methods.
- **Opaque transformations**: “data processing” is described but not specified or versioned.
- **Selective publication**: headline KPIs are published without required core observables, distributions, or uncertainty bands.
- **Non-reproducibility**: third parties cannot reproduce results under the stated access regime.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- If measurement-washing indicators are present, the affected metrics MUST be treated as invalid for compatibility claims.
- Any compatibility label relying on washed metrics MUST be downgraded to **non-conform** until full lineage, method registration,
  and reproducibility are restored.

---

## B00.CH12.P12.S02 — Capture of audits: auditors dependent on auditees (institutional leak)

Audit capture occurs when the auditing function is institutionally or financially dependent on the entity being audited, creating
systematic incentives to overlook defects.

### Capture indicators (non-exhaustive)
- Auditors paid or appointed in ways that depend on favorable outcomes without structural independence safeguards.
- Repeated clean opinions despite unresolved lineage breaks, missing evidence, or failed reproducibility checks.
- Restricted audit scope, denial of access to critical artifacts, or “attestation-only” reviews without verification.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- If audit capture is detected or independence cannot be established, audit outputs MUST be treated as unreliable, and dependent
  compatibility claims MUST be treated as **uncertain** and therefore **non-conform** (fail-closed).
- Restoring conformity requires role separation, mandated access, and independent review (including external audit where applicable).

---

## B00.CH12.P12.S03 — Over-measurement: surveillance risk from data maximalism (non-conform)

Over-measurement occurs when data collection expands beyond what is required for order evaluation, creating surveillance capacity and
capture risk without improving enforceability.

### Over-measurement indicators (non-exhaustive)
- Collection of granular behavioral or identity-linked data not justified by a binding observable/test requirement.
- Retention of high-resolution data beyond necessary windows without documented necessity.
- Broad data sharing or access regimes without minimization mapping and access control.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Measurement systems MUST implement data minimalism: every collected field requires a published justification mapping to an OBA
  observable/test.
- If data maximalism is present without binding justification, the measurement system MUST be treated as **non-conform** until the
  data inventory is reduced and the pipeline is redesigned to minimal necessary data.

---

## B00.CH12.P12.S04 — Fail-closed: when reproducibility/lineage is lost, decision space is curtailed

When measurement integrity fails (loss of lineage, integrity proofs, reproducibility, or audit access), OBA must prevent decisions
from being made on unverified evidence.

### Fail-closed rule
- Loss of reproducibility or lineage triggers **curtailment**: only decisions that can be supported by verified evidence may proceed.
- Compatibility privileges (status labels, access to OBA-gated programmes, cap pass assertions, channel permissibility claims) are
  suspended for affected scope until integrity is restored.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- If lineage or reproducibility is lost, dependent compatibility status MUST be suspended or downgraded to **non-conform** immediately.
- Restoration requires: re-established versioned artifacts, integrity proofs, reproducible recomputation, and (where required)
  independent audit verification.
