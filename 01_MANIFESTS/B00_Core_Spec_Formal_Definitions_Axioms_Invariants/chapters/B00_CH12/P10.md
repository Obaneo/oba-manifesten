# B00.CH12.P10 — Anti-Goodhart: metric governance

This paragraph defines metric governance rules that prevent OBA from collapsing into KPI-chasing. Any metric used as a target becomes
a gaming surface; therefore, OBA requires **multi-metric governance**, **triangulation**, and active monitoring for strategic
responses. Decisions that affect compatibility status, caps, channel permissibility, or enforcement MUST NOT be made on a single
metric without corroboration.

---

## B00.CH12.P10.S01 — Metric rotation: multiple metrics per invariant to limit gaming

Each invariant must be monitored through a set of metrics, not a single number. Rotation limits exploitation of known targets.

### Metric set rule
- For each invariant, define a **metric set** with:
  - a primary metric (core observable-based),
  - one or more secondary metrics (different operationalization or window),
  - one or more adversarial metrics (designed to detect gaming or edge cases).

### Rotation rule
- Metric sets may rotate on a schedule or under defined triggers (e.g., detected gaming, distribution shift), without changing
  canonical meaning.
- Rotation affects monitoring emphasis, not the invariant definition.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Implementations MUST publish the metric set per invariant and the rotation policy (frequency, triggers, governance).
- Gaming surfaces discovered in a metric MUST trigger rotation and/or expansion of the metric set; failure to adapt constitutes
  a monitoring failure and may trigger status review.

---

## B00.CH12.P10.S02 — Triangulation: alternative data/methods as mandatory counterweight

Triangulation requires verifying key claims using alternative data sources and/or alternative methods to reduce dependence on one
pipeline.

### Triangulation requirements (minimum)
- **Alternative data**: at least one independent data route for critical observables, where feasible.
- **Alternative method**: at least one independent method implementation of the same canonical definition (different transformations or sampling).
- **Convergence band**: predeclared tolerance bands for agreement between triangulated estimates.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Critical decisions (status label assignment, cap pass/fail, forbidden channel determinations) MUST have triangulation coverage unless
  canonically exempted with published justification.
- Where triangulated results diverge beyond the declared band, the decision MUST be treated as **uncertain** and therefore must
  fail closed (no privilege granted) until resolved.

---

## B00.CH12.P10.S03 — Monitoring of gaming: detection of strategic responses to KPIs

OBA must monitor for strategic adaptation: actors changing behavior to satisfy metrics while preserving underlying extraction or
avoidance.

### Gaming indicators (non-exhaustive)
- **Threshold clustering**: values bunching just below triggers/caps.
- **Channel substitution**: shifting to unmonitored channels when monitored channels tighten.
- **Proxy manipulation**: improving reported proxies while underlying observables worsen (e.g., reclassification, relabeling, timing shifts).
- **Distribution hiding**: improving averages while tails worsen (cohort-level deterioration).
- **Audit evasion**: increased missingness, delayed reporting, or opacity spikes after metric targeting.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Implementations MUST publish a gaming-monitoring plan: indicators, detection thresholds, and escalation steps.
- Detected gaming MUST trigger: (i) forensic audit, (ii) metric set update/rotation, and (iii) anti-avoidance review. Failure to act
  triggers status review and may require downgrade.

---

## B00.CH12.P10.S04 — Ban on single-metric governance: no decision on one KPI without corroboration

Single-metric decisions are prohibited for compatibility-critical determinations because they create a dominant gaming target and hide
trade-offs.

### Rule
- No decision that grants a privilege (status label, cap compliance, channel permissibility, access to OBA-gated programmes) may be
  based on a single KPI without corroboration from:
  - an independent metric within the metric set, and
  - at least one robustness check (alternative window, alternative data/method, or distribution check).

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- If corroboration is missing, the decision MUST be deferred or must fail closed (no privilege granted) until corroboration is produced.
- Any system that operationally collapses decisions onto a single metric MUST be treated as non-conform and requires redesign.
