# B00.CH12.P09 — Dispute protocol: contesting measurements and claims

This paragraph defines the dispute protocol for contesting OBA measurements and measurement-based claims. Disputes are treated as a
normal integrity mechanism: measurements can be challenged, reviewed under evidentiary rules, corrected via restatement, and—when
structural failure is detected—compatibility status can be suspended under fail-closed rules.

---

## B00.CH12.P09.S01 — Initiation: who may challenge a measurement claim and on what grounds

A dispute may be initiated by any actor with standing to contest measurement validity, provided the challenge specifies scope and
grounds.

### Eligible challengers (minimum)
- **Affected parties** within the claim scope (entities or cohorts impacted by a measurement-based decision).
- **Auditors / registrars / certifiers** responsible for compatibility declarations.
- **Researchers / journalists** operating within published access procedures.
- **Public-interest bodies** designated by governance (where applicable).

### Permitted grounds for challenge (minimum)
- **Lineage failure**: missing or unverifiable claim → data → transformation → metric → decision chain.
- **Method defect**: unregistered method, version mismatch, undocumented transformations, parameter out-of-bounds.
- **Data defect**: provenance issues, sampling bias, missingness distortion, reconciliation gaps beyond tolerance.
- **Reproducibility failure**: third party cannot reproduce outputs from declared inputs/method.
- **Misrepresentation**: public claim exceeds what evidence supports (overclaiming).

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Challenges MUST specify: the claim scope, contested metric(s), referenced method/version, and the alleged defect category.
- Gatekeepers MUST accept validly scoped challenges and MUST not dismiss challenges solely on reputational or rhetorical grounds.

---

## B00.CH12.P09.S02 — Procedure: time-bounded steps, evidentiary rules, independent review

Disputes must be resolved through a defined, time-bounded procedure that prevents indefinite ambiguity.

### Minimum procedure (required)
1. **Filing**: challenge submission with scope, grounds, and references.
2. **Admissibility check**: verify standing, scope, and that the challenge targets registered artifacts.
3. **Evidence exchange**: parties provide required artifacts (datasets/snapshots or access route; method spec; logs; lineage chain).
4. **Independent review**: review by an independent reviewer/audit function with mandate to access evidence under the access regime.
5. **Finding**: determination (uphold / partial uphold / reject) with documented rationale and evidence references.
6. **Remedy order**: required corrections, restatements, recalculation, or status update.
7. **Appeal**: optional time-bounded appeal step with independence requirements.

### Evidentiary rules (minimum)
- Only **registered, versioned artifacts** count as method evidence.
- Assertions without lineage or reproducibility are not admissible as proof.
- Where evidence cannot be produced under the access regime, the issue is treated as unresolved and fails closed.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- The dispute timeline MUST be defined and published; failure to meet deadlines triggers escalation rules.
- Reviewers MUST be independent of the auditee’s control and financial dependence (role separation).

---

## B00.CH12.P09.S03 — Correction: restatements, recalculation, public rectification

When a dispute is upheld, corrections must be made in a way that preserves semantic continuity and prevents silent edits.

### Correction mechanisms (minimum)
- **Restatement**: revised publication of affected series using corrected methods, with mapping from old to new.
- **Recalculation**: recomputation of metrics and any dependent decisions for the relevant scope/time window.
- **Public rectification**: correction notice identifying the incorrect claim, affected period, and corrected outputs/status.
- **Register updates**: update compatibility/audit registers with corrected status and references.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Corrections MUST be versioned releases with changelogs and integrity proofs where required.
- Any public claim relying on corrected measurements MUST be updated; failure to rectify constitutes misrepresentation.

---

## B00.CH12.P09.S04 — Escalation: in case of structural measurement failure → suspension of conformity status (fail-closed)

Some disputes reveal structural measurement failure: systemic lineage breaks, repeated non-reproducibility, or widespread method/data
defects. In such cases, OBA protects integrity by curtailing decision space and suspending compatibility status.

### Structural failure indicators (non-exhaustive)
- Persistent inability to reproduce results across periods or reviewers.
- Missing or corrupted logs/versions preventing lineage reconstruction.
- Evidence access denial preventing verification of compatibility-critical metrics.
- Repeated method changes without versioning or public disclosure.
- Material divergence between independent replications without resolution.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- If structural measurement failure is detected, the affected implementation’s compatibility status MUST be **suspended** or
  downgraded to **non-conform** until measurement integrity is restored (fail-closed).
- During suspension, the implementation MUST NOT use compatibility labels, and any OBA-gated privileges tied to the status MUST be
  paused for the affected scope, subject to governance-defined continuity exceptions.
