# B00.CH12.P05 — Reproducibility and replication

This paragraph defines **reproducibility** and **replication** as binding requirements for OBA measurement credibility. A compatibility
claim is not valid unless a third party can reproduce the reported outcomes from the stated inputs and methods, and unless key results
can be replicated using independent data or alternative methods within a declared tolerance band. Where reproducibility or replication
fails, OBA applies fail-closed rules and treats the affected scope as **non-conform** until corrected.

---

## B00.CH12.P05.S01 — Reproducible result: third party can obtain the same outcome from the same inputs

A result is **reproducible** if an independent party can obtain the same outputs given the same inputs, the same method version, and
the same parameter settings.

### Minimum reproducibility requirements
- **Fixed inputs**: the exact input dataset snapshot (or a verifiable access route to the same snapshot) is specified.
- **Fixed method**: a registered method artifact (specification and/or code) with version, parameters, and execution environment notes.
- **Deterministic execution**: any non-determinism is eliminated or bounded and disclosed (e.g., fixed seeds, stable ordering).

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Any metric used for compatibility status MUST be reproducible by a third party under the defined audit access regime.
- If reproduction fails beyond declared tolerances, the metric MUST be treated as invalid for compatibility until the method/data
  are corrected and re-registered.

---

## B00.CH12.P05.S02 — Replication requirement: independent data or methods must converge within a band

A result must also be **replicable**: independent evidence streams must converge sufficiently to prevent single-pipeline artifacts
from determining compatibility claims.

### Minimum replication requirements
- **Independent data**: alternative datasets or collection routes that measure the same canonical observable, or
- **Independent methods**: different measurement approaches that implement the same canonical definition.
- **Convergence band**: a predeclared tolerance band (interval) within which replicated estimates must fall, with justification.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- For critical compatibility determinations (status labels, cap enforcement, forbidden channel determinations), at least one
  independent replication route MUST be available unless canonically exempted.
- If independent replications diverge beyond the declared band and the divergence cannot be resolved by lineage/audit correction,
  the claim MUST be treated as **uncertain** and therefore **non-conform** (fail-closed).

---

## B00.CH12.P05.S03 — Open method, closed data: privacy-compatible publication (differential disclosure)

OBA requires public methods even when raw data cannot be published due to privacy or security constraints. This is handled by an
**open method / closed data** regime that preserves auditability without maximal disclosure.

### Minimum privacy-compatible publication requirements
- **Open methods**: full public specification of measurement procedures, transformations, thresholds, and uncertainty handling.
- **Differential disclosure**: publication of sufficient statistics, redacted extracts, or privacy-preserving summaries that allow
  verification without revealing protected raw data.
- **Controlled access**: a defined audit access pathway for qualified auditors to verify raw data under constraints.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Inability to publish raw data does not waive reproducibility requirements; it shifts the burden to open methods + audit access.
- Any privacy regime that prevents independent verification of lineage and reproducibility invalidates compatibility claims that
  depend on that data (fail-closed).

---

## B00.CH12.P05.S04 — Ban on “trust me” models: untestable claims are non-conform

Any measurement or decision pipeline that cannot be tested, inspected, or reproduced is prohibited as a basis for compatibility.

### Prohibited patterns (non-exhaustive)
- “Trust me” scoring or classification that cannot be reproduced outside the originating institution.
- Methods that depend on undisclosed features, undisclosed transformations, or inaccessible training data where those elements affect outcomes.
- Claims justified by authority, reputation, or certification without test artifacts and reproducible evidence.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- If a claim depends on an untestable model or non-reproducible pipeline, the claim MUST be treated as **non-conform** immediately.
- To restore conformity, the method must be made reproducible and auditable (open method + verifiable lineage), or the model must be
  removed from the compatibility-critical path.
