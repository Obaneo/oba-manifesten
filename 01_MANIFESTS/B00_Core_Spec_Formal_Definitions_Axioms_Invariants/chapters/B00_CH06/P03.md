# B00.CH06.P03 — I-base: what counts in the cap base

This paragraph defines the canonical **I-base** for I-MAX: which income components count toward the cap base.
I-base must be broad and functional. Narrow “tax income” bases are structurally leaky because high-income subjects
can shift flows across labels (wage → fees → carry → perks → equity) and across entities/vehicles.

Core rule: if a flow is **economic benefit** attributable to an ultimate unit within the measurement period (under
the canonical attribution rule), it is presumptively in I-base unless explicitly carved out under tightly bounded
exceptions (and such exceptions must not enable stock/flow arbitrage with W-MAX).

---

## B00.CH06.P03.S01 — Labour income: wages, bonuses, equity comp, options (at fair value)

Labour income is included in I-base at the level of **economic value received or earned**, not only cash salary.

Included components (minimum set):
- wages/salary,
- bonuses/commissions/variable pay,
- signing/retention/severance packages,
- overtime premia and shift premia where applicable,
- equity compensation and equity-linked remuneration (restricted stock, RSUs, options, SARs, phantom equity),
- employer-paid “cash equivalents” delivered through third parties when attributable to labour.

Valuation rule (binding):
- equity compensation and options are included at **fair value** under declared valuation method and timing rule
  (grant/vesting/payout as defined in CH06.P02.S03 and CH06.P04).
- if fair value depends on models, the model must be disclosed and auditable; otherwise conservative inclusion applies.

Anti-relabelling rule:
- compensation does not exit I-base by being paid via a controlled entity or by being labeled “consulting” where it
  is functionally labour remuneration.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any I-MAX regime MUST include equity compensation and options at fair value; excluding equity comp is a dominant
  leakage channel.
- If labour remuneration is delivered through vehicles, consolidation to the ultimate unit is mandatory.

---

## B00.CH06.P03.S02 — Capital income: dividends, interest, rent, capital gains (canonical treatment)

Capital income is included in I-base because capital returns are a primary channel for concentrated flow capture and
a key bridge between W and I.

Included components (minimum set):
- dividends and distributions,
- interest (including implicit interest components in structured products),
- rent (including concession-like rent and licensing rent),
- royalties and IP licensing revenue,
- realized capital gains under canonical realization/attribution rules.

Canonical treatment requirements:

- **Dividends/buybacks linkage:** distributions and buyback-derived value realization must be treated as I-relevant
  where they function as flow capture (interaction detailed in CH06.P09 and CH05.P15).
- **Rent and royalties:** treat as I even when routed through IP boxes/holdings; apply consolidation.
- **Capital gains:** must be treated with explicit timing rules to prevent lock-in and deferral avoidance (detailed in
  CH06.P04.S02). A regime may choose realization-based or mark-to-market options, but must be explicit and anti-deferral.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- A regime excluding dominant capital-income channels (rent, royalties, capital gains treatment) is non-conform for
  OBA-grade I-MAX claims.
- If capital-income timing is not specified, the system is leaky (lock-in/deferral) and must fail closed.

---

## B00.CH06.P03.S03 — Entrepreneurial income: profit distributions, management fees, profit shares

Entrepreneurial/managerial flows are included because they are a primary bridge between labour-like remuneration and
capital-like returns, often engineered for classification advantage.

Included components (minimum set):
- profit distributions from closely held businesses,
- partnership allocations and profit shares,
- management fees and advisory fees (including via controlled entities),
- performance fees and carried interest where functionally remuneration for control/labour,
- “founder” compensation structures that blend labour and capital returns.

Canonical rule:
- classify by function: if the flow is compensation for labour/control/management, it is included regardless of label.
- if flows are mixed (part labour, part capital), they must be decomposed under auditable rules (see CH06.P10).

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Management/performance fee channels MUST be included; excluding them makes I-base trivially bypassable.
- If profit distributions are retained in controlled entities to suppress I, attribution/deferral rules must pull
  them into I-base under anti-avoidance logic (CH06.P04, CH06.P09).

---

## B00.CH06.P03.S04 — Quasi-income: perks, expense accounts, benefits, deferred comp (requalification)

Quasi-income consists of economic benefit that is not labeled income but functions as income or as a flow-equivalent
claim.

Included components (minimum set):
- personal consumption via expense accounts (non-business consumption),
- employer-provided housing/transport/services for personal benefit,
- below-market loans, loan forgiveness, and preferential purchase rights,
- benefits in kind (insurance, tuition, concierge services, private security) where personal,
- deferred compensation where entitlement is earned but payment is postponed.

Requalification rule (binding):
- if a component functions as consumption-equivalent benefit, include it as I at fair value for the period.
- if a component functions as a durable right/claim (e.g., concession, preferential access), treat as I-equivalent
  or W-equivalent as appropriate; it cannot be “neither”.

Fail-closed posture:
- if quasi-income valuation or classification cannot be audited, include conservatively or trigger provisional cap
  enforcement until clarified.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any I-MAX regime MUST include quasi-income channels and define valuation rules; otherwise high earners shift into
  perks and bypass I-MAX.
- Black-box compensation packages (opaque perks, undisclosed deferred comp schedules) are non-conform by default.
