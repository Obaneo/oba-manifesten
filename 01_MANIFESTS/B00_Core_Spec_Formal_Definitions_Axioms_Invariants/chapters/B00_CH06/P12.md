# B00.CH06.P12 — Reporting and publication duty (minimum disclosure)

This paragraph defines the **reporting and publication** requirements for I-MAX to ensure full **transparency** and
**auditability**. Reporting is not merely for public knowledge; it is the enforcement tool that verifies compliance
with the income cap and exposes any attempts to evade or game the system.

Canonical posture: a **minimum dataset** must be reported, including income components, valuation schedules, deferral
details, and entity-level disclosure. Public statistics on top earners and distribution shares must be made available,
and the **frequency** of reporting must match the potential for evasion via non-periodic compensation.

---

## B00.CH06.P12.S01 — Minimum dataset: income components, valuation, deferral schedules, entities

The **minimum dataset** required for I-MAX compliance must include the following:

### (a) Income components
- **Detailed income breakdown:** wages, bonuses, dividends, capital income, royalties, carried interest, and other
  forms of I.
- **In-kind benefits:** all non-cash income items must be valued at fair market value and included in the dataset.
- **Deferral schedules:** full transparency on income that is deferred, including schedules for when deferred income
  will be realised.

### (b) Valuation and attribution
- **Valuation methods:** clear disclosure of the methods used to value equity compensation, carried interest, and other
  variable/complex income components (e.g., discount rate, vesting assumptions, market conditions).
- **Attribution schedules:** income must be attributed to the correct periods, especially for deferred or stock-based
  compensation (linked to CH06.P04).

### (c) Entities and vehicles
- **UBC look-through:** for any cross-entity income flow (e.g., management fees, royalties, dividends), the disclosure
  must identify the ultimate beneficiary unit.
- **Entity-level reporting:** income flows between related entities (e.g., self-employment, partnerships, family offices)
  must be traced to the individual units involved.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- The **minimum dataset** must be fully auditable and publicly accessible to ensure the integrity of I-MAX enforcement.
- Any missing components or non-disclosure of key items (e.g., deferral schedules, income attribution) triggers fail-closed
  enforcement and suspends the compatibility claim for that subject.

---

## B00.CH06.P12.S02 — Public statistics: distributions, top shares, under-α correlations with R/B

Public reporting must provide relevant statistical insights to monitor and improve I-MAX’s effectiveness in achieving
**economic justice** and **R/B stability**. The system must include:

### (a) Distributions
- **Income distribution breakdown:** top earners, median wage ratio, and tail percentiles (e.g., top 1%, top 0.1%).
- **R/B correlation:** track the relationship between the income distribution (top earners) and the **R/B profiles** (life-world buffers).
- **Under-α share:** percentage of the population below the **α R/B floor** and how income distribution shifts correlate with these.

### (b) Top shares
- **Top income share** of total reported I, including income from high compensation, equity returns, and other top-end flows.

### (c) R/B indicators
- **R/B stability tracking:** monitor how changes in I distribution (e.g., compression, top earners) affect R/B (particularly
  the buffer for vulnerable cohorts).

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Public statistics must be made **regularly available** and **disaggregated** by key categories (R/B, income sources, top
  earners).
- Any regime failing to report these statistics on a consistent basis must be considered non-conform with OBA principles.

---

## B00.CH06.P12.S03 — Frequency: periodic + event-driven (bonus cycles, equity vesting)

Reporting frequency must support **real-time compliance monitoring** and avoid strategic timing exploitation.

### (a) Periodic reporting
- **Annual/quarterly** reporting of income flows for subjects above defined thresholds, with clear **reference dates**
  or **averaging windows**.

### (b) Event-driven reporting
- **Major financial events**: large bonuses, stock option exercises, carried interest crystallization, large equity
  vesting events, and major income transactions must trigger **immediate reporting** or review.
- **Cross-border reporting:** income realized in different jurisdictions, or linked to **re-domiciliation**, must be
  disclosed in the reporting period of occurrence.

Event-driven reports must be **immediate**, transparent, and subject to verification (with traceability to the full
audit trail of cap breaches).

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any I-MAX regime must define **event-driven reporting triggers** for bonuses, equity vesting, and major transactions.
- If major event-driven income is not reported, the system must apply **fail-closed** enforcement, including skimming or
  provisional inclusion until clarified.

---

## B00.CH06.P12.S04 — Independent attestation and liability in case of misrepresentation

A strong **audit framework** is necessary for I-MAX compliance to ensure the accuracy and integrity of the reported data.
Independent **attestation** from certified auditors is required for all high-income subjects.

### (a) Independent attestation
- **Audit scope:** verify reported income, deferred compensation, cross-entity relations, and income attribution.
- **Audit independence:** attestation must come from a third-party independent audit firm to avoid conflicts of interest.

### (b) Liability for misrepresentation
- **Liability clause:** if income data is misrepresented, either through omission or incorrect classification, the subject
  will be held liable for damages.
- **Penalties for non-compliance:** failure to comply with I-MAX reporting standards or misrepresentation of income
  data will trigger proportional penalties, including back-payment of owed taxes and fines.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any I-MAX regime must require **independent attestation** for all relevant subjects and define **liability** in case of
  misrepresentation.
- If attestation is not provided, or if false information is submitted, **fail-closed** enforcement applies, and the
  regime’s compatibility claim is suspended.
