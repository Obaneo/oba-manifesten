# B00.CH06.P01 — Purpose, scope and normative status of I-MAX

This paragraph defines the **purpose**, **scope**, and **normative status** of **I-MAX**: the canonical cap on
**income/flows (I)** as a time-indexed flow variable.

I-MAX is an order instrument. It exists because unconstrained top-end flows create durable structural effects:
they reward and amplify extraction (E), expand claim layers, and rapidly rebuild wealth (W) through deferral and
conversion pathways. Without a flow-side brake, stock-side caps (W-MAX) can be circumvented by timing, retention,
and rewrapping of compensation streams.

Normative status: any document, dataset, or implementation claiming OBA conformance/compatibility that asserts an
income cap, top-income limit, “excess compensation” brake, or flow skimming mechanism MUST map to this chapter’s
I definition, I-base, deferral rules, deductibility limits, enforcement posture, and fail-closed requirements (or an
explicitly versioned extension that does not redefine canonical meaning).

---

## B00.CH06.P01.S01 — I-MAX as order invariant: limiting top income and flow accumulation

I-MAX functions as an **order invariant** by limiting the accumulation of **top-end flows** that translate into:
- concentrated power,
- persistent extraction incentives,
- and rapid compounding into W.

Canonical causal chain:
- high flows → capacity to purchase scarce essentials and influence institutions → sustained toll capacity → E growth
  and fee-stack layering → L pressure (essentials burdens) → reduced R and weaker R/B distribution tails.

I-MAX is therefore not merely a “redistribution” lever. It constrains the dynamic where extreme flow capture becomes
a reinforcing mechanism of dominance and structural fragility.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any I-MAX regime MUST define I as a time-indexed flow and must apply the cap to the consolidated flow reality, not
  only to a narrow taxable subset.
- If the cap applies only to a subset that can be bypassed via relabeling (fees, carry, perks), it is non-conform.

---

## B00.CH06.P01.S02 — Purpose: brake on E-driven flow extraction and build-up of W via deferral

I-MAX has two primary purposes:

1) **Brake on E-driven flow extraction**
   - reduce rewards to toll/lock-in rents, monopoly margins, platform take-rates, fee stacks, and other E-income
     channels that incentivize extraction strategies.

2) **Brake on W rebuild via deferral and conversion**
   - prevent high-flow actors from converting income into wealth stock (W) through retention, buybacks, equity
     grants, deferred compensation, synthetic exposures, or entity-layer consolidation that suppresses recognized I.

I-MAX complements P/E/C by constraining the flow side of extraction and complements W-MAX by preventing stock/flow
arbitrage.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any I-MAX implementation MUST include anti-deferral logic (realisation/attribution rules) sufficient to prevent
  systematic conversion I→W as a bypass channel.
- If high earners can remain below I-MAX through deferral while compounding W, the regime is non-conform.

---

## B00.CH06.P01.S03 — Scope: wages, bonuses, profit distributions, interest, rent, royalties, fees, carried interest

I-MAX scope is functional: it includes all material channels by which economic benefit is received as a flow, whether
in cash or in kind (valuation rules apply in P02–P03).

Canonical scope classes (minimum set; non-exhaustive):

- **Labour income:** wages, bonuses, commissions, equity compensation and option value (fair value treatment),
  severance and signing packages.
- **Entrepreneurial/managerial flows:** profit distributions, management fees, performance fees, profit shares.
- **Capital income:** dividends, interest, rent, royalties, realized capital gains (canonical treatment specified
  later), licensing revenue.
- **Hybrid/edge flows:** carried interest and similar instruments where labor remuneration is labeled as capital return.
- **Quasi-income:** perks, benefits, expense accounts, deferred comp with economic availability.

Scope must be consolidated across entities/vehicles where substance indicates the same ultimate benefit unit.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any I-MAX regime MUST state included components explicitly and must not exclude dominant high-income channels
  (carry, fees, equity comp, perks) without a canonical carve-out (tightly bounded) and anti-avoidance proof.
- If a component cannot be measured or classified, it is included conservatively or triggers fail-closed posture.

## B00.CH06.P01.S04 — Relation to W-MAX, P/E/C, R/B and ΔM (linkages and substitution risks)

I-MAX sits inside the broader OBA stack and must be coherent with other canonical instruments.

### Link to W-MAX (stock/flow substitution risk)
- Without I-MAX, high flows can quickly rebuild W even under W-MAX by shifting timing and form.
- Without W-MAX, high W can generate continuous I through rents/fees/royalties while preserving dominance.
- Joint coherence requires cross-checks: changes in I should reconcile with changes in W under UBC consolidation.

### Link to P/E/C (E-income vs P-income)
- P/E/C provides classification of flows by function; I-MAX constrains the top-end accumulation regardless of label.
- E-income channels (toll/lock-in rents, fee stacks) are primary targets for braking; mixed roles require decompositions.

### Link to R/B (life-world floor)
- I-MAX is justified by its contribution to stabilizing R/B profiles: reducing extraction rewards that drive L up and
  that compress remaining space for cohorts below α.
- Evaluation must check distributional tails, not only averages: top-end compression without tail improvement is suspect.

### Link to ΔM
- ΔM allocations can inflate asset prices and feed top flows (capital income). I-MAX provides a complementary brake on
  flow capture from ΔM-driven price channels.
- ΔM channels that amplify top-end flow capture without improving R/B are non-conform; I-MAX monitoring supports this.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any I-MAX implementation claiming OBA compatibility MUST specify its interaction assumptions with W-MAX, P/E/C, R/B,
  and ΔM (monitoring + escalation + anti-arbitrage).
- If the regime allows substitution (income relabeled as exempt flows, or converted into W without detection),
  compatibility claims are suspended until remediated.
