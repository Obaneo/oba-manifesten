# B00.CH05.P13 — Measurement and reporting duty (minimum disclosure)

This paragraph defines the minimum **measurement and reporting duty** required for any W-MAX regime to be
OBA-compatible. A cap on wealth/claims is only enforceable if the cap base can be:
- identified (asset/exposure coverage),
- consolidated (UBC look-through relations),
- valued (valuation basis + uncertainty),
- and audited (attestation + liability).

OBA imposes privacy-minimalism: disclose only what is needed for auditability and enforcement, but do not use privacy
as a blanket exemption to avoid traceability.

---

## B00.CH05.P13.S01 — Minimum dataset: asset list, exposures, control/benefit relations, valuation basis

Minimum dataset must support full reconstruction of consolidated W and cap events.

Canonical minimum fields (grouped):

### (a) Subject and boundary identifiers
- subject/unit ID (person/household/ultimate unit) within regime privacy rules,
- jurisdiction/residency regime ID and relevant nexus flags,
- reporting period and reference date/averaging window.

### (b) Asset and exposure inventory (W-base coverage)
- asset/exposure type classification (cash, listed equity, private equity, real estate, IP, concessions, derivatives, etc.),
- instrument identifiers where applicable (ISIN-like, registry IDs) or structured descriptors for illiquid assets,
- quantity/position size,
- location/custody/vehicle node (account/custodian/vehicle ID under privacy constraints),
- flags for economic equivalents (options, swaps, structured notes) and embedded exposures.

### (c) Vehicle and UBC relations (control/benefit/block graph edges)
- entity/vehicle list (holdings, SPVs, trusts, foundations, nominees),
- control relations (voting/control rights; governance covenants),
- benefit relations (cashflow rights, upside participation, preferential rights),
- block relations (veto rights, negative control, lock-in rights),
- ownership percentages if relevant, but not sufficient alone,
- conflict/overlap flags (shared control, split benefit).

### (d) Liabilities and netting inputs (for net W treatment)
- liabilities list with counterparties and terms,
- collateral/pledge indicators and rehypothecation flags where applicable,
- related-party indicators and consolidation links to detect circular liabilities,
- recognition status (recognized / not recognized / haircut) with rationale.

### (e) Valuation basis and uncertainty
- valuation basis per item (market/book/model) per hierarchy,
- valuation inputs (market quote source, book statement reference, model method tag),
- haircuts/bands where illiquid or model-based,
- timestamp of valuation and revaluation triggers.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- If the dataset cannot reconstruct the UBC consolidation graph and valuation basis, W-MAX is non-auditable and
  compatibility claims must fail closed.
- “Summary total wealth” without itemization and relation edges is non-conform for any enforceable W-MAX claim.

## B00.CH05.P13.S02 — Publication level: aggregates + methodology; privacy minimalism where possible

OBA distinguishes between:
- **regulator/auditor-level disclosure** (necessary for enforcement and reproducibility), and
- **public disclosure** (aggregates and methods, privacy-minimal).

Canonical publication minimum (public level):

- **methodology package:** definitions used (W, W-base, UBC rules), valuation hierarchy, haircut/band regime, reporting
  frequency, requalification triggers, dispute protocol references.
- **aggregate distributions:** W distribution metrics (quantiles, tail mass above W-MAX), breach counts, skim totals,
  remedy categories, reallocation totals to C (with ΔG reporting links where admissible).
- **avoidance indicators:** high-level metrics on requalification events, opacity flags, enforcement escalations
  (without doxxing individuals).
- **changelog/versioning:** definition-set ID and changes across releases.

Privacy minimalism principles:
- do not publish personal itemized lists publicly unless explicitly required by regime; publish aggregates instead.
- but do not allow privacy claims to block auditor-level disclosure necessary for enforcement.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- A W-MAX regime MUST publish aggregate outcomes and full methodology; unpublished methods are non-conform for OBA-grade claims.
- Privacy cannot be used to deny auditor-level traceability; if traceability is blocked, the subject/channel becomes
  non-certifying and triggers fail-closed enforcement posture.

## B00.CH05.P13.S03 — Frequency: periodic + event-driven (major transactions/structural changes)

W-MAX requires both periodic reporting and event-driven triggers to prevent timing avoidance and window dressing.

Canonical frequency requirements:

### Periodic reporting
- regular reporting cadence (e.g., quarterly/annual) with declared reference dates or averaging windows.

### Event-driven reporting (mandatory triggers)
- major asset acquisitions/disposals,
- creation/restructuring of vehicles (trusts, SPVs, foundations, holding layers),
- large derivative/exposure creation or unwinds,
- cross-border moves (residency changes, custody moves, re-domiciliation),
- changes in control rights (voting blocks, veto rights, concession awards),
- large borrowing/leverage events materially affecting netting or exposure.

Event triggers must link to reassessment rules (cap event potential) and revaluation duties (CH05.P08).

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any regime relying only on annual reporting without event triggers is leaky and non-conform where avoidance is feasible.
- Failure to report event-driven triggers results in non-compliance status and may trigger conservative provisional
  enforcement and sanctions under enforcement regime rules.

## B00.CH05.P13.S04 — Mandatory attestation: independent audit, liability in case of misrepresentation

Because self-reporting is a dominant failure mode, W-MAX requires mandatory attestation and liability.

Canonical attestation requirements:

- **Independent audit/attestation** for subjects above defined thresholds (e.g., near/above W-MAX or complex structures),
  including verification of:
  - completeness of asset/exposure inventory,
  - correctness of UBC graph relations,
  - valuation basis integrity and haircut/band application,
  - liability recognition and related-party detection.
- **Liability for misrepresentation:** defined legal/administrative liability for omission, false valuation, sham
  structures, or obstruction. Liability must be proportional but materially deterrent.
- **Audit trail retention:** record retention for sufficient horizon to detect window dressing and delayed disclosure.

Attestation must not become a private-market capture mechanism (auditors selling bespoke interpretations). Therefore
audit standards and method tags must be canonical and versioned.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- A W-MAX regime MUST require independent attestation for relevant subjects and must define liability for
  misrepresentation; otherwise the cap is non-auditable and non-enforceable.
- If audit is compromised (black-box valuation, refusal to disclose, obstruction), the system must fail closed and
  treat affected W positions as non-certifying with enforcement escalation.
