# B00.CH05.P01 — Purpose, scope and normative status of W-MAX

This paragraph defines the **purpose**, **scope**, and **normative status** of **W-MAX**: the canonical cap on
**wealth/claims/rights (W)** as a **stock** variable.

W-MAX is an order instrument, not a moral statement. It exists because claim positions compound into durable power:
without a stock-side brake, extraction (E) can be temporarily constrained but will regenerate via accumulated claims,
control rights, and rent channels.

Normative status: any document, dataset, implementation, or policy instrument claiming OBA conformance/compatibility
that asserts a wealth cap, claim cap, or “anti-concentration” mechanism MUST map to this chapter’s W-MAX definitions,
bases, consolidation rules, valuation discipline, and fail-closed posture (or an explicitly versioned extension that
does not redefine canonical meaning).

---

## B00.CH05.P01.S01 — W-MAX as order invariant: limiting claim-stacks and power

W-MAX functions as an **order invariant**: it constrains the ability to accumulate and retain claim stacks that
translate into durable power over essentials, markets, and institutions.

Canonical mechanism:
- **W (stock of claims/rights)** → control/benefit/block power → sustained toll capacity → **E** → essentials burdens
  and claim layers (**L**) → reduced remaining space (**R**) and weakened buffer (**R/B**).

W-MAX is therefore not primarily about equality; it is about **preventing structural domination** via stored claims.
If W can grow without bound, then:
- rent channels become self-reinforcing,
- concentration becomes stable,
- and “flow-side” corrections (taxes, transfers, I-MAX) become continuously bypassed through storage, rewrapping,
  and intergenerational compounding.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any W-MAX regime MUST be defined as a cap on economically meaningful claim storage (including equivalents and
  control rights), not merely on a narrow legal asset list.
- If the W base excludes dominant forms of claim power (vehicles, derivatives, control rights), the instrument is
  cosmetic and non-conform for OBA-grade claims.

---

## B00.CH05.P01.S02 — Purpose: reducing E by putting a brake on claim accumulation and rent-seeking

The primary purpose of W-MAX is to reduce **E** by constraining the **storage** that sustains extraction.

W-MAX targets the upstream condition of persistent extraction:
- accumulation of assets and rights that enable tolls, monopolies, gatekeeping, and fee-stack capture,
- compounding advantage that allows claimholders to outbid others for scarce essentials,
- political and institutional influence that preserves rent channels.

W-MAX is designed to:
- limit the **durability** of rent-seeking strategies,
- weaken feedback loops that regenerate E,
- and create room for C/P regimes to lower L and stabilize R/B.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any W-MAX proposal claiming to reduce E MUST specify which extraction channels are constrained (which assets/rights
  map to which toll powers) and how the cap prevents storage-driven regeneration.
- If a cap reduces reported W but leaves extraction capacity unchanged (e.g., via rewrapping into excluded equivalents),
  the proposal is non-conform.

---

## B00.CH05.P01.S03 — Scope: natural persons, households, vehicles, trusts, foundations, holdings

W-MAX applies to the economic reality of claim storage, not to a single legal persona. Scope is therefore defined by
**UBC look-through** across structures.

Canonical scope includes (non-exhaustive):

- **Natural persons** (primary subjects of ultimate control/benefit).
- **Households** (joint control/benefit regimes; shared economic reality).
- **Vehicles and intermediaries**: holding companies, SPVs, funds, nominee structures, custodial accounts.
- **Trusts and foundations** (including discretionary, charitable, and hybrid forms) where a person/household has
  control, benefit, or block power.
- **Holdings and cross-holdings** (multi-layer ownership, pyramids, circular structures).

The scope is intentionally broad to prevent avoidance by dispersion or re-domiciliation of legal form.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any W-MAX regime MUST define scope via UBC (control/benefit/block) and must apply look-through consolidation to
  vehicles and structures.
- If the regime limits scope to “direct personal assets” only, it is leaky by design and non-conform.

## B00.CH05.P01.S04 — Relation to I-MAX, P/E/C, R/B, ΔM and commons (linkages)

W-MAX is a stock-side instrument that must be interpreted within the OBA stack.

### Link to I-MAX (flow-side cap)
- I-MAX constrains claim flows (income). W-MAX constrains the accumulated stock and prevents **income→wealth**
  deferral as avoidance.
- The pair is required to prevent stock/flow arbitrage: if only one exists, avoidance shifts to the other.

### Link to P/E/C
- W-MAX is not defined by P/E/C, but it constrains the storage of **E-derived surplus** and therefore reduces the
  capacity to sustain extraction.
- W valuation and base inclusion rules must treat E-driven asset channels (monopoly rents, fee-stack claims) as
  cap-relevant, regardless of labels.

### Link to R/B (life-world floor)
- The purpose of capping claim storage is to reduce L inflation and strengthen cohort R/B profiles over time.
- W-MAX must therefore be evaluated partly by its effect on essentials burdens (L) and tail share below α.

### Link to ΔM
- ΔM allocations can interact with W via asset price channels; W-MAX prevents ΔM from translating into runaway
  wealth concentration via asset inflation.
- ΔM channel rules should avoid supporting assets in ways that raise L; W-MAX is a complementary brake.

### Link to commons (C)
- Remedy paths above W-MAX can feed **C** only under traceable allocation and anti-capture governance.
- “Reallocation” that returns to the same owners via backflow or captured institutions is non-conform.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any OBA-grade W-MAX implementation MUST specify its interaction assumptions with I-MAX, ΔM, and R/B (what is
  monitored; how avoidance is prevented).
- Claims that W-MAX “helps the commons” or “improves R/B” require traceable mechanisms (cap event → remedy →
  C/L effects) and cannot be asserted by narrative.
