# B00.CH09.P08 — Standard of proof and documentation duty

This paragraph outlines the **standard of proof** and **documentation duty** necessary to ensure compliance with the
**control**, **benefit**, and **block tests** within the OBA framework. Proper documentation and an established **audit
trail** are critical for transparency, accountability, and the prevention of fraud or hidden ownership. The burden of proof
is placed on entities to substantiate claims of **control**, **benefit**, or **block** power, ensuring that all structures
and relationships are **fully auditable** and **transparent**.

---

## B00.CH09.P08.S01 — Minimum documentation: shareholder registers, contracts, side letters, governance docs

The **minimum documentation** requirement ensures that all **ownership** and **control** relationships are clearly
**documented** and verifiable. This includes all relevant governance and contractual documents that define how entities or
individuals exert control or benefit from assets, entities, or flows.

### Key documents required:
- **Shareholder registers**: official records that list the **shareholders** of a company and their respective ownership
  stakes, ensuring that all parties with an interest in the company are identified.
- **Contracts**: any **formal agreements** that specify the terms of ownership, control, or benefit, including **partnership**,
  **shareholder** agreements, or **investment contracts**.
- **Side letters**: supplementary agreements that alter or clarify the terms of primary contracts, often used to provide
  additional benefits, privileges, or obligations to specific parties.
- **Governance documents**: documents that define the **decision-making processes** and **control structures** within an
  entity, such as **corporate charters**, **bylaws**, **board meeting minutes**, or **voting rights agreements**.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- All entities must maintain up-to-date and accurate documentation, including **shareholder registers**, **contracts**, and
  **governance documents**.
- **Failure to provide** these documents or misrepresentation in any of these records leads to **non-compliance**.

---

## B00.CH09.P08.S02 — Burden of proof: in case of uncertainty, count towards consolidation (conservatism)

When there is **uncertainty** regarding ownership, control, or benefit in complex structures, the **burden of proof** lies
with the **entity or party** claiming **ownership** or **control**. If the claim cannot be substantiated, the position will
be treated as part of a **consolidated group**, in line with **OBA’s conservative principles**.

### Key principles:
- **Burden of proof**: it is the responsibility of the party claiming **control**, **benefit**, or **block** to provide
  **verifiable evidence** supporting their claim. If no such evidence is provided, the structure must be **consolidated**
  with other related entities.
- **Conservatism**: when in doubt, **err on the side of consolidation**, ensuring that all relationships are fully accounted
  for and that **opaque structures** are eliminated.
  
This approach ensures that **non-transparent ownership** or **control** does not go unchallenged and that resources are
not diverted or misallocated due to lack of clarity.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- **Entities must provide evidence** of ownership, control, or benefit, and in cases of **uncertainty**, the position must
  be consolidated and disclosed.
- **Failure to prove ownership or control** or to provide transparency in **complex structures** results in **non-compliance**.

---

## B00.CH09.P08.S03 — Audit trail: reproducible graph evaluation and decision logs

An **audit trail** is essential for tracking and verifying all decisions, allocations, and ownership changes. The **audit trail**
must allow for **reproducible graph evaluation** and the ability to trace decisions from initiation to outcome.

### Key principles:
- **Reproducible graph evaluation**: all decisions, allocations, and ownership structures must be recorded in a manner
  that allows for **reproduction** and **evaluation** by independent auditors. The relationships between entities must
  be traceable, even in complex or multi-layered structures.
- **Decision logs**: every decision related to **ownership**, **control**, and **benefit** must be clearly recorded and
  available for audit, ensuring that the decision-making process is transparent and accountable.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- All decisions regarding **ownership**, **control**, and **benefit** must be logged and linked to their respective **audit
  trails**.
- **Failure to maintain** a clear, **traceable audit trail** leads to **non-compliance**, and the related flow or structure
  must be addressed immediately.

---

## B00.CH09.P08.S04 — Privacy minimalism: only data needed for control/benefit/block (link to Book 4)

**Privacy minimalism** ensures that only the **necessary data** for assessing **control**, **benefit**, or **block power**
is collected and processed. This principle respects **privacy rights** while ensuring that sufficient information is
available for proper **ownership**, **control**, and **benefit** attribution.

### Key principles:
- **Minimal data collection**: only collect **data directly necessary** for determining **control**, **benefit**, or
  **block**. This prevents unnecessary collection of personal or sensitive data and ensures that individuals' privacy is
  respected.
- **Link to Book 4**: the principle aligns with the **data privacy standards** outlined in **Book 4**, ensuring that data
  collection remains minimal and directly relevant to the determination of **ownership** and **control**.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Entities must only collect **data necessary** for proving **ownership**, **control**, or **benefit** and must limit the
  collection of **unnecessary** personal or sensitive information.
- **Failure to adhere** to **privacy minimalism** principles results in **non-compliance** and must be corrected immediately.
