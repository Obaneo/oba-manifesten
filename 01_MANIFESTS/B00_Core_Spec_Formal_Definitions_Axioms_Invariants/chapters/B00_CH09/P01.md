# B00.CH09.P01 — Purpose, scope and normative status of control/benefit/block tests

This paragraph defines the **purpose**, **scope**, and **normative status** of the **control**, **benefit**, and **block**
tests within the OBA framework. These tests are crucial for ensuring that **ownership structures** and **beneficiary
relationships** are transparent, non-extractive, and in compliance with public goods provision principles. The tests
are designed to prevent **sham structures**, **nominee arrangements**, or any efforts to obscure real control or benefit,
which could undermine the integrity of commons and wealth distribution systems.

---

## B00.CH09.P01.S01 — Tests as canonical basis for UBC (ultimate beneficial control) and consolidation

The **control**, **benefit**, and **block tests** are the **canonical basis** for determining **ultimate beneficial control** (UBC) and for establishing **consolidation** rules. These tests help identify who truly **controls**,
**benefits from**, or **can block** decisions within entities or structures, ensuring that all relevant parties are held
accountable and that **non-extractive ownership** is maintained.

### Key functions of these tests:
- **UBC (Ultimate Beneficial Control)**: identifying the individuals or entities that ultimately **control** or **benefit**
  from assets or flows.
- **Consolidation**: determining when multiple positions or entities should be combined for regulatory or compliance
  purposes, based on control, benefit, and block relationships.

These tests ensure that **ownership** and **control** are fully transparent and comply with OBA's objectives of ensuring
**public goods provision** and **non-extractive resource allocation**.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- The **control**, **benefit**, and **block tests** must be applied to all ownership structures, entities, and transactions
  to ensure compliance with OBA.
- Any attempt to bypass these tests or obscure real control/benefit relationships through **sham structures** or opaque
  arrangements is **non-compliant** and must be addressed immediately.

---

## B00.CH09.P01.S02 — Purpose: preventing sham structures, nominees and “legal fog”

The primary **purpose** of the **control**, **benefit**, and **block tests** is to prevent the creation of **sham structures**,
**nominee arrangements**, and **“legal fog”**—all of which are methods used to hide or obscure real ownership, control, or
benefit. These practices undermine the integrity of the commons system by allowing private parties to **circumvent regulations** 
or **conceal true ownership** of public resources.

### Key principles:
- **Sham structures**: legal entities created to disguise the **true nature** of ownership or control (e.g., shell companies,
  family trusts, opaque ownership chains).
- **Nominee arrangements**: the use of **nominees** or **intermediaries** who hold legal ownership or control on behalf of
  another party to obscure the true **economic owner**.
- **Legal fog**: complex or opaque legal structures designed to confuse or hide real ownership or control, making it
  difficult to assess compliance or responsibility.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any structure or entity that uses **sham arrangements**, **nominee ownership**, or **legal fog** to obscure real
  ownership, control, or benefit is **non-compliant** and must be **restructured or disqualified**.

---

## B00.CH09.P01.S03 — Scope: wealth (W), income (I), ΔM channels, commons governance, platforms

The **scope** of the **control**, **benefit**, and **block tests** extends across all key areas of the OBA framework,
including **wealth (W)**, **income (I)**, **ΔM channels**, **commons governance**, and **platforms**. These areas are
central to the functioning of the monetary system and the allocation of public goods.

### Key scope components:
- **Wealth (W)**: assets, holdings, and ownership of resources, including **financial assets**, **real estate**, and
  **capital**.
- **Income (I)**: flows of resources, including **wages**, **profits**, **fees**, and **dividends**.
- **ΔM channels**: the pathways through which **monetary flows** are allocated to public goods, infrastructure, and commons
  services.
- **Commons governance**: oversight and decision-making processes for managing shared resources and services (e.g., **public
  services**, **natural resources**, **common-pool resources**).
- **Platforms**: digital platforms and systems that mediate access to resources, services, and information, with particular
  attention to issues like **data ownership**, **control**, and **exclusivity**.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- The **control**, **benefit**, and **block tests** must apply to all entities and relationships within the scope of **wealth**,
  **income**, **ΔM**, **commons governance**, and **platforms**.
- Any failure to **apply these tests** to areas within the defined scope results in **non-compliance**.

---

## B00.CH09.P01.S04 — Relation to caps, P/E/C, R/B, auditing and anti-avoidance

The **control**, **benefit**, and **block tests** are closely related to other OBA principles, including **W/I caps** (wealth/income),
**P/E/C** (production/extraction/commons), **R/B** (life-world buffers), **auditing** practices, and **anti-avoidance** rules.

### Key relationships:
- **Caps (W/I)**: ensuring that **control** and **benefit** do not enable **wealth concentration** or **income inequality**,
  thus violating the **caps** on wealth and income.
- **P/E/C**: the tests ensure that **P** (productive activities), **E** (extractive activities), and **C** (commons building)
  are accurately classified and that monetary flows are allocated in line with these principles.
- **R/B**: the tests contribute to the **stabilisation of R/B** by ensuring that control and benefit are properly distributed
  and do not lead to **financial extraction**.
- **Auditing**: these tests provide a **framework for auditing** the relationships of control, benefit, and block within the
  system, ensuring transparency and accountability.
- **Anti-avoidance**: the tests prevent **avoidance behaviors** such as creating **opaque ownership structures** to bypass
  OBA regulations.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- The **control**, **benefit**, and **block tests** must be applied in conjunction with **caps**, **P/E/C**, and **R/B**
  principles to ensure that the system remains non-extractive and compliant with OBA.
- **Failure to integrate** these tests with broader OBA principles results in **non-compliance** and must be rectified.
