# B00.CH09.P11 — Enforcement and dispute regime

This paragraph establishes the **enforcement** and **dispute resolution** procedures for ensuring compliance with the
**control**, **benefit**, and **block tests** within the OBA framework. It defines the processes for **mandatory
registration**, **objection/appeal**, and **sanctions** related to ownership and control structures, ensuring transparency
and accountability across all entities. These mechanisms are designed to uphold the integrity of the framework and prevent
evasion or misrepresentation of control, benefit, or ownership.

---

## B00.CH09.P11.S01 — Mandatory registration of control/benefit/block relationships (minimum disclosure)

**Mandatory registration** requires entities to **disclose** and **register** all **control**, **benefit**, and **block**
relationships within their ownership structures. This ensures that all relevant parties involved in decision-making,
benefit receipt, and the ability to block decisions are **clearly documented** and accessible for review.

### Key principles:
- **Control/benefit/block relationships**: all entities must **register** individuals or groups who have significant **control**,
  **benefit**, or **blocking** power over an entity.
- **Minimum disclosure**: the registration must include **basic information** about the nature of the relationships (e.g.,
  ownership percentages, voting rights, financial benefits).
- **Transparency**: the disclosure of these relationships ensures that ownership structures are **transparent** and can be
  audited.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- **Failure to disclose** or **register** these relationships results in **non-compliance**, and the entity must take corrective
  actions immediately.
- Any **failure to provide full and accurate disclosure** of **control**, **benefit**, and **block** relationships may
  result in **penalties** or **sanctions**.

---

## B00.CH09.P11.S02 — Objection/appeal: contestability with time-bounded procedures

Entities or individuals affected by a control, benefit, or block decision under OBA must have the ability to **contest** or
**appeal** the decision through a defined process. This ensures that disputes can be **addressed** efficiently and fairly,
with clear timelines for resolution.

### Key principles:
- **Objection process**: parties affected by control/benefit/block assessments must be allowed to **challenge** decisions
  through an **objection** process, which should be based on clear legal or factual grounds.
- **Appeal process**: if the objection is not resolved satisfactorily, parties must have the right to **appeal** to a higher
  authority or regulatory body.
- **Time-bounded procedures**: all objections and appeals must be resolved within a **defined timeframe** to ensure
  timely accountability and prevent unnecessary delays.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- **Failure to provide a fair** and **time-bounded objection/appeal process** results in **non-compliance**.
- Entities must **ensure that dispute procedures** are **clearly defined** and followed within the established time limits.

---

## B00.CH09.P11.S03 — Sanctions: misrepresentation, non-disclosure, obstruction, reclassification + fines

Entities that fail to comply with the **control**, **benefit**, and **block tests** or engage in **misrepresentation**, 
**non-disclosure**, or **obstruction** of transparency will be subject to **sanctions**. These sanctions are designed to
enforce compliance and ensure that individuals or entities cannot **evade accountability**.

### Key sanctions:
- **Misrepresentation**: penalties for intentionally misrepresenting **ownership**, **control**, or **benefit** information
  to mislead auditors or regulatory bodies.
- **Non-disclosure**: sanctions for failing to disclose **control**, **benefit**, or **block** relationships in a timely or
  complete manner.
- **Obstruction**: penalties for intentionally obstructing audits, investigations, or the **disclosure of relevant information**.
- **Reclassification**: if an entity is found to have **misclassified** control or ownership structures, it must be reclassified
  in accordance with OBA standards, and financial penalties may apply.
- **Fines**: financial penalties for non-compliance, misrepresentation, or failure to provide the required documentation.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- **Failure to disclose** or **misrepresent control/benefit/block relationships** will result in **fines** and other **sanctions**.
- **Obstruction** or **failure to comply with audit and disclosure requirements** will result in **non-compliance** and
  corrective action must be taken.

---

## B00.CH09.P11.S04 — Hard stops: black-box ownership, refusal of disclosure → non-conform status

**Hard stops** are immediate actions taken to **suspend** or **terminate** a relationship, entity, or transaction that
involves **black-box ownership** or **refusal of disclosure**. These actions prevent non-compliant entities from operating
within the OBA framework.

### Key principles:
- **Black-box ownership**: if an entity refuses to **disclose** the **actual ownership** or **control** structures (i.e.,
  **black-box ownership**), it will be **suspended** from the system until full transparency is achieved.
- **Refusal of disclosure**: entities that **refuse to disclose** control, benefit, or block relationships will be placed
  in **non-conform status** and subject to **immediate corrective action**.
- **Non-conform status**: entities that fail to comply with disclosure or transparency requirements will be considered
  **non-compliant** and will not be allowed to participate in the system until they comply.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- **Black-box ownership** or **refusal to disclose** relevant information results in **immediate suspension** of
  the entity or relationship, and it will be classified as **non-compliant**.
- Entities must **immediately correct** any failure to disclose or provide transparent ownership and control structures.
