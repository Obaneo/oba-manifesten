# B00.CH08.P09 — Relational tests: ΔM and P/E/C classification

This paragraph defines the **relational tests** for categorizing the flow of monetary resources (ΔM) within the OBA
framework and ensuring that monetary allocations are in line with the **P/E/C classification**. The goal is to verify that
the allocation of resources is **non-extractive** and that it fulfills the intended objectives of **producing public goods**,
**reducing burdens (L)**, and improving **R/B profiles**. The relational tests establish which monetary flows should be
classified under **P**, **E**, or **C**, ensuring alignment with commons principles and public welfare.

---

## B00.CH08.P09.S01 — ΔM→P: credit that yields productive output/maintenance (measurable)

Monetary resources allocated as **ΔM** that lead to **productive output** or the **maintenance of productive capacity**
(denoted as **P**) must be **measurable** and directly linked to public goods generation.

### Key principles for ΔM→P:
- **Productive output**: monetary allocations that fund productive capacity, such as infrastructure development, public
  services, and employment programs.
- **Maintenance**: credit used to maintain or enhance existing infrastructure, ensuring **continuity** and **quality** of
  public goods (e.g., maintaining schools, roads, hospitals).
- **Measurability**: the outcomes of these investments must be **auditable** and directly measurable (e.g., number of
  houses built, miles of road repaired, number of people employed).

These allocations must yield tangible, **real-world benefits** that improve public welfare and are aligned with **P/E/C** goals.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any **ΔM allocation** to **productive output** or **maintenance** must be **measurable** and directly linked to
  **commons-building**.
- If a monetary flow cannot be measured or traced to **productive outcomes**, it is **non-compliant** and must be
  **fail-closed**.

---

## B00.CH08.P09.S02 — ΔM→C: allocation that yields commons status and ΔG output (traceable)

Monetary flows allocated as **ΔM** for the creation or support of **commons** (denoted as **C**) must contribute directly
to **ΔG** (capacity/quality/continuity) outcomes, and the allocation must be **traceable** from source to destination.

### Key principles for ΔM→C:
- **Commons status**: resources must be allocated to **public goods** and **commons projects** (e.g., healthcare,
  housing, education) that are non-extractive and accessible to all.
- **ΔG output**: the allocation must **directly improve the capacity or quality** of commons services, with measurable
  **ΔG** metrics.
- **Traceability**: all funds must be **traceable** from their source to their final destination, ensuring that no funds are
  diverted for private gain or speculative purposes.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any **ΔM allocation** that supports **commons projects** must be clearly linked to **ΔG outcomes** and **auditable**.
- If the allocation cannot be **traced** or does not result in measurable **ΔG improvements**, it is **non-compliant**.

---

## B00.CH08.P09.S03 — ΔM→E (prohibited): allocation that consolidates toll/lock-in/claim layers

Monetary flows allocated as **ΔM** that lead to the **consolidation of tolls**, **lock-in effects**, or the creation of
**claim layers** (denoted as **E**) are prohibited. These allocations result in extractive practices that harm public
welfare and are inconsistent with OBA principles.

### Key principles for ΔM→E (prohibited):
- **Toll/lock-in**: allocations that create financial **tolls** (fees, interest, monopolistic pricing) or lock-in users
  into specific service providers or financial systems.
- **Claim layers**: monetary allocations that lead to the accumulation of **financial claims** that are not linked to
  productive or commons-related outcomes (e.g., speculative investments, asset-backed securities, or rent-seeking behaviors).
- **Non-compliance**: these allocations undermine the **non-extractive principle** of OBA and must be eliminated.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any **ΔM allocation** that results in **extraction**, **lock-in**, or **claim accumulation** is strictly **non-compliant**.
- If **ΔM flows** lead to **E-income** or similar behaviors, they must be **fail-closed** immediately and removed from
  the system.

---

## B00.CH08.P09.S04 — Mixed programmes: decomposition, redesign, or declaration as non-conform

**Mixed programmes** occur when a single allocation of monetary resources serves both **productive purposes** and
**extractive purposes**, leading to a **hybrid outcome** that is difficult to classify as strictly **P**, **E**, or **C**.

### Key principles for mixed programmes:
- **Decomposition**: mixed programmes must be **decomposed** into their constituent parts (i.e., separating productive
  outcomes from extractive components) to ensure compliance with OBA principles.
- **Redesign**: where decomposition is not possible, the programme must be **redesigned** to ensure that it serves only
  **non-extractive purposes**.
- **Non-compliance**: if the programme cannot be properly classified or restructured, it must be **declared non-conform**.

### Conformance requirements (binding)

- Any programme that involves a **mixed allocation** of **ΔM** must be carefully evaluated, decomposed, and restructured
  if necessary.
- If decomposition or redesign is not possible, the programme is **non-compliant** and must be excluded from the system.
