# B00.CH11.P04 — Compatibility tests (formal checks)

This paragraph defines the **minimal formal checks** required to assert OBA compatibility labels. These checks are designed to be
**mechanically applicable** and to fail closed when inputs, methods, or traceability are missing. Passing them is necessary (but not
always sufficient) for a compatibility claim; failing any check invalidates the claim scope until remedied.

---

## B00.CH11.P04.S01 — Definition-set closure: all used terms traceable back to Kernel Spec

A compatibility claim MUST be **definition-closed**: every term used in rules, computations, classifications, and disclosures MUST
be traceable to a canonical Kernel definition (directly or via Codex/Standards that remain Kernel-consistent).

### Test intent
Prevent “shadow terms”, silent redefinitions, and meaning drift by enforcing a closed vocabulary.

### Minimum test procedure
- Enumerate all terms used in the claim scope (including derived terms, metrics, labels, classifications, thresholds, and flags).
- For each term, provide a **canonical reference**: `{definition_set_id, version, source_path, anchor}`.
- Verify that no term is defined only in commentary, marketing, or ad-hoc implementation notes.

### Pass / fail criteria
- **PASS**: 100% of terms have canonical references; derived terms have explicit derivation rules referencing canonical terms.
- **FAIL**: any term lacks canonical reference, or is redefined outside canonical governance.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Definition-set closure MUST be demonstrated in a published mapping table or equivalent artifact.
- If closure fails, the claim MUST be treated as **non-conform** until corrected; partial compliance claims MUST reduce scope
  explicitly and republish the closure set.

---

## B00.CH11.P04.S02 — Auditability check: measurability, reproducibility, public methods (minimum requirement)

A compatibility claim MUST be auditable: required inputs, methods, and outputs must support independent verification.

### Minimum auditability requirements
- **Measurability**: each required variable is observable, collected, and defined with units, time resolution, and provenance.
- **Reproducibility**: an independent auditor can reproduce outputs from the published method + input dataset (or disclosed access route).
- **Public methods**: the method specification (rules, calculations, thresholds, decision logic) is publicly available at sufficient detail.

### Minimum test procedure
- Identify all required inputs and their sources.
- Provide the full method specification (including any transformations, aggregation, thresholds, and decision logic).
- Demonstrate reproducibility on a reference dataset or reproducible audit trace.

### Pass / fail criteria
- **PASS**: methods are disclosed and deterministic; required inputs are accessible to audit; outputs reproduce within stated tolerances.
- **FAIL**: black-box methods, undisclosed transformations, un-auditable inputs, or non-reproducible outputs.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Auditability is a minimum requirement for any “OBA-” label other than “OBA-inspired”.
- If auditability cannot be met for any component, that component MUST be excluded from claim scope or the claim MUST be downgraded
  to **non-conform** (fail-closed).

---

## B00.CH11.P04.S03 — Anti-avoidance check: shifts covered; reclassification present; leak tests

A compatibility claim MUST include anti-avoidance coverage sufficient to prevent circumvention by structural shifts.

### Minimum anti-avoidance requirements
- **Shift coverage**: legal/financial/technical/international shift patterns relevant to the scope are enumerated and addressed.
- **Reclassification rule**: substance-over-form reclassification exists and is triggered by defined indicators.
- **Leak tests**: explicit leakage tests exist for channels that can move extractive components out of scope or conceal them.

### Minimum test procedure
- Map the scope’s known avoidance surfaces (structure, ownership/control, pricing/fees, cross-border routing, technical gating).
- Demonstrate that each surface has (i) detection triggers, (ii) reclassification procedure, and (iii) remedies.
- Validate that the avoidance layer cannot be bypassed by redefining terms, narrowing scope, or discretionary exemptions.

### Pass / fail criteria
- **PASS**: avoidance surfaces are covered with enforceable reclassification + leak tests; no obvious bypass remains unaddressed.
- **FAIL**: missing shift category coverage, missing reclassification, missing leak tests, or reliance on discretionary waivers.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Anti-avoidance coverage MUST be published and MUST be part of the compatibility evidence set.
- If anti-avoidance fails, the claim MUST be treated as **non-conform** for the affected scope until coverage is restored.

## B00.CH11.P04.S04 — Outcome check: R/B profiles and E indicators move consistently with claims

Compatibility claims MUST be consistent with observable outcomes. If an implementation claims OBA compatibility while outcomes move in
the opposite direction (e.g., increasing extraction indicators or degrading R/B profiles), the claim requires explanation, audit,
and potential downgrade.

### Minimum outcome requirements
- **R/B consistency**: R/B profiles must move consistently with stated policy/mechanism claims, within defined lag windows.
- **E indicator consistency**: extraction indicators (E components, toll proxies, leakage markers) must not systematically rise while
  being claimed as reduced or constrained.
- **Attribution discipline**: where outcomes diverge, the implementation must provide a causal account and testable remediation plan.

### Minimum test procedure
- Declare baseline period, evaluation window, and lag assumptions.
- Publish the R/B distribution profile set and the E-indicator set used for monitoring.
- Compare observed changes against claim assertions, with predeclared acceptance bands.

### Pass / fail criteria
- **PASS**: outcomes are directionally and materially consistent with claims, or divergences are explained with auditable evidence.
- **FAIL**: persistent divergence without auditable explanation; selective reporting; post-hoc metric substitution.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Outcome monitoring MUST be published for any ongoing compatibility status beyond “OBA-inspired”.
- Unexplained adverse divergence MUST trigger a **status review** and MAY require downgrade to **non-conform** until remedied
  (fail-closed where auditability or integrity is compromised).
