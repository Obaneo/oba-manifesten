# B00.CH11.P09 — Compatibility and communication (anti-washing)

This paragraph defines the rules that bind **public communication** to **compatibility evidence**. Because “OBA” status labels are
formal claims, communication is not a separate, permissive layer: it must remain consistent with canonical sources, published tests,
and auditable disclosures. Anti-washing rules treat mislabeling as a compatibility breach.

---

## B00.CH11.P09.S01 — Ban on “compatibility by branding”: label requires evidence and publication

Compatibility cannot be asserted through slogans, narrative alignment, or visual identity. A label is valid only if the required
evidence set is published and the formal checks are passed.

### Prohibited patterns (non-exhaustive)
- “OBA-compatible” used as a marketing adjective without test results and evidence bundle.
- Implying compatibility through phrasing (“built on OBA”, “OBA certified”) without a published status claim record.
- Selective publication (publishing only favorable metrics while omitting required disclosure elements).

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Any use of an OBA status label MUST be accompanied by public references to: (i) definition-set ID + version, (ii) evidence bundle,
  and (iii) compatibility test outcomes for the claim scope.
- If evidence is not published, the label MUST NOT be used. Use “OBA-inspired” (with disclaimer) or remove OBA reference.

---

## B00.CH11.P09.S02 — Misrepresentation and duty to correct: rectification in case of incorrect OBA claim

When an incorrect or unverifiable OBA claim is made, a formal duty to correct applies. Correction is not optional reputational
management; it is a compatibility requirement.

### Rectification requirements (minimum)
- **Public correction notice**: identifies the incorrect claim, the period it was communicated, and the corrected status label.
- **Scope specification**: clarifies what is and is not covered (modules, sectors, jurisdictions).
- **Evidence update**: publishes missing evidence or withdraws the claim pending evidence.
- **Propagation**: corrections must be applied across all channels where the claim was made (web, reports, press, partner decks).

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Failure to correct within the time bounds specified by enforcement procedure MUST trigger downgrade to **non-conform** and may
  trigger sanctions under name-usage enforcement.
- Repeated misrepresentation is treated as a hard-stop trigger and may require forced fork status designation.

---

## B00.CH11.P09.S03 — Audit on the gatekeeper: external audits on compatibility declarations

Any entity that declares compatibility status (a “gatekeeper”: registry, certifier, standards body, platform, authority, or large
institution) is itself subject to audit to prevent capture, bias, and silent exception stacking.

### Gatekeeper audit scope (minimum)
- **Method integrity**: published methods match applied methods (no hidden scoring, no discretionary shortcuts).
- **Independence**: governance and funding arrangements do not create conflict-of-interest capture.
- **Consistency**: equal treatment of like cases; exception handling is published and justified.
- **Traceability**: every status decision references canonical sources and evidence bundles.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Gatekeepers MUST accept periodic external audits and MUST publish audit reports (or publish redacted versions with justification).
- Refusal of audit, black-box decisioning, or selective disclosure triggers **non-conform** status for the gatekeeper’s declarations
  and invalidates dependent claims (fail-closed).

---

## B00.CH11.P09.S04 — Transparency: public register of conformity, exceptions and forks

Compatibility is a public claim and therefore requires a public record. Transparency prevents laundering status through fragmented or
ephemeral communications.

### Minimum public register contents (required)
- **Entity / implementation identifier**
- **Status label** (OBA-conform / OBA-compatible / OBA-inspired / non-conform / fork)
- **Definition-set ID + version**
- **Scope** (modules, sectors, jurisdictions, time window)
- **Evidence bundle links** (tests, methods, datasets/access route)
- **Exceptions** (if any) with canonical basis and expiry (sunset)
- **Fork declarations** and bridging docs where applicable
- **Change history** (status changes, parameter updates, remedial actions)

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Any claim that cannot be located in the public register MUST be treated as **non-conform** until registered.
- Exceptions MUST be explicitly listed with expiration and review; unpublished exceptions are forbidden and constitute a fork trigger.
