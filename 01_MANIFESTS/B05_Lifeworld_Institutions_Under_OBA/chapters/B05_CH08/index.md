# B05.CH08 — Energy and mobility: infrastructure, tariffs, resilience, and decarbonisation under caps/ΔM rails

This chapter specifies energy and mobility as **essentials infrastructure** under OBA: systems whose reliability and affordability
directly determine L and R/B tails. Energy grids, heating, fuels, and mobility networks (public transport, roads/rail, logistics) are
treated as governance rails because they combine natural-monopoly surfaces, high capital intensity, and strong susceptibility to
vendor capture and rentier cashflow engineering. Under OBA, investment and tariff regimes must reduce L, increase resilience, and
expand real commons capacity (ΔG/maintenance), while strictly prohibiting ΔM leakage into asset/claim support and lock-in.

CH08 binds energy/mobility governance to canonical definition sets (P/E/C; L domain logic; caps W/I; ΔM allowed/forbidden channels;
commons standards; audit and procurement rails). It provides diagnostics, baseline access floors, tariff discipline, commons
infrastructure design, decarbonisation as an admissible investment channel (without asset support), mobility system redesign away from
car lock-in, shock/emergency regimes with sunset discipline, rules for private providers within rails, integration with caps, and
publication requirements. It is designed to fail-closed: opacity, vendor lock-in, tariff fee-stacks, or asset-support leakage are
non-conforming until corrected.
