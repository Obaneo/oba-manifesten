# B05.CH08.P11 — Measurement and publication requirements (lifeworld test: energy/mobility)

This paragraph specifies measurement and publication requirements for energy and mobility under OBA. Because these domains are
essentials infrastructure, drift often manifests first as burden tails, reliability degradation, lock-in, and opaque fee-stacks—not
as a single headline price. Without public statistics, toll ecosystems and underinvestment can grow invisibly, and emergency regimes
can become permanent claim support. OBA therefore requires a **public measurement layer** that makes affordability, reliability,
baseline access, arrears/disconnections, mobility accessibility, and infrastructure maintenance (ΔG) contestable as time series—while
enforcing privacy minimalism so measurement cannot be repurposed as person-tracking or commercial profiling. If core indicators cannot
be published safely and reproducibly, scope must be reduced to auditable coverage (fail-closed against invisible L).

---

<a id="b05ch08p11s01"></a>
## B05.CH08.P11.S01 — Energy: affordability (energy burden), reliability (SAIDI/SAIFI), baseline access coverage, arrears/disconnects

OBA requires baseline energy indicators that reveal affordability and service reliability, including tails and seasonal stress.

### Core energy statistics (binding)
- **Energy burden** distributions: household energy cost as share of income (median/p90/p95), by cohort and region, including seasonal
  peak stress periods.
- **Baseline access coverage**: share of households covered by baseline protection; baseline supply events; baseline exceptions.
- **Arrears and disconnections**: arrears rates, disconnection events, disconnection duration, reconnection cycle times, and dispute
  volumes/outcomes (aggregated).
- **Reliability metrics**: SAIDI/SAIFI (or jurisdictional equivalents), outage duration distributions, major incident frequency, and
  restoration time distributions.

### Definition stability (binding)
- Event definitions (disconnection, outage, arrears) must be standardised and stable across time to prevent reclassification games.

### Publication requirement (binding)
- Publish time series by region and provider segment; include p90/p95 tails and explicit seasonal breakouts where relevant.

<a id="b05ch08p11s02"></a>
## B05.CH08.P11.S02 — Mobility: accessibility, travel time, punctuality, coverage, affordability, modal split (aggregated)

Mobility measurement must reveal whether baseline accessibility exists without car dependence and whether mobility costs are reducing L.

### Core mobility statistics (binding)
- **Accessibility**: share of population within baseline access thresholds to essential nodes (parameters), by region type.
- **Travel time** distributions (median/p90/p95) for typical essential trips (work/education/health), including reliability variance.
- **Punctuality and cancellations**: on-time performance, cancellation rates, and recovery times for public transport/rail networks.
- **Coverage and frequency**: service coverage maps and frequency metrics by corridor/time band (aggregated).
- **Affordability**: fare burden indicators and penalty fee incidence (avoid fee-stack drift).
- **Modal split** (aggregated): share of trips by public transport, active mobility, and car; track whether decarbonisation reduces car
  dependence.

### Publication requirement (binding)
- Publish as time series with stable region taxonomy; provide tail distributions and baseline-gap indicators (where standards are not
  met).

<a id="b05ch08p11s03"></a>
## B05.CH08.P11.S03 — ΔG metrics: capex/opex, maintenance backlog, incident logs, cost per unit

Energy and mobility infrastructure must be measurable as commons capacity: output, maintenance discipline, and incident handling.

### ΔG and maintenance metrics (binding)
- **Capex/opex separation**: publish capex and opex accounts by infrastructure segment (grid, rail, charging, control systems) with
  standardised categories.
- **Maintenance backlog**: size, severity distribution, age of backlog, SLA compliance for repairs, and repeat incident rates.
- **Incident logs**: aggregated incident counts and categories (cyber, physical, weather, equipment failure), outage/delay impacts, and
  restoration times.
- **Cost per unit**: cost per delivered capacity unit (e.g., reinforcement km/MW, storage capacity, charging points, rail capacity
  upgrades) and cost per achieved reliability improvement unit, with standardised accounting.

### Conformance requirement (binding)
- Publish these metrics as time series with p90/p95 tails for cycle times and incident impacts; include reconciliation notes where
  methods change.

<a id="b05ch08p11s04"></a>
## B05.CH08.P11.S04 — Privacy minimalism: no person-tracking; aggregates, suppression, auditpacks for decisions

The measurement layer must not become surveillance infrastructure.

### Privacy minima (binding)
- **No person-tracking**: utilities and mobility measurement outputs may not include individual-level movement or behavioural traces.
  Smart-meter and mobility data must be aggregated and purpose-limited.
- **Aggregates and suppression**: apply small-cell suppression, bucketing, and top-coding to prevent re-identification.
- **No commercial profiling**: published datasets may not be repackaged for marketing, steering, tenant/consumer scoring, or
  collections optimisation.

### Auditpacks for decisions (binding)
- While public outputs are aggregated, decision-critical actions (tariff changes, disconnection events, emergency trigger activation,
  procurement awards) must have **audit packs** available to authorised auditors: reproducible calculations, rationale, and rule
  references.
- Audit packs must be privacy-minimal: include only data necessary to reproduce decisions.

### Hard stop (binding)
- Evidence of re-identification, commercial re-profiling, or tracking-based exclusion triggers immediate suspension of affected data
  feeds and mandatory redesign of the measurement pipeline (fail-closed against surveillance rent).
