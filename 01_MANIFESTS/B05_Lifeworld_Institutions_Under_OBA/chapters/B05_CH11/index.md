# B05.CH11 — Food and basic essentials: supply chains, price discipline, commons logistics, and shock resilience

This chapter specifies food and basic essentials as an OBA **lifeworld essentials domain**: affordability and reliability of basic
provision directly determine L and R/B tails, and shocks propagate rapidly through supply chains, input markets, and logistics.
Because food chains are prone to concentration, intermediary fee-stacks, contract clamp, and cognitive toll at the consumer interface,
OBA treats food governance as a rail discipline problem: decompose prices and margins, neutralise toll layers (E), build commons
logistics capacity (ΔG), and maintain shock resilience via reserves and emergency regimes with strict sunset discipline. ΔM channels
must not be used as backdoor asset/claim support for commodity positions, incumbents, or rentier cashflows.

CH11 binds food governance to canonical rails (P/E/C classification with toll/fee-stack detection; ΔM allowed/forbidden channels;
commons standards for logistics/reserves; R/B tail discipline; audit packs and chain transparency). It provides diagnostics, baseline
basket and price rails, chain attribution and decomposition protocols, commons logistics design, input market remedies, labour rails,
consumer-interface anti-cognitive-toll rules, emergency reserve regimes, import/export and geopolitics hooks, publication requirements,
and hard stops. It is designed to fail-closed: price discipline without chain transparency and auditable decomposition is treated as
washing and is non-conforming until corrected.
