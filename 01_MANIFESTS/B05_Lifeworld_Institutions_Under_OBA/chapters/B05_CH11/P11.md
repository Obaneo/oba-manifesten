# B05.CH11.P11 — Measurement and publication requirements (food as lifeworld test)

This paragraph specifies measurement and publication requirements for food and basic essentials under OBA. Food-system drift is often
hidden unless measured: prices can rise through shrinkflation and opaque surcharges; margins can expand inside concentrated chains;
fee-stacks can grow in intermediaries; and shocks can be managed by narrative rather than by reserves and logistics readiness. OBA
therefore requires a **public measurement layer** that makes price dynamics, chain transparency, shock resilience, and cognitive toll
contestable as time series. Publication is treated as conformance infrastructure: if core indicators cannot be published safely and
reproducibly, governance must restrict scope to auditable coverage and trigger redesign (fail-closed against invisible essentials-L and
E).

---

<a id="b05ch11p11s01"></a>
## B05.CH11.P11.S01 — Price metrics: essentials-basket index, unit prices, mark-up bands, shrinkflation incidence

Price measurement must reveal baseline affordability, not only headline inflation.

### Price statistics (binding)
- **Essentials-basket index**: index for the baseline basket (P03) with stable composition and published method_id; report median and
  tail burden impacts where possible.
- **Unit prices**: mandatory unit price series (per kg/litre/unit) by product group and region; include distributions (median/p90/p95)
  to capture dispersion and deserts.
- **Mark-up bands**: observed mark-ups relative to benchmark cost bands (P04), with frequency and magnitude of deviations beyond bands.
- **Shrinkflation incidence**: frequency of package-size reductions or quality downgrades without proportional price change, by product
  group and retailer segment.

### Publication requirement (binding)
- Publish as time series with stable definitions; include exception registers for justified seasonal deviations.

<a id="b05ch11p11s02"></a>
## B05.CH11.P11.S02 — Chain transparency: margin decomposition shares, fee-stack depth, concentration indicators

Chain transparency measurement must reveal where value is captured and where toll layers (E) are growing.

### Transparency statistics (binding)
- **Margin decomposition shares**: aggregate shares of consumer price attributable to inputs, processing, logistics, retail, and
  identified fee-stacks (P04 decomposition protocol).
- **Fee-stack depth**: number and size of intermediary fee categories (brokerage, trading spreads, slotting fees, “services”) per
  product group and region.
- **Concentration indicators**: HHI or equivalent for processing, retail, and key input suppliers, plus dependency indicators for
  single-buyer regions and shelf-access control.

### Conformance implication (binding)
- Rising fee-stack depth or margin shares without corresponding ΔG or quality improvements triggers audit escalation and structural
  remedies.

<a id="b05ch11p11s03"></a>
## B05.CH11.P11.S03 — Shock metrics: reserve levels, lead times, shortage incidents, distribution uptime

Shock measurement must reveal whether resilience infrastructure is real and deployable.

### Shock resilience statistics (binding)
- **Reserve levels**: reserve coverage days/weeks for staples and linked essentials, rotation performance, and readiness test results
  (aggregate).
- **Lead times**: procurement and delivery lead-time distributions (median/p90/p95) for key product groups and inputs.
- **Shortage incidents**: stockout incidence, duration, and geographic clustering; institutional shortage events (schools/care/hospitals).
- **Distribution uptime**: logistics hub uptime, cold-chain failure incidents, spoilage rates, and recovery times (link to P05).

### Publication requirement (binding)
- Publish as time series; major shock events require post-mortem audit summaries (link P09).

<a id="b05ch11p11s04"></a>
## B05.CH11.P11.S04 — Anti-cognitive toll: dark-pattern reports, complaints, enforcement outcomes (aggregated)

Consumer-interface measurement must reveal whether manipulation is being eliminated or merely rebranded.

### Cognitive toll indicators (binding)
- **Dark-pattern reports**: frequency of confirmed misleading discounts, shrinkflation disclosure violations, bundling traps, and
  loyalty lock-in abuses (P08).
- **Complaints**: complaint volumes and categories (mispricing, unit-price labeling, loyalty coercion), with resolution times.
- **Enforcement outcomes**: fines, restitution actions, contract rewrites, and repeat-offender indicators by retailer segment.

### Publication requirement (binding)
- Publish aggregated enforcement and complaint statistics as time series; publish public warnings for systemic violations (privacy-safe
  aggregation; no individual customer data).

### Fail-closed implication (binding)
- Refusal to provide auditable price histories or repeated manipulation triggers restrictions on promotional practices and escalation
  to licensing/procurement consequences (fail-closed against cognitive toll regrowth).
