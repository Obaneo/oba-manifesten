# B05.CH11.P03 — Essentials basket and price discipline (baseline food access)

This paragraph specifies baseline food access under OBA through an essentials basket and price discipline rails. Food affordability is
a direct L channel: when staple prices and access costs rise, households cannot substitute away without health degradation, and R/B
tails worsen via malnutrition, stress, and instability. OBA therefore requires (i) a defined baseline basket with minimum nutritional
coverage and regional variants, (ii) price rails that keep mark-ups bounded and surcharges transparent (no junk fees), (iii)
non-discrimination in access, including remedies for “food deserts” and mobility constraints, and (iv) fast dispute flow to correct
mispricing, fraud, and shrinkflation using auditable evidence packs. Basket parameters vary by jurisdiction, but the rails—measurability,
contestability, and fail-closed enforcement—are binding.

---

<a id="b05ch11p03s01"></a>
## B05.CH11.P03.S01 — Baseline basket: minimum caloric/nutrition profile (parameters) + regional variants

Baseline access requires an explicit basket definition: what constitutes “basic provision” in measurable terms.

### Basket definition (parameters; binding requirement to set and publish)
- Define a baseline basket with a minimum **caloric** and **nutrition** profile sufficient for health and participation (macro and key
  micro nutrients), with age/household-size adjustments as parameters.
- Include regional/cultural variants so the basket is feasible and not culturally punitive; variants must meet the same nutrition
  profile.
- Define product-group categories (staples, proteins, fruits/vegetables, fats, infant/medical diets where relevant) to prevent gaming
  by substituting into low-quality equivalents.

### Conformance requirement (binding)
- Publish basket composition, weighting, and revision policy (method_id, definitions, and update cadence).

<a id="b05ch11p03s02"></a>
## B05.CH11.P03.S02 — Price rails: indexation, bounded mark-ups, transparent surcharges (no junk fees)

Price discipline must prevent essentials from becoming toll pricing under scarcity and concentration.

### Price rails (binding)
- **Indexation discipline**: where indexation is used (contracts, price caps), it must reference published, standard indices and be
  time-bounded; discretionary “market adjustment” clauses are prohibited.
- **Bounded mark-ups**: set bounded mark-up bands (parameters) for baseline basket items, with clear separation of cost components
  (inputs, processing, logistics, retail service).
- **Transparent surcharges**: any surcharge must be itemised, cost-based, and auditable (e.g., delivery costs, cold-chain handling).
  Bundled “service” charges without cost basis are classified as E.

### Prohibited patterns (binding)
- Junk fees, loyalty-gated pricing that functions as discrimination, and complex promotion structures that obscure unit prices.
- “Shrinkflation” (reducing quantity while maintaining price) without clear labeling is treated as mispricing fraud.

### Conformance requirement (binding)
- Publish aggregate price tables for baseline items: unit prices, mark-up bands, surcharge shares, and deviations beyond bands with
  justification codes.

<a id="b05ch11p03s03"></a>
## B05.CH11.P03.S03 — Non-discrimination: access in “food deserts”, delivery/collective solutions

Baseline affordability is meaningless if physical access is absent. OBA treats access gaps as an essentials governance failure.

### Non-discrimination minima (binding)
- Prohibit exclusion by region through systematic under-provisioning of low-income or remote areas without remedial access pathways.
- Prohibit discriminatory pricing through profiling or loyalty schemes that punish those without stable identity or data access (link to
  digital rails CH10 where relevant).

### Access remedies (binding)
- **Food deserts**: require coverage norms (parameters) for baseline basket availability within reasonable travel time.
- **Delivery/collective solutions**: enable commons logistics, mobile markets, community distribution points, and group purchasing
  mechanisms where retail coverage is weak.
- **Institutional procurement linkage**: schools/care/hospitals can be used as anchor demand for local distribution hubs where it
  improves baseline access.

### Publication requirement (binding)
- Publish aggregated access indicators: baseline basket availability by region, travel-time proxies, delivery coverage, and shortage
  incidents (privacy-safe aggregation).

## B05.CH11.P03.S04 — Dispute flow: rapid handling of mispricing, fraud, shrinkflation (auditable)

Dispute and enforcement must be fast enough to prevent mispricing and deception from becoming normalised.

### Fast dispute procedures (binding)
- Time-bounded procedures for complaints about mispricing, shelf-label discrepancies, hidden surcharges, and shrinkflation.
- During disputes, retailers must provide evidence packs: unit pricing calculations, package size history, promotion terms, and supplier
  invoices where relevant.

### Auditable detection (binding)
- Require standardised unit pricing on shelves and online, enabling reproducible comparisons.
- Maintain an exceptions register for justified deviations (seasonality, genuine supply shocks) with sunset discipline.

### Remedies (binding)
- Immediate correction, refunds/credits where applicable, fines for repeated deception, and mandatory label/format rewrites.
- Repeat offenders trigger escalation: audits of pricing systems and, where necessary, licence/procurement consequences.

### Publication requirement (binding)
- Publish aggregated dispute/enforcement statistics: complaint volumes, categories (mispricing, shrinkflation, fraud), resolution times,
  outcomes, and repeat-offender indicators (privacy-safe aggregation).
