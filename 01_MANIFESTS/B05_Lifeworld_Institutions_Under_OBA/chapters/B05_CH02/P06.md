# B05.CH02.P06 — Wage formation and income structure under I-MAX

This paragraph specifies how income is formed under an I-MAX regime (income cap) without recreating extraction through compensation
engineering. Under OBA, wage formation is not only a labour-market outcome; it is a primary **anti-evasion surface** where actors
attempt to bypass caps via perks, deferrals, affiliated entities, or equity structures that function as hidden claim stacks.

The objective is a compensation structure that is (1) **legible** (auditable), (2) **bounded** (cap-conforming), and (3)
**functionally aligned** with production and maintenance rather than with rent extraction. The paragraph distinguishes between:
- **wage income** (cash pay tied to work),
- **commons baseline** (in-kind access to essentials delivered by non-extractive commons/rails),
- and **capital/equity components** (permitted only under canonical cap standards and valuation discipline).

---

## B05.CH02.P06.S01 — Base structure: wages + complementary in-kind commons (essentials baseline)

Under I-MAX, the base household income structure is intentionally dual:
1) **Wages** remain the primary cash mechanism for discretionary choice and personal agency.
2) **Commons/in-kind baseline** reduces the need for high cash income by providing non-extractive access to essentials (housing rails,
energy access, care access, mobility, digital access) within explicit boundary rules.

This is not “welfare” framing; it is an order design: lowering L by substituting toll positions with commons access reduces clamp and
reduces the political pressure to defeat I-MAX via compensation arms races.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Wage policy MUST be specified together with the essentials baseline: if essentials remain toll-gated, wage demands become a proxy
  for toll compensation and caps pressure increases.
- Any claim that I-MAX “works” MUST be testable in lifeworld terms (B/T/N/L/R): wages + commons baseline must reduce clamp exposure,
  not just compress headline inequality.

---

## B05.CH02.P06.S02 — Variable pay: allowed within I-MAX; prohibited to bypass via perks/deferrals

Variable pay is permitted as a bounded incentive mechanism, but it is a dominant evasion route when designed as a cap-bypass channel.

### Allowed (within rails)
- Bonuses and variable pay MAY exist if:
  - they are included in the I-MAX accounting base (no exclusion by label),
  - they are formula-bound and published (anti-discretion),
  - they are subject to clawback rules tied to restatements/misreporting.

### Prohibited (cap-bypass patterns)
The following are treated as non-conforming because they function as hidden compensation or claim stacks:
- **Perk substitution**: housing, travel, “wellness,” private security, exclusive access, education packages, or luxury services used
  as quasi-income outside cap accounting.
- **Deferral games**: rolling compensation into deferred vehicles, offshore entities, or bespoke instruments to shift timing and evade
  cap applicability.
- **Affiliated pass-throughs**: paying via suppliers/consulting shells, IP licensing, or “advisory” contracts that are economically
  wage compensation.

### Binding rule
- If compensation is economically income, it MUST be treated as income for I-MAX purposes regardless of contractual form (function
  over label), and MUST be auditable through registries and disclosure.

## B05.CH02.P06.S03 — Equity comp: fair-value rules, vesting, timing (link to Codex caps standard)

Equity compensation is the highest-risk interface between work income and claim income. OBA allows equity comp only under strict
valuation and timing rules so it cannot be used as a shadow wage channel or as a mechanism to recreate uncapped upside.

### Equity comp rails (binding)
- **Fair-value discipline**: equity grants MUST be valued under canonical fair-value methods specified in the Codex caps standard
  (reference by definition-set / method_id; no local improvisation).
- **Vesting and timing**: cap applicability MUST follow a published rule for when value is realised/recognised; “indefinite deferral”
  to avoid I-MAX is non-conforming.
- **Instrument controls**: complex derivatives, side letters, guaranteed buybacks, or synthetic leverage that convert capped labour
  income into uncapped claim streams MUST be prohibited or requalified into the cap base.
- **Related-party controls**: grants via affiliates, SPVs, foundations, or vendor vehicles MUST be consolidated for cap accounting
  where they are functionally compensation.

### Hard stop
- If equity comp cannot be valued reproducibly (no method_id, no definition-set reference, no restatement policy), it MUST be treated
  as non-recognisable for compatibility claims (fail-closed against “trust me” valuation).

## B05.CH02.P06.S04 — Pay transparency: public bands, internal ratios, auditability as anti-evasion

Pay transparency is a constitutive anti-evasion mechanism: without publication surfaces, cap compliance becomes a private accounting
exercise and drifts into negotiated exceptions.

### Transparency surface (binding)
- **Public pay bands**: publish role ladders and pay bands (including variable pay ranges and eligibility rules).
- **Internal ratios**: publish bounded dispersion ratios (e.g., top-to-median within entity/sector) as auditable outputs, not PR.
- **Total comp decomposition**: disclose the split between cash, variable, equity, and in-kind components.
- **Auditability**: maintain decision logs and registries sufficient for independent audit (sampling + triggers), with restatements
  when misclassification or evasion is detected.

### Audit triggers
- Rapid growth in non-wage compensation categories (perks, consulting, licensing, deferrals) MUST trigger a cap-evasion audit.
- Persistent divergence between published ratios and observed lifeworld clamp signals MUST trigger review of wage formation rails.
