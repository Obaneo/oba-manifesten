# B05.CH02.P01 — Purpose, scope, and normative status of “work under OBA”

This paragraph defines the purpose of Book 5, Chapter 2: to specify **“work under OBA”** as a **lifeworld implementation layer**
where macro invariants must become **practice**, **institutions**, and **measurable effects**. Work is treated as an order-critical
interface because it is simultaneously (1) a dominant allocation mechanism (who gets access to income, roles, training, and status),
(2) a primary surface for **power relations** and **clamp mechanics** (contract lock-in, platform gating, coercive dependency), and
(3) a main channel through which P/E/C structures, caps, commons, and ΔM channel rules become felt in daily life.

The chapter is not a generic labour sociology or political programme. It is a **normative translation**: it takes canonical concepts
from the Kernel Spec and Book 1 (P/E/C, B/T/N/L/R, caps, commons ΔG, ΔM channel discipline) and specifies what must be true in labour
markets, wage formation, labour relations, and transition instruments for an implementation to claim compatibility. The test standard
is concrete: policies must alter experienced **BURDENS (L)**, **RESIDUAL SPACE (R)**, the **R/B profile** (volatility and tail-risk),
and the **distribution and contestability of power** at work.

Delimitation is strict: this chapter does not redefine macro constructs; it operationalises them into **work practice** and defines
the required evidence surfaces (publication tables, dispute rails, enforcement and audit triggers). If the work layer cannot be
executed as a test (measurable outcomes + contestable procedures), the implementation is incomplete and must fail-closed.

---

## B05.CH02.P01.S01 — Work as an allocation mechanism and a source of agency (not merely an income source)

Under OBA, work is treated as a dual object:

1) **Allocation mechanism**: work allocates not only money, but access to essentials (via employer-tied channels), training and
upskilling, social insurance, mobility, and future bargaining position. Where labour markets are structured by extraction, this
allocation function becomes a clamp: people accept worsening terms because the alternative is loss of access, not merely lower pay.

2) **Agency surface**: work is a primary domain where individuals exercise competence, negotiation, refusal, exit, voice, and
collective action. Agency is not captured by wage alone; it is shaped by contract portability, scheduling control, transparency of
evaluation, and the ability to contest discipline and misclassification.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Any “work policy” claim MUST specify its intended effect on **agency** (exit/voice/contestability) and not only on wages.
- Any arrangement that increases dependency while masking it with nominal wage gains MUST be treated as suspect: compatibility is
  assessed on **L, R/B, and power relations**, not on income deltas alone.

## B05.CH02.P01.S02 — Scope: labour markets, sector allocation, wage formation, labour relations, transition instruments

CH02 covers the work domain as a system of interfaces, not as a single market:

### In scope
- **Labour markets**: entry, mobility, unemployment dynamics, matching, credentialing, and gating (including platform labour).
- **Sector allocation**: how labour is pulled into or trapped within sectors; how essentials and commons staffing is stabilised.
- **Wage formation and compensation stacks**: base pay, variable pay, tips, bonuses, piece rates, benefits, and opaque fee/chargebacks.
- **Labour relations**: bargaining structures, unions/collectives, works councils, dispute mechanisms, strike/exit protections.
- **Transition instruments**: reskilling, job guarantees/public options, portability reforms, procurement and classification shifts.

### Out of scope (but referenced)
- Canonical definitions and measurement primitives (Kernel Spec / Book 1).
- Full constitutional design of institutions (Book 3), except where labour-specific governance rails are required.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Each policy proposal MUST declare which interface(s) it affects (market entry, wage formation, relations, transition) and which
  test outputs (L/R/B/power) will demonstrate effectiveness.

## B05.CH02.P01.S03 — Binding: translation of the Kernel Spec (P/E/C, R/B, caps, commons, ΔM) into work practice

Work under OBA is not an independent doctrine; it is the **practice translation** of canonical order rules:

- **P/E/C in work**: classify labour relations and compensation stacks by whether they are productive, extractive, or clamp-based.
  This includes detecting fee-stacks, misclassification, exclusivity, take-rate mechanics, and “employer as gate” constructs.
- **B/T/N/L/R mapping**: wages are only one component; levies timing/predictability, essential burdens, and residual space determine
  effective autonomy and the lived feasibility of refusal/exit.
- **Caps (W/I)**: status and income caps shape labour incentives, managerial rent, and the arms race for positional advantage; work
  institutions must prevent cap circumvention from becoming the hidden objective of careers and compensation engineering.
- **Commons (ΔG)**: staffing and capability in essential commons (care, energy, housing maintenance, digital public options) must be
  stabilised; execution capacity is itself a commons and is measured as ΔG (maintenance-first).
- **ΔM channel discipline**: labour policy cannot be bypassed by hidden channels (subsidy → private toll gate; procurement → vendor
  lock-in; tax credits → claim support that recreates extraction).

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Any work reform MUST reference the canonical constructs it operationalises (definition-set ID + version) and MUST not restate them
  as local reinterpretations.
- Any implementation that uses work policy to create prohibited ΔM effects (claim/asset support that entrenches toll positions) MUST
  be treated as drifting or non-conforming.

## B05.CH02.P01.S04 — Fail-closed: policy without measurable effects on L/R/B and power relations is incomplete

“Work under OBA” is testable. Policies must not be assessed on intent, rhetoric, or macro averages alone. They must show:

- **L**: essential burdens for working households decline or become contestable (exit without clamp).
- **R/B profile**: residual space increases and volatility/tail risk decreases (predictability, reduced exposure to shocks).
- **Power relations**: bargaining position improves via real contestability rails (portability, transparency, dispute enforcement,
  anti-retaliation, collective mechanisms).

If those effects cannot be measured, published, and disputed (join-safe tables, reproducible methods, dispute triggers), the policy is
incomplete.

### Hard rule (binding)
- Any “work policy” artefact MUST include a measurement plan and publication surface (cohorts, wage/benefit stacks, lock-in proxies,
  dispute volumes/outcomes, misclassification rates, scheduling volatility, enforcement actions).
- If the test cannot be executed (privacy violation, capacity failure, opaque computation, non-public outputs), the implementation
  MUST fail-closed: no compatibility claim can be made until corrected.
