# B05.CH07.P07 — Ownership market and finance: avoiding credit-driven price inflation

This paragraph specifies how ownership markets and housing finance must operate under OBA to prevent housing from reverting into an
asset-inflation channel. In an essentials domain, expanded purchasing power under constrained supply tends to capitalise into higher
prices, higher leverage, and higher L. OBA therefore treats many “homeownership support” policies as potential ΔM leakage into assets.
Ownership is admissible as a tenure form, but finance must be structured as a bounded access instrument: transparent all-in pricing,
bounded leverage, portability to reduce lock-in, and strict prohibition of fee-stacks and demand-stimulus schemes that inflate prices.
Where credit or subsidy channels primarily support prices rather than capacity (ΔG) and L reduction, they are non-conforming and must
be terminated or redesigned.

---

<a id="b05ch07p07s01"></a>
## B05.CH07.P07.S01 — Prohibited demand stimulus: subsidies/credit that inflate prices (ΔM leakage)

Policies that increase buying power without expanding real housing capacity are treated as prohibited channels under OBA.

### Prohibited demand stimulus (binding)
- **Credit easing**: underwriting relaxation, high-LTV expansion without countervailing supply measures, or policy signals intended to
  “support the market”.
- **Buyer subsidies that capitalise into price**: grants, tax credits, guarantees, and interest subsidies applied broadly in scarcity
  conditions (especially first-buyer targeting without supply expansion).
- **Price-floor programmes**: interventions whose stated or functional objective is to stabilise or raise house prices.
- **Investor stimulation**: tax or credit advantages for buy-to-let/portfolio expansion that increase speculative demand.

### Leakage test (binding)
- If a programme’s expected incidence is primarily higher prices, higher leverage, or higher land values (rather than additional
  units, faster delivery, or reduced operating costs), it is presumed ΔM leakage and is non-conforming.

### Hard stop (binding)
- Under detected inflationary incidence (rising price-to-income, rising mortgage burden tails, rising leverage share), the channel
  must be suspended and redirected to ΔG (build/renovation) and housing commons.

<a id="b05ch07p07s02"></a>
## B05.CH07.P07.S02 — Permissible financing: bounded LTV, transparent all-in APR, portability, no junk fees

Housing finance is admissible only as a bounded access instrument with auditable costs and limited leverage.

### Permissible financing rails (binding)
- **Bounded leverage (LTV/LTI)**: maximum loan-to-value and loan-to-income limits (jurisdictional parameters) with conservative stress
  testing; exceptions are time-bounded and publicly logged in aggregate.
- **Transparent all-in pricing**: mandatory all-in APR and total-cost-of-credit disclosure including all compulsory fees, broker
  compensation, and add-ons.
- **No junk fees**: fee-stacks, penalty cascades, forced add-ons, and opaque servicing charges are prohibited; where they appear they
  are classified as E and remediated.
- **Portability**: refinancing and moving must not be blocked through punitive break fees, lock-in clauses, or administrative barriers.
  Standard portability options must be offered (parameterised) to reduce clamp and enable contestability.

### Conformance requirement (binding)
- Lenders must provide reproducible pricing decomposition (funding/risk/ops vs E) for sampled contracts and publish aggregate ranges
  by product class.

<a id="b05ch07p07s03"></a>
## B05.CH07.P07.S03 — Shared equity and alternatives: non-speculative models with capped upside (parameters)

OBA permits ownership-adjacent tenure and finance models that provide stability without opening a full speculative upside channel.

### Admissible model classes (parameters; binding rails)
- **Shared equity**: buyer and public/commons partner share equity with explicit caps on private upside and explicit rules for resale,
  valuation, and buyout.
- **Limited-equity co-ops**: member occupancy rights with resale price formulae that prevent windfall extraction while preserving
  maintenance incentives.
- **Rent-to-own with safeguards**: permitted only where pricing is transparent, fee-stacks are prohibited, and conversion terms are
  bounded and auditable.

### Rail requirements (binding)
- **Capped upside**: private appreciation capture must be bounded to prevent speculative demand amplification.
- **Non-speculation**: units must not be tradable as financial assets (no flip incentives, no derivative exposure, no carve-out vehicles
  for investors).
- **Portability/exit**: clear exit rules with bounded friction; dispute flows and standard contracts.

### Publication requirement (binding)
- Publish aggregate performance: uptake, resale outcomes, arrears/default rates, and L impact compared to standard ownership finance.

<a id="b05ch07p07s04"></a>
## B05.CH07.P07.S04 — Default/foreclosure: dignified procedures, preserve baseline access, restructuring over liquidation

Default processes in housing are high-stakes: they can trigger homelessness, family collapse, and long-term clamp. OBA therefore
requires dignified procedures that preserve baseline access and prefer restructuring over liquidation where viable.

### Procedure minima (binding)
- **Baseline protection**: enforcement must not push households below baseline housing security; emergency pathways and temporary
  stabilisation must be available (linked to P03 emergency access).
- **Fast restructuring first**: before foreclosure, lenders must offer compliant restructuring options with bounded fees, rate
  reductions, and (where needed) principal correction when E components or mispricing contributed to unsustainability.
- **No profit-from-distress**: foreclosure and collections fee-stacks are prohibited; enforcement charges are capped and auditable.

### Dignified foreclosure (binding)
- Where foreclosure is unavoidable, the process must be time-bounded, transparent, and contestable, with clear valuation and sale
  procedures to prevent opportunistic underpricing.
- Post-event outcomes must preserve re-entry to housing access pathways without indefinite blacklisting or debt peonage.

### Publication requirement (binding)
- Publish foreclosure pipeline statistics (arrears-to-foreclosure transitions, cycle time, restructuring offers vs completions,
  displacement outcomes) as privacy-safe aggregates to detect clamp drift.
