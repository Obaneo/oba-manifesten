# B05.CH12.P12 — Measurement and publication requirements (local institutions as lifeworld test)

This paragraph specifies measurement and publication requirements for local institutions under OBA. Local governance drift is often
invisible unless measured: lead times and drop-off become normalised, exceptions become patronage, procurement capture becomes
“standard practice”, and audit becomes theatre. OBA therefore requires a **public measurement layer** that makes execution performance
(administrative L), commons delivery (access and ΔG/maintenance), governance integrity (exceptions, procurement anomalies, audits), and
local R/B tail exposure contestable as time series. Publication is treated as conformance infrastructure: if required indicators cannot
be published safely and reproducibly, local autonomy must be restricted to auditable modules until integrity is restored (fail-closed
against invisible capture).

---

<a id="b05ch12p12s01"></a>
## B05.CH12.P12.S01 — Execution: lead times, backlog, error/appeal rates, drop-off (administrative L)

Execution measurement must show whether services are reachable in practice, not only “available on paper”.

### Execution statistics (binding)
- **Lead times**: end-to-end processing time distributions (median/p90/p95) per pathway (permits, benefits, housing allocation, care
  access, enforcement decisions).
- **Backlogs**: backlog size and age distributions per pathway; breach thresholds and escalation events.
- **Error/appeal rates**: reversal rates, correction/restatement rates, and appeal volumes; reason-code distributions for common errors.
- **Drop-off**: abandonment/non-completion rates by pathway stage (proxy for friction and hidden exclusion).

### Publication requirement (binding)
- Publish as time series with stable pathway taxonomy and definitions; prohibit reclassification games that hide waiting.

---

<a id="b05ch12p12s02"></a>
## B05.CH12.P12.S02 — Commons: access/ΔG/incident/maintenance metrics per domain (aggregated)

Commons measurement must show whether local capacity assets are maintained and delivering baseline access.

### Commons statistics (binding)
- **Access**: coverage and waitlist distributions (median/p90/p95) for key commons domains (housing units, care services, mobility
  options, community spaces, digital service desks).
- **ΔG/capacity**: capacity levels and utilisation, throughput where relevant (e.g., shelter throughput), and service availability.
- **Maintenance**: backlog size/age/severity, SLA compliance, downtime incidents, and recovery times.
- **Incidents**: safety incidents, service failures, and major complaints categories with resolution times.

### Publication requirement (binding)
- Publish aggregated by domain and region category; include conformance breach events and remediation progress.

---

<a id="b05ch12p12s03"></a>
## B05.CH12.P12.S03 — Governance: exceptions register, procurement anomalies, audit findings, remediation rates

Governance measurement must reveal whether discretion is controlled and whether procurement and audits are functioning.

### Governance statistics (binding)
- **Exceptions register metrics**: volume of exceptions by domain, reason-code distributions, average duration until sunset, and repeat
  exceptions (signals of rule failure or patronage).
- **Procurement anomalies**: sole-source award rates, change-order volumes and inflation indicators, vendor concentration, and exit-cost
  risk proxies.
- **Audit findings**: counts and severity distribution of audit findings by domain (execution, procurement, commons), including repeat
  findings.
- **Remediation rates**: remediation plan completion rates, deadline adherence, and escalation actions for missed deadlines.

### Publication requirement (binding)
- Publish conformance status dashboards with method_id and remediation actions; dashboards without remedies are non-conforming.

<a id="b05ch12p12s04"></a>
## B05.CH12.P12.S04 — R/B: local cohort profiles and essentials indices (privacy-safe)

Local execution must be evaluated against lifeworld tail exposure and essentials burdens.

### R/B and essentials indices (binding)
- **Local cohort profiles** (privacy-safe): aggregate indicators for vulnerability and tail exposure (income distributions, disability
  prevalence proxies, housing insecurity proxies, age structure) sufficient to interpret needs.
- **Essentials indices**: local housing burden, energy burden, mobility accessibility, and food basket affordability indices (linked to
  CH07/CH08/CH11), as aggregates and time series.
- **Tail events**: homelessness incidents, disconnection events, crisis shelter usage, and other local tail indicators (aggregated).

### Privacy minimalism (binding)
- Publish aggregates with suppression and bucketing; no individual-level addresses or identifiable case traces in public outputs.
- Regulated micro-access is permitted for auditors under strict access logging and purpose limitation (see P10).

### Conformance implication (binding)
- If essentials indices worsen or tail events rise without corrective action, local execution is drifting toward incompatibility and must
  trigger escalation and redesign (fail-closed against silent tail growth).
