# B05.CH09 — Healthcare and education: access, quality, staffing, maintenance logic, and anti-toll design

This chapter specifies healthcare and education as **essentials institutions** under OBA: domains where access failures, scarcity, and
bureaucratic tolls translate directly into L escalation, R/B tail events, and agency loss. Under OBA, these sectors must operate as
commons-like capacity systems (ΔG/maintenance) with baseline access guarantees, wait-time rails, and quality logic that is
multi-dimensional and anti-Goodhart. Because both domains are highly exposed to fee-stacks (billing/claims industries, management
layers, compliance consulting) and digital capture (proprietary EHR/LMS systems, data lock-in, opaque algorithms), OBA treats
interoperability, procurement rails, and auditability as constitutive safeguards.

CH09 binds healthcare/education governance to canonical rails (commons standards for access/maintenance/ΔG; R/B tail discipline;
P/E/C anti-toll classification; audit and procurement rails; privacy minimalism). It provides diagnostics, baseline packages and
wait-time escalation, quality triangulation, staffing as infrastructure, maintenance logic for physical and digital systems, anti-toll
design, funding architectures that avoid selection/cherry-picking, digital rails without surveillance rent, local governance with
audit-of-the-auditor, lifeworld impact mapping to R/B and agency, publication requirements, and hard stops. It is designed to
fail-closed: opaque triage, fee-stacks, normalised waiting without escalation, and black-box decisioning are non-conforming until
corrected.
