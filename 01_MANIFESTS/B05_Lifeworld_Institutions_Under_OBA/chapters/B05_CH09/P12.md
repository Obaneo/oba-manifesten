# B05.CH09.P12 — Measurement and publication requirements (healthcare & education as lifeworld test)

This paragraph specifies measurement and publication requirements for healthcare and education under OBA. Because these are
essentials institutions, drift is often invisible unless measured: waiting becomes normalised, selection hides in denials and
bureaucracy, fee-stacks grow in “administration”, and vendor lock-in becomes permanent. OBA therefore requires a **public measurement
layer** that makes access, quality, capacity/maintenance, and anti-toll signals contestable as time series—while enforcing privacy
minimalism. Publication is treated as conformance infrastructure: if core indicators cannot be published safely and reproducibly, the
system must reduce scope to auditable coverage (fail-closed against invisible L and E).

---

<a id="b05ch09p12s01"></a>
## B05.CH09.P12.S01 — Access: coverage, wait times per pathway, denial/deferral rates, regional distribution

Access measurement must reveal whether baseline rights exist in practice, including tails and regional mismatch.

### Access statistics (binding)
- **Coverage**: baseline package coverage rates by domain, cohort, and region (health and education), including baseline gaps.
- **Wait times**: distributions (median/p90/p95) per pathway and urgency class (appointments, diagnostics, treatment, placements,
  admissions).
- **Denial/deferral rates**: denial, deferral, and “incomplete documentation” churn, with reason-code distributions.
- **Regional distribution**: capacity and access differences by region type (urban/rural), travel burden proxies, and cross-region
  transfers.

### Publication requirement (binding)
- Publish time series with stable pathway taxonomy and event definitions; include breach events and escalation actions.

<a id="b05ch09p12s02"></a>
## B05.CH09.P12.S02 — Quality: safety incidents, outcome proxies, continuity metrics, experience reporting (procedural)

Quality measurement must be multi-dimensional and anti-Goodhart, aligned with CH09.P04.

### Quality statistics (binding)
- **Safety incidents**: adverse events, safeguarding failures, preventable harm proxies, and near-miss reporting rates (with severity
  breakdown).
- **Outcome proxies**: domain-appropriate outcomes (health stabilisation, preventable admissions, functional status; learning
  progression, completion, foundational skill proxies), with risk adjustment where required.
- **Continuity metrics**: handover failures, care/learning pathway fragmentation, repeated re-documentation events, and drop-out or
  discontinuity rates.
- **Experience reporting (procedural)**: structured experience signals focused on dignity, clarity, responsiveness, and accessibility,
  with method definitions and sampling discipline.

### Transparency requirement (binding)
- All published indicators must include method_id, definitions, uncertainty bands where applicable, and revision policies.

<a id="b05ch09p12s03"></a>
## B05.CH09.P12.S03 — Capacity/maintenance: staffing ratios, burn-out proxies, maintenance backlog, downtime, supply incidents

Capacity measurement must reveal whether services are sustainably deliverable, or whether rationing is occurring via burnout and decay.

### Capacity and maintenance statistics (binding)
- **Staffing ratios** and skill mix indicators by institution type and region; supervisor coverage metrics where applicable.
- **Burn-out proxies**: overtime, sick leave, turnover, vacancy duration, temporary-staff reliance, contact-time compression proxies.
- **Maintenance backlog**: size, severity distribution, age, SLA compliance for buildings/equipment/IT.
- **Downtime**: IT downtime events, equipment unavailability, classroom closures, and service cancellations attributable to maintenance.
- **Supply incidents**: stockouts and continuity breaches (medications, assistive devices, teaching materials) with recovery times.

### Publication requirement (binding)
- Publish as time series with tail emphasis (p90/p95) and link to escalation triggers (capacity breach and status downgrade events).

<a id="b05ch09p12s04"></a>
## B05.CH09.P12.S04 — Anti-toll: fee-share metrics, admin-time burden, consulting share, vendor lock-in indicators

Anti-toll measurement must reveal when bureaucracy and intermediaries are absorbing resources and creating hidden L.

### Anti-toll statistics (binding)
- **Fee-share metrics**: administrative overhead share, billing/claims spend share (where applicable), management overhead share, and
  intermediary fee volumes.
- **Admin-time burden**: documentation time share proxies, form counts, repeated data entry events, and time spent on eligibility or
  claims disputes (aggregated).
- **Consulting share**: consulting and vendor-services spend, growth rates, and concentration (risk of dependence).
- **Vendor lock-in indicators**: contract duration concentration, proprietary interface usage, integration paywall spend, switching
  costs, and downtime linked to vendor dependency.

### Fail-closed implication (binding)
- Rising anti-toll indicators without access/quality improvement triggers audit and mandatory simplification; refusal to disclose
  contracts and spend categories triggers conformance restrictions (fail-closed against bureaucratic extraction).
