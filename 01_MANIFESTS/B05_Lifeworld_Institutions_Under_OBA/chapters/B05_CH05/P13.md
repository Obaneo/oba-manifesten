# B05.CH05.P13 — Local vs national financing: parameter space without fragmentation

This paragraph specifies how OBA handles local versus national financing. OBA permits parameter variation, but it does not permit
fragmentation of basic rights or baseline access. The fiscal layer is a prime driver of “postcode outcomes”: different burdens,
different access, different execution quality. Without strict rails, decentralisation becomes a mechanism for selective execution,
capture, and legitimacy collapse.

CH05 therefore defines central minima (uniform rights and baseline access), allows local tax mixes only within anti-regression and
essentials rails, requires transparent and auditable intergovernmental transfer formulas, and treats unexplained locality divergence
as a failure mode that creates both legitimacy breach and mobility/migration friction.

---

## B05.CH05.P13.S01 — Central minima: basic rights and baseline access are uniform; local variation within rails

OBA establishes a national (or central) floor: baseline access and essential rights cannot depend on municipality wealth, politics, or
administrative capacity.

### Central minima (binding)
- **Uniform baseline access**: essential services and security floors have central minima (coverage and lead-time rails) that all
  localities must meet.
- **Uniform definition sets**: fiscal bases and classifications (T in B,T,N,L,R; caps integration) are centrally pinned; local texts
  cannot redefine canon.
- **Variation only in parameters**: local governments may vary rates, thresholds, and mix only inside the allowable parameter space.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Any local variation must declare which parameters vary and prove compatibility with central minima and lifeworld tests.
- If local variation produces systematic shortfalls in baseline access, escalation triggers activate (support, intervention, or
  temporary constraint of local autonomy until corrected).

---

## B05.CH05.P13.S02 — Local tax mix: allowed if anti-regression rails and essentials rails are respected

Local tax autonomy can improve responsiveness, but it also amplifies regressive drift if localities rely on easy-to-collect charges,
fixed fees, or quasi-taxes on essentials.

### Local mix rule (binding)
- Local levies and charges are permitted only if:
  - anti-regression holds (tails not worsened),
  - essentials rails hold (baseline access not undermined),
  - incidence and overhead are published (join-safe),
  - and discretionary carve-outs are prohibited.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Local systems must publish cohort incidence and administrative friction metrics; if burdens shift onto low-capacity groups, redesign
  is mandatory.
- Mandatory local charges that create fee-stacks or private intermediaries are non-conforming and must be replaced or requalified.

---

## B05.CH05.P13.S03 — Intergovernmental transfers: transparent formulas, no political arbitrariness, auditable

Transfers are the mechanism that prevents fragmentation: they equalise capacity so that baseline rights are not hostage to local tax
bases or economic shocks.

### Transfer discipline (binding)
- **Formula-based**: transfers must follow published formulas based on measurable need/cost/capacity variables.
- **No political arbitrariness**: ad-hoc grants, partisan reward/punishment, and opaque “special deals” are prohibited.
- **Auditability**: transfer amounts, triggers, and exceptions are published; revisions are logged.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- If transfers become discretionary or non-auditable, the system creates a capture surface and must trigger governance escalation.
- Transfer design must include anti-perverse-incentive checks (avoid rewarding maintenance neglect or under-collection via opacity).

---

## B05.CH05.P13.S04 — Failure mode: postcode lottery without explanation → legitimacy breach and migration frictions

A postcode lottery is not merely “inequality.” In OBA it is a structural governance failure: people experience rights as contingent and
the system becomes politically unstable.

### Failure mechanism
- **Uneven outcomes**: baseline access, burdens, and execution lead-times diverge by locality.
- **Opacity**: divergences are not visible because reporting is aggregated or suppressed beyond join-safety necessity.
- **Mobility pressure**: households relocate for access/affordability; local labour markets and housing systems destabilise.

### Conformance implications (binding)
- Publication must include local cohort profiles and baseline access indicators; unexplained divergence triggers audits and
  interventions.
- If divergence persists, scaling of additional local fiscal autonomy is blocked and central corrective mechanisms must activate
  (transfer adjustment, capacity support, procurement remedy, or governance reset).
