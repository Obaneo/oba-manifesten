# B05.CH05 — Taxes, contributions, and public finance: T-architecture and legitimacy without extraction

This chapter defines **T-architecture** (taxes, contributions, premiums, and quasi-taxes) as a **legitimacy-critical interface** in
the lifeworld layer. Under OBA, T is not merely a revenue mechanism: it is the explicit exchange that funds **commons capacity** and
stabilises **R/B tails**—and it must do so **without** recreating extraction through fee-stacks, lock-in intermediation, or
administrative tolling.

CH05 treats fiscal design as a conformance surface: incidence-first logic, anti-regression rails, integration with caps (W/I), strict
ΔM channel discipline, and auditability requirements that prevent carve-outs and semantic arbitrage. T must remain testable as a
contract: destination, overhead, and lifeworld effects must be publishable under privacy minimalism.
