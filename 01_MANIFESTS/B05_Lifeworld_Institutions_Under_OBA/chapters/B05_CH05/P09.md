# B05.CH05.P09 — Execution and compliance: collection without clamp or surveillance rent

This paragraph specifies execution and compliance rails for T: how levies are collected and enforced without turning the fiscal layer
into a clamp or surveillance system. In OBA, execution quality is not “back office.” It is lifeworld impact: complexity, delays,
proof pressure, and opaque decisions function as hidden L and create an ecosystem of paid intermediaries (E) that undermines both
legitimacy and accuracy.

CH05 therefore binds T execution to (i) uniform schemas and identifiers sufficient for correctness and auditability, (ii) simplicity
and pre-fill as default, (iii) due process with reproducible calculations, and (iv) strict privacy minimalism: tax systems must not
become general behaviour-monitoring infrastructure.

---

## B05.CH05.P09.S01 — Single-source of truth: uniform identifiers and schemas (link Codex) without data maximalism

A tax system cannot be auditable if every agency and vendor uses divergent identifiers, definitions, and data formats. But the remedy
must not be “collect everything.” OBA requires a minimal but consistent data substrate.

### Execution rail (binding)
- Use **uniform identifiers and schemas** for entities, positions, contracts, and flows where needed to compute T and to audit
  incidence and compliance.
- Pin definitions and schema versions (definition-set ID + method_id discipline); prevent silent drift.
- Design for join-safety and minimal necessary fields: build correctness without creating an omniscient profile system.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Any fiscal computation that cannot declare the schema version and definition-set reference is non-conforming (fail-closed).
- Any proposal that expands data collection beyond order-necessary fields must justify necessity; “nice to have” data is prohibited.

---

## B05.CH05.P09.S02 — Pre-fill and simplicity: minimise filing complexity; complexity is often an E ecosystem

Complexity is a predictable extraction surface: it creates paid optimisation, increases errors, and shifts burdens onto low-capacity
groups. Under OBA, simplicity is a fairness and anti-capture requirement.

### Simplicity rail (binding)
- **Pre-fill by default**: where the system already has lawful data, it should pre-populate obligations and require only corrections.
- **Standard forms and templates**: minimise bespoke regimes, special cases, and interpretive ambiguity.
- **Minimise proof burden**: requests for additional evidence must be rare, targeted, and time-bounded.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- If compliance requires professional assistance for ordinary cases, the system has created a toll ecosystem and must be simplified.
- Complexity that exists to police “abuse” rather than to compute correct incidence is treated as bureaucratic clamp (non-conform).

---

## B05.CH05.P09.S03 — Due process: fast, reproducible objection/appeal with access to calculations

Enforcement without due process becomes selective power. OBA requires contestability to prevent capture and to correct errors quickly.

### Due process rail (binding)
- **Access to calculations**: taxpayers must be able to see the computation basis (inputs, rules applied, version references).
- **Reproducible decisions**: determinations must be rerunnable from logged inputs under pinned methods.
- **Fast objection/appeal**: time-bounded pathways with interim relief where delay would create arrears cascades.
- **Precedent and restatement discipline**: publish aggregated outcomes and restate when method errors are discovered.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Black-box assessments are prohibited: if the decision cannot be reproduced, it is non-conforming.
- Delay-as-punishment (long appeal cycles, repeated reassessments) is treated as administrative L and triggers redesign.

---

## B05.CH05.P09.S04 — Privacy: fiscal instruments must not become general behaviour monitoring; only order-necessary data

Fiscal administration often drifts into surveillance: “to enforce compliance, monitor everything.” OBA treats this as a categorical
risk because it converts T into a coercive behavioural control axis and creates new rent surfaces.

### Privacy rail (binding)
- **Purpose limitation**: data collected for T computation and enforcement cannot be repurposed for general social control.
- **Minimal necessary data**: collect only what is required to compute bases and enforce rules; avoid continuous monitoring.
- **Tiered access and retention caps**: strict access controls, logging, time-bounded retention; publish incident policies.
- **No behavioural scoring**: risk models may be used narrowly for audit sampling, but must be auditable, contestable, and cannot
  become citizen scoring or eligibility gating infrastructure.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- If compliance requires pervasive monitoring (transactions-as-default surveillance, mandatory profiling), the system is non-conforming
  and must be redesigned toward rule-based, minimal-data enforcement.
- Join safety is mandatory: do not build linkable datasets that create a de facto universal dossier.
