# B05.CH04 — Security and buffers: income floor, risks, shocks, insurance, and fallback commons

This chapter specifies **security and buffers** as lifeworld infrastructure: mechanisms that stabilise **R/B tails** and prevent
small shocks from cascading into lock-in, arrears, and crisis-driven dependency. Under OBA, security is not “welfare policy” in the
moral sense; it is an order constraint that preserves exit power and prevents extraction from re-entering via debt, insurance clamp,
or bureaucratic tolling.

CH04 is fail-closed: security that depends on **lock-in**, **surveillance**, **humiliation**, or **discretionary deals** is
non-conforming. Buffers must be rule-bound, auditable, and compatible with caps and ΔM rails. If stability claims cannot be evidenced
with public tables and reproducible methods, the system cannot claim lifeworld compatibility.
