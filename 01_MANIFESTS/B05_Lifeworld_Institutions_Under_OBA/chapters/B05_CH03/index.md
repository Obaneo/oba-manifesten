# B05.CH03 — Time, care, and social reproduction: maintaining people and communities as capacity (not a residual)

This chapter specifies **time/care/social reproduction** as an order-critical lifeworld layer: the infrastructure that maintains
people, households, and communities as capacity. Under OBA, care is not treated as a residual “private matter” to be externalised to
households, and time is not treated as an infinite input adjustable by coercion. The chapter binds care/time to **maintenance-first
logic (ΔG analogue)**, lifeworld burdens (hidden L), and R/B tail stability, while enforcing strict privacy rails and resisting KPI
totalisation. If care access, quality, or administrative friction cannot be made testable without surveillance, the system must
fail-closed.
