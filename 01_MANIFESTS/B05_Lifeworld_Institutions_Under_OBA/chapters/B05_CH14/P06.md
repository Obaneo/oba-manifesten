# B05.CH14.P06 — OBA intervention set (after): what changes structurally

This paragraph specifies the **OBA intervention set** used to generate the **post-OBA (“after”)** version of Day-in-the-Life Case I. The intervention set is not a wishlist; it is a **structured rails package** that must produce measurable changes in **L**, reduce tail fragility in **R/B**, and make **exit real** across essentials by collapsing fee stacks, limiting punitive enforcement, and reducing administrative time capture. The output is an auditable list of interventions with linkage to (i) baseline lock-in surfaces (P03), (ii) fee/toll layers (P04), (iii) shock propagation (P05), and (iv) the case dashboard (P09).

---

<a id="b05ch14p06s01"></a>
## B05.CH14.P06.S01 — L reduction: baseline access (energy/food), rent rails/commons housing, fee-stack collapse

L reduction is achieved by converting essentials from threat-based toll systems into **baseline access** plus contestable add-ons, and by substituting gatekept markets with **commons/public options** where contestability cannot be restored.

### Intervention components (minimum set)
- `baseline_access_energy`:
  - a guaranteed minimum service band (non-disconnectable up to baseline threshold),
  - transparent tariffs with itemized bills,
  - elimination of reconnection fee traps and punitive advance-payment regimes.
- `baseline_access_food`:
  - a baseline nutrition access mechanism (not charity-gated),
  - removal of pay-to-access intermediaries for essential staples.
- `rent_rails_and_commons_housing`:
  - rent rail constraints (indexation caps, termination constraints, deposit rules),
  - expanded commons/public housing exit option with reduced moving friction.
- `fee_stack_collapse`:
  - prohibition or strict cap of non-optional intermediary fees in essentials,
  - unbundling rules where bundles function as toll gates,
  - mandatory plain-language disclosure and standardized fee taxonomy.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The intervention set **MUST** map each L-reduction component to a baseline lock-in surface and an E-layer category (P03/P04).
- “Baseline access” **MUST** include a disconnection/eviction constraint for essentials (where applicable) and a dispute-safe regime (no enforcement while contesting within the time bound).
- Fee-stack collapse **MUST** be expressed as concrete prohibitions/caps with evidence requirements (what must disappear from bills and contracts).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-LRED-01 (Mapping):** each L reduction element references at least one baseline lock-in and one fee-stack category.
- **DL-LRED-02 (Bill delta test):** post-case bills show item-level removal/capping of targeted fees (or the claim fails).
- **DL-LRED-03 (Threat reduction):** disconnection/eviction threat timeline is materially altered (longer, contestable, or removed within baseline threshold).

## B05.CH14.P06.S02 — Security/buffers: income floor, emergency buffer, fast restructuring (link CH04/CH06)

Buffers prevent small shocks from converting into cascades. In OBA, buffers are designed to reduce tail risk without creating new gatekeeping or high-proof administrative traps.

### Intervention components (minimum set)
- `income_floor`:
  - a predictable minimum net cashflow (N) floor or equivalent stabilizer,
  - designed to reduce volatility exposure (B/T) rather than only compensate after failure.
- `emergency_buffer`:
  - fast-access emergency liquidity for small shocks (durables, urgent repairs),
  - non-punitive repayment terms and no fee cascade surface.
- `fast_restructuring`:
  - a rapid restructuring path for arrears/debt that freezes fee cascades,
  - standardized settlement terms and time-bounded decisions.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Buffer mechanisms **MUST** be accessible with minimal proof burden and must not introduce new identity/portal lock-in.
- Emergency buffer access **MUST** be faster than the fee cascade timeline (otherwise it is non-functional).
- Restructuring **MUST** freeze penalty accrual and collections escalation while the case is in contestation, within defined time bounds.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-BUF-01 (Access speed):** emergency buffer `time_to_access` is shorter than the baseline cascade trigger window.
- **DL-BUF-02 (Non-punitive terms):** buffer products have no rollover fees and no penalty stack; itemized verification required.
- **DL-RES-01 (Freeze rule):** when restructuring is initiated, fee accrual and enforcement escalation stop by rule (declared and evidenced).

<a id="b05ch14p06s03"></a>
## B05.CH14.P06.S03 — Exit rails: portability, ban on punitive fees, public options in essentials

Exit rails convert “choice in theory” into **choice in practice**. The goal is not to force switching, but to ensure that gatekeepers cannot punish exit or block portability.

### Intervention components (minimum set)
- `portability`:
  - identity portability (account recovery, authentication continuity),
  - service portability (number/email/address continuity where relevant),
  - data portability for platform-dependent work or essential accounts.
- `ban_punitive_fees`:
  - prohibition of early termination penalties that function as switching deterrents,
  - prohibition of retaliatory pricing and blacklisting for lawful switching/disputes.
- `public_options_essentials`:
  - guaranteed-access public/commons option for housing/energy/digital (as applicable),
  - activated when market contestability fails or scarcity makes exit structurally impossible.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Exit rails **MUST** be expressed as enforceable rights plus enforcement mechanisms (appeal path + sanctions for non-compliance).
- Portability **MUST** include recovery/continuity guarantees to prevent identity lockout from neutralizing exit.
- If a public option is invoked as the remedy, it **MUST** be defined as a real operational alternative (not a symbolic programme) with measurable access conditions.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-EXIT-01 (Switching cost delta):** the post-case switching cost (cash/time) is lower than baseline by declared margin or the claim fails.
- **DL-EXIT-02 (Penalty removal):** punitive termination/retaliation clauses are absent or capped in post-case contract surface.
- **DL-EXIT-03 (Public option realism):** existence and accessibility of the public option is evidenced by eligibility rules + access timeline bands.

<a id="b05ch14p06s04"></a>
## B05.CH14.P06.S04 — Execution: one-stop interface, minimal proof burden, time-bounded contestability

Execution is where order collapses in practice. OBA therefore treats delivery rails as first-class: a one-stop interface, low proof burden, and strict time bounds for decisions and corrections.

### Intervention components (minimum set)
- `one_stop_interface`:
  - single intake for essentials support, restructuring, and dispute initiation,
  - integrated status tracking with transparent logs.
- `minimal_proof_burden`:
  - default acceptance of standard evidence classes (contracts/bills/paystubs),
  - no repeated verification loops unless anomaly triggers fire,
  - assistance for offline/low-digital-access households.
- `time_bounded_contestability`:
  - fixed maximum times for initial decision, appeal decision, and provisional relief,
  - automatic escalation and interim relief when deadlines are breached.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The post-case execution model **MUST** reduce administrative time burden compared to baseline (measurable proxies required).
- Contestability **MUST** include **provisional relief** where enforcement would otherwise create irreversible harm (eviction/disconnection/garnishment).
- Decision deadlines **MUST** be enforced via fail-safe mechanisms (auto-approval, freeze, or escalation), not only via “targets”.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-EXEC-01 (Time burden delta):** post-case admin steps/time band is lower than baseline for at least two key processes (benefits/disputes/restructuring).
- **DL-EXEC-02 (Provisional relief):** relief exists and triggers automatically under defined conditions.
- **DL-EXEC-03 (Deadline enforcement):** missed deadlines produce automatic protective action (freeze/escalation), evidenced by rule statement.

---

### Output format (intervention set summary)

At minimum, the post-case **MUST** emit an intervention set summary (table or structured list) with:

- `intervention_id` | `domain` | `target_lock_in_surface` | `target_fee_layer` | `mechanism` | `expected_delta` | `evidence_required` | `time_bound`

If expected deltas are stated without measurement linkage (P09) and evidence requirements, the “after” becomes non-conform storytelling under CH14 fail-closed rules.
