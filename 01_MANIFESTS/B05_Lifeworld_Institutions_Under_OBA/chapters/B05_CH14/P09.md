# B05.CH14.P09 — Measurable “before/after” outputs (case dashboard)

This paragraph specifies the **case dashboard**: the minimum measurable outputs required to claim a valid “before/after” Day-in-the-Life case. The dashboard binds narrative to measurement and prevents storytelling-washing. Outputs are presented with `method_id`, uncertainty bands, and (where applicable) cohort percentiles for interpretability. If the dashboard cannot be emitted, the case is **NON-CONFORM** under CH14 fail-closed rules.

---

<a id="b05ch14p09s01"></a>
## B05.CH14.P09.S01 — B,T,N,L,R: values/percentiles + uncertainty bands + method_id

The primary dashboard layer reports the B→T→N→L→R chain, pre and post, with explicit uncertainty and method provenance.

### Required fields (template)
- `definitionset_id`
- `method_id` (versioned; links to calculation rules)
- `time_window_pre`, `time_window_post`
- `case_mode` ∈ {`observed_after`, `simulated_after`}
- `B_pre`, `B_post` (value + unit + timebase)
- `T_pre`, `T_post` (timing/friction index or declared components; see below)
- `N_pre`, `N_post` (value + unit + timebase)
- `L_pre`, `L_post` (value + unit + timebase; with decomposition pointers)
- `R_pre`, `R_post` where `R = N − L` (value + unit + timebase)
- `RB_pre`, `RB_post` where `RB = R / B` (ratio; bounded if B=0 cases are handled explicitly)
- `uncertainty_band` for each headline output (at minimum: R and RB)

### Timing / friction representation (T)
`T` **MUST** be represented as either:
- `T_components[]` (recommended): {withholding_timing, pay_arrival_delay, bill_due_mismatch, clawback_probability_band, admin_friction_time_band}, or
- `T_index` (allowed): a documented composite with disclosed component weights.

### Percentiles (optional but recommended)
If cohort percentiles are used:
- `percentile_ref` (which cohort definition; aggregated)
- `B_percentile`, `N_percentile`, `L_percentile`, `R_percentile`, `RB_percentile` (pre and post)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The dashboard **MUST** include `method_id` and time windows; numbers without provenance are non-conform.
- The dashboard **MUST** report uncertainty bands; point estimates without bands are non-conform.
- `R` and `RB` **MUST** be derivable from declared N and L (or explicitly state any adjustments).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-KPI-01 (Provenance):** `definitionset_id` + `method_id` + time windows present.
- **DL-KPI-02 (Banding):** uncertainty bands present for R and RB.
- **DL-KPI-03 (Derivability):** R equals N−L within declared rounding/uncertainty rules.

<a id="b05ch14p09s02"></a>
## B05.CH14.P09.S02 — Lock-in metrics: switching costs, penalty incidence, arrears/disconnects, dispute duration

This layer measures whether “exit becomes real” and whether threat-based discipline loses leverage.

### Required metrics (minimum set)
For each domain `{housing, energy, digital, debt}`:
- `switching_cost_cash_pre/post` (band)
- `switching_cost_time_pre/post` (band)
- `penalty_incidence_pre/post` (frequency band or count per window)
- `arrears_incidence_pre/post` (frequency band or count per window)
- `enforcement_threat_pre/post` (e.g., eviction/disconnection/garnishment: present/absent + timeline band)
- `dispute_duration_pre/post` (time-to-initial decision and time-to-resolution; bands acceptable)
- `opacity_flag_pre/post` (present/absent; and whether itemization exists)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Lock-in metrics **MUST** be domain-specific; a single aggregated “lock-in score” is non-conform unless it is explicitly decomposed and reproducible.
- Switching costs **MUST** separate cash vs time; combining them hides coercion via time poverty.
- Dispute duration **MUST** include deadline enforcement status (whether missed deadlines create protective action).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-LI-01 (Domain coverage):** housing/energy/digital/debt each have switching cost and threat metrics.
- **DL-LI-02 (Cash/time separation):** switching costs include both cash and time bands.
- **DL-LI-03 (Dispute measurability):** dispute durations exist (even as bands) and are comparable pre/post.

<a id="b05ch14p09s03"></a>
## B05.CH14.P09.S03 — Shock absorption: time-to-recovery, buffer adequacy, default/eviction avoidance

This layer evaluates whether the “after” case actually improves tail resilience under standardized shocks (P05), without relying on moralizing or heroic coping.

### Required metrics (minimum set)
For each shock `{small_durable_failure, time_shock, price_shock_essentials, administrative_shock}`:
- `shock_cost_net_pre/post` (net cost to household, including fees/penalties; band)
- `buffer_adequacy_pre/post` (can the household absorb without arrears/collections? yes/no + threshold band)
- `cascade_multiplier_pre/post` (fees/penalties relative to principal or baseline cost; band)
- `time_to_recovery_pre/post` (time until R returns to baseline band; band)
- `default_or_termination_avoided`:
  - {eviction_avoided, disconnection_avoided, garnishment_avoided, collections_escalation_avoided} (per shock; yes/no)
- `administrative_resolution_time_pre/post` (for administrative shock)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Shock absorption **MUST** be evaluated using the standardized shocks (P05) and must not be replaced by custom “best case” events.
- Buffer adequacy **MUST** be defined against concrete thresholds (e.g., “absorbs without arrears within the window”), not feelings.
- Claims of avoidance (eviction/disconnection/default) **MUST** be linked to rule changes or evidence classes, not inferred optimism.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-SHK-ABS-01 (Shock set):** all four standardized shocks are included or explicitly marked “not applicable” with justification.
- **DL-SHK-ABS-02 (Recovery metric):** time_to_recovery exists pre/post for at least two shocks.
- **DL-SHK-ABS-03 (Cascade delta):** cascade_multiplier decreases post or the intervention set is insufficient.

<a id="b05ch14p09s04"></a>
## B05.CH14.P09.S04 — Commons metrics: access coverage, wait times, incident rates, service levels (aggregated)

This layer reports whether commons/public options are real operational alternatives and whether they improve access conditions without introducing new exclusionary lock-ins.

### Required metrics (minimum set, aggregated)
Per domain where a commons/public option is invoked:
- `access_coverage` (eligible share or availability proxy; aggregated)
- `time_to_access_band` (wait times; bands acceptable)
- `service_level_markers[]` (e.g., uptime, continuity, dispute responsiveness; domain-specific)
- `incident_rate_band` (complaints, service failures, lockouts; aggregated)
- `exclusion_markers` (eligibility gates, documentation burden, denial rate band; aggregated)
- `portability_support` (whether portability/recovery is supported operationally)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Commons metrics **MUST** be reported as operational realities (coverage, wait time, service levels). “Exists in law” without operational metrics is non-conform.
- Commons options **MUST** be checked for new lock-ins (exclusion, punitive enforcement, opaque criteria); absence of this check is non-conform.
- All commons reporting **MUST** remain aggregated and privacy-safe.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-COM-01 (Operationality):** coverage + time_to_access_band are present where commons exits are claimed.
- **DL-COM-02 (No new lock-in check):** exclusion markers are reported (present/absent + proxy).
- **DL-COM-03 (Service levels):** at least one service-level marker is reported per invoked domain.

---

### Output format (dashboard schema)

At minimum, the case **MUST** emit the dashboard in a machine-parseable structure (table(s) or structured lists) with:

1) **Headline chain table**
- `window` | `B` | `T` | `N` | `L` | `R` | `RB` | `uncertainty_R` | `uncertainty_RB` | `method_id`

2) **Lock-in table (by domain)**
- `domain` | `switch_cost_cash` | `switch_cost_time` | `penalty_incidence` | `arrears_incidence` | `threat_timeline` | `dispute_duration` | `opacity_flag`

3) **Shock table**
- `shock_type` | `cascade_multiplier` | `buffer_adequacy` | `time_to_recovery` | `avoidance_flags`

4) **Commons table**
- `domain` | `coverage` | `time_to_access` | `service_level_markers` | `incident_rate` | `exclusion_markers`

If any of these tables cannot be produced, the case is **NON-CONFORM**.
