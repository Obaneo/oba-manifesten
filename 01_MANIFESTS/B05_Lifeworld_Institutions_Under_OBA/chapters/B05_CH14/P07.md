# B05.CH14.P07 — “Commons exits”: concrete switches out of lock-in contracts

This paragraph specifies **commons exits**: concrete, operational switches that move the household from lock-in contracts and toll layers into **commons/public options** (or interoperability-based alternatives) where exit becomes real and enforcement threats lose leverage. “Commons exit” is defined functionally: an exit is valid only if it reduces (i) switching costs, (ii) punitive penalties, (iii) opacity, and (iv) threat-based discipline, while preserving service continuity.

The output is a domain-by-domain switch log with evidence classes, time bounds, and measurable deltas. Symbolic “access in principle” is non-conform.

---

<a id="b05ch14p07s01"></a>
## B05.CH14.P07.S01 — Housing: from private lock-in renting → commons/public alternative; moving friction down

Housing exit is the highest-friction switch and therefore the strongest test of whether “exit is real” under OBA.

### Exit definition (housing)
- `exit_from`: private rent contract with scarcity-driven leverage and punitive termination/deposit risk.
- `exit_to`: commons/public housing option or contestable rent-rails regime with enforceable rights and dispute-safe protection.

### Required switch fields (template)
- `current_contract_class` (lease type + key constraints)
- `exit_destination` ∈ {public_housing_unit, coop_unit, regulated_rent_rail_unit, transitional_commons, other_verified}
- `eligibility_path` (what is required; steps and time bands)
- `moving_friction_pre` and `moving_friction_post`:
  - `cash_cost_band`, `time_cost_band`, `documentation_steps`
- `deposit_and_penalty_delta`:
  - deposit return rules, termination penalties, blacklist exposure (pre vs post)
- `continuity_risk` (risk of homelessness gap; time-to-housing band)
- `evidence_class[]` (offer letter, lease terms summary, deposit receipt, rules)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- A housing commons exit **MUST** reduce moving friction (cash and/or time) or provide compensating rails (moving support) that makes the switch feasible for low R/B households.
- A housing commons exit **MUST** provide dispute-safe protection: eviction pathways are time-bounded and contestable with provisional relief.
- If exit depends on a waitlist, the case **MUST** report wait time bands and what happens during the transition (gap handling); otherwise “exit” is symbolic.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-HX-01 (Feasibility):** exit is feasible under the household’s baseline liquidity/time constraints (bands acceptable, but must be stated).
- **DL-HX-02 (Penalty reduction):** punitive termination penalties/blacklist exposure are reduced or neutralized by rails.
- **DL-HX-03 (Continuity):** continuity risk is disclosed and mitigated by a defined transition mechanism.

## B05.CH14.P07.S02 — Energy: from penalty/advance-payment lock-in → baseline access + transparent tariffs

Energy commons exit removes the disconnection/fee trap and replaces it with baseline access and contestable billing.

### Exit definition (energy)
- `exit_from`: tariff/contract regime with punitive advance payment, opaque surcharges, and rapid disconnection threats.
- `exit_to`: baseline access regime with itemized transparent tariffs and dispute-safe rules.

### Required switch fields (template)
- `provider_regime_pre` and `provider_regime_post` (private/municipal/prepaid/baseline-access)
- `disconnection_regime_delta`:
  - time-to-disconnection, grace windows, reconnection fees (pre vs post)
- `tariff_transparency_delta`:
  - itemization availability, surcharge flags, billing dispute path
- `advance_payment_delta`:
  - deposit/prepayment requirements and triggers
- `switching_cost_delta`:
  - fees, paperwork steps, identity access requirements
- `evidence_class[]` (tariff sheet, bill samples, disconnection notices, rule statement)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- An energy commons exit **MUST** eliminate punitive disconnection for baseline thresholds and **MUST** remove reconnection fee traps.
- Tariffs **MUST** be itemized and understandable; opaque surcharges **MUST NOT** be allowed in the baseline regime.
- Switching **MUST** be possible without identity lockout dependence (recovery paths required).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-EX-01 (Threat reduction):** disconnection threat timeline is materially reduced or neutralized within baseline access bands.
- **DL-EX-02 (Opacity removal):** opaque surcharge markers present in baseline are absent or made contestable with itemization.
- **DL-EX-03 (Switching friction):** switching steps/time band is reduced or supported by the one-stop interface.

## B05.CH14.P07.S03 — Digital: from platform lock-in → interoperable baseline services and portability

Digital commons exit targets dependency chains where identity, communication, and service access are controlled by a small set of gatekeepers.

### Exit definition (digital)
- `exit_from`: platform/device/account ecosystem where access can be revoked, fees can be imposed, and portability is limited.
- `exit_to`: interoperable baseline services with portability and recovery guarantees (identity continuity, data portability, service continuity).

### Required switch fields (template)
- `baseline_services_post`:
  - connectivity baseline, messaging/email/ID access baseline, service portal access baseline
- `portability_bundle`:
  - identity recovery, number/email portability, data export/import, account escrow (where relevant)
- `device_lock_delta`:
  - carrier lock, app store dependency, repair/upgrade constraints
- `platform_fee_delta`:
  - fees/take-rates that gate access to work/services (pre vs post)
- `deplatforming_block_delta`:
  - whether loss of an account still blocks income/rights; mitigation rails
- `evidence_class[]` (policy statement, portability procedure, service terms)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- A digital commons exit **MUST** provide identity recovery and continuity guarantees; without recovery, portability is cosmetic.
- Baseline digital services **MUST** be interoperable and non-exclusionary by default; paid tiers may exist but **MUST NOT** gate essential access.
- If a platform remains a dependency for income, the exit **MUST** include contestability rails against arbitrary deactivation and opaque ranking penalties.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-DX-01 (Recovery):** identity/account recovery path exists with time bounds.
- **DL-DX-02 (Portability):** at least one concrete portability action is executed (export/import or equivalent) or the case cannot claim portability.
- **DL-DX-03 (Dependency reduction):** dependency chains are reduced or protected by enforceable rails.

## B05.CH14.P07.S04 — Debt: from collections ecosystem → restructuring + fee waivers + stop on cascades

Debt commons exit breaks the fee cascade and enforcement asymmetry that turns temporary shocks into persistent tail events.

### Exit definition (debt)
- `exit_from`: collections-driven ecosystem with compounding fees, enforcement threats, and slow dispute/correction.
- `exit_to`: restructuring regime with fee waivers, freeze rules, and time-bounded decisions.

### Required switch fields (template)
- `debt_inventory_pre` (classes + principal bands)
- `collections_stage_pre` and `collections_stage_post`
- `freeze_rule`:
  - what stops immediately upon entry (fees, interest, enforcement escalation)
- `fee_waiver_schedule`:
  - which fee categories are waived/capped and under what conditions
- `repayment_plan_terms`:
  - affordability logic, duration band, default handling
- `dispute_and_error_path`:
  - wrong balance correction, documentation burden, provisional relief
- `evidence_class[]` (restructuring agreement, balance statement, enforcement notices, logs)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Entry into restructuring **MUST** trigger an immediate freeze of fee accrual and enforcement escalation for the contested period (time bounded).
- Fee waivers **MUST** target the cascade surface first (collections fees, penalties, punitive interest).
- The post regime **MUST** provide fast correction for wrong balances and protect against retaliation or intimidation.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-DTX-01 (Freeze enforcement):** freeze rule exists and is evidenced (contract/rule/log).
- **DL-DTX-02 (Cascade collapse):** cascade fee categories are reduced or waived; itemized delta required.
- **DL-DTX-03 (Threat asymmetry reduction):** enforcement speed vs correction speed gap is reduced (bands acceptable).

---

### Output format (commons exit log)

At minimum, the post-case **MUST** emit a commons exit log (table or structured list) with:

- `domain` | `exit_from` | `exit_to` | `switching_cost_cash_delta` | `switching_cost_time_delta` | `penalty_delta` | `threat_delta` | `portability_or_continuity` | `evidence_class`

If the exit log cannot be produced with evidence linkage, the “after” case is NON-CONFORM under CH14 fail-closed rules.
