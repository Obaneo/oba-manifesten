# B05.CH14.P04 — Fee stacks and toll layers (E in the lifeworld)

This paragraph specifies how **fee stacks** and **toll layers** operationalize **E** in the lifeworld. In low R/B households, extraction is rarely a single visible price; it is more often a layered structure: intermediaries, administrative charges, penalty surfaces, and design patterns that convert limited choice into recurring tolls. The output of this paragraph is an **E-layer inventory** linked to evidence classes, enabling measurable attribution and later “collapse” claims in the post-OBA case.

---

<a id="b05ch14p04s01"></a>
## B05.CH14.P04.S01 — Intermediaries: brokerage, administration, collections, insurance layers, pay-to-access

Intermediaries frequently act as toll collectors between the household and essentials. They may be formal (regulated) or informal (market power), but the diagnostic is functional: whether they introduce non-optional fees that do not correspond to necessary cost.

### Required fields (template)
- `intermediary_layers[]` with, per layer:
  - `domain` ∈ {housing, energy, food, digital, debt, other_essential}
  - `layer_type` ∈ {brokerage, administration, collections, insurance, payment_processor, identity_verifier, subscription_gate, repair_service_gate, other}
  - `fee_items[]` (itemized; no lumping)
  - `is_optional` (true/false) + basis
  - `trigger_condition` (when the fee applies)
  - `beneficiary` (who receives the fee; entity class)
  - `evidence_class` (contract, invoice, notice, payment record)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Each intermediary layer **MUST** be classified as **optional** or **non-optional** from the household’s perspective (exit realism, not legal theory).
- All fee items **MUST** be itemized and linked to an evidence class.
- Layers that gate access to essentials via pay-to-access (fees required to apply, connect, dispute, or remain eligible) **MUST** be flagged as **toll_candidates**.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-INT-01 (Itemization):** every intermediary layer lists fee_items[] (no “misc fees” without breakdown).
- **DL-INT-02 (Non-optional flag):** `is_optional=false` is accompanied by the lock-in reason (scarcity, contract, identity, threat).
- **DL-INT-03 (Evidence linkage):** each layer has at least one evidence_class.

## B05.CH14.P04.S02 — Dark patterns: misleading discounts, bundles, penalty clauses

“Dark patterns” are treated as an extraction mechanism when they systematically produce higher realized L through deception, misdirection, or friction that prevents contestation.

### Required fields (template)
- `dark_patterns[]` with, per pattern:
  - `domain`
  - `pattern_type` ∈ {misleading_discount, teaser_rate, bundle_lock, hidden_fee, forced_opt_in, cancellation_friction, renewal_trap, penalty_clause, ambiguous_terms, other}
  - `mechanism` (how it converts choice limitation into cost)
  - `observed_effect` (what cost/constraint it creates)
  - `evidence_class` (contract screenshot/terms, invoice deltas, complaint record, notice)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- If a discount/teaser exists, the case **MUST** report the **effective price over time** (post-discount period), not only the headline offer.
- Bundles **MUST** be decomposed into components where possible; inability to decompose **MUST** be tagged as an opacity marker.
- Penalty clauses **MUST** be treated as part of the baseline cost surface when they are predictably triggered under low R/B volatility (i.e., they are structurally “priced in” by vulnerability).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-DP-01 (Effective price):** any discount claim includes post-discount price/time window.
- **DL-DP-02 (Bundle decomposition):** bundles are decomposed or explicitly tagged opaque.
- **DL-DP-03 (Penalty realism):** penalty clauses are mapped to likely trigger conditions (timing mismatch, volatility, admin friction).

## B05.CH14.P04.S03 — Paperwork as E: forms burden as a gate and disciplining mechanism

Paperwork is treated as **E-like** when it functions as a gate or punishment surface: it increases time burden, induces errors, and makes rights non-exercisable, converting administrative capacity into a scarcity barrier.

### Required fields (template)
- `paperwork_gates[]` with, per gate:
  - `process_name` (e.g., benefit verification, tariff switch, dispute filing, debt restructuring request)
  - `steps_count` (coarse is acceptable)
  - `time_burden_band` (e.g., <1h, 1–3h, 3–10h, >10h per month/event)
  - `deadline_risk` (low/med/high) + typical failure mode
  - `error_cost` (what happens when it fails: clawback, denial, penalty, delay)
  - `evidence_class` (letters, portal screenshots, required docs list)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Paperwork burdens **MUST** be represented as measurable proxies (steps/time/deadlines), not as narrative complaint.
- If paperwork gates access to essentials or rights, the case **MUST** tag it as **gatekeeping_surface=true**.
- If the baseline includes repeated verification loops, the case **MUST** report whether failures produce financial penalties or loss of access (disciplining function).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-PW-01 (Measurable proxy):** each paperwork gate includes steps_count + time_burden_band.
- **DL-PW-02 (Gate tagging):** gates that determine eligibility/access are flagged as gatekeeping surfaces.
- **DL-PW-03 (Error consequence):** each gate declares an error_cost category.

## B05.CH14.P04.S04 — Evidence pack: minimum data/contracts needed to prove E-layers

To prevent storytelling-washing, E-layer claims require a minimum evidence pack. Evidence may be redacted, but evidence *classes* and the logic of attribution must be disclosed.

### Minimum evidence pack (required classes)
The case **MUST** include, at minimum, evidence classes sufficient to support each asserted E-layer:
- **Contracts/terms** for each essentials domain (or declared equivalents where contracts are absent).
- **Bills/invoices** showing base charges and fee items (at least one recent sample per domain).
- **Notices** related to arrears, penalties, disconnection/eviction, renewals, and collections.
- **Payment records** (bank statements or ledger summaries) sufficient to verify realized payments vs billed amounts.
- **Dispute/appeal artefacts** (if any): tickets, letters, portal logs, response times (redacted allowed).
- **Collections artefacts** (if any): balance statements, fee breakdowns, enforcement notices.

### Evidence linkage rules (binding)
- Every fee item in the E-layer inventory **MUST** reference an evidence class (contract, bill, notice, payment record).
- If an item cannot be evidenced, it **MUST** be marked as `unverified` and **MUST NOT** be used as a headline “E reduced” claim later.
- Redaction is permitted, but the case **MUST** preserve: dates (coarse), fee categories, and computational logic needed for reproduction.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-EVP-01 (Coverage):** every asserted E-layer has at least one supporting evidence class.
- **DL-EVP-02 (Unverified handling):** unverified items are explicitly tagged and excluded from conformance deltas.
- **DL-EVP-03 (Reconstruction):** the evidence pack supports reconstructing at least the headline L decomposition (base vs fees vs penalties) for the baseline window.

---

### Output format (E-layer inventory summary)

At minimum, the case **MUST** emit a compact E-layer inventory (table or structured list) with:

- `domain` | `layer_type` | `fee_item` | `trigger_condition` | `is_optional` | `toll_candidate` | `opacity_flag` | `evidence_class`

If the inventory cannot be produced with evidence linkage, the case is NON-CONFORM under the CH14 fail-closed rule.
