# B05.CH14.P11 — Residual frictions and edge cases (honesty about what remains)

This paragraph specifies the **residual frictions and edge cases** that remain even after the OBA intervention set is applied. Its function is anti-PR: it prevents “after” cases from becoming utopian or unfalsifiable. Residual frictions must be surfaced, measured where possible, and routed into fallback commons and redesign triggers. If the case claims “problem solved” while suppressing residual tail risks, it is non-conform.

---

<a id="b05ch14p11s01"></a>
## B05.CH14.P11.S01 — Transition costs: moving, reskilling, temporary wait times

Even when exit rails exist, transitions require time, coordination, and sometimes upfront costs. These frictions must be explicit to avoid hidden exclusion.

### Residual friction inventory (minimum)
- `moving_transition_costs`:
  - cash cost band, time cost band, documentation steps, temporary accommodation risk.
- `reskilling_transition_costs` (if applicable):
  - time-to-qualification band, income dip risk during training, childcare compatibility.
- `temporary_wait_times`:
  - wait time bands for commons/public options (housing units, service onboarding, dispute resolution backlog).
- `coordination_load`:
  - number of concurrent processes required (switching, appeals, onboarding, logistics).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Transition costs **MUST** be reported as explicit bands (cash/time/steps), not vague warnings.
- If a public/commons option includes wait times, the case **MUST** specify how the household remains protected during the wait (gap handling).
- Transition costs that are structurally exclusionary for low R/B households **MUST** be flagged as redesign candidates (e.g., moving support, interim service continuity).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-TRN-01 (Bands present):** at least two transition cost categories are reported with cash/time bands.
- **DL-TRN-02 (Gap handling):** for any wait time, a defined protection mechanism exists (interim relief / continuity).
- **DL-TRN-03 (Exclusion flag):** any transition cost that exceeds baseline feasibility is flagged with a remedy candidate.

<a id="b05ch14p11s02"></a>
## B05.CH14.P11.S02 — Non-eliminable shocks: severe illness/regional crises; role of fallback commons

Some shocks are not eliminable through ordinary rails (severe illness, regional crisis, major system disruptions). OBA must therefore specify a **fallback commons** layer: continuity under extreme conditions without punitive eligibility gatekeeping.

### Non-eliminable shock classes (minimum)
- `severe_illness_or_disability_shock` (household function collapses temporarily/long-term)
- `regional_crisis_shock` (housing market shock, energy disruption, disaster)
- `systemic_service_outage` (digital identity outage, banking disruption, mass billing errors)

### Fallback commons functions (required)
- `continuity_of_essentials` (housing/energy/food/digital minimum access preserved)
- `emergency_income_or_relief` (rapid stabilization without prolonged proof burden)
- `suspension_of_enforcement` (pause eviction/disconnection/garnishment during declared crisis windows)
- `rapid dispute triage` (fast correction paths when error volumes spike)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The case **MUST** explicitly state which shock classes are treated as non-eliminable and what fallback commons function applies.
- Fallback commons **MUST** be operational (coverage + time-to-access bands), not merely legal or aspirational.
- Crisis-mode measures **MUST** be bounded and reversible, with clear activation/deactivation criteria, to prevent permanent overreach.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-FBK-01 (Class-to-function mapping):** each non-eliminable shock class maps to a fallback commons function.
- **DL-FBK-02 (Operationality):** fallback includes access coverage and time-to-access bands (aggregated).
- **DL-FBK-03 (Bounded crisis mode):** activation/deactivation criteria are declared.

## B05.CH14.P11.S03 — Cultural adaptation: status/identity shifts with changes in work and consumption

Structural changes alter status signals, routines, and identity narratives. This is not treated as a policy target but as a predictable adaptation surface that can generate resistance or new gatekeeping.

### Adaptation surfaces (minimum)
- `work_identity_shift` (from survival juggling to more predictable routines; potential stigma changes)
- `consumption_pattern_shift` (less penalty-driven spending; more baseline stability)
- `status_signal_shift` (less conspicuous survival signalling; more competence/contribution signalling potential)
- `institutional_contact_shift` (fewer adversarial interactions; new interfaces)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The case **MUST** treat cultural adaptation as a **risk surface** (misrecognition, new gatekeeping, resentment), not as a morality play.
- Cultural observations **MUST** be clearly labeled as qualitative experience signals, not as causal proof of success.
- If cultural adaptation generates new exclusionary behaviour (e.g., credential or status gatekeeping), the case **MUST** route it to the relevant rails (contestability, portability, audit).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-CUL-01 (Label discipline):** cultural/identity statements are tagged as qualitative signals (not KPIs).
- **DL-CUL-02 (Risk routing):** any identified new gatekeeping surface is linked to a mitigation rail (contestability/portability/audit).

<a id="b05ch14p11s04"></a>
## B05.CH14.P11.S04 — Risk: “lower R/B group” gets stigmatized; mitigate via universal design

A core failure mode is stigmatization: creating a visible “low R/B group” category that becomes a new target for blame, surveillance, or exclusion. OBA mitigates this via **universal design** and anti-stigma rails.

### Stigmatization mechanisms (minimum inventory)
- `labeling_risk` (formal categories that become identities)
- `surveillance_drift` (extra monitoring applied only to low R/B recipients)
- `discretionary_shaming` (frontline gatekeeper moralizing)
- `access_segregation` (separate, lower-quality service channels)

### Universal design mitigations (required)
- `universal_baseline_access` (essentials baselines available broadly, not charity-gated)
- `minimized means-testing` (where possible; rely on structural rails rather than intrusive eligibility)
- `privacy-preserving measurement` (aggregated indicators; no behavioural scoring)
- `single-channel dignity` (same service quality and interface for all, with accessibility support)
- `no-retaliation + appeal` (for discriminatory treatment)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Interventions **MUST** be designed to avoid creating a visible “recipient caste” where feasible (universal baselines preferred).
- Any targeting that remains **MUST** be privacy-preserving, minimally intrusive, and contestable; disproportionate monitoring is non-conform.
- The case **MUST** include an explicit anti-stigma check: whether the “after” design introduces new labels, segregated channels, or surveillance drift.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-STG-01 (Anti-stigma check present):** the case reports whether labeling/surveillance/segregation risks exist post.
- **DL-STG-02 (Universal baseline):** at least one essential baseline is delivered universally or through non-stigmatizing mechanisms.
- **DL-STG-03 (No behavioural scoring):** explicit prohibition and audit check against behavioural scoring for eligibility/enforcement.

---

### Output format (residual friction register)

At minimum, the case **MUST** emit a residual friction register (table or structured list) with:

- `friction_or_edge_case` | `domain` | `severity_band` | `who_is_affected` | `mitigation_or_fallback` | `measurement_proxy` | `redesign_trigger`

If residual risks are not disclosed, the case is non-conform as it becomes unfalsifiable.
