# B05.CH14.P01 — Purpose, scope, and method for the “day-in-the-life” case

This paragraph defines the purpose, scope, and method for Day-in-the-Life Case I: a low **R/B** household before and after an OBA intervention set. The case is a **conformance instrument**, not a narrative artefact: it exists to show—concretely and measurably—how **BRUTO (B) → NETTO (N) → LASTEN (L) → RESTRUIMTE (R)** changes, and where lock-ins and toll layers (E) structurally pin the household.

---

<a id="b05ch14p01s01"></a>
## B05.CH14.P01.S01 — Purpose: concretely show how BRUTO → NETTO → LASTEN → RESTRUIMTE changes (no PR)

The purpose is to make the lifeworld legible to OBA rails. The household’s day structure is treated as an observable output of contracts, frictions, and exit constraints—not as an emotive story.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The case **MUST** produce a *before/after* mapping of **B, T, N, L, R** (where **T** captures timing/withholdings/clawback risk and administrative friction affecting cash-flow reality).
- The case **MUST** identify the concrete lock-in surfaces that generate block power (housing/energy/digital/debt) and connect them to observed frictions (penalties, arrears threats, exclusivity, identity lockout, collection cascades).
- The case **MUST** distinguish *observed facts* from *assumptions* and from *normative claims*; normative language is allowed only as explicit conformance requirements (**MUST/SHOULD/MAY**).
- The case **MUST NOT** function as persuasion, branding, or “feel-good” proof. Narrative detail is permitted only insofar as it anchors measurable claims.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-PUR-01 (Chain completeness):** B,T,N,L,R appear *before* and *after* with explicit time windows and `method_id`.
- **DL-PUR-02 (Lock-in visibility):** at least one lock-in diagnosis per essentials domain is stated, or the case fails.
- **DL-PUR-03 (Non-PR):** every qualitative sentence is traceable to (a) a measurable claim, (b) an assumption declaration, or (c) a conformance requirement; otherwise flagged as storytelling.

<a id="b05ch14p01s02"></a>
## B05.CH14.P01.S02 — Scope: low R/B household (precarity), essentials (housing/energy/food/digital), work/care, debts

The scope is deliberately narrow: a low R/B household where tail risk is driven by lock-ins, fee stacks, volatility, and timing shocks.

### In-scope (binding)
- Household type: *low R/B* cohort (precarity exposure), described in aggregated terms (no identifying details).
- Essentials basket: **housing, energy, food, digital access** (and any mandatory intermediaries that gate them).
- Work and care: schedules, volatility, caregiving constraints, and the time burden of administration and disputes.
- Debts and collections: fee cascades, garnishment/attachment threats, arrears mechanics, and restructuring access.

### Out-of-scope (explicit)
- Macro claims (inflation, GDP effects), political campaigning, or general moral judgement.
- “Lifestyle optimisation” advice, consumer-choice preaching, or blaming/valorising the household.
- Individual medical outcomes as performance KPIs (experience signals may be included later, but not as success metrics).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Scope boundaries **MUST** be stated at the start of the case file.
- Any deviation **MUST** be listed as a `scope_exception` with rationale and measurement impact.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-SCP-01 (Essentials coverage):** housing/energy/food/digital each appear at least once in the lock-in map or fee-stack inventory.
- **DL-SCP-02 (Precarity relevance):** the baseline includes at least one volatility or timing mechanism affecting N or L (hours variation, pay timing, clawback risk, administrative friction).

## B05.CH14.P01.S03 — Method: standardized case template + measurement linkage (definitionset_id, method_id, uncertainty)

A day-in-the-life case is a **standardized specimen**, comparable across regions and time. It is therefore defined by a template and a measurement linkage.

### Required case fields (template)
- `definitionset_id` — the normative definitions in force for B,T,N,L,R; lock-in; E-layers; dispute durations.
- `method_id` — calculation method + data handling + aggregation rules; versioned.
- `case_mode` ∈ {`observed_after`, `simulated_after`}
  - If `simulated_after`, the intervention assumptions **MUST** be enumerated and uncertainty widened.
- `time_window_pre` and `time_window_post` — periods used; must be comparable.
- `data_sources[]` — evidence classes (contracts, bills, paystubs, ledgers); may be redacted, but must remain auditable in class.
- `uncertainty_band` — for each headline output (at minimum for R and L; include drivers of uncertainty).

### Minimum method sequence
1) Capture baseline observables (B,T,N,L,R) and contract inventory.
2) Produce the lock-in map (surfaces, switching costs, penalties, identity/eligibility gates).
3) Identify fee stacks / toll layers (E-like layers) and attribute them to intermediaries/contracts.
4) Run shock moments (small, time, price, administrative) to expose tail behaviour.
5) Apply the OBA intervention set (rails, public options/commons exits, contestability/time bounds).
6) Recompute post-case outputs (B,T,N,L,R + lock-in metrics + dispute durations).
7) Emit the case dashboard with `method_id` + uncertainty bands (link to P09).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The case **MUST** be reproducible from the declared template fields and `method_id` (same inputs → same outputs).
- The case **MUST** surface uncertainty rather than bury it (wide bands are acceptable; hidden bands are non-conform).
- The case **MUST** maintain privacy by aggregation/redaction; however, privacy **MUST NOT** be used to avoid disclosing the *class* of evidence or the logic of computation.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-MTH-01 (Template completeness):** `definitionset_id`, `method_id`, `case_mode`, time windows, evidence classes, and uncertainty are present.
- **DL-MTH-02 (Reproducibility):** a reader can re-derive headline numbers from the declared method, or the case fails as a conformance object.
- **DL-MTH-03 (Observed vs simulated clarity):** if post is simulated, all assumptions are explicitly listed and tagged.

## B05.CH14.P01.S04 — Fail-closed: a case without lock-in diagnosis and without measurable “before/after” claims is non-conform storytelling

This chapter uses a hard rule: the case exists to bind lifeworld claims to measurable structure. If that binding is absent, the artefact is disqualified.

### Fail-closed rules (binding)
A day-in-the-life case is **NON-CONFORM** if any of the following holds:
- No explicit *before/after* values for **B,T,N,L,R** are provided (with time windows and `method_id`).
- No lock-in diagnosis is provided for essentials (housing/energy/digital/debt) *as concrete mechanisms* (penalties, arrears threats, identity lockouts, switching costs, eligibility gates).
- The case includes “after improvement” claims without measurable deltas and without uncertainty bands.
- The artefact blurs observed facts and assumptions (e.g., simulation presented as observation).
- The artefact omits minimal evidence classes (contracts/bills/paystubs or declared equivalents) while still asserting fee stacks or toll layers.

### Remedy requirement (binding)
- If a case is flagged non-conform, it **MUST** be rewritten by adding missing measurements, lock-in mapping, and method linkage; narrative polishing is explicitly insufficient.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-FC-01 (Measurement gate):** missing B,T,N,L,R → automatic NON-CONFORM.
- **DL-FC-02 (Lock-in gate):** missing lock-in map → automatic NON-CONFORM.
- **DL-FC-03 (Delta gate):** “after” claims without deltas + uncertainty → automatic NON-CONFORM.
