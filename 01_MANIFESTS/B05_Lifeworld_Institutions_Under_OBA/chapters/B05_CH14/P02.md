# B05.CH14.P02 — Baseline profile (pre-OBA): inputs and observables

This paragraph specifies the **baseline (pre-OBA)** profile for Day-in-the-Life Case I. The baseline is not an anecdote: it is the **minimum observable input surface** required to compute **B, T, N, L, R**, diagnose lock-in, and later make **measurable before/after claims**. The baseline therefore declares cohort/context, income structure and volatility, cashflow timing and administrative friction, and the essentials basket with contract constraints.

---

<a id="b05ch14p02s01"></a>
## B05.CH14.P02.S01 — Cohort and context: region, household type, employment status, dependencies (aggregated)

The case begins with a cohort/context declaration. It is aggregated and non-identifying, but must be concrete enough to interpret prices, access options, and institutional frictions.

### Required fields (template)
- `region_class` — e.g., {urban, peri_urban, rural} + relevant jurisdiction layer(s) (municipality/region/country).
- `household_type` — e.g., {single_adult, single_parent, two_adults, multigenerational} + number of dependents.
- `employment_status` — e.g., {part_time, variable_hours, temp_agency, self_employed_micro, unemployed, disability_partial} (one or more).
- `dependency_profile` — dependents, care burden, disability constraints (categorical; no sensitive personal detail).
- `housing_tenure_pre` — e.g., {private_rent, public_rent_waitlist, informal, owner_with_mortgage}.
- `baseline_access` — whether the household has stable access to essentials at baseline (yes/no per domain).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The case **MUST** use aggregated descriptors; it **MUST NOT** include personally identifying details.
- The case **MUST** specify jurisdiction layers relevant to disputes, benefits, and contract enforcement (where “block power” can be exercised).
- The case **MUST** state any “context assumptions” used later (e.g., typical tariffs, median rents) and mark them as assumptions.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-CTX-01 (Non-identifying concreteness):** region + household type + dependency profile are present without identifying markers.
- **DL-CTX-02 (Jurisdiction clarity):** at least one jurisdiction layer is stated that determines benefits/disputes/enforcement.

## B05.CH14.P02.S02 — BRUTO (B): income mix and volatility (hours, variable shifts, small side jobs)

The baseline must reveal not only *level* of gross income, but also *structure*: volatility, fragmentation, and exposure to schedule capture.

### Required fields (template)
- `B_components[]` — income sources with categories: {wage_primary, wage_secondary, tips, gig_platform, temp_agency, cash_informal_declared, benefits_gross, alimony_child_support, other}.
- `B_timebase` — {weekly, biweekly, monthly} + pay schedule if irregular.
- `hours_pattern` — {fixed, variable, on_call} + typical range (min/max or bands).
- `volatility_markers` — e.g., shift cancellations, seasonal drops, platform demand variance.
- `side_job_dependency` — whether the household relies on micro side jobs to close gaps (yes/no + rough share band).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- **B** **MUST** be represented as a distribution (range/bands) when volatility is material; a single point estimate is permitted only if volatility is declared low.
- The baseline **MUST** record at least one volatility mechanism if the cohort is “low R/B”.
- The baseline **MUST** expose whether income is contingent on gatekeepers (platform ratings, agency assignment, employer scheduling discretion).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-B-01 (Mix completeness):** at least one B component + timebase are present.
- **DL-B-02 (Volatility visibility):** either (a) volatility markers exist or (b) an explicit “low volatility” declaration exists with rationale.
- **DL-B-03 (Gate dependency):** if platform/agency/on-call appears, the dependency mechanism is stated.

## B05.CH14.P02.S03 — NETTO (N) and T: withholdings, timing, clawback risk, administrative friction

Net income (N) is not only a tax outcome; it is a **cashflow reality** shaped by timing (T), withholding, clawback exposure, and administrative friction.

### Required fields (template)
- `withholdings_profile` — taxes, social contributions, mandatory deductions (categories + rough bands).
- `net_timing_profile` — pay arrival timing, delays, and mismatches versus bill due dates.
- `clawback_risk` — presence of benefits/credits with retroactive correction risk (yes/no + category).
- `admin_friction_markers` — forms burden, verification loops, missed deadlines, call-wait times, documentation requirements.
- `error_exposure` — likelihood of wrong decisions and time-to-correction (qualitative + if available, baseline observed durations).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The baseline **MUST** represent **N** together with **T**: if N is stated without timing, the profile is incomplete.
- The baseline **MUST** state whether the household faces **clawback** risk or retroactive corrections; silence is non-conform.
- Administrative friction **MUST** be treated as a measurable burden class (time + failure probability), not as “personal inconvenience”.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-N-01 (N+T coupling):** N and at least one timing marker are present.
- **DL-T-01 (Clawback clarity):** clawback risk is explicitly declared (present/absent) with category if present.
- **DL-AF-01 (Friction evidence class):** admin friction markers are listed with at least one measurable proxy (time spent, number of steps, delay bands).

## B05.CH14.P02.S04 — LASTEN (L): essentials basket + contract lock-in indicators (penalties, exclusivity, arrears)

The essentials basket (L) is where lifeworld extraction manifests: fee stacks, penalties, and lock-in clauses that convert vulnerability into block power.

### Required fields (template)
For each essentials domain `{housing, energy, food, digital}`:
- `provider_type` — {private, public, coop, informal} and whether a public option exists.
- `contract_features` — term length, renewal, early termination conditions, deposit/advance payment, bundled obligations.
- `price_components[]` — base price + fees + surcharges + penalties (classified).
- `arrears_state` — {none, occasional, chronic} + triggers (missed pay timing, price shock).
- `lock_in_indicators[]` (minimum set)
  - penalties / early termination fees,
  - exclusivity / bundling / “must buy” ties,
  - disconnection/eviction threats and processes,
  - switching costs (financial + administrative + identity),
  - eligibility gates (documentation, credit scoring, blacklisting).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The baseline **MUST** provide an itemized essentials basket; a single “living costs” lump sum is non-conform.
- The baseline **MUST** record at least one lock-in indicator per essentials domain, or explicitly state “no lock-in detected” with supporting contract evidence class.
- The baseline **MUST** separate *price* from *penalty* and from *fee stack* layers; bundling that hides fees is itself a reportable indicator.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL-L-01 (Basket completeness):** housing/energy/food/digital all represented with price components.
- **DL-LI-01 (Lock-in per domain):** at least one lock-in indicator exists for each domain, or a justified “none detected” statement exists.
- **DL-ARR-01 (Arrears visibility):** arrears/disconnection/eviction exposure is declared for at least housing and energy.
