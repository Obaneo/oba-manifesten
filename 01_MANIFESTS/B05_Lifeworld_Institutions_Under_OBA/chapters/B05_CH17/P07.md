# B05.CH17.P07 — Data and evidence minimum per sector case (machine-readable)

This paragraph specifies the **data and evidence minimum** required for any sector case to be reusable, auditable, and machine-bindable.
Sector cases must not rely on bespoke datasets or hidden analyst judgment. They must emit a minimal set of standardized datasets,
identifiers, and method bindings—while enforcing strict privacy rails (aggregation, suppression, join-safety). Microdata publication is
prohibited.

---

<a id="b05ch17p07s01"></a>
## B05.CH17.P07.S01 — Minimal datasets: pricing tables, contract templates, capacity/backlog, incident logs, cohort aggregates

Each sector case must expose the minimum datasets required to prove bottlenecks, E sources, commons option design, and lifeworld effects.

### Required dataset classes (minimum set)
- `pricing_tables`:
  - itemized tariffs/price lists, fee schedules, penalty schedules (where applicable)
- `contract_templates`:
  - standard contract forms and clauses used in the sector (leases, service agreements, platform terms, insurance policies)
- `capacity_and_backlog`:
  - capacity metrics, utilization proxies, backlog/waitlist/queue measures (bands acceptable)
- `incident_logs`:
  - service outages, safety incidents, billing incidents, identity lockouts (sector-specific)
- `cohort_aggregates`:
  - aggregated cohort-level metrics for L components, tail events, time friction proxies, dispute durations
- `procurement_records_min` (when commons option exists):
  - tender criteria, vendor selection rationale, change order counts (aggregated), conflict checks (categorical)

### Required dataset fields (common)
For each dataset:
- `dataset_id`
- `dataset_class`
- `time_window`
- `source_class` (public record, operator log, survey aggregate, modeled proxy)
- `update_cadence`
- `quality_notes` + `uncertainty_notes`
- `privacy_tier` + suppression rules

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Minimal datasets **MUST** be sufficient to verify: pricing/fees, contract constraints, bottleneck proxies, incident rates, and cohort
  outcomes.
- Datasets **MUST** be versioned and time-bounded; snapshot ambiguity is non-conform.
- Where measurement is proxy-based, the dataset **MUST** include uncertainty notes and method binding (S03).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-DATA-01 (Coverage):** pricing_tables, contract_templates, capacity_and_backlog, incident_logs, and cohort_aggregates all exist.
- **SC-DATA-02 (Time windows):** each dataset declares a time_window and update cadence.
- **SC-DATA-03 (Uncertainty notes):** proxy-based datasets include uncertainty notes.

<a id="b05ch17p07s02"></a>
## B05.CH17.P07.S02 — Identifiers: actor_id, position_id, contract_id, flow_id, service_id, commons_id

Machine-bindability requires stable identifiers. Sector cases must not be “PDF logic”; they must be linkable across datasets and logs.

### Required identifiers (template; Codex-aligned)
- `actor_id`:
  - entity or role-holder identifier (providers, operators, auditors; not individuals in microdata form)
- `position_id`:
  - sector position identifiers (landlord, network operator, platform gate, insurer, licensor)
- `contract_id`:
  - contract/template instance identifiers (or template IDs + version)
- `flow_id`:
  - ΔM channel flow identifiers (funding instruments, transfers)
- `service_id`:
  - service units/offerings identifiers (tariff product, care pathway, identity service)
- `commons_id`:
  - commons/public option instance identifiers

### Identifier properties (required)
- `id_namespace`
- `id_version`
- `linkability_rules` (what can join to what under privacy rails)
- `deprecation_rules` (how ids are retired or replaced)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Identifiers **MUST** be stable across time windows and datasets; ad hoc names are non-conform.
- Identifier linkage **MUST** respect privacy rails; join safety is mandatory (see S04).
- Contract identifiers **MUST** include versioning; “current contract” is non-conform.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-ID-01 (Presence):** all six identifiers exist in the sector case schema.
- **SC-ID-02 (Versioning):** contract_id and service_id include template/version references.
- **SC-ID-03 (Join rules):** linkability rules exist and reference privacy rails.

## B05.CH17.P07.S03 — Method binding: definitionset_id, method_id, benchmark versions, uncertainty reporting

Sector cases must bind metrics and classifications to versioned methods and benchmarks, so claims are reproducible and comparable.

### Required method binding fields (template)
- `definitionset_id` (canonical definitions in force)
- `method_id` (calculation rules and aggregation rules; versioned)
- `benchmark_versions[]`:
  - which benchmark sets are used (pricing, capacity thresholds, incident definitions, cohort definitions)
- `uncertainty_reporting`:
  - bands or intervals per metric class
  - rules for proxy metrics and estimation
- `revision_policy`:
  - how restatements occur when methods or data are corrected
- `calculation_trace_ref` (where applicable):
  - reproducible trace or declared equivalent

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Any metric output **MUST** reference method_id and benchmark versions; otherwise it is non-conform.
- Uncertainty reporting **MUST** be explicit for proxy and estimated values; hiding uncertainty is non-conform.
- Restatement policy **MUST** exist; unversioned silent edits are non-conform.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-MTH-01 (Binding):** definitionset_id and method_id exist in the sector case.
- **SC-MTH-02 (Benchmark versions):** benchmarks are versioned and referenced.
- **SC-MTH-03 (Restatement):** revision policy exists and is loggable.

<a id="b05ch17p07s04"></a>
## B05.CH17.P07.S04 — Privacy rails: aggregation, suppression, join-safety; microdata publication prohibited

Sector cases must be auditable without becoming surveillance regimes. Privacy rails are constitutive: they constrain what data can be
stored, joined, and published.

### Required privacy rails (minimum set)
- `aggregation_rules`:
  - minimum cell sizes, cohort grouping rules, time window aggregation
- `suppression_rules`:
  - suppression of small cells, rare event suppression, top-coding where needed
- `join_safety_rules`:
  - allowed joins only (e.g., contract_id ↔ pricing tables; service_id ↔ incident logs) under k-anonymity-like constraints (method-defined)
  - prohibition of joins that enable re-identification
- `microdata_prohibition`:
  - explicit prohibition of publishing household-level or person-level microdata
- `access_tiers`:
  - public aggregates vs restricted audit access (still not microdata; evidence packs remain class-based)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Microdata publication **MUST NOT** occur. Sector cases that require microdata are NON-CONFORM.
- Join safety **MUST** be enforced by design; “we promise not to reidentify” is non-conform.
- Any expansion of data collection **MUST** pass a necessity test and be logged; data maximalism is a failure mode (P10).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-PRV-01 (Rails present):** aggregation, suppression, join-safety rules exist and are referenced.
- **SC-PRV-02 (Microdata ban):** explicit microdata prohibition exists.
- **SC-PRV-03 (Collection necessity):** additional data fields require logged justification and audit.

---

### Output format (data/evidence annex)

At minimum, each sector case **MUST** emit a machine-readable data/evidence annex with:

1) **Dataset inventory table**
- `dataset_id` | `dataset_class` | `time_window` | `update_cadence` | `source_class` | `privacy_tier` | `method_ref`

2) **Identifier table**
- `identifier` | `namespace` | `versioning` | `allowed_joins` | `deprecation_rule`

3) **Method binding table**
- `definitionset_id` | `method_id` | `benchmark_versions` | `uncertainty_rules` | `revision_policy`

4) **Privacy rails table**
- `rail` | `rule_summary` | `enforcement_mechanism` | `log_ref`

If these cannot be produced, the sector case is NON-CONFORM and not reusable.
