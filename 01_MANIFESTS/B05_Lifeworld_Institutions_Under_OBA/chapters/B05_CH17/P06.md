# B05.CH17.P06 — Template fields for governance and execution

This paragraph specifies the **governance and execution** field set required in every sector-case template instance. Sector cases are not
credible if they describe design without execution: roles, decision logs, contestability, and meta-audit must be explicit. Governance is
treated as part of the technical spec: it defines who can decide, who can benefit, who can block, and how errors/capture are detected and
corrected.

---

<a id="b05ch17p06s01"></a>
## B05.CH17.P06.S01 — Roles: mandator, executor, operator, auditor, ombuds/dispute arbiter

Each sector case must declare the minimal role set and how authority is separated to reduce capture and enable auditability.

### Required roles (template)
- `mandator`:
  - sets objectives, minima, and compatibility constraints; cannot self-execute delivery.
- `executor`:
  - implements programs/procurement and operational changes; accountable to logs and audits.
- `operator`:
  - runs day-to-day service delivery (commons option and/or regulated services); produces operational metrics.
- `auditor`:
  - verifies compliance, runs sampling, checks leakage tests, enforces auditpacks.
- `ombuds_or_dispute_arbiter`:
  - independent dispute handling, protection, and correction triggers; ensures contestability.

### Required role fields
For each role:
- `role_id` + `actor_class` (public body/co-op/private operator/etc.)
- `powers[]` (what decisions they can make)
- `constraints[]` (what they cannot do)
- `accountability_outputs[]` (what they must publish/log)
- `conflict_controls` (minimum constraints; disclosure/rotation)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Roles **MUST** be separable; combined mandator+auditor or executor+auditor without safeguards is non-conform.
- Each role **MUST** have explicit powers and constraints; implicit authority is a capture surface.
- Ombuds/dispute must be structurally independent from operators it reviews.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-GOV-01 (Role completeness):** all five roles are declared (or explicit justified merge with safeguards).
- **SC-GOV-02 (Separation):** auditor and ombuds are independent of the operator/executor they oversee.
- **SC-GOV-03 (Accountability outputs):** each role has required outputs defined.

## B05.CH17.P06.S02 — Decision logs: rules, exceptions, procurement, incidents (tamper-evident)

Sector cases must define the **decision log system**. Without logs, enforcement becomes narrative, exceptions become invisible, and vendor
capture becomes untraceable.

### Required log classes (template)
- `rule_log`:
  - rules and parameter versions; changes, rationales, effective dates.
- `exception_log`:
  - any deviation from standard rules, with justification and review date (exceptions are suspect by default).
- `procurement_log`:
  - tenders, evaluation criteria, bids (as allowed), selection rationale, conflict checks, contract changes, change orders.
- `incident_log`:
  - outages, safety incidents, service failures, data/privacy incidents; response and remediation steps.
- `audit_log`:
  - audit starts, findings, remedies, sanctions, follow-ups.

### Required log properties
For each log entry:
- `log_id`
- `timestamp`
- `actor_role_ref`
- `rule_or_contract_ref` (if applicable)
- `reason_code` (standardized category)
- `evidence_refs[]` (document classes; not necessarily content)
- `tamper_evidence` (hash/append-only or equivalent)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Decision logs **MUST** be tamper-evident and append-only (or equivalent). Editable logs are non-conform.
- Exceptions **MUST** be explicitly logged and time-bounded; perpetual exceptions are capture risk.
- Procurement must be auditable: criteria, conflicts, and change orders must be loggable.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-LOG-01 (Log presence):** all log classes exist.
- **SC-LOG-02 (Tamper evidence):** logs are append-only/tamper-evident.
- **SC-LOG-03 (Exception time bounds):** exception entries include review/sunset date.

## B05.CH17.P06.S03 — Contestability: objections/appeals, escalation, protection against retaliation

Contestability is a core OBA rail: users and providers must have defined objection and appeal paths, with time-bounded correction and
protection from retaliation.

### Required contestability fields (template)
- `objection_paths[]`:
  - who can object, how, and within what window
- `appeal_paths[]`:
  - escalation beyond the operator, to ombuds/arbiter
- `time_bounds`:
  - `t_ack`, `t_relief` (if harm risk), `t_decision`, `t_final`
- `provisional_relief_rules`:
  - when enforcement/service denial can cause irreversible harm, provisional relief must apply.
- `retaliation_protection`:
  - prohibited retaliation behaviors + sanction mechanisms + protected reporting channels.
- `publication`:
  - aggregated appeals stats (volumes, durations, reversal rates)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Contestability **MUST** be time-bounded; slow procedures are functional denial.
- Provisional relief **MUST** exist where irreversibility risk exists (eviction/disconnection/lockout/penalty escalation).
- Retaliation protection **MUST** be enforceable and logged; “policy statement” is insufficient.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-CON-01 (Time bounds):** dispute/appeal time bounds exist and have enforcement consequences.
- **SC-CON-02 (Relief):** provisional relief rules exist for high-harm domains.
- **SC-CON-03 (Stats):** aggregated appeals stats publication exists.

<a id="b05ch17p06s04"></a>
## B05.CH17.P06.S04 — Audit-on-the-auditor: meta-audits, rotation, conformance status

Because auditors can be captured, the template requires meta-audit: audits of enforcement quality, conflicts, and selective execution.

### Required meta-audit fields (template)
- `meta_audit_scope`:
  - sampling of audit decisions, conflict checks, consistency tests, selective execution checks.
- `rotation_rules`:
  - auditor rotation cadence; cooling-off periods; procurement restrictions.
- `conformance_status_system`:
  - status values ∈ {CONFORM, PARTIAL, NON_CONFORM, UNDER_REVIEW}
  - status triggers: leakage test failure, repeated exception use, selective execution, privacy breach.
- `publication_rules`:
  - aggregated publication of meta-audit results and conformance status changes (privacy-safe).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Audit-on-the-auditor **MUST** exist; without it, capture is a predictable failure mode.
- Rotation rules **MUST** be explicit; indefinite auditor-provider relationships are non-conform.
- Conformance status changes **MUST** be rule-derived, logged, and published in aggregated form.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-META-01 (Meta-audit presence):** scope and cadence exist.
- **SC-META-02 (Rotation):** rotation and conflict controls exist and are enforceable.
- **SC-META-03 (Status publication):** conformance status changes are published (aggregated) with rule/version refs.

---

### Output format (governance/execution annex)

At minimum, each sector case **MUST** emit a governance/execution annex (table(s) or structured lists) with:

1) **Roles table**
- `role` | `actor_class` | `powers` | `constraints` | `required_outputs`

2) **Logs table**
- `log_class` | `required_fields` | `tamper_evidence` | `publication_level`

3) **Contestability table**
- `path` | `t_ack` | `t_relief` | `t_decision` | `provisional_relief_rule` | `retaliation_protection`

4) **Meta-audit table**
- `meta_audit_scope` | `rotation_rule` | `status_triggers` | `publication_rule`

If these cannot be produced, the sector case is NON-CONFORM.
