# B05.CH17.P05 — ΔM channel: financing and forbidden routes

This paragraph specifies the **ΔM channel discipline** for sector cases: how money enters the sector intervention (financing), which
routes are allowed, which are forbidden, how leakage into claims/fees is detected and prevented, and how temporary programs sunset and
rollback. Sector cases often fail by smuggling in asset support, concessions, or subsidy structures that inflate claims and recreate
extraction. CH17 therefore requires explicit channel objects, tagging, leakage tests, and auditpacks bound to Codex schemas.

---

<a id="b05ch17p05s01"></a>
## B05.CH17.P05.S01 — Allowed channels: commons capex/maintenance; transition instruments with traceability

Allowed ΔM channels are those that build or maintain **commons capacity** and can be traced from source to outcome without turning into
private claim inflation.

### Allowed channel classes (minimum set)
- `commons_capex_channel`:
  - financing for building commons/public option capacity (infrastructure, facilities, core systems)
- `commons_maintenance_channel`:
  - financing for ongoing maintenance, backlog reduction, reliability capacity (ΔG continuity)
- `transition_instrument_channel`:
  - time-bounded transition instruments (loans/guarantees/purchases/subsidies) with traceability and explicit sunset
- `procurement_capacity_channel`:
  - funding for procurement and workforce capacity needed to execute commons build/maintenance

### Required fields (template; per channel)
- `flow_id`
- `source` (public/commons fund category)
- `instrument_type` ∈ {loan, guarantee, purchase, subsidy, tax_credit, reserve, swap} (Codex-aligned)
- `destination` (commons_id/service_id)
- `conditions` (bounded returns, transparency, auditability)
- `traceability_requirements`:
  - tagging rules, reporting cadence, evidence classes
- `outcome_link`:
  - service level targets, bottleneck relief proxies, lifeworld metrics linkage (P04)
- `sunset_rule_ref` (if temporary)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Allowed channels **MUST** be traceable end-to-end (flow_id → destination → outcome proxies).
- Instruments **MUST** be bounded by rules preventing claim inflation (no open-ended upside for intermediaries).
- Maintenance funding **MUST** be explicit; capex-only funding is a known failure mode (build then decay).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-DM-AL-01 (Traceability):** each channel has tagging/reporting requirements and evidence classes.
- **SC-DM-AL-02 (Outcome linkage):** each channel links to bottleneck and lifeworld metric proxies (P02/P04).
- **SC-DM-AL-03 (Maintenance present):** maintenance channel exists or is explicitly justified absent.

<a id="b05ch17p05s02"></a>
## B05.CH17.P05.S02 — Forbidden channels: asset/claim support, bubble subsidies, concessions with excess returns

Forbidden channels are routes that convert ΔM into inflated asset prices, private claims, or durable toll positions. These routes are
structurally incompatible with OBA.

### Forbidden channel classes (minimum set)
- `asset_support_channel`:
  - any program primarily supporting asset prices (housing bubbles, equity price support) rather than capacity/maintenance
- `claim_support_channel`:
  - programs that subsidize or guarantee private claims without reducing underlying bottlenecks (socialized downside, privatized upside)
- `bubble_subsidy_channel`:
  - demand stimulus in scarcity sectors that raises prices and rents
- `concession_excess_return_channel`:
  - concessions/PPP structures with excess returns, opaque terms, long lock-ins, or guaranteed margins
- `opacity_channel`:
  - off-balance vehicles, undisclosed side letters, non-auditable intermediaries

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Forbidden channels **MUST NOT** be used in a conform sector case. If present, the sector case is NON-CONFORM.
- Any structure with excess returns for gatekeepers **MUST** be treated as E-like extraction and either rewritten or replaced by commons.
- Demand stimulus in scarcity sectors **MUST** be presumed bubble-inducing unless proven otherwise with bottleneck evidence.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-DM-FB-01 (Forbidden flagging):** each channel is classified allowed/forbidden/conditional with explicit justification.
- **SC-DM-FB-02 (No concessions with excess returns):** concessions include bounded returns and auditability; otherwise NON-CONFORM.
- **SC-DM-FB-03 (No asset support):** asset support bias triggers NON-CONFORM unless removed.

<a id="b05ch17p05s03"></a>
## B05.CH17.P05.S03 — Leakage tests: where money can leak into claims/fees; prevention via tagging and audits

Leakage occurs when ΔM intended for capacity/maintenance leaks into higher claims, fee stacks, or intermediary rents. Sector cases must
define leakage tests and prevention mechanisms.

### Leakage surfaces (minimum)
- `fee_stack_leakage` (intermediaries capture ΔM via fees)
- `price_pass_through_leakage` (subsidy raises prices due to scarcity)
- `contractual_leakage` (PPP terms embed guaranteed margins, step-ups, or penalties)
- `shadow_channel_leakage` (funds routed through opaque vehicles)
- `procurement_leakage` (vendor capture, change-order inflation)

### Required leakage test fields (template)
For each leakage test:
- `leakage_test_id`
- `leakage_surface_type`
- `signal_proxy` (fee share, price spikes, take-rate growth, contract margin anomalies)
- `threshold_or_condition` (bands allowed)
- `tagging_rules` (flow tagging, invoice line-item tags, contract clause tags)
- `auditpack_ref` (minimum evidence pack to verify)
- `trigger_action`:
  - {audit_start, contract_rewrite, procurement_suspension, channel_reclassification, rollback_instrument}

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Leakage tests **MUST** exist for the main channel surfaces; absence is non-conform (ΔM becomes blind).
- Tagging **MUST** be applied at the line-item/contract level where feasible; aggregated-only tagging is insufficient for audit.
- When leakage triggers, an audit **MUST** start within time bounds and remedial actions must be logged.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-DM-LK-01 (Test coverage):** at least one leakage test exists for fee stacks and for procurement leakage (when relevant).
- **SC-DM-LK-02 (Tagging):** tagging rules exist and are bound to identifiers (flow_id/contract_id).
- **SC-DM-LK-03 (Trigger-to-action):** trigger conditions map to mandatory actions with time bounds.

## B05.CH17.P05.S04 — Sunset/rollback: how temporary programs are wound down and evaluated

Temporary programs often become permanent rent structures. Sector cases must include explicit sunset and rollback rules.

### Required sunset/rollback fields (template)
- `program_id` (for each temporary instrument)
- `start_date` / `end_date` (or end condition)
- `sunset_criteria` (what must be true to end)
- `renewal_criteria` (what must be true to extend; versioned approval)
- `rollback_plan`:
  - how to unwind without disruption,
  - how to prevent “grandfathered” excess returns
- `evaluation_pack_ref`:
  - required outputs (R/B deltas, tail stabilization, leakage test results)
- `deprecation_rules`:
  - deprecate legacy contract forms and stop new issuance

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Temporary instruments **MUST** have explicit sunset and renewal criteria; “review later” is non-conform.
- Renewal **MUST** be criteria-based and published; discretionary extensions create capture risk.
- If evaluation fails (no demonstrable L reduction or tail stabilization), scaling and renewal **MUST** be blocked (fail-closed).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-DM-SR-01 (Sunset present):** each temporary program has sunset and renewal criteria.
- **SC-DM-SR-02 (Rollback defined):** rollback plan exists and references contract deprecation.
- **SC-DM-SR-03 (Evaluation gate):** renewal depends on measurable outputs (P04) and leakage results.

---

### Output format (ΔM channel annex)

At minimum, each sector case **MUST** emit a ΔM channel annex (table(s) or structured lists) with:

1) **Channel inventory table**
- `flow_id` | `instrument_type` | `destination` | `allowed_or_forbidden` | `conditions` | `tagging_rule_ref` | `auditpack_ref` | `sunset_ref`

2) **Leakage tests table**
- `leakage_test_id` | `surface_type` | `signal_proxy` | `threshold` | `trigger_action` | `auditpack_ref`

3) **Sunset/rollback table**
- `program_id` | `sunset_criteria` | `renewal_criteria` | `rollback_plan_ref` | `evaluation_pack_ref` | `status`

If these cannot be produced, the sector case is NON-CONFORM.
