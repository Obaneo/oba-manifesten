# B05.CH17.P01 — Purpose, scope, and normative status of sector cases as templates

This paragraph defines the purpose, scope, and normative status of **sector cases as templates**. A sector case is not a report or an
essay; it is a **reusable, auditable module** that operationalizes the sector canvas into a lifeworld case format. The module is designed
to be replicated across jurisdictions and time without drifting in definitions or measurement. Narrative is permitted only as a carrier.
The truth condition is **template completeness + measurement binding**.

---

<a id="b05ch17p01s01"></a>
## B05.CH17.P01.S01 — Purpose: operationalize the sector canvas into repeatable lifeworld case formats (replicable, auditable)

The purpose is to transform sector reasoning into a **canonical object** that can be reused, compared, audited, and extended. Sector
writing tends to become either macro ideology or micro anecdotes. OBA requires neither. It requires a repeatable structure that exposes:
bottlenecks, E sources, commons/public options, caps compatibility, R/B impact, and ΔM channels—mapped to evidence and governance rails.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- A sector case **MUST** be a template-driven artefact with fixed fields and deterministic outputs.
- The case **MUST** be auditable: every claim about bottlenecks, fee stacks, lock-in, or outcomes must link to evidence classes and
  method provenance.
- The case **MUST** be comparable across implementations via stable identifiers and versioned methods (Codex binding).
- The case **MUST NOT** rely on persuasion, branding, or moral theatre; it exists to generate testable conformance claims.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-01 (Template integrity):** required field groups are present (context, bottlenecks, E sources, commons option, caps compatibility,
  R/B effects, ΔM channel, governance, evidence).
- **SC-02 (Auditability):** each field group has evidence class linkage and a method/provenance pointer.
- **SC-03 (Comparability):** the case declares `definitionset_id`, `method_id`, and benchmark versions where metrics are used.

<a id="b05ch17p01s02"></a>
## B05.CH17.P01.S02 — Scope: housing/energy/healthcare/digital (minimum), extensible to other essentials

CH17 mandates four core exemplar sectors as the minimum baseline and defines extension rules for additional sectors.

### In-scope (binding)
- Core sectors (minimum required set):
  - `housing`
  - `energy`
  - `healthcare`
  - `digital`
- Each sector case must include:
  - sector boundaries and users,
  - bottleneck mapping,
  - E source mapping,
  - commons/public option design,
  - caps compatibility mapping,
  - R/B and lifeworld metrics,
  - ΔM channel discipline,
  - governance/execution fields,
  - data/evidence minimum and privacy rails.

### Extensibility (binding constraint)
- Additional sectors may be added only via **sector-specific parameters and benchmarks** (P09), without adding new foundational
  definitions.

### Out-of-scope (explicit)
- Macro policy manifestos masquerading as sector cases.
- Surveillance-first “data solutions” that rely on microdata publication.
- Bespoke sector carve-outs that break caps compatibility/no-arbitrage.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-04 (Minimum coverage):** CH17 includes the four core sector exemplars (P08).
- **SC-05 (Extension discipline):** extensions add parameters/benchmarks only; no new base definitions are introduced.

## B05.CH17.P01.S03 — Binding: sector-canvas fields (bottlenecks, E sources, commons option, caps compatibility, R/B effect, ΔM channel) + Codex schemas/auditpacks

Sector cases are **binding**: they must instantiate the sector-canvas fields and conform to Codex schemas so they remain machine-readable
and auditable.

### Binding field groups (required)
A sector case **MUST** include, at minimum:
- `context` (sector boundaries, users, dependencies, choke points)
- `bottlenecks` (scarcity/capacity/permits/skills/infrastructure; time constraints)
- `E_sources` (fee stacks, lock-in, market power, dark patterns, clamp contracts)
- `commons_option` (public/commons alternative; governance minima; maintenance plan; procurement rails)
- `caps_compatibility` (W/I exposure mapping, incentives, no-arbitrage, remedies)
- `RB_effects` (B,T,N,L,R impacts by cohorts; tails; time friction; experience inputs as procedural signals)
- `deltaM_channel` (allowed/forbidden routes; leakage tests; sunset/rollback)
- `governance_execution` (roles, logs, contestability, audit-on-auditor)
- `data_evidence_minimum` (datasets, identifiers, method binding, privacy rails)

### Codex binding (required)
- Each sector case **MUST** reference:
  - `definitionset_id`, `method_id`
  - required identifiers (actor_id, position_id, contract_id, flow_id, service_id, commons_id)
  - auditpack references for each high-risk claim (bottleneck, E source, forbidden channel, major remedy)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Field groups **MUST** be present and non-empty; placeholders must be explicitly marked `TODO` and render the case NON-CONFORM for reuse.
- Auditpacks **MUST** be referenced for (i) E sources, (ii) forbidden channels/leakage risk, and (iii) commons option procurement and
  governance.
- Method binding **MUST** include uncertainty reporting rules for any estimated values (capacity, costs, incident rates, etc.).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-06 (Field completeness):** all binding field groups exist with at least minimal content.
- **SC-07 (Codex schema compliance):** identifiers and method binding fields exist and conform to the schema.
- **SC-08 (Auditpack linkage):** auditpack references exist for E sources, forbidden/leakage claims, and commons procurement.

<a id="b05ch17p01s04"></a>
## B05.CH17.P01.S04 — Fail-closed: a sector case without template fields and without measurement binding is not reusable and is NON-CONFORM

CH17 is explicitly FAIL-CLOSED. A sector case that cannot be reused safely must not be treated as a valid template instance.

### FAIL-CLOSED conditions (NON-CONFORM)
A sector case is **NON-CONFORM** if any of the following holds:
- Missing any binding field group (context/bottlenecks/E/commons/caps/RB/ΔM/governance/data-evidence).
- Missing `definitionset_id` or `method_id` where metrics or classifications are used.
- Claims of bottlenecks, E sources, or ΔM routes without evidence class linkage and auditpack references.
- Commons/public option described without governance minima, maintenance plan, and procurement rails.
- Time friction and tails omitted (case becomes macro-only and non-lifeworld).
- Privacy rails violated (microdata publication or join-unsafe datasets).

### Remedy requirement (binding)
- A NON-CONFORM case **MUST** be rewritten by filling missing template fields and binding measurements; narrative improvement is
  insufficient.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-FC-01 (Template gate):** missing field group → automatic NON-CONFORM.
- **SC-FC-02 (Measurement gate):** missing method binding for metrics → automatic NON-CONFORM.
- **SC-FC-03 (Evidence gate):** missing auditpack references for E/ΔM/commons procurement → automatic NON-CONFORM.
