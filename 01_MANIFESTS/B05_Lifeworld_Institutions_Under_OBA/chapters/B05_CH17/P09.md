# B05.CH17.P09 — Portability to other sectors (template extension)

This paragraph specifies how the sector-case template is extended to additional sectors without definition drift. Extension must preserve
OBA compatibility: **no new base definitions**, only sector-specific parameters, benchmarks, and evidence packs. The extension process is
a disciplined onboarding pipeline (baseline → bottleneck scan → E scan → commons design → measurement pack), with fork management for
local variants and a rollout protocol that is pilot-first and audit-gated.

---

<a id="b05ch17p09s01"></a>
## B05.CH17.P09.S01 — Extension rules: no new definitions; only sector-specific parameters/benchmarks

Template portability fails when extensions introduce new definitions, new scoring schemes, or un-auditable bespoke metrics. CH17 forbids
that.

### Extension constraints (binding)
- `no_new_definitions`:
  - extensions **MUST NOT** introduce new foundational definitions (B,T,N,L,R; E; bottleneck; commons; caps compatibility; ΔM channel).
- `parameter_only_extension`:
  - extensions **MAY** add:
    - sector-specific parameter values (thresholds, service-level minima),
    - sector-specific benchmarks (what counts as backlog, incident, wait-time),
    - sector-specific auditpacks and evidence classes.
- `schema_preservation`:
  - required field groups (P02–P07) must remain present and structurally consistent.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Any new metric **MUST** be expressible using existing definitions and method binding; otherwise it is rejected.
- Sector-specific benchmarks **MUST** be versioned and referenced; ad hoc numbers are non-conform.
- Extensions **MUST** preserve privacy rails; no sector extension can justify microdata publication.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-EXT-01 (Definition drift check):** extension introduces no new base definitions.
- **SC-EXT-02 (Versioned benchmarks):** all new benchmarks are versioned and referenced.
- **SC-EXT-03 (Schema integrity):** required template field groups remain unchanged in presence and meaning.

<a id="b05ch17p09s02"></a>
## B05.CH17.P09.S02 — Sector onboarding: baseline map, bottleneck scan, E scan, commons design, measurement pack

Onboarding is a fixed pipeline. The outcome is a reusable sector-case instance with auditable measurements and governance rails.

### Onboarding pipeline (required sequence)
1) `baseline_map`:
   - sector boundary, users, dependencies, choke points (P02.S01)
2) `bottleneck_scan`:
   - scarcity/capacity/permits/skills/infrastructure + time constraints (P02.S02)
3) `E_scan`:
   - fee stacks, lock-in, market power, dark patterns; evidence packs (P02.S03)
4) `commons_design`:
   - commons/public option with governance minima, maintenance plan, procurement rails (P02.S04)
5) `caps_compatibility_annex`:
   - UBC exposure mapping, incentive analysis, no-arbitrage constraints, remedy menu (P03)
6) `measurement_pack`:
   - lifeworld metrics annex (B,T,N,L,R; tails; time friction; experience inputs) (P04)
7) `deltaM_pack`:
   - allowed/forbidden channels, leakage tests, sunset/rollback (P05)
8) `execution_pack`:
   - roles, logs, contestability, meta-audit (P06)
9) `data_evidence_pack`:
   - minimal datasets, identifiers, method binding, privacy rails (P07)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- A sector case **MUST** complete the onboarding pipeline; skipping steps yields NON-CONFORM.
- Each pipeline stage **MUST** produce named outputs (annexes/registers) with method binding and evidence classes.
- If a commons option cannot be specified, the case must explicitly state why (and will often fail caps compatibility/no-arbitrage).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-ONB-01 (Stage completion):** all pipeline stages produce outputs.
- **SC-ONB-02 (Evidence linkage):** bottlenecks and E scans include evidence pack references.
- **SC-ONB-03 (Measurement linkage):** measurement pack references `definitionset_id` and `method_id`.

<a id="b05ch17p09s03"></a>
## B05.CH17.P09.S03 — Fork management: local variants within compatibility; naming and versioning

Local variants are expected. Fork management prevents fragmentation and incompatibility drift.

### Fork rules (required)
- `compatibility_core_unchanged`:
  - the template schema and base definitions remain unchanged.
- `variant_parameters_only`:
  - local forks may change only:
    - parameter values,
    - benchmark versions,
    - implementation details within the same field groups.
- `naming_convention` (example)
  - `sector_id::jurisdiction_id::variant_id::vX`
- `versioning`:
  - semantic versioning for:
    - template version,
    - benchmark version,
    - method_id version,
    - auditpack version.
- `merge_policy`:
  - how improvements upstream/downstream are reconciled; deprecations and restatements are logged.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Forks **MUST** be explicitly named and versioned; silent divergence is non-conform.
- Variant differences **MUST** be declared as parameter/benchmark diffs; hidden diffs are capture risk.
- Restatements **MUST** occur when methods or benchmarks change; otherwise comparisons become invalid.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-FORK-01 (Version tags):** fork includes template/method/benchmark versions.
- **SC-FORK-02 (Diff visibility):** parameter/benchmark diffs are declared.
- **SC-FORK-03 (Restatement policy):** restatement triggers exist and are loggable.

<a id="b05ch17p09s04"></a>
## B05.CH17.P09.S04 — Rollout: pilot → audit → scale → restatement; deprecate legacy contract forms

Rollout must be audit-gated. Scaling a sector intervention without measurable outputs and leakage control is forbidden.

### Rollout protocol (required)
- `pilot`:
  - limited deployment with full logging and measurement pack emission
- `audit`:
  - auditpacks applied; leakage tests run; contestability stats reviewed
- `scale`:
  - only after pilot passes measurable L reduction/tail stabilization gates
- `restatement`:
  - if methods/benchmarks change, restate results and maintain comparability
- `deprecate_legacy_contracts`:
  - stop issuance of non-conform contract templates; provide migration path and portability support

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Scaling **MUST** be blocked if the pilot does not demonstrate L reduction or tail stabilization beyond uncertainty bands.
- Legacy contract forms that embed E sources or lock-in **MUST** be deprecated with a time-bounded migration plan.
- Rollout must include privacy compliance; surveillance drift is a hard stop (P10).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SC-ROL-01 (Audit gate):** scale requires audit pass and leakage tests within thresholds.
- **SC-ROL-02 (Outcome gate):** scale requires demonstrable lifeworld deltas (P04).
- **SC-ROL-03 (Deprecation):** legacy contract deprecation plan exists with portability and gap handling.

---

### Output format (extension + rollout annex)

At minimum, the sector extension **MUST** emit an annex with:

1) **Extension declaration**
- `sector_id` | `template_version` | `added_benchmarks` | `parameter_set_ref` | `auditpack_refs` | `method_id`

2) **Fork registry**
- `variant_name` | `jurisdiction_id` | `diff_summary` | `benchmark_versions` | `status`

3) **Rollout registry**
- `phase` | `entry_criteria` | `exit_criteria` | `measured_outputs_ref` | `audit_results_ref` | `decision_log_ref`

If these cannot be produced, extension and rollout are not controlled, and the sector case is NON-CONFORM.
