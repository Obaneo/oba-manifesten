# B05.CH17.P08 — The four core cases as exemplars (housing/energy/healthcare/digital)

This paragraph specifies the **four core exemplar sector cases** required by CH17: housing, energy, healthcare, and digital. These are
not “examples” in a narrative sense; they are **reference implementations** of the sector-case template (P02–P07). Each exemplar
instantiates: bottlenecks, E sources, commons/public option design, caps compatibility, R/B effects, ΔM channels, governance/execution,
and data/evidence minimums. They serve as canonical starting points and compatibility anchors for extensions.

---

<a id="b05ch17p08s01"></a>
## B05.CH17.P08.S01 — Housing exemplar: scarcity → rent clamp → commons-housing pipeline + lock-in metrics

### Canonical chain
**Scarcity/capacity bottleneck → rent clamp/E surfaces → commons-housing supply & maintenance pipeline → lock-in collapse + L reduction**

### Template instantiation (minimum)
- **Context**
  - sector boundary: dwellings as essentials (rental + ownership interfaces), with dependencies on permitting, construction labor, finance.
- **Bottlenecks**
  - scarcity via permits/land constraints, construction capacity, renovation backlog; time as waitlists and lead-times.
- **E sources**
  - rent clamp via scarcity pricing; intermediary fees; blacklisting; deposit capture; punitive lease terms; moving friction.
- **Commons/public option**
  - commons-housing pipeline: build/renovate capacity + allocation rules + maintenance-first regime + transparent contracts.
- **Caps compatibility**
  - map landlord/holding structures and UBC; deconcentration remedies where portfolios become W-linked block power.
- **R/B effects**
  - L_housing share down; tail events dampened: arrears cascades, eviction threats, moving lock-in.
- **ΔM channel**
  - allowed: capex + maintenance for commons stock; forbidden: demand stimulus that inflates prices; leakage tests for contractor capture.
- **Key metrics (minimum)**
  - `rent_burden_share_pre/post`, `eviction_threat_incidence_pre/post`, `switching_cost_moving_band_pre/post`,
    `waitlist_time_band_pre/post`, `deposit_dispute_duration_band_pre/post`.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Housing exemplar **MUST** include explicit lock-in metrics (switching costs, penalty incidence, blacklisting risk proxies).
- Housing exemplar **MUST** forbid bubble subsidies/demand stimulus as ΔM channels.

## B05.CH17.P08.S02 — Energy exemplar: network monopoly → tariff rails → baseline access + reliability/incident discipline

### Canonical chain
**Network monopoly bottleneck → tariff opacity/fee stacks → tariff rails + baseline access → reliability discipline + disconnection tail reduction**

### Template instantiation (minimum)
- **Context**
  - sector boundary: distribution network + retail interface; dependencies on generation mix, grid maintenance, metering/IT.
- **Bottlenecks**
  - natural monopoly infrastructure; maintenance capacity; outage response capacity; peak constraints.
- **E sources**
  - opaque surcharges; advance payments; penalty ladders; disconnection threats; contract traps; switching friction.
- **Commons/public option**
  - baseline access product with transparent tariffs; dispute-safe protections; maintenance plan and reliability minima.
- **Caps compatibility**
  - map ownership/control of networks and retailers; bounded returns regime for monopoly positions.
- **R/B effects**
  - L_energy volatility down; tails dampened: arrears → disconnection cascade; shock absorption improved via baseline access.
- **ΔM channel**
  - allowed: grid maintenance/capacity upgrades; forbidden: concessions with excess returns; leakage tests for procurement/change-order abuse.
- **Key metrics (minimum)**
  - `tariff_itemization_completeness`, `outage_incidence_rate`, `restoration_time_band`,
    `disconnection_threat_incidence_pre/post`, `advance_payment_incidence_pre/post`, `billing_dispute_duration_band`.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Energy exemplar **MUST** treat reliability/incident discipline as a first-class output, not only price levels.
- Energy exemplar **MUST** enforce dispute-safe protections against disconnection tails.

## B05.CH17.P08.S03 — Healthcare exemplar: wait times/claims industry → capacity/maintenance-first + quality triangulation

### Canonical chain
**Wait-time bottleneck + claims friction → pathway clarity + capacity/maintenance-first → reduced admin toll + quality triangulation**

### Template instantiation (minimum)
- **Context**
  - sector boundary: access pathways (referrals, diagnostics, treatment), claims/insurance interfaces where applicable.
- **Bottlenecks**
  - capacity constraints (staffing, clinics, equipment), maintenance backlog, referral bottlenecks; time as queues and pathway delays.
- **E sources**
  - claims industry tolls; paperwork loops; denial-by-friction; opaque billing; gatekeeping via complexity.
- **Commons/public option**
  - baseline pathway guarantees with time bounds; standard evidence classes; maintenance-first capacity planning.
- **Caps compatibility**
  - map ownership/control of claims intermediaries and private chains; bounded returns and contract rewrite for toll positions.
- **R/B effects**
  - time friction down; tails dampened: prolonged incapacity due to delays; catastrophic admin burden spikes.
- **ΔM channel**
  - allowed: staffing/capacity build and maintenance; forbidden: perverse incentive subsidies that increase claims extraction; leakage tests for vendor capture.
- **Key metrics (minimum)**
  - `wait_time_band_pre/post` (per pathway), `referral_steps_pre/post`,
    `claims_admin_time_band_pre/post`, `decision_time_band_pre/post`,
    `incident_reporting_completeness`, `quality_triangulation_proxy`.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Healthcare exemplar **MUST** treat wait times and pathway clarity as core bottleneck outputs.
- Healthcare exemplar **MUST** use quality triangulation (multiple indicators) and forbid single-KPI optimization.

## B05.CH17.P08.S04 — Digital exemplar: platform toll/identity lockout → interop/public option + portability metrics

### Canonical chain
**Platform toll + identity choke points → interoperability + baseline public option → portability + lockout tail reduction**

### Template instantiation (minimum)
- **Context**
  - sector boundary: baseline digital identity, communications, access gateways for essentials; dependencies on platforms and standards.
- **Bottlenecks**
  - interoperability gaps; identity recovery fragility; concentrated platform control; network effects.
- **E sources**
  - subscription bundles; take-rate stacks; pay-to-be-seen/verification; lockout penalties; opaque ranking gates.
- **Commons/public option**
  - interoperable baseline services with due process; assisted/offline paths for identity recovery; portability by design.
- **Caps compatibility**
  - map platform ownership and fee extraction; bounded take rates; contestability for ranking/deactivation.
- **R/B effects**
  - L_digital down and less volatile; tails dampened: account lockout from essentials; dispute loops; forced upgrades.
- **ΔM channel**
  - allowed: commons digital infrastructure and maintenance; forbidden: subsidies that entrench monopoly platforms; leakage tests for take-rate creep.
- **Key metrics (minimum)**
  - `portability_success_rate_pre/post`, `time_to_portability_band_pre/post`,
    `lockout_incidence_pre/post`, `recovery_time_band_pre/post`,
    `effective_take_rate_band`, `deactivation_dispute_duration_band`.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Digital exemplar **MUST** treat identity recovery and due process as constitutive; portability without recovery is cosmetic.
- Digital exemplar **MUST** measure effective take rates (all mandatory layers), not headline fees.

---

### Output format (exemplar conformance matrix)

At minimum, CH17 **MUST** emit an exemplar matrix (table or structured list) with:

- `sector_id` | `primary_bottlenecks` | `primary_E_sources` | `commons_option_core` | `caps_compatibility_focus` |
  `RB_primary_L_component` | `tail_events` | `deltaM_allowed` | `deltaM_forbidden` | `key_metrics[]`

If exemplar cases are described without template instantiation and key metrics, they become narrative and are NON-CONFORM.
