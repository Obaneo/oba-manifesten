# B05.CH15.P11 — Measurable “before/after” outputs (case dashboard)

This paragraph specifies the **case dashboard** for CH15: the minimum measurable outputs required to claim a valid “before/after”
middle-household Day-in-the-Life case. The dashboard binds narrative to measurement and prevents cosmetic “normality” claims. Outputs are
reported with `method_id`, uncertainty bands, and (where applicable) aggregated/survey-based provenance for time metrics. If the
dashboard cannot be emitted, the case is **NON-CONFORM** under CH15 fail-closed rules.

---

<a id="b05ch15p11s01"></a>
## B05.CH15.P11.S01 — B,T,N,L,R: median values + uncertainty bands + method_id

The primary dashboard layer reports the B→T→N→L→R chain, pre and post, with explicit uncertainty and method provenance. For CH15, the
default summary statistic is **median** (middle-household case), not tail percentiles.

### Required fields (template)
- `definitionset_id`
- `method_id` (versioned; links to calculation rules)
- `time_window_pre`, `time_window_post`
- `case_mode` ∈ {`observed_after`, `simulated_after`}
- `B_pre_median`, `B_post_median` (value + unit + timebase)
- `T_pre`, `T_post` (timing/friction representation; see below)
- `N_pre_median`, `N_post_median`
- `L_pre_median`, `L_post_median`
- `R_pre_median`, `R_post_median` where `R = N − L`
- `RB_pre_median`, `RB_post_median` where `RB = R / B`
- `uncertainty_band` for each headline output (at minimum: L, R, RB)

### Timing / friction representation (T)
`T` **MUST** be represented as either:
- `T_components[]` (recommended): {pay_arrival_delay_band, bill_due_mismatch_band, clawback_probability_band, admin_delay_band}, or
- `T_index` (allowed): documented composite with disclosed component weights.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The dashboard **MUST** include `method_id` and time windows; numbers without provenance are non-conform.
- The dashboard **MUST** report uncertainty bands (at minimum for L, R, RB); point estimates without bands are non-conform.
- `R` and `RB` **MUST** be derivable from declared N and L (or explicitly state adjustments).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-KPI-01 (Provenance):** `definitionset_id` + `method_id` + time windows present.
- **DL15-KPI-02 (Banding):** uncertainty bands present for L, R, RB.
- **DL15-KPI-03 (Derivability):** R equals N−L within declared rounding/uncertainty rules.

<a id="b05ch15p11s02"></a>
## B05.CH15.P11.S02 — L components: housing/energy/childcare/digital/mobility shares; volatility

CH15 requires decomposition of L into key components and explicit volatility indicators. The middle household’s failure mode is often
“stable income, unstable costs”.

### Required metrics (minimum set)
- Component shares (median, pre/post):
  - `L_housing_share`
  - `L_energy_share`
  - `L_childcare_share` (if applicable)
  - `L_digital_share`
  - `L_mobility_share`
  - `L_insurance_share` (if applicable)
  - `L_debt_service_share` (if applicable)
- Volatility indicators (pre/post):
  - `reset_exposure_markers` (mortgage rate reset, contract renewal/indexation exposure)
  - `penalty_incidence` (late fees, cancellation penalties, fine cascades; aggregated)
  - `surprise_charge_incidence` (opaque surcharges; aggregated)
  - `maintenance_spike_frequency` and `maintenance_spike_severity_band` (if homeowner)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- L decomposition **MUST** be component-level; a single lump sum is non-conform.
- Volatility **MUST** be measured as proxies (reset exposure, surprise charges, penalty incidence), not feelings.
- If a component is not applicable (e.g., childcare), it **MUST** be explicitly marked `N/A` with rationale.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-LD-01 (Component coverage):** at least housing/energy/digital/mobility shares are present; others are present or explicitly N/A.
- **DL15-LD-02 (Volatility visibility):** at least two volatility indicators are present pre/post.
- **DL15-LD-03 (Opacity tagging):** surprise charges/opaque surcharges are either measured or explicitly declared absent with evidence class.

## B05.CH15.P11.S03 — Time metrics: schedule predictability, admin-time burden, commute variance (aggregated/survey-based)

Time is a first-class output in CH15. Because time measures may rely on aggregated or survey-based inputs, provenance must be explicit.

### Required time metrics (minimum set, pre/post)
- `schedule_notice_time_band`
- `last_minute_change_frequency_band`
- `cancellation_or_mandatory_overtime_incidence_band` (if relevant)
- `admin_time_band` (forms/calls/appointments/disputes; weekly or monthly)
- `admin_steps_count_band` (for key processes: claims/disputes/switching; optional but recommended)
- `commute_time_band`
- `commute_variance_band` (reliability proxy)

### Provenance fields (required for time metrics)
- `time_measurement_mode` ∈ {observed_log, diary_estimate, survey_aggregated, modeled_proxy}
- `time_sample_notes` (aggregated; sample size band or methodology pointer)
- `uncertainty_band_time` (at minimum for admin_time and commute_variance)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Time metrics **MUST** declare provenance (`time_measurement_mode`); otherwise they are non-conform.
- Commute **MUST** include variance (reliability). Average-only commute time is non-conform.
- Admin time **MUST** be reported; omission hides a major toll layer.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-TM-01 (Provenance):** time_measurement_mode exists.
- **DL15-TM-02 (Variance):** commute_variance exists (even coarse band).
- **DL15-TM-03 (Admin time):** admin_time exists and has uncertainty band.

<a id="b05ch15p11s04"></a>
## B05.CH15.P11.S04 — Shock absorption: time-to-recovery, arrears/late payments, use of fallback commons

This layer evaluates whether the “after” case improves recovery from typical middle-household risk events (P04) without drifting into
precarity.

### Required metrics (minimum set, pre/post)
For each risk event `{health_shock_temporary, work_transition, price_shock_bundle, care_shock}`:
- `time_to_recovery_band` (time until L and time metrics return to baseline bands)
- `arrears_or_late_payment_incidence` (housing, energy, childcare; aggregated)
- `buffer_or_insurance_use` (present/absent + category; not moralized)
- `fallback_commons_use` (present/absent + category)
- `dispute_or_claim_decision_time_band` (where pathways are involved)
- `cascade_indicator` (present/absent): whether fees/penalties compound the event

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Shock absorption **MUST** be evaluated using the standardized risk events (P04); custom “best case” events are non-conform.
- Recovery claims **MUST** include time-to-recovery bands and must declare whether arrears/late payments were avoided.
- If fallback commons is invoked, it **MUST** be operationally evidenced (access-time band + activation rule).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-ABS-01 (Event coverage):** all four risk events are included or explicitly marked not applicable with justification.
- **DL15-ABS-02 (Recovery metric):** time_to_recovery exists pre/post for at least two events.
- **DL15-ABS-03 (No tail regression):** arrears/late payment incidence does not increase post (FAIL-CLOSED if it does).

---

### Output format (dashboard schema)

At minimum, the case **MUST** emit the dashboard in machine-parseable structure (table(s) or structured lists) with:

1) **Headline chain table**
- `window` | `B_median` | `T` | `N_median` | `L_median` | `R_median` | `RB_median` | `uncertainty_L` | `uncertainty_R` | `method_id`

2) **L decomposition table**
- `component` | `share_pre` | `share_post` | `volatility_proxy_pre` | `volatility_proxy_post` | `notes`

3) **Time metrics table**
- `metric` | `pre_band` | `post_band` | `delta` | `measurement_mode` | `uncertainty_band`

4) **Shock absorption table**
- `risk_event_type` | `time_to_recovery` | `arrears_or_late_payment` | `fallback_commons_use` | `decision_time_band` | `cascade_indicator`

If any of these tables cannot be produced, the case is **NON-CONFORM**.
