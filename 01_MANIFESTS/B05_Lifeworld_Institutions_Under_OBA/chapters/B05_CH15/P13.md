# B05.CH15.P13 — Residual frictions (honesty about what remains)

This paragraph specifies the **residual frictions** that remain even after the OBA intervention set is applied in the middle-household
case. Its function is anti-PR: it prevents CH15 from becoming an unfalsifiable “normality restored” story. Residual frictions must be
surfaced, measured where possible, and routed into redesign triggers, minima enforcement, and fallback mechanisms. If the case hides
residual volatility or time capture, it is non-conform.

---

<a id="b05ch15p13s01"></a>
## B05.CH15.P13.S01 — Transition costs: reskilling, temporary wait times, infrastructure rebuild

Even when rails exist, transitions consume time, coordination, and sometimes money. Middle households often fail through “temporary” dips
that become persistent due to slow pathways.

### Residual transition frictions (minimum inventory)
- `reskilling_transition_costs`:
  - time-to-entry band, time-to-completion band, income dip risk band, care compatibility markers.
- `temporary_wait_times`:
  - childcare placement, healthcare pathways, public transport improvements, housing mobility support—wait time bands.
- `infrastructure_rebuild_lags`:
  - time-to-service-level improvements for mobility, childcare capacity, digital baseline services.
- `coordination_load`:
  - number of concurrent processes required (switching, applications, disputes, onboarding).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Transition frictions **MUST** be reported as explicit bands (cash/time/steps) rather than vague warnings.
- If wait times remain, the case **MUST** specify **gap handling** (provisional access, temporary supports, continuity rules).
- Transition frictions that are structurally exclusionary **MUST** be flagged for remedy (moving support, interim services, accelerated
  pathways).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-TRN-01 (Bands present):** at least two transition friction categories are reported with time bands.
- **DL15-TRN-02 (Gap handling):** each significant wait time has a declared continuity/protection mechanism.
- **DL15-TRN-03 (Exclusion flag):** frictions exceeding baseline feasibility are flagged with remedy candidates.

<a id="b05ch15p13s02"></a>
## B05.CH15.P13.S02 — Regional variation: differences in pace/quality; mitigate via minima and escalation

Middle-household experience can diverge sharply by region due to service capacity and local execution. This is a legitimacy risk if OBA
becomes a postcode lottery.

### Residual variation surfaces (minimum)
- `pace_variation`:
  - rollout speed differences (childcare capacity, transport reliability, digital baseline availability).
- `quality_variation`:
  - service level differences (incident rates, wait times, dispute durations).
- `local_discretion_variation`:
  - differences in proof burden, enforcement aggressiveness, and appeal outcomes.

### Mitigation rails (required)
- `minima_standards`:
  - baseline service minima for each domain (coverage, wait times, dispute time bounds).
- `comparative_publication`:
  - aggregated cross-region metrics to expose variance (privacy-safe).
- `escalation_protocol`:
  - thresholds for intervention: audit, contract rewrite, capacity injection, or public option expansion.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Regional variation **MUST** be measured and published in aggregated form for key proxies (wait times, dispute durations, incident rates).
- Variance beyond declared tolerance **MUST** trigger escalation; explanation without correction is non-conform.
- Minima **MUST** be enforceable (not aspirational targets).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-REG-01 (Variance visibility):** cross-region metrics exist for at least two domains.
- **DL15-REG-02 (Minima):** declared minima exist and are linked to enforcement mechanisms.
- **DL15-REG-03 (Escalation):** variance triggers route into audit/remedy actions.

## B05.CH15.P13.S03 — Cultural adaptation: status norms and consumption patterns shift slowly

Even when costs and time burdens decline, cultural adaptation can lag: status norms, consumption scripts, and institutional expectations
shift slowly and can generate new frictions or resistance.

### Adaptation surfaces (minimum)
- `status_norm_shift` (prestige attached to scarcity signals vs competence/contribution signals)
- `consumption_pattern_shift` (bundle creep decline; more stability-driven choices)
- `institutional_expectation_shift` (schools, employers, providers adapting to new rails)
- `new_gatekeeping_risk` (credentials/networks as alternative status arenas)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Cultural adaptation statements **MUST** be labeled as qualitative signals, not causal proof or KPIs.
- If cultural adaptation generates new exclusionary behaviour (credential/network gatekeeping), it **MUST** route to monitoring/audit
  rails (link P07.S04 and P14).
- OBA **MUST NOT** rely on cultural virtue narratives to justify missing structural deltas.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-CUL-01 (Label discipline):** cultural observations are tagged as qualitative signals.
- **DL15-CUL-02 (Routing):** any new gatekeeping surfaces link to a mitigation rail (contestability/portability/audit).

## B05.CH15.P13.S04 — Risk: “the middle class feels nothing” if L does not visibly fall → need transparent case dashboards

A core legitimacy risk for CH15 is perceptual: middle households will not credit OBA if everyday costs and time burdens do not visibly
improve. This is not a marketing problem; it is an evidence problem. If the dashboard does not show visible deltas, either the rails are
insufficient or the measurement is incomplete.

### Legitimacy mechanism (explicit)
- If `L_share` and `L_volatility` remain effectively unchanged, and if time proxies do not improve, the household experiences OBA as
  “nothing happened”, even if macro indicators improve.
- This generates susceptibility to counter-narratives and undermines adoption of rails.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The chapter **MUST** publish transparent dashboards (P11) with clear deltas and uncertainty bands; opacity is a legitimacy breach.
- If visible deltas are not achieved (beyond uncertainty bands), the case **MUST** treat it as a design insufficiency and route to
  redesign (P14 FAIL-CLOSED logic), not to narrative explanation.
- Communication **MUST** remain non-PR: it may summarize measurements, but must not substitute sentiment for deltas.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-LEG-01 (Dashboard publication):** dashboards exist and are linkable to method_id and uncertainty.
- **DL15-LEG-02 (Visible delta check):** if deltas are within uncertainty (no visible improvement), a redesign trigger is raised.
- **DL15-LEG-03 (No narrative substitution):** absence of deltas cannot be “explained away” as success.

---

### Output format (residual friction register)

At minimum, the case **MUST** emit a residual friction register (table or structured list) with:

- `friction` | `domain` | `severity_band` | `who_is_affected` | `measurement_proxy` | `mitigation_or_gap_handling` | `redesign_trigger`

If residual frictions are not disclosed, the case becomes unfalsifiable and is non-conform.
