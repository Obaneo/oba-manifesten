# B05.CH15.P02 — Baseline profile (pre-OBA): income mix and time structure

This paragraph specifies the **baseline (pre-OBA)** profile for Day-in-the-Life Case II (middle household). The baseline is not an
anecdote: it is the **minimum observable input surface** required to compute **B, T, N, L, R**, diagnose frictions (lock-ins, time
capture, administrative tolls), and later make **measurable before/after claims**. The baseline therefore declares income structure and
predictability, cashflow timing and clawback exposure, the household time budget, and the essentials basket including debt service.

---

<a id="b05ch15p02s01"></a>
## B05.CH15.P02.S01 — BRUTO (B): wages + variable (bonus/overtime), predictability, contract type

The baseline must reveal not only the level of gross income, but its **structure**: contract type, predictability, and variable
components that shape volatility and time trade-offs.

### Required fields (template)
- `employment_contracts[]` with, per earner:
  - `contract_type` ∈ {permanent, fixed_term, temp_agency, self_employed, hybrid}
  - `hours_mode` ∈ {fixed, variable, on_call}
  - `base_wage_timebase` ∈ {hourly, monthly, annual}
  - `base_hours_band` (if hourly/variable)
- `B_components[]`:
  - `wage_base`
  - `bonus_variable` (if applicable)
  - `overtime_pay` (if applicable)
  - `other_income` (side work, dividends small, etc., if materially present)
- `predictability_markers`:
  - notice time band for schedules (if variable),
  - bonus/overtime variability band,
  - probability of “hours dip” events (coarse).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- **B** **MUST** be represented as a distribution (range/bands) when variable components are material; a single point estimate is
  permitted only if variability is declared low with rationale.
- The baseline **MUST** record the contract type and any schedule capture mechanism (penalties for refusal, rating impacts, implicit
  overtime expectations) when present.
- Variable income **MUST** be separated from base wage (bonus/overtime cannot be merged into one “salary” number).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-B-01 (Contract clarity):** at least one contract_type + hours_mode is declared per earner.
- **DL15-B-02 (Variable separation):** bonus/overtime (if present) are itemized separately from base wage.
- **DL15-B-03 (Predictability proxy):** at least one predictability marker is present (or explicit “high predictability” declaration).

## B05.CH15.P02.S02 — NETTO (N) and T: withholdings, allowances, timing, clawback risk

Net income is a cashflow reality shaped by withholding, allowances, timing mismatches, and the risk of retroactive corrections (clawback)
in tax/benefits interfaces.

### Required fields (template)
- `withholdings_profile`:
  - tax and social contributions categories (bands acceptable),
  - mandatory deductions (pensions, union fees if mandatory, etc., where relevant).
- `allowances_and_credits`:
  - allowances/credits that affect N (categories; do not require disclosure of sensitive details).
- `net_timing_profile`:
  - pay arrival timing and regularity,
  - mismatch vs bill due dates (coarse bands).
- `clawback_risk`:
  - presence of retroactive corrections (yes/no) + category (tax credit, childcare subsidy, benefit adjustment).
- `admin_friction_markers`:
  - documentation requirements, verification loops, response delays.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The baseline **MUST** represent **N together with T**: N without timing/mismatch is incomplete.
- The baseline **MUST** explicitly declare clawback exposure (present/absent). Silence is non-conform.
- Administrative friction **MUST** be treated as a measurable burden class (time + delay + failure probability proxies), not as
  “minor inconvenience”.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-N-01 (N+T coupling):** N and at least one timing/mismatch marker are present.
- **DL15-T-01 (Clawback clarity):** clawback risk is explicitly declared.
- **DL15-AF-01 (Friction proxy):** admin friction markers include at least one measurable proxy (steps/time band or delay band).

## B05.CH15.P02.S03 — Time budget: working hours, commute, childcare, informal care, “administrative time”

CH15 treats time as a first-class conformance surface: middle households often “fail quietly” through time capture even when incomes look
stable. The baseline must therefore emit a time budget with comparable pre/post proxies.

### Required fields (template)
- `time_budget_pre` (bands acceptable; weekly recommended):
  - `paid_work_hours_band`
  - `commute_time_band` + `commute_variance_band`
  - `childcare_time_band` (care provision + coordination)
  - `informal_care_time_band` (eldercare/other)
  - `household_labor_time_band` (baseline domestic load)
  - `administrative_time_band` (forms/calls/appointments/disputes)
- `schedule_predictability_pre`:
  - notice time band,
  - last-minute change frequency band,
  - cancellation/mandatory overtime indicators.
- `wait_time_exposure_pre`:
  - childcare/education/healthcare wait time bands where relevant (aggregated).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The baseline **MUST** include administrative time as a measurable proxy; omission is non-conform (it hides a primary toll layer).
- Commute time **MUST** include a variance proxy (reliability matters more than average for care coordination).
- Time budget claims **MUST** be expressed as bands and must specify whether they are measured, estimated, or surveyed.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-TIME-01 (Admin time present):** administrative_time_band exists.
- **DL15-TIME-02 (Commute variance):** commute_variance_band exists (even coarse).
- **DL15-TIME-03 (Predictability):** at least one schedule predictability proxy exists.

## B05.CH15.P02.S04 — LASTEN (L): housing/energy/food/insurance/digital + debt service

The essentials basket for the middle household includes not only basics but also “normal” contractual layers that produce volatility:
insurance/claims friction, subscription bundles, and debt service sensitivity.

### Required fields (template)
For each domain `{housing, energy, food, insurance, digital, mobility, debt_service}`:
- `provider_type` ∈ {private, public, coop, mixed}
- `price_components[]` — base charge + fees + surcharges + penalties (itemized; no lumping)
- `volatility_markers` — variable rates, rate resets, indexation triggers, seasonal swings
- `contract_constraints` — term length, renewal, cancellation friction, bundling, penalties
- `arrears_or_late_payment_exposure` — present/absent + frequency band
- `dispute_or_claim_path_friction` (where relevant: insurance, mortgage servicing, utilities billing)

### Special requirements (housing + debt service)
- Housing **MUST** declare:
  - rent vs mortgage,
  - `rate_reset_risk` (if mortgage),
  - maintenance/renovation spike exposure (bands).
- Debt service **MUST** declare:
  - `debt_service_ratio_band` (portion of N consumed),
  - sensitivity to rate changes (if variable).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The baseline **MUST** provide an itemized L basket; a single “cost of living” lump sum is non-conform.
- The baseline **MUST** separate base price from fee/penalty surfaces; bundling that hides fees is itself an opacity marker.
- Volatility channels (rate resets, indexation, seasonal variation) **MUST** be explicitly declared where present.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-L-01 (Basket coverage):** housing/energy/food/insurance/digital/mobility/debt_service are represented (or explicitly marked N/A
  with rationale).
- **DL15-L-02 (Itemization):** at least one domain shows itemized fee components (not only base price).
- **DL15-L-03 (Volatility visibility):** at least one volatility marker is declared for housing, energy, or debt_service (if applicable).
