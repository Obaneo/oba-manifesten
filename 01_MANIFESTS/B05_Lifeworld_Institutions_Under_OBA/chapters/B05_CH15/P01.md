# B05.CH15.P01 — Purpose, scope, and method for the middle-household case

This paragraph defines the purpose, scope, and method for Day-in-the-Life Case II: a **middle-household** before and after an OBA
intervention set. The case is a **conformance instrument**, not a narrative artefact: it exists to show—concretely and measurably—how
**BRUTO (B) → NETTO (N) → LASTEN (L) → RESTRUIMTE (R)** and **time security** change under OBA, and which frictions and lock-ins
previously consumed “normal life” through volatility, administrative toll, and time capture.

---

<a id="b05ch15p01s01"></a>
## B05.CH15.P01.S01 — Purpose: show how “normal” households experience OBA: lower L/lock-in, more time security, more stable R/B

The purpose is to make “normality” testable. Middle households are not primarily defined by crisis events, but by persistent frictions
that accumulate: wait times, refinancing and contract switching barriers, schedule mismatch with care, mobility lock-in, subscription
bundles, and administrative time capture. OBA’s claim here is structural: reduce L volatility and lock-in, reduce time capture, and make
contestability real.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The case **MUST** produce a *before/after* mapping of **B, T, N, L, R** and a minimal **time security** set (schedule predictability,
  admin-time burden, commute variance).
- The case **MUST** identify the concrete friction surfaces that constrain the middle household (housing/mortgage, childcare/education,
  mobility, digital, insurance/claims pathways where relevant) and connect them to measurable proxies.
- The case **MUST** distinguish *observed facts* from *assumptions* and from *normative claims*; normative language is allowed only as
  explicit conformance requirements (**MUST/SHOULD/MAY**).
- The case **MUST NOT** function as persuasion, branding, or “feel-good proof”. Narrative detail is permitted only insofar as it anchors
  measurable claims.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-PUR-01 (Chain completeness):** B,T,N,L,R appear *before* and *after* with explicit time windows and `method_id`.
- **DL15-PUR-02 (Time security presence):** at least three time proxies exist pre/post: schedule predictability, admin-time burden, and
  commute variance (bands acceptable).
- **DL15-PUR-03 (Friction traceability):** every qualitative improvement claim maps to a measurable delta, an assumption declaration, or
  a conformance requirement.

## B05.CH15.P01.S02 — Scope: dual earners or single + children (variant), middle incomes, work/care combination, mortgage/rent, mobility

The scope is deliberately representative of the “normal” household: stable enough to avoid constant crisis, but still exposed to
volatility and friction that erode time and R/B.

### In-scope (binding)
- Household variants:
  - `variant_A`: dual earners (with children optional),
  - `variant_B`: single earner + children (explicitly marked as variant).
- Income band: middle incomes, with possible variable components (bonus/overtime) and moderate volatility.
- Work/care: paid work + childcare and/or informal care; schedule constraints are first-class.
- Housing: mortgage or rent, including refinancing/rate-reset exposure and maintenance/renovation spikes.
- Mobility: commute and logistics (car dependence vs public transport quality), including time variance and fee stacks (parking/tolls).
- Digital: subscription bundles, vendor lock-in, identity processes, platform fees where they gate work/services.
- Debt service: mortgage debt and/or consumer debt service where it materially affects L volatility.

### Out-of-scope (explicit)
- Macro claims (GDP, inflation policy debate) and political campaigning.
- Individual medical outcome optimization as performance KPI.
- “Lifestyle” prescriptions or moral judgement of consumption choices.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Scope boundaries **MUST** be stated at the start of the case file and **MUST** list any deviations as `scope_exception` with rationale
  and measurement impact.
- If variants are used, the case **MUST** declare variant tags and **MUST NOT** mix variants without separation.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-SCP-01 (Variant clarity):** exactly one declared household variant is used per case instance, or variants are separated.
- **DL15-SCP-02 (Domain coverage):** housing + work/care + mobility + digital appear at least once in baseline friction mapping or time
  structure.

## B05.CH15.P01.S03 — Method: fixed case template with before/after and measurement linkage (definitionset_id, method_id, uncertainty)

A middle-household day-in-the-life case is a **standardized specimen**, comparable across regions and time. It is therefore defined by a
fixed template and strict measurement linkage.

### Required case fields (template)
- `definitionset_id` — normative definitions in force for B,T,N,L,R; lock-in/friction; dispute durations; time proxies.
- `method_id` — calculation method + data handling + aggregation rules; versioned.
- `case_mode` ∈ {`observed_after`, `simulated_after`}
  - If `simulated_after`, the intervention assumptions **MUST** be enumerated and uncertainty widened.
- `time_window_pre`, `time_window_post` — comparable periods.
- `data_sources[]` — evidence classes (contracts, paystubs, bills, schedules, commuting logs/proxies); may be redacted but must be
  auditable in class.
- `uncertainty_band` — for each headline output (at minimum: L, R, R/B, and key time proxies).

### Minimum method sequence
1) Capture baseline observables: B,T,N,L,R and time security proxies.
2) Produce friction map: domain-by-domain constraints (wait times, switching costs, schedule mismatches, admin toll).
3) Run risk events: health/work/price/care shocks to expose volatility and recovery.
4) Apply OBA intervention set: rails for essentials baseline, time security, buffers, transitions.
5) Recompute post-case outputs and time proxies with evidence linkage.
6) Emit the case dashboard (P11) with uncertainty bands and provenance.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The case **MUST** be reproducible from declared inputs and `method_id` (same inputs → same outputs).
- The case **MUST** surface uncertainty rather than bury it (wide bands acceptable; hidden bands non-conform).
- Privacy is required, but privacy **MUST NOT** be used to conceal evidence *classes* or computational logic.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-MTH-01 (Template completeness):** `definitionset_id`, `method_id`, `case_mode`, time windows, evidence classes, and
  uncertainty bands are present.
- **DL15-MTH-02 (Reproducibility):** headline outputs can be re-derived from the declared method or the case fails as a conformance
  object.
- **DL15-MTH-03 (Observed vs simulated clarity):** simulated “after” states list assumptions explicitly and tag all derived claims.

## B05.CH15.P01.S04 — Fail-closed: a case without explicit frictions (administration, lock-in, time poverty) is cosmetic and NON-CONFORM

This chapter uses a hard rule: the case exists to bind “normal life” claims to measurable frictions and rails. If that binding is absent,
the artefact is disqualified.

### FAIL-CLOSED rules (binding)
A middle-household day-in-the-life case is **NON-CONFORM** if any of the following holds:
- No explicit *before/after* values for **B,T,N,L,R** are provided (with time windows and `method_id`).
- No explicit friction diagnosis is provided for at least **three** friction domains:
  - administration/time burden,
  - housing/mortgage friction,
  - childcare/education wait-time or mismatch,
  - mobility/commute friction,
  - digital subscription/identity friction.
- The case asserts “more time security” without pre/post time proxies (schedule predictability, admin-time burden, commute variance).
- The artefact blurs observed facts and assumptions (e.g., simulated outputs presented as observed).
- The artefact claims improvement while hiding residual frictions and volatility channels that remain materially binding.

### Remedy requirement (binding)
- If a case is flagged **NON-CONFORM**, it **MUST** be rewritten by adding missing measurements, friction mapping, and method linkage;
  narrative polishing is explicitly insufficient.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-FC-01 (Measurement gate):** missing B,T,N,L,R → automatic NON-CONFORM.
- **DL15-FC-02 (Friction gate):** fewer than three friction domains diagnosed → automatic NON-CONFORM.
- **DL15-FC-03 (Time proxy gate):** missing time security proxies → automatic NON-CONFORM.
