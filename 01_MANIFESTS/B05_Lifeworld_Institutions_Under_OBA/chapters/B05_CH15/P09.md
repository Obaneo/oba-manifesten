# B05.CH15.P09 — Mobility and logistics (after): less car lock-in

This paragraph specifies the **post-OBA mobility and logistics** state for the middle-household case. The goal is not to moralize car
use; it is to reduce **car lock-in** and **travel-time volatility** by improving public transport reliability/coverage, enabling safe
active mobility, and collapsing mobility fee stacks that function as toll layers (parking extraction, opaque surcharges). The output is a
set of enforceable rails and measurable deltas linked to baseline mobility frictions (P03) and the case dashboard (P11).

---

<a id="b05ch15p09s01"></a>
## B05.CH15.P09.S01 — Public transport quality: higher reliability and coverage; more predictable travel time

Middle households experience “mobility tax” primarily through unpredictability: cancellations, missed connections, and poor coverage force
car dependence and consume time.

### Post-OBA public transport rails (minimum set)
- `reliability_rails`:
  - service reliability targets translated into enforceable operational obligations (headway adherence, cancellation handling),
  - transparent incident reporting (aggregated).
- `coverage_rails`:
  - minimum coverage standards for key corridors (work/school/care routes),
  - time-bounded remediation plans where coverage gaps exist.
- `integration_rails`:
  - integrated ticketing and coordinated transfers to reduce missed-connection time loss,
  - dispute-safe refunds/credits for service failures (time-bounded).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Public transport quality **MUST** be measured by **reliability and variance**, not only by average travel time.
- Coverage improvements **MUST** be stated as operational service levels (frequency bands, route access bands), not policy intent.
- Service failure handling **MUST** be contestable and time-bounded (refund/credit rules and appeal).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-PT-01 (Reliability proxy):** reliability/incident proxy exists pre/post (e.g., cancellation rate band, headway variance band).
- **DL15-PT-02 (Coverage proxy):** coverage proxy exists (access to key routes within time band or service frequency band).
- **DL15-PT-03 (Failure handling):** a dispute-safe mechanism exists for service failures (rule + time bound).

<a id="b05ch15p09s02"></a>
## B05.CH15.P09.S02 — Active mobility: better infrastructure → time/health as a side effect, not a KPI target

Active mobility infrastructure (walking/cycling) reduces reliance on car trips for short distances and improves time predictability. Health
benefits are a side effect, not a KPI target.

### Post-OBA active mobility rails (minimum set)
- `safe_infrastructure`:
  - continuous protected routes where feasible,
  - safe crossings, lighting, winter maintenance where relevant.
- `network_connectivity`:
  - connectivity between housing, schools, transit hubs, and essentials.
- `parking_and_storage`:
  - secure bicycle storage at key nodes to reduce theft risk (a hidden lock-in driver).
- `accessibility_support`:
  - inclusive design for mixed mobility needs (strollers, disabilities; not exclusionary).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Active mobility claims **MUST** be grounded in operational infrastructure proxies (coverage/continuity/safety), not health outcomes.
- Infrastructure **MUST** be designed as universal access where possible; exclusionary “sport infrastructure” framing is non-conform.
- Maintenance (including seasonal) **MUST** be specified; “built but unusable half the year” is a hidden lock-in.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-AM-01 (Continuity proxy):** presence of continuous routes and critical-node connectivity is evidenced (proxy/band acceptable).
- **DL15-AM-02 (Maintenance):** maintenance responsibility and service level are declared.
- **DL15-AM-03 (Non-KPI health):** explicit statement that health is not used as a KPI/eligibility signal.

<a id="b05ch15p09s03"></a>
## B05.CH15.P09.S03 — Cost structure: lower volatility in mobility costs; fewer fee stacks (tolls/parking extraction)

Mobility costs include both predictable recurring costs and volatile spikes (repairs, fines, parking extraction). Post-OBA, the cost
structure must be less volatile and less toll-like.

### Post-OBA mobility cost rails (minimum set)
- `fee_stack_controls`:
  - transparency and caps for tolls/parking fees where they function as unavoidable toll layers,
  - limits on penalty escalation (fine cascades).
- `car_dependency_reduction`:
  - enabling alternatives (public transport, active mobility) so that car use becomes a choice, not a coercive necessity.
- `repair_shock_mitigation` (if car remains material):
  - contestable repair pricing and reduced exploitative urgency pricing,
  - emergency buffer alignment (link P05) without cascade products.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Mobility fee stacks **MUST** be itemized and measured (no “transport costs” lump sum).
- Penalty escalation **MUST** be bounded; fine cascades that behave like E-layers are non-conform.
- Where parking/tolls are unavoidable, the regime **MUST** provide transparency and contestability (appeal path + time bounds).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-MC-01 (Itemization):** mobility cost components are itemized (fuel/parking/tolls/maintenance/insurance where applicable).
- **DL15-MC-02 (Volatility delta):** mobility cost volatility proxies decline (spike frequency/severity or penalty incidence).
- **DL15-MC-03 (Contestability):** appeal pathways exist for penalties and erroneous charges.

## B05.CH15.P09.S04 — Measurable effect: commute-time variance down, modal options up, mobility burden down

This section defines the minimum measurable outputs required to claim that mobility normality improved.

### Required measurable outputs (minimum)
- `commute_time_band_pre/post`
- `commute_variance_band_pre/post` (variance/reliability proxy)
- `modal_options_pre/post`:
  - viable mode count for key trips (work/school/essentials), with viability constraints disclosed.
- `mobility_cost_share_pre/post` (share of N)
- `mobility_cost_volatility_pre/post` (spike/penalty incidence bands)
- `service_incident_proxy_pre/post` (public transport cancellations/delays; aggregated)
- `parking_toll_fee_share_pre/post` (where material)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Mobility improvement claims **MUST** include variance reduction or compensating rails that neutralize variance (e.g., reliable fallback
  routes).
- “More options” claims **MUST** be grounded in viability (coverage, safety, time feasibility), not theoretical mode existence.
- Outputs **MUST** link to `method_id` and include uncertainty bands (via P11).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-MOB-M1 (Variance delta):** commute_variance_band declines post-OBA.
- **DL15-MOB-M2 (Options delta):** modal_options increase or coercive dependence declines (explicitly evidenced).
- **DL15-MOB-M3 (Cost volatility delta):** penalty incidence and/or cost spikes decline, or are neutralized by rails.

---

### Output format (mobility summary)

At minimum, the post-case **MUST** emit a mobility summary (table or structured list) with:

- `trip_type` | `pre_modes_viable` | `post_modes_viable` | `commute_time_band` | `commute_variance_band` | `cost_share` | `cost_volatility_proxy` | `rail_link`

Where trip_type includes work commute, school/childcare, and essentials access.
