# B05.CH15.P08 — Childcare, education, and care (after): access without wait-time lock-ins

This paragraph specifies the **post-OBA childcare, education, and care** state for the middle-household case. The goal is not to
eliminate all scarcity, but to remove **wait-time lock-ins** and **schedule mismatch** as structural coercion mechanisms, reduce
administrative toll, and ensure that baseline quality is strong enough that “pay-to-win” dynamics do not become the default. The output
is a set of enforceable rails and measurable deltas linked to baseline frictions (P03), risk events (P04), and the case dashboard (P11).

---

<a id="b05ch15p08s01"></a>
## B05.CH15.P08.S01 — Childcare: baseline access, predictability, quality; lower “time burden”

Childcare is time-security infrastructure. Post-OBA, childcare access must be predictable and compatible with work schedules, not a
waitlist-driven lock-in that forces private toll payment or work reduction.

### Post-OBA childcare rails (minimum set)
- `baseline_childcare_access`:
  - baseline coverage guarantee (operational access, not merely entitlement),
  - transparent eligibility criteria, minimal proof burden.
- `predictability_and_hours_fit`:
  - operating hours compatible with common work schedules,
  - predictable closures and change notice rules,
  - constraints on penalty fees for schedule mismatch.
- `quality_floor`:
  - baseline staffing and safety standards,
  - transparent incident reporting (aggregated),
  - contestability pathways when quality diverges.
- `admin_toll_reduction`:
  - one-stop intake for placement/subsidy/billing issues,
  - limited verification loops (anomaly-triggered only),
  - time-bounded placement decisions.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Childcare baseline access **MUST** be operationally evidenced (coverage + time-to-placement bands).
- Wait times **MUST** be treated as a lock-in mechanism; if wait times remain, the case **MUST** specify protection during the wait
  (gap handling).
- Administrative time **MUST** be measurable and must decline post-OBA for childcare processes (applications, changes, disputes).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-CC-01 (Operationality):** coverage and time-to-placement bands exist.
- **DL15-CC-02 (Mismatch reduction):** schedule mismatch proxies decline or compensating rails exist (hours fit, penalties removed).
- **DL15-CC-03 (Admin-time delta):** steps/time bands for childcare administration decline post-OBA.

<a id="b05ch15p08s02"></a>
## B05.CH15.P08.S02 — Education: less pay-to-win; stronger baseline provision; contestability when quality diverges

Education is a core site where middle households experience “soft coercion”: quality dispersion, catchment scarcity, and informal markets
(tutoring, gated programmes) that function as pay-to-win structures.

### Post-OBA education rails (minimum set)
- `baseline_quality_strengthening`:
  - stronger baseline provision to reduce reliance on private supplementation for minimal adequacy.
- `anti_pay_to_win_constraints`:
  - restrictions on gated access mechanisms that create de facto tolls for baseline quality,
  - transparency around programme selection criteria and resource allocation.
- `contestability_of_quality`:
  - complaint/appeal pathways for quality failures,
  - audit mechanisms when divergence becomes structural (not anecdotal).
- `administrative_simplification`:
  - reduced paperwork for enrollment/support,
  - time-bounded decisions on placements and support services.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- “Less pay-to-win” claims **MUST** be grounded in measurable proxies (reduced gating, reduced required paid supplements, transparency of
  selection/resource allocation).
- Where selection criteria exist (programmes, tracks), they **MUST** be disclosed and contestable; opaque discretion is non-conform.
- Education quality divergence **MUST** trigger an auditable response path when thresholds are exceeded (link P12/P14).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-EDU-01 (Proxy grounding):** at least one pay-to-win proxy is defined and reported pre/post (e.g., paid supplement prevalence
  band, gating incidents, selection opacity markers).
- **DL15-EDU-02 (Contestability):** appeal/audit pathways exist and are time-bounded.
- **DL15-EDU-03 (Transparency):** selection criteria/resource allocation rules are disclosed at least at the procedural level.

## B05.CH15.P08.S03 — Healthcare: shorter waits, clear pathways, less claims industry and paperwork

Healthcare friction is a major time and tail driver even for middle households: unclear pathways, long waits, and paperwork-heavy claims
processes extend incapacity and consume administrative time.

### Post-OBA healthcare rails (minimum set)
- `pathway_clarity`:
  - clear referral pathways, published steps, and expected timelines (bands acceptable).
- `wait_time_reduction`:
  - capacity and triage improvements targeting high-friction bottlenecks,
  - transparent wait-time reporting (aggregated).
- `claims_and_paperwork_toll_reduction`:
  - standard evidence classes,
  - fewer verification loops,
  - time-bounded claim decisions with deadline enforcement.
- `provisional_relief`:
  - protection against irreversible harm due to delays (e.g., temporary accommodations for incapacity where applicable).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Healthcare improvements **MUST** be expressed as pathway and wait-time deltas (bands acceptable), not satisfaction claims.
- Paperwork reduction **MUST** be measurable (steps/time bands) and must not shift burden onto the household via digital lockouts.
- Any automation in claims/eligibility **MUST** be contestable with restatement and logged reasons (link P12).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-HC-01 (Wait-time delta):** at least one wait-time band improves or bottleneck pathway is restructured with evidence.
- **DL15-HC-02 (Paperwork delta):** verification loops or steps/time bands decline post-OBA.
- **DL15-HC-03 (Contestability):** claim/decision pathways include appeal + restatement where relevant.

## B05.CH15.P08.S04 — Measurable effect: admin burden down, incident rates transparent, satisfaction as procedural input

This section defines the minimum measurable effects required to claim that care systems improved “normality” without substituting
measurement with sentiment.

### Required measurable outputs (minimum)
- Childcare:
  - `time_to_placement_pre/post` (band),
  - `schedule_fit_proxy_pre/post` (hours gap band, penalty incidence),
  - `childcare_admin_time_pre/post` (steps/time bands).
- Education:
  - `gating_incidence_pre/post` (opaque selection, denial-by-friction incidents; aggregated),
  - `quality_divergence_proxy_pre/post` (incident reporting, staffing proxy, or published outcomes proxy; aggregated),
  - `education_admin_time_pre/post` (steps/time bands).
- Healthcare:
  - `wait_time_band_pre/post` (selected pathways),
  - `pathway_steps_pre/post`,
  - `claims_admin_time_pre/post` and `decision_time_band_pre/post`.

### Satisfaction as procedural input (explicit constraint)
- `satisfaction_inputs[]` may be collected only as:
  - aggregated experience signals,
  - feeding audit/inspection prioritization,
  - not as eligibility scoring or performance KPI optimization.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The “after” case **MUST** show admin burden reductions in at least two of the three domains (childcare/education/healthcare).
- Incident reporting **MUST** be transparent and aggregated; absence of incident visibility is an opacity marker and non-conform.
- Satisfaction **MUST NOT** be used as a KPI for individual providers in a way that becomes gameable or punitive; it is procedural input
  only (triage/audit prioritization).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL15-CARE-M1 (Admin delta):** admin steps/time bands decline in at least two domains.
- **DL15-CARE-M2 (Incident transparency):** at least one incident-rate proxy is reported per domain (aggregated).
- **DL15-CARE-M3 (Non-KPI rule):** explicit statement and audit check that satisfaction is not used for eligibility/scoring.

---

### Output format (care systems summary)

At minimum, the post-case **MUST** emit a care-systems summary (table or structured list) with:

- `domain` | `pre_proxy` | `post_proxy` | `delta` | `uncertainty_band` | `method_id_ref` | `rail_link` | `incident_transparency`

Where domains include childcare, education, and healthcare.
