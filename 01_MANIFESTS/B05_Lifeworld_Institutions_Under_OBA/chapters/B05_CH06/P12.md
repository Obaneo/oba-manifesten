# B05.CH06.P12 — Measurement and publication requirements (lifeworld test for debt/credit)

This paragraph specifies the measurement and publication requirements for debt and credit under OBA from a lifeworld perspective.
Without public statistics, credit markets drift into opacity: fee-stacks regrow, clamp becomes normalised, and policy cannot be
contested. OBA therefore requires a **public measurement layer** that makes debt/credit outcomes comparable, time-series visible, and
dispute-capable—while enforcing privacy minimalism so the measurement layer cannot be repurposed as a commercial profiling asset.
Publication is treated as conformance infrastructure: if the system cannot measure and publish core indicators safely, the system must
scale back to auditable scope (fail-closed against invisible clamp).

---

## B05.CH06.P12.S01 — Core statistics: debt-to-income, arrears, default, repeat borrowing, fee share, refinancing friction

Credit governance requires baseline indicators that reveal affordability, distress dynamics, and extraction surfaces.

### Core statistics (binding)
- **Debt-to-income (DTI)** distributions by cohort (income deciles, age bands, household type) and product class.
- **Debt service ratio (DSR)** / payment burden distributions, including tails (p90/p95) to detect clamp risk.
- **Arrears rates** (30/60/90+ days) by product class and issuer segment.
- **Default rates** (event definition must be standardised) and recovery paths (time-to-cure vs time-to-write-off).
- **Repeat borrowing** and rollover frequency (especially revolving and short-term products), including “borrow-to-pay” signatures.
- **Fee share of total cost**: proportion of total paid that is fees/penalties vs interest vs principal (P/E decomposition proxy).
- **Refinancing friction**: rejection rates, average time-to-refinance, and cost/fee barriers; measure lock-in that sustains E pricing.

### Conformance requirement (binding)
- All metrics must be published as time series with standard definitions, a public method note, and stable product/issuer taxonomy to
  prevent reclassification games.

## B05.CH06.P12.S02 — Essentials credit: mortgage burden ratio, housing affordability, foreclosure/eviction rates

Because essentials credit (especially housing) directly determines L and R/B stability, it requires a dedicated lifeworld test.

### Essentials credit indicators (binding)
- **Mortgage burden ratio** distributions (payment-to-income), including stress scenarios for variable rates.
- **Housing affordability** measures: price-to-income, rent-to-income, and entry-cost indices, with regional resolution sufficient to
  detect local clamp.
- **Foreclosure and eviction rates** (event definitions standardised), including time-to-resolution and post-event displacement
  outcomes.
- **Arrears-to-foreclosure pipeline**: transition probabilities and median durations, to detect enforcement-as-profit dynamics.

### Publication rule (binding)
- Essentials indicators must be reported with explicit tail focus (p90/p95) and with separation between: owner-occupied mortgages,
  investor/landlord finance, and rent-related enforcement pathways where data permits.

## B05.CH06.P12.S03 — Restructuring: completion rates, time-to-resolution, tail-risk outcomes post-restructuring

Restructuring is mandatory infrastructure; its effectiveness must be observable.

### Restructuring indicators (binding)
- **Initiation rate**: share of distressed accounts that enter restructuring processes (by product/issuer).
- **Completion rates**: percentage that reach a signed compliant outcome within time bounds.
- **Time-to-resolution**: median and tail durations from request to final outcome.
- **Outcome quality**: post-restructuring arrears and re-default rates at standard horizons (e.g., 3/6/12 months) and changes in DSR
  distributions.
- **Tail-risk outcomes**: incidence of insolvency after attempted restructuring; recurrence of clamp signatures; return-to-essentials
  stability (proxy via arrears on essentials and payment-rail lockout events where applicable).
- **Remediation volumes**: refunds/credits issued due to E reclassification, disclosure failures, or calculation errors.

### Conformance requirement (binding)
- Data must distinguish between restructurings that reduce E (fee waivers, rate cuts, principal corrections) and those that simply
  extend term while preserving extraction.

---

## B05.CH06.P12.S04 — Privacy minimalism: aggregates, suppression, no commercial re-profiling of debt data

The measurement layer must not become a secondary market in behavioural vulnerability.

### Privacy minima (binding)
- **Aggregation-first**: publish only aggregated tables and distributions; no individual-level releases and no persistent pseudonymous
  identifiers in public datasets.
- **Suppression rules**: apply k-anonymity style thresholds (or jurisdictional equivalent) for small cells; use top-coding and bucketed
  ranges to prevent re-identification via tails.
- **Purpose limitation**: debt/credit measurement data may be collected and processed only for order governance, audit, and public
  reporting—not for marketing, steering, or private risk discrimination beyond what the compliant underwriting rail already permits.
- **No commercial re-profiling**: explicit prohibition on selling, licensing, or repackaging debt/credit datasets for commercial
  profiling, lead generation, or collections optimisation.

### Conformance and hard stop (binding)
- Any breach of purpose limitation or evidence of re-identification/commercial re-profiling triggers immediate suspension of affected
  data feeds and mandatory redesign of the publication layer (fail-closed against surveillance rent).
