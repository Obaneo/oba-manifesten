# B05.CH01.P07 — Non-reductive measurement rails: autonomy, privacy, and contestability

This paragraph defines **non-reductive measurement rails** for the lifeworld layer. The lifeworld layer exists to validate OBA
against experienced reality, but it must not mutate into a **single-score regime** that collapses agency into a KPI, nor into a
**surveillance rent system** that extracts data as the price of legitimacy. Measurement is treated as **bounded signal production**:
enough to detect clamp dynamics, washing, and drift; not so much that measurement itself becomes the dominant institution.

The rails here are therefore constitutive: (1) **ban reduction**, (2) protect **autonomy** against coercive optimisation, (3) admit
**experience evidence** as formal input under a testable procedure, and (4) treat **cognitive capture** (well-intended framing and
metric worship) as a first-class risk mitigated through plurality and contestability.

---

## B05.CH01.P07.S01 — Ban on reduction: wellbeing/agency is not a single KPI; metrics are bounded signals

The lifeworld layer MUST NOT define “works for people” as a single number. Agency, dignity, and lived feasibility are plural and
contextual; reducing them to one KPI creates a **target that can be gamed** and a **language monopoly** that enables capture.

### Measurement doctrine
- **Plurality by design**: publish distributions, tails, and cohort splits rather than a single composite.
- **Signal bounding**: every metric is explicitly limited in scope (“what it does and does not claim”).
- **No hidden weights**: if any composite index exists, its weights and sensitivity analysis must be published and contestable.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Any “single score” that is used as a governing objective MUST be treated as non-conform unless it is clearly labeled as a bounded
  proxy and is not used as the primary decision driver.
- CSA/lifeworld publications MUST include distributional and tail indicators; median-only reporting is non-conform where tail harm is
  plausible (clamp dynamics are tail-sensitive by default).

---

## B05.CH01.P07.S02 — Autonomy rails: measurement duty must not become behavioural coercion or surveillance rent

Measurement obligations create power. Without rails, they evolve into behavioural steering (“optimise the citizen”) or into a data
economy where institutions extract granular traces to justify their authority. The lifeworld layer rejects both.

### Autonomy rails (minimum)
- **Data minimalism**: measure the minimum set needed for the lifeworld test; prohibit speculative collection “for future insight.”
- **No person tracking**: CSA outputs are cohort/aggregate; join safety and small-n suppression are mandatory.
- **Prohibited linkages**: measurement datasets must not become eligibility engines, policing substrates, or vendor profiling assets.
- **No punitive optimisation**: individuals must not be penalised for not conforming to a metric-implied behaviour.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Any implementation that relies on persistent individual identifiers for lifeworld validation MUST be treated as non-conform unless
  a narrowly scoped, sunset-bound exception is logged and independently audited.
- If measurement is used to condition access to essentials, rights, or basic participation (beyond fraud/security minima), this is a
  surveillance-rent indicator and triggers escalation.

---

## B05.CH01.P07.S03 — Experience evidence: qualitative signals as formal input (with a testable procedure, not “anecdote”)

Not everything that matters is natively quantitative, but qualitative evidence must not be treated as mere storytelling either.
The lifeworld layer therefore formalises **experience evidence**: qualitative signals that are admissible under a procedure that
makes them **reviewable, sampleable, and contestable**.

### Experience evidence protocol (minimum)
- **Structured capture**: standard prompts, role/context metadata, and explicit claim typing (what is observed vs inferred).
- **Sampling discipline**: recruitment rules, representativeness notes, and saturation criteria; publish known biases.
- **Triangulation**: link qualitative claims to quantitative proxies where possible (e.g., lock-in signals ↔ exit-fee proxies).
- **Audit trail**: versioned coding schema, reviewer notes, and dispute hooks (“challenge this claim/mapping”).

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Qualitative inputs MUST be processed through a published procedure (method_id + coding rules). Unstructured testimonials cannot be
  used as compatibility proof.
- Quantitative outputs MUST expose where qualitative evidence influenced interpretation (no hidden narrative overlay).

---

## B05.CH01.P07.S04 — Risk: cognitive capture via “well-intended” optimisation; mitigation via plurality and contestability

The dominant risk in “human-centred measurement” is **cognitive capture**: a well-intended optimisation frame becomes the new
orthodoxy, alternatives are delegitimised, and the lifeworld layer turns into a normative ideology engine. This is capture without
bribes: it is capture by framing.

### Cognitive capture indicators
- **Metric monoculture**: one index crowds out other signals; dissent is treated as ignorance.
- **Language monopoly**: a single expert group controls definitions and acceptable interpretations.
- **Closed modelling**: black-box methods, proprietary tooling, or undisclosed assumptions determine public claims.
- **Policy laundering**: interventions justified by “the dashboard” without contestable causal chains.

### Mitigation (binding rails)
- **Plurality**: multiple metrics, multiple cohorts, multiple methods where feasible; publish sensitivity analyses.
- **Contestability**: dispute flow, external replication, and audit-of-auditors are mandatory.
- **Open methods**: publish definitions, methods, and revision policy; prohibit closed computation for compatibility labeling.
- **Role separation**: analysts do not unilaterally set thresholds or interventions; governance must log decisions and rationales.

### Conformance requirements (binding)
- Any lifeworld/CSA regime that cannot be independently replicated and disputed MUST be treated as drift-prone and therefore
  non-conform for “lifeworld-compatible” labeling.
- Where cognitive capture indicators rise (metric monoculture, dispute suppression, closed modelling), escalation MUST trigger: method
  diversification, rotation, and re-publication under revised governance rails.
