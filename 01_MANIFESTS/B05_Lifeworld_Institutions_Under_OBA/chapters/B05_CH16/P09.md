# B05.CH16.P09 — Dispute-flow: how high positions appeal (and where the boundary lies)

This paragraph specifies the **dispute-flow** rails for the high-income/wealth case. OBA requires contestability and due process, but it
also requires enforceability under **opacity** and **flight risk**. High positions have strong incentives and resources to litigate,
delay, and create complexity. The dispute-flow therefore has explicit boundaries: reproducible calculations and evidence access for
legitimate appeals, but **no automatic suspension** under opacity/obstruction, and **escrow/lockbox** requirements to neutralize delay as
evasion. The output is a dispute-flow register with time bounds, presumptions, and precedent publication.

---

<a id="b05ch16p09s01"></a>
## B05.CH16.P09.S01 — Right to appeal: reproducible calculations, access to methods and evidence packs

The right to appeal is constitutive: subjects must be able to reproduce how caps, valuations, and look-through attribution were applied.

### Required appeal objects (template)
- `decision_id` (cap event or remedy decision)
- `decision_type` ∈ {I_MAX_assessment, W_MAX_assessment, UBC_attribution, valuation_method, exposure_equivalence, remedy_selection,
  presumption_application, sanction, restriction}
- `appeal_ground` ∈ {calculation_error, evidence_error, valuation_dispute, attribution_dispute, procedure_violation, proportionality,
  other}
- `method_access`:
  - `definitionset_id`, `method_id`, rule/version references
- `evidence_pack_access`:
  - list of evidence classes used (contracts, statements, valuations, graphs),
  - redaction rules and privacy tier applied
- `calculation_trace_ref`:
  - re-runnable trace (inputs → rules → outputs) or declared equivalent
- `time_bounds`:
  - `t_ack`, `t_hearing_or_review`, `t_decision`

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Appeals **MUST** have access to methods and evidence packs sufficient for reproduction (within privacy/security constraints).
- Decisions **MUST** be explainable: reasons, rule versions, and key inputs must be disclosed at least at class level.
- The system **MUST** provide a restatement/recalculation pathway for disputes over cap computations and exposure/valuation.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-APP-01 (Reproducibility):** an appeal includes `method_id` and a calculation trace reference.
- **DL16-APP-02 (Evidence access):** evidence classes used are disclosed and accessible under the privacy tier.
- **DL16-APP-03 (Time bounds):** appeal time bounds exist and are logged.

<a id="b05ch16p09s02"></a>
## B05.CH16.P09.S02 — Limits: no suspension under flight risk/opacity; escrow/lockbox for disputed amounts

High positions can convert delay into effective evasion via asset migration, structure changes, and liquidity extraction. Dispute rights
therefore do not imply enforcement suspension under risk conditions.

### Boundary conditions (minimum)
- `flight_risk_flag`:
  - indicators: residency relocation, asset migration attempts, cross-border custody moves, sudden leverage/cashout, secrecy nodes
- `opacity_flag`:
  - missing UBC disclosure, missing valuation support, missing side letters, refusal to provide auditpacks
- `provisional_enforcement_allowed`:
  - conservative valuation/exposure presumptions,
  - restrictions on transfers under leakage triggers,
  - withholding hooks where relevant
- `escrow_lockbox_required`:
  - disputed amounts/assets placed in escrow/lockbox pending outcome
- `no_automatic_stay`:
  - dispute initiation does not automatically halt cap consequences under flight risk/opacity

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- If `flight_risk_flag` or `opacity_flag` is true, enforcement **MUST NOT** be automatically stayed.
- Escrow/lockbox **MUST** be required for disputed amounts under risk conditions, to neutralize delay incentives.
- Provisional enforcement actions **MUST** be time-bounded, logged, and contestable, but remain in force until compliance or decision.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-LIM-01 (Stay rule):** explicit “no automatic stay” rule exists under flight risk/opacity.
- **DL16-LIM-02 (Escrow trigger):** escrow/lockbox is triggered for disputed amounts when risk flags are present.
- **DL16-LIM-03 (Logging):** provisional actions and flags are logged with rule/version references.

## B05.CH16.P09.S03 — Transparency: aggregated publication of dispute outcomes and precedents (versioned)

To prevent private bargaining and selective enforcement, dispute outcomes must be published in aggregated form and precedents must be
versioned.

### Required public outputs (aggregated)
- `precedent_catalogue` (versioned):
  - rule interpretations, classification outcomes, remedy patterns (no identifying details)
- `dispute_outcome_summary`:
  - volumes, median durations, reversal rates, presumption usage rates, sanction outcomes (bands acceptable)
- `publication_privacy_tier`:
  - what granularity is allowed; privacy controls detail, not accountability

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Precedents **MUST** be versioned and published in aggregated form to prevent hidden drift and carve-outs.
- Dispute outcome statistics **MUST** be published; secrecy is a legitimacy breach except for narrow security exceptions.
- Publication **MUST NOT** leak identifying details; privacy tiers control detail, not whether outcomes are visible.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-TRN-01 (Precedent publication):** a versioned precedent catalogue exists.
- **DL16-TRN-02 (Outcome stats):** aggregated dispute outcome stats exist and are updated per release cadence.
- **DL16-TRN-03 (No identifier leakage):** outputs respect privacy tier constraints.

<a id="b05ch16p09s04"></a>
## B05.CH16.P09.S04 — Failure mode: legal delay as evasion → deadlines, presumptions, sanction multipliers

Delay is a classic evasion route at high positions. The system must treat obstructive delay as a substantive risk, not a neutral
procedural choice.

### Delay patterns (minimum taxonomy)
- `serial_requests` (endless document requests with shifting scope)
- `forum_shopping` (jurisdiction hopping to stall)
- `structure_mutation` (reorgs during dispute to break lineage)
- `selective_disclosure` (partial evidence drip-feed)
- `procedural_overload` (flooding the system with low-value motions)

### Countermeasures (minimum set)
- `hard_deadlines`:
  - strict time bounds for submission and decision
- `default_presumptions`:
  - if evidence not supplied by deadline, conservative presumptions apply
- `sanction_multipliers`:
  - penalties escalate for obstruction and repeated deadline breach
- `status_downgrades`:
  - conformance status moves to UNDER_REVIEW/NON_CONFORM under persistent obstruction
- `structure_freeze` (where permissible):
  - restrictions on asset/structure mutations during dispute under risk conditions

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Deadlines **MUST** be enforceable; missed deadlines **MUST** trigger presumptions and/or sanctions (not merely “extensions denied”).
- Obstruction **MUST** be logged as a compliance signal and reflected in conformance status and remedy severity.
- Structure mutation during dispute **MUST** be treated as risk escalation and may trigger restrictions or presumptions.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-DLY-01 (Deadline enforcement):** missed deadlines trigger presumption and/or sanction multipliers.
- **DL16-DLY-02 (Obstruction logging):** obstruction events are logged and influence conformance status.
- **DL16-DLY-03 (Freeze/risk response):** structure mutation triggers risk controls (escrow/transfer restrictions) when applicable.

---

### Output format (dispute-flow register)

At minimum, the case **MUST** emit a dispute-flow register (table or structured list) with:

- `decision_id` | `decision_type` | `appeal_ground` | `risk_flags` (opacity/flight) | `escrow_required` | `t_ack` | `t_decision` |
  `presumption_applied` | `outcome` | `precedent_ref` | `status`

If dispute-flow is described without boundaries (no-stay under risk) and precedent publication, the case is NON-CONFORM.
