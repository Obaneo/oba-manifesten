# B05.CH16.P13 — Residual risks and the adaptation war

This paragraph specifies the **residual risks** that remain even with robust cap enforcement and anti-evasion rails. CH16 is inherently a
“war” condition: high positions will innovate in structure and channel design. The purpose here is anti-PR and operational: identify the
cat-and-mouse dynamic, legislate versioning and rapid reclassification, mitigate capture risk via audit-on-the-auditor, anticipate
international retaliation scenarios, and prevent overreach that would choke legitimate entrepreneurship. Residual risks must be disclosed,
measured where possible, and routed into escalation and parameter tuning rather than bespoke exemptions.

---

<a id="b05ch16p13s01"></a>
## B05.CH16.P13.S01 — “Cat-and-mouse”: innovation in evasion; need versioning and rapid reclassification

Evasion is adaptive. When one route is closed, new forms appear (new derivatives, new custody patterns, new treaty structures, new digital
channels). The system must be able to update classification without hidden drift.

### Residual risk surfaces (minimum)
- `novel_instruments` (new derivatives, token wrappers, hybrid instruments)
- `novel_structure_patterns` (new layering, new nominee schemes, modular SPV kits)
- `novel_channel_patterns` (new mixers/bridges, new settlement platforms, new data rent products)
- `novel_contract_forms` (new fee/carry relabels, “services” wrapping rents)

### Required capability (binding)
- `taxonomy_versioning`:
  - shift taxonomy and thresholds are versioned; changes are published as precedents.
- `rapid_reclassification_path`:
  - new patterns can be classified within short cycles (time-bounded governance), without waiting years for legislation.
- `backward_compatibility_rules`:
  - how new classifications apply to ongoing structures (look-back rules; anti-grandfathering unless explicitly justified).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Detection and remedy rules **MUST** be versioned and publicly auditable in aggregated form.
- Rapid reclassification **MUST** preserve due process: published criteria + contestability, but must not be delayable by opacity tactics.
- Where novelty is used to evade disclosure, conservative presumptions **MUST** apply until evidence is supplied.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-CM-01 (Versioning):** taxonomy versions are recorded and referenced in decisions and dashboards.
- **DL16-CM-02 (Cycle time):** time-to-classification for new patterns is bounded (declared).
- **DL16-CM-03 (Presumption under novelty):** novelty + missing evidence triggers conservative default classification.

## B05.CH16.P13.S02 — Capture risk: pressure on legislation/auditors/media; mitigated via audit-on-the-auditor

High positions will attempt to capture the rule-maker, the enforcer, and the narrative apparatus. Capture risk is not a moral claim; it is
a structural vulnerability and must be managed as such.

### Capture vectors (minimum inventory)
- `legislative_capture` (lobby carve-outs, exemptions, complexity insertion)
- `regulatory_capture` (enforcement under-resourcing, selective interpretation)
- `audit_capture` (auditor conflicts, revolving doors, intimidation via litigation)
- `media_capture` (agenda setting, reputation laundering, “strategic capital” narratives)

### Mitigation rails (required)
- `audit_on_the_auditor`:
  - independent audit of enforcement bodies (sampling, conflict checks, decision consistency tests)
- `conflict_of_interest_controls`:
  - cooling-off rules, disclosure of relationships, procurement integrity
- `precedent_transparency`:
  - versioned publication of enforcement outcomes and interpretations (aggregated)
- `anti_carve_out_gate`:
  - explicit hard stop against bespoke exceptions (linked to P14.S01)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Enforcement institutions **MUST** be subject to audit-on-the-auditor; without it, capture is a predictable failure mode.
- Conflicts of interest **MUST** be disclosed and mitigated; secrecy is non-conform.
- Precedents **MUST** be published in aggregated form; hidden interpretation drift is a legitimacy breach.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-CAP-01 (Auditor audited):** audit-on-the-auditor mechanism exists and produces published aggregated outputs.
- **DL16-CAP-02 (Conflict controls):** conflict rules exist and are enforced (loggable).
- **DL16-CAP-03 (Carve-out gate):** attempted carve-outs are detectable and routed to hard-stop logic.

## B05.CH16.P13.S03 — International pressure: sanctions/retaliation; scenarios in Book 2

Robust cap enforcement and anti-leakage measures can provoke international retaliation: sanctions threats, treaty disputes, capital
flight narratives, and competitive deregulation. CH16 must recognize these as scenario drivers without collapsing into geopolitics.

### International pressure patterns (minimum)
- `treaty_dispute_pressure` (DoB and withholding contested)
- `sanctions_or_countermeasures` (targeted restrictions against enforcement jurisdiction)
- `financial_center_competition` (jurisdictions competing to host shadow regimes)
- `capital_flight_messaging` (political/media pressure to weaken rails)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The case **MUST** treat international pressure as a residual risk and link it to scenario handling in Book 2 (no need to solve it here).
- Mobility controls **MUST** be temporary, criteria-based, and sunset (link P10); permanent emergency becomes capture.
- Policy reaction **MUST** remain consistent with core invariants; weakening rails under pressure is a compatibility breach.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-INT-01 (Scenario linkage):** international pressure is explicitly linked to Book 2 scenario rails.
- **DL16-INT-02 (Sunset discipline):** any control escalation includes sunset and contestability.
- **DL16-INT-03 (Invariant integrity):** the case states which invariants cannot be traded away.

## B05.CH16.P13.S04 — Unintended collateral: do not choke legitimate entrepreneurship; parameter tuning within rails

A key residual risk is overenforcement: rules so blunt that they deter legitimate entrepreneurship, innovation, and productive
risk-taking. OBA addresses this via **parameter tuning** within fixed rails, not bespoke exemptions.

### Collateral risk surfaces (minimum)
- `startup_and_scale_risk` (illiquid equity valuation, founder liquidity timing)
- `productive_investment_chilling` (risk of discouraging long-horizon capital)
- `compliance_overhead` (audit burden becomes disproportionate for legitimate actors)
- `false_positive_shift_flags` (over-triggering presumptions on benign structures)

### Mitigation approach (required)
- `parameter_tuning_within_rails`:
  - adjust thresholds, time windows, valuation bands, and safe-harbor criteria as versioned rule changes.
- `safe_harbor_definitions`:
  - limited, general safe harbors based on substance and transparency (not identity-based carve-outs).
- `error_correction_loop`:
  - dispute outcomes and false positive rates feed into version updates (without weakening core invariants).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Collateral mitigation **MUST** occur via general, versioned parameter changes; bespoke exceptions are non-conform.
- Safe harbors **MUST** be substance-based and transparent; “strategic capital” identity claims are prohibited.
- If false positives rise, the system **MUST** tune detection thresholds and evidence requirements while preserving fail-closed logic under
  opacity/obstruction.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-COL-01 (Parameter governance):** a versioned parameter tuning process exists with published change notes.
- **DL16-COL-02 (No bespoke):** mitigation does not introduce identity-based carve-outs.
- **DL16-COL-03 (Feedback loop):** false positive indicators (reversals, over-trigger rates) are tracked and used for updates.

---

### Output format (residual risk register)

At minimum, the case **MUST** emit a residual risk register (table or structured list) with:

- `risk_surface` | `mechanism` | `indicator_proxy` | `mitigation_rail` | `escalation_trigger` | `status`

If residual risks are not disclosed, the case becomes unfalsifiable and drifts into PR.
