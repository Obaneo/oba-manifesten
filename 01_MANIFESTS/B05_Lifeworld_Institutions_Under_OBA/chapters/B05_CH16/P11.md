# B05.CH16.P11 — Lifeworld impact: what it feels like “at the top” (without sentimentality)

This paragraph specifies the **lifeworld impact** of caps and anti-evasion enforcement for the high-income/wealth case. The goal is not
sentimentality or moral theatre; it is to describe, in structurally testable terms, what changes in consumption, power, status, and
strategic pressure when I-MAX/W-MAX are enforced with look-through and presumptions. Qualitative statements are permitted only as
experience carriers that map to measurable proxies and rails.

---

<a id="b05ch16p11s01"></a>
## B05.CH16.P11.S01 — Consumption and freedom: more bounded by I-MAX, but broad choice remains within the cap

Under I-MAX, marginal escalation is bounded. The “after” lifeworld is constrained at the top end, but broad consumption freedom remains
within the cap.

### Structural claims (must be testable)
- `consumption_continuity_within_cap`:
  - the subject can continue high-quality consumption and services within I-MAX, without needing cap breach to maintain lifestyle.
- `marginal_escalation_constrained`:
  - ultra-high escalation (multiple residences as status arms race, extreme perks, unlimited luxury spend) is more constrained.
- `perks_reclassified`:
  - in-kind benefits are valued and counted; “corporate-paid lifestyle” cannot bypass I-MAX.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Consumption claims **MUST** map to I-MAX compliance: declared spending band within cap and declared perk valuation rules.
- The case **MUST** separate “freedom of choice” from “freedom to accumulate claim power”.
- If consumption is constrained beyond intended I-MAX logic due to enforcement error, the case **MUST** route to dispute-flow correction
  (P09) and record it as an exception.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-LIFE-01 (Cap coupling):** consumption narrative references I-MAX bands and perk valuation inclusion.
- **DL16-LIFE-02 (Perk closure):** in-kind perks are included or explicitly absent with evidence class.
- **DL16-LIFE-03 (Error routing):** any undue constraint due to error routes to dispute-flow with restatement.

<a id="b05ch16p11s02"></a>
## B05.CH16.P11.S02 — Power: less block power via concentrated capital, platform positions, and claim stacks

The main purpose of caps is to reduce **block power** and **claim-stack dominance**, not to create austerity. Post-OBA, concentrated
positions and toll infrastructure are the primary targets.

### Power surfaces (minimum inventory)
- `capital_concentration`:
  - concentrated equity/real estate positions reduced via W-MAX enforcement and deconcentration remedies.
- `platform_chokepoints`:
  - API gating, marketplace fee stacks, ranking control (technical evasion surfaces).
- `claim_stacks`:
  - fee stacks, carry waterfalls, related-party contracts that extract without productive contribution.
- `political_and_media_influence_channels` (if present in structure graph):
  - governance capture attempts routed to audit-on-the-auditor (P13/P14).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Power reduction claims **MUST** be grounded in measurable proxies: concentration metrics, fee stack depth, platform take-rate bounds,
  UBC block vectors.
- The case **MUST** explicitly separate “wealth” from “power”: the enforcement target is the power surface, not the private preference set.
- If enforcement creates arbitrary/selective outcomes, it becomes a legitimacy breach (linked to P14.S03).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-PWR-01 (Proxy grounding):** at least two power proxies are reported (concentration + fee stack or platform chokepoint).
- **DL16-PWR-02 (UBC block vectors):** block power surfaces appear in the UBC map and are referenced in remedies.
- **DL16-PWR-03 (No arbitrariness):** enforcement actions reference rule versions and published precedents.

## B05.CH16.P11.S03 — Status: shifts toward reputation/competence; risk of new elite forms (CH13)

With monetary escalation bounded, status competition can migrate toward reputation and competence signals, but also toward new elite forms
via gatekeeping institutions and network control.

### Status migration surfaces (minimum inventory)
- `reputation_capital`:
  - status derived from demonstrable competence, contribution, and transparent track records.
- `gatekeeping_institutions`:
  - credential bodies, boards, curation institutions, elite networks.
- `platform_visibility`:
  - verification badges, ranking control, algorithmic curation.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Status claims **MUST** be treated as a risk surface when they create new block power; CH13 rails apply (contestability, audit-of-auditor,
  portability).
- Monitoring **MUST** be privacy-preserving and aggregated; it **MUST NOT** become behavioural scoring.
- If new elite gatekeeping emerges, it **MUST** trigger redesign or replacement of the gate surface (fail-closed against elite capture).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-STAT-01 (Surface identification):** at least one status surface is explicitly identified (reputation vs gatekeeping).
- **DL16-STAT-02 (Rail linkage):** any gatekeeping surface references contestability and audit rails (CH13).
- **DL16-STAT-03 (Trigger logic):** drop-off/concentration indicators exist to detect elite formation (aggregated).

## B05.CH16.P11.S04 — Evasion pressure: higher pressure to be creative; the system must keep shift detection robust

Caps increase the incentive to search for loopholes. Post-OBA, the “top” lifeworld includes higher evasion pressure and compliance
strategy work. This is expected and must be addressed structurally: robust detection, fast versioning, and fail-closed presumptions.

### Evasion pressure indicators (minimum)
- increase in `shift_attempts[]` frequency or novelty
- growth of opacity layers (new SPVs, nominee layers, offshore nodes)
- migration to technical rails (crypto, platform arbitrage, synthetic exposure)
- escalation of dispute delay tactics (procedural overload)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The system **MUST** treat evasion pressure as normal and respond via versioned shift taxonomy updates (P13.S01) and rapid
  reclassification capability.
- Under structural opacity/obstruction, stricter presumptions and rail restrictions **MUST** auto-activate (fail-closed).
- Enforcement **MUST** remain legitimate: transparent precedents and proportional sanctions (avoid arbitrariness).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-EVP-01 (Detection robustness):** shift taxonomy and leakage tests are versioned and updated under observed innovation.
- **DL16-EVP-02 (Fail-closed escalation):** opacity/obstruction triggers stricter presumptions and restrictions automatically.
- **DL16-EVP-03 (Legitimacy):** dispute outcomes and precedents are published in aggregated form (P09.S03).

- `dimension` (consumption/power/status/evasion_pressure) | `pre_proxy` | `post_proxy` | `delta` | `method_id_ref` |

Where proxies include: I-MAX compliance bands, perk valuation inclusion, concentration and fee-stack metrics, platform take-rate bounds,
status gatekeeping indicators, and shift attempt counts.
