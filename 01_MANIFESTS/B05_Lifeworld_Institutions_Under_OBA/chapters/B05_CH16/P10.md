# B05.CH16.P10 — International mobility: exit, re-domiciliation, and capital flight

This paragraph specifies how **international mobility** is handled in the high-income/wealth case: exit, re-domiciliation, and capital
flight. CH16 treats mobility as legitimate in principle but **non-arbitrageable** against core invariants (caps, UBC attribution, and
anti-evasion). The system therefore requires explicit exit triggers, look-through allocation, treaty denial-of-benefits where substance
fails, and—under leakage—temporary capital controls with sunset and contestability. The output is a mobility trigger map with enforcement
rails and measurable consequences.

---

<a id="b05ch16p10s01"></a>
## B05.CH16.P10.S01 — Exit triggers: end of residency, relocation of control center, asset migration (link to exit-tax templates)

Exit is not only “moving the person”; it is moving the **control center** and **assets**. The system therefore uses multiple triggers,
tied to UBC and economic exposure.

### Exit trigger taxonomy (minimum set)
- `residency_end_trigger`:
  - end of tax residency (legal + factual residence indicators)
- `control_center_relocation_trigger`:
  - relocation of management/control functions for key vehicles (where decisions are made)
- `asset_migration_trigger`:
  - movement of custody, title, or beneficial exposure across borders (including synthetics and wrapped assets)
- `entity_redomiciliation_trigger`:
  - moving holding companies, funds, trusts, or IP vehicles to different jurisdictions
- `service_provider_shift_trigger`:
  - switch to offshore custodians/nominees that reduce traceability

### Required fields (template)
- `mobility_event_id`
- `trigger_type[]` (one or more)
- `effective_date` + `look_back_window` (anti-bunching, anti-sudden-move logic)
- `affected_entities[]` (links to entity_graph)
- `affected_assets[]` (links to W_assets/exposures)
- `UBC_subjects[]` impacted
- `evidence_class[]` (residency indicators class, board minutes class, custody statements, migration ledgers)
- `exit_tax_template_ref` (if applicable) + `valuation_method_ref`

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Mobility triggers **MUST** apply to control and exposure, not only formal residency labels.
- Asset migration **MUST** include beneficial exposure and synthetics; “no title moved” is not sufficient.
- When an exit trigger fires, the system **MUST** require an exit assessment (valuation bands + disclosure), and **MUST** route to
  escrow/lockbox if opacity/flight risk applies.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-EXIT-01 (Multi-trigger):** at least one control-center or asset-migration trigger is considered (not only residency end).
- **DL16-EXIT-02 (Exposure coverage):** synthetic/beneficial exposures are included in migration assessment.
- **DL16-EXIT-03 (Template linkage):** exit-tax template and valuation method references exist where applicable.

<a id="b05ch16p10s02"></a>
## B05.CH16.P10.S02 — Capital controls: reporting, withholding, approval regimes under leakage (temporary, sunset)

Capital controls are treated as **exceptional** and **temporary** escalation tools under systemic leakage. They must be bounded,
contestable, and sunset by default.

### Control instruments (minimum set)
- `enhanced_reporting`:
  - higher-frequency reporting for high-risk channels and cross-border transfers
- `withholding_hooks`:
  - automatic withholding at exit points (custodians, platforms, payment rails) when conditions trigger
- `approval_regimes`:
  - prior approval for large transfers under active leakage conditions
- `transfer_restrictions`:
  - temporary restrictions on certain channels (e.g., forbidden/conditional channels per Codex)
- `sunset_rule`:
  - automatic expiry unless renewed under published criteria

### Activation criteria (minimum)
- `leakage_tests_triggered` (from Codex/channel leakage tests)
- `shadow_regime_growth` signals (offshore/crypto/opaque custody expansion)
- `audit_capacity_overload` (enforcement cannot keep up; fail-closed to auditable scope)
- `flight_risk_cluster` (multiple exit attempts around cap events)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Capital controls **MUST** be time-bounded and include sunset; permanent controls require separate constitutional-level justification.
- Activation **MUST** be criteria-based and published (aggregated). Discretion without criteria is non-conform.
- Controls **MUST** be contestable and logged; however, contestation **MUST NOT** automatically suspend controls when leakage risk is active.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-CC-01 (Sunset):** each control has a defined sunset and renewal rule.
- **DL16-CC-02 (Criteria publication):** activation criteria are declared and mapped to measurable leakage tests.
- **DL16-CC-03 (Contestability + logging):** controls have appeal paths and are logged with rule/version refs.

<a id="b05ch16p10s03"></a>
## B05.CH16.P10.S03 — Treaty mismatch: denial-of-benefits, look-through allocation, withholding hooks

International structures exploit treaty mismatches and legal form gaps. Remedies must be systematic: denial-of-benefits where substance
fails, look-through allocation to UBC subjects, and withholding hooks to prevent leakage.

### Treaty mismatch patterns (minimum taxonomy)
- `treaty_hub_routing` (income routed through treaty jurisdictions)
- `mailbox_substance` (minimal activity claimed as substance)
- `hybrid_mismatch` (instrument treated differently across jurisdictions)
- `residence_claim_conflict` (dual residency claims to avoid attribution)
- `withholding_avoidance` (structures designed to defeat withholding)

### Remedy toolkit (minimum set)
- `denial_of_benefits` (DoB):
  - deny treaty benefits when substance proxies fail
- `look_through_allocation`:
  - allocate income/wealth to UBC subjects based on control/benefit/block vectors
- `withholding_hooks`:
  - apply withholding at source/custody/payment rails under mismatch/opacity
- `conservative_presumptions`:
  - apply conservative attribution/valuation when treaty documentation is incomplete

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Treaty benefits **MUST** be conditional on evidenced substance; absent substance, DoB applies.
- Look-through allocation **MUST** use UBC mapping; formal residency claims do not defeat attribution.
- Withholding hooks **MUST** be available under mismatch and opacity to prevent delay-as-evasion.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-TTY-01 (DoB rule):** denial-of-benefits criteria and application path are declared.
- **DL16-TTY-02 (UBC allocation):** look-through allocation references UBC vectors and paths.
- **DL16-TTY-03 (Withholding readiness):** withholding hooks are specified for at least one exit/transfer surface.

<a id="b05ch16p10s04"></a>
## B05.CH16.P10.S04 — Consequence: mobility remains possible, but not as arbitrage against core invariants

The goal is not to imprison capital; it is to prevent mobility from functioning as a cap-avoidance strategy. Mobility should remain
possible if disclosure and settlement occur under the rules.

### Consequence logic (explicit)
- `mobility_allowed_with_settlement`:
  - exit/re-domiciliation remains possible after disclosure, valuation, and remedy settlement (or escrow under dispute).
- `no_arbitrage_boundary`:
  - caps and UBC attribution follow exposure/control; changing labels or addresses does not erase obligations.
- `flight_risk_boundary`:
  - under active flight risk/opacity, restrictions and escrow apply until compliance.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The system **MUST** preserve lawful mobility paths with clear procedures and time bounds.
- The system **MUST NOT** allow mobility to nullify cap events or remedies via delay or opacity.
- Under flight risk, provisional enforcement **MUST** apply (escrow/lockbox, restrictions), with contestability and logging.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-MOB-01 (Procedure clarity):** mobility procedure includes required steps, time bounds, and evidence classes.
- **DL16-MOB-02 (No arbitrage):** obligations are defined by exposure/control; residency change alone does not defeat them.
- **DL16-MOB-03 (Risk boundary):** flight risk triggers escrow/restrictions; absence is non-conform.

---

### Output format (mobility trigger map)

At minimum, the case **MUST** emit a mobility trigger map (table or structured list) with:

- `mobility_event_id` | `trigger_type` | `affected_assets/entities` | `risk_flags` (opacity/flight/leakage) |
  `required_disclosure` | `exit_assessment_ref` | `withholding_or_control_action` | `escrow_required` | `status`

If mobility is discussed without triggers, settlement/escrow logic, and no-arbitrage boundaries, the case is NON-CONFORM.
