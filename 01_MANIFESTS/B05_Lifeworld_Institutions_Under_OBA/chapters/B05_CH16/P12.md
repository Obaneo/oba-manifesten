# B05.CH16.P12 — Measurable “before/after” outputs (case dashboard)

This paragraph specifies the **case dashboard** for CH16: the minimum measurable outputs required to claim a valid “before/after”
high-income/wealth Day-in-the-Life case. The dashboard binds cap enforcement and anti-evasion to measurable events, indicators, and
outcomes. Outputs are reported with `definitionset_id`, `method_id`, explicit time windows, and uncertainty bands where valuation/exposure
are estimated. If the dashboard cannot be emitted, the case is **NON-CONFORM** under CH16 fail-closed rules.

---

<a id="b05ch16p12s01"></a>
## B05.CH16.P12.S01 — Cap events: frequency, magnitude, remedies chosen, compliance timing

This layer reports the operational reality of caps: how often events occur, how large they are, what remedies were chosen, and whether
compliance happens within bounded time.

### Required metrics (minimum set, pre/post windows)
- Provenance:
  - `definitionset_id`, `method_id`
  - `time_window_pre`, `time_window_post`
  - `case_mode` ∈ {`observed_after`, `simulated_after`}
- Cap event counts:
  - `I_MAX_event_count`
  - `W_MAX_event_count`
  - `cross_cap_arbitrage_flag_count`
  - `opacity_presumption_count`
- Event magnitude (bands acceptable; uncertainty required when valuation-driven):
  - `I_MAX_excess_amount_band_total`
  - `W_MAX_excess_amount_band_total`
  - `largest_event_band` (I and W)
- Remedy selection:
  - `remedy_type_distribution` (levy/clawback / commons conversion / divestment / escrow-lockbox)
  - `escrow_lockbox_usage_rate`
- Compliance timing:
  - `time_to_compliance_band` (median and max)
  - `deadline_breach_count`
  - `default_remedy_auto_applied_count`

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Cap event reporting **MUST** separate I-MAX and W-MAX events and include cross-cap arbitrage flags.
- Magnitudes **MUST** include uncertainty bands when derived from valuation or exposure equivalence.
- Compliance timing **MUST** be measured; “event handled” without time-to-compliance is non-conform.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-DASH-C1 (Event log coherence):** dashboard event counts reconcile with the cap event log (P03).
- **DL16-DASH-C2 (Uncertainty):** valuation-driven magnitudes include uncertainty bands.
- **DL16-DASH-C3 (Deadline enforcement):** deadline breaches are counted and have defined consequences (default remedy/sanctions).

<a id="b05ch16p12s02"></a>
## B05.CH16.P12.S02 — Evasion indicators: structure patterns, offshore exposure, synthetic usage, fee-stack depth (aggregated)

This layer measures whether the system is winning the adaptation war: whether structures become more transparent and less evasive, or
whether a shadow regime grows.

### Required indicators (minimum set; aggregated)
- Structure complexity:
  - `entity_layer_depth_band`
  - `nominee_trust_layer_count_band`
  - `opacity_gap_count_band` (missing disclosure points)
- Offshore exposure:
  - `offshore_node_share_band`
  - `treaty_hub_routing_incidence_band`
  - `mailbox_substance_flag_count`
- Synthetic/financial shifts:
  - `synthetic_exposure_presence_rate`
  - `collateralized_borrowing_presence_rate`
  - `related_party_pricing_flag_count`
- Fee stack indicators:
  - `fee_stack_depth_band`
  - `effective_fee_rate_band`
  - `side_letter_presence_rate`
- Technical channel indicators (if applicable):
  - `crypto_shadow_channel_flag_rate`
  - `platform_take_rate_band` (effective take rate)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Indicators **MUST** be aggregated and privacy-safe; they **MUST NOT** become behavioural scoring.
- Offshore and synthetic exposure **MUST** be explicitly tracked; omission is non-conform because these are primary evasion surfaces.
- Structure complexity indicators **MUST** include opacity gaps (not only node counts).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-DASH-E1 (Coverage):** structure, offshore, synthetic, and fee-stack indicators are all present (or explicitly N/A with rationale).
- **DL16-DASH-E2 (Opacity visibility):** opacity gaps are measured and trendable.
- **DL16-DASH-E3 (Channel linkage):** technical channel indicators link to channel classification outputs (Codex) when present.

<a id="b05ch16p12s03"></a>
## B05.CH16.P12.S03 — Enforcement: audits, finding severities, dispute durations, sanction outcomes

This layer measures enforceability: whether audits happen, how severe findings are, how disputes resolve, and whether sanctions are applied
consistently without arbitrariness.

### Required enforcement metrics (minimum set; aggregated)
- Audits:
  - `audit_initiation_count`
  - `audit_start_time_band` (from trigger to audit start)
  - `audit_duration_band`
- Findings:
  - `finding_severity_distribution` (e.g., low/med/high/critical)
  - `presumption_application_rate`
  - `obstruction_flag_count`
- Disputes:
  - `dispute_count`
  - `dispute_duration_band` (median and max)
  - `reversal_rate_band`
  - `precedent_reference_rate` (how often decisions cite versioned precedents)
- Sanctions and remedies:
  - `sanction_outcome_distribution` (fines/limitations/procurement exclusion/etc.)
  - `sanction_multiplier_usage_rate` (for obstruction/delay)
  - `status_downgrade_count` (CONFORM→UNDER_REVIEW/NON_CONFORM)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Enforcement metrics **MUST** include time-to-audit and dispute durations; speed is part of enforceability.
- Presumptions and obstruction must be tracked; opacity is not “noise” but a core signal.
- Precedent references **MUST** be versioned and used to prevent arbitrariness.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-DASH-EN1 (Time metrics):** audit start/duration and dispute durations exist.
- **DL16-DASH-EN2 (Severity tracking):** finding severities are categorized and published in aggregated form.
- **DL16-DASH-EN3 (Legitimacy proxy):** precedent reference rate exists (decisions cite versioned rules/precedents).

<a id="b05ch16p12s04"></a>
## B05.CH16.P12.S04 — System impact: effects on concentration, platform take-rates, essentials L (macro→micro link)

This layer links top-end enforcement to system outcomes without collapsing into macro ideology. The objective is to show whether cap
enforcement reduces concentrated power surfaces and whether that translates into measurable reductions in extraction channels that affect
the broader lifeworld (e.g., platform tolls, essentials pricing).

### Required system impact metrics (minimum set; aggregated)
- Concentration:
  - `top_concentration_proxy` (e.g., concentration band for controlled assets/exposures; method-defined)
  - `deconcentration_event_count` (divestments/restructures executed)
- Platform tolls (if relevant):
  - `platform_take_rate_trend_band` (effective take rates)
  - `API_gating_incidence_band` (if measured)
- Commons funding conversion:
  - `commons_conversion_amount_band` (C flows from remedies)
  - `commons_capacity_marker` (domain-specific, aggregated)
- Macro→micro linkage:
  - `essentials_L_proxy_trend_band` (housing/energy/digital cost proxies; aggregated)
  - `fee_stack_pressure_proxy` (intermediary fee prevalence; aggregated)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- System impact claims **MUST** use declared proxies and method definitions; vague “inequality down” is non-conform.
- Platform take-rate claims **MUST** use effective take rate (all mandatory layers).
- Macro→micro linkage **MUST** remain aggregated and privacy-safe; the dashboard shows correlation/proxy movement, not moral claims.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-DASH-S1 (Proxy definition):** each system impact metric references method-defined proxy definitions.
- **DL16-DASH-S2 (Take-rate discipline):** take-rate metrics use effective take rates, not headline commission.
- **DL16-DASH-S3 (Linkage discipline):** essentials L proxies are reported only in aggregated form with uncertainty where relevant.

---

### Output format (dashboard schema)

At minimum, the case **MUST** emit the dashboard in machine-parseable structure (table(s) or structured lists) with:

1) **Cap event table**
- `window` | `I_MAX_events` | `W_MAX_events` | `excess_amount_band` | `remedy_distribution` | `escrow_usage` | `time_to_compliance`

2) **Evasion indicator table**
- `indicator` | `pre_band` | `post_band` | `delta` | `uncertainty_band` | `method_ref`

3) **Enforcement table**
- `audits` | `severity_distribution` | `dispute_duration` | `reversal_rate` | `presumption_rate` | `sanction_outcomes` | `precedent_ref_rate`

4) **System impact table**
- `proxy` | `pre_band` | `post_band` | `delta` | `method_ref` | `notes`

If any of these tables cannot be produced, the case is **NON-CONFORM**.
