# B05.CH16.P06 — Evasion routes I: legal shifts (form over substance)

This paragraph specifies **legal evasion routes**: form-based shifts that preserve economic control/benefit/block while changing labels to
avoid I-MAX/W-MAX consequences. The chapter treats these routes as detectable patterns and binds them to **substance-over-form** remedies:
look-through attribution, denial-of-benefits, recharacterization, and automatic presumptions under missing disclosure.

---

<a id="b05ch16p06s01"></a>
## B05.CH16.P06.S01 — Reclassification: wage→fee→royalty; dividend→loan; rent→service contract

Reclassification shifts attempt to move value across categories so that the same economic benefit is treated as outside the cap or outside
the reporting duty.

### Reclassification patterns (minimum taxonomy)
- `wage_to_fee_to_royalty`:
  - employment compensation re-labeled as consulting fees or IP royalties.
- `dividend_to_loan`:
  - distributions replaced by loans, advances, or “repayable” instruments that rarely repay.
- `rent_to_service_contract`:
  - rental income re-labeled as “service”, “management”, or “maintenance” contract fees.
- `carry_to_fee_or_deferred_vehicle`:
  - performance allocation re-labeled into fee streams or deferred vehicles.

### Detection signals (minimum)
For each pattern:
- `contract_signature`:
  - sudden change of contract type with same parties/activities,
  - fee/royalty pricing detached from market comparables,
  - non-arm’s-length terms (unusual cancellation clauses, exclusivity).
- `cashflow_signature`:
  - similar net flows continue despite label change,
  - circular payments through controlled entities,
  - “loan” with no repayment schedule or with perpetual rollovers.
- `timing_signature`:
  - clustering around cap events, vesting dates, or W-MAX triggers.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Reclassification patterns **MUST** be treated as shift candidates and routed to substance-over-form tests.
- “Loan” instruments **MUST** be analyzed as economic exposure/cashout when repayment is non-credible; labels do not control.
- Where evidence is insufficient, conservative presumptions **MUST** apply (recharacterize into the economically nearest class).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-LGL-01 (Tagging):** at least one reclassification pattern is tested against baseline B/W components.
- **DL16-LGL-02 (Signature evidence):** each tagged shift lists contract + cashflow signatures.
- **DL16-LGL-03 (Presumption):** missing repayment credibility triggers recharacterization presumption.

<a id="b05ch16p06s02"></a>
## B05.CH16.P06.S02 — IP-box and treaty shopping: profit shifting via licensing and mailbox “substance”

IP-box and treaty shopping routes shift profits to jurisdictions through licensing and mailbox entities that claim “substance” while
economic activity and control remain elsewhere.

### Profit shifting patterns (minimum taxonomy)
- `IP_licensing_shift`:
  - operating entities pay royalties/fees to IP-holding entities in low-tax jurisdictions.
- `treaty_hub_routing`:
  - income routed through treaty jurisdictions to reduce withholding or exposure.
- `mailbox_substance_claim`:
  - minimal local presence claimed as “substance” to access benefits.
- `cost_plus_mispricing`:
  - transfer pricing arrangements engineered to drain profits.

### Detection signals (minimum)
- `jurisdiction_signature`:
  - presence of low-tax/secrecy jurisdictions with disproportionate profit capture.
- `substance_signature`:
  - low employee/activity footprint relative to profit,
  - outsourced “substance” (service providers) without operational reality.
- `contract_signature`:
  - sudden licensing arrangements with inflated rates,
  - related-party contracts lacking comparables.
- `graph_signature`:
  - hub-and-spoke flows to a small set of entities.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Treaty/IP benefits **MUST** be denied where substance is not evidenced (denial-of-benefits logic).
- Related-party licensing **MUST** be subject to conservative presumptions without independent evidence.
- Profit shifting signals **MUST** trigger auditpack demand; refusal triggers presumptions and provisional enforcement.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-IP-01 (Substance test):** at least one substance proxy is applied (activity vs profit).
- **DL16-IP-02 (DoB routing):** denial-of-benefits or reallocation logic is specified for mailbox patterns.
- **DL16-IP-03 (Auditpack):** detected patterns map to required evidence classes (agreements, transfer pricing docs, activity proof).

## B05.CH16.P06.S03 — Nominee/beneficial ownership: hiding UBC; trustees; layered vehicles

Nominee and trustee layers attempt to sever UBC attribution by replacing visible ownership with intermediaries, while control/benefit/block
remain.

### Ownership obfuscation patterns (minimum taxonomy)
- `nominee_title_holding`:
  - title held by nominee with undisclosed beneficial owner.
- `trust_layering`:
  - discretionary trusts with opaque beneficiaries and protector powers.
- `multi_SPV_layering`:
  - chains of SPVs across jurisdictions with circular holdings.
- `foundation_or_charity_shielding`:
  - structures used to obscure control while retaining benefit/control vectors.

### Detection signals (minimum)
- `UBC_gap_signature`:
  - missing beneficiary disclosure, missing protector/control rights, inconsistent filings.
- `graph_signature`:
  - unusually deep entity chains, circular ownership, repeated intermediary nodes.
- `control_signature`:
  - contractual control (veto, management rights) inconsistent with legal ownership.
- `bank_custody_signature`:
  - custody arrangements that resist disclosure or fragment records.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- UBC attribution **MUST** use look-through; nominee title **MUST NOT** defeat attribution.
- Where beneficial ownership is withheld, the system **MUST** apply beneficial ownership presumptions and escalate audit.
- Trustee/protector powers **MUST** be treated as control vectors under UBC.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-UBO-01 (Look-through path):** at least one explicit look-through path is shown through nominee/trust layers.
- **DL16-UBO-02 (Presumption under opacity):** missing beneficiary/protector disclosure triggers presumptions and provisional actions.
- **DL16-UBO-03 (Graph depth trigger):** excessive layering triggers auditpack demand.

## B05.CH16.P06.S04 — Remedy: substance-over-form, look-through, denial-of-benefits, automatic presumptions

This section specifies the remedy toolkit for legal shifts. Remedies must be rule-bound, reproducible, and fail-closed under opacity.

### Remedy toolkit (minimum set)
- `recharacterization`:
  - relabeled flows are reclassified into economically nearest category for cap purposes.
- `UBC_look_through`:
  - attribution to controlling subjects via control/benefit/block vectors.
- `denial_of_benefits`:
  - treaty or regime benefits denied where substance is not evidenced.
- `conservative_pricing_presumption`:
  - related-party pricing treated under conservative assumptions absent comparables.
- `automatic_presumptions_under_opacity`:
  - default attribution/valuation rules apply when disclosure is missing.
- `escrow_lockbox`:
  - disputed amounts held pending resolution, especially under flight risk.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Remedies **MUST** apply to economic substance and exposure; form labels are irrelevant.
- When a shift is detected, the system **MUST** select a default remedy route unless the subject supplies the required auditpack within time
  bounds.
- Presumptions **MUST** be logged and contestable, but disputes **MUST NOT** automatically suspend enforcement under opacity/flight risk.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-RMDL-01 (Default route):** every detected legal shift maps to a default remedy route.
- **DL16-RMDL-02 (Auditpack gate):** supplying the required auditpack can change the presumption; refusal leaves presumption in force.
- **DL16-RMDL-03 (Logging):** remedy selection and rule versions are logged.

---

### Output format (legal shift register)

At minimum, the case **MUST** emit a legal shift register (table or structured list) with:

- `shift_id` | `pattern_type` | `signal_set` | `UBC_link` | `opacity_flag` | `default_presumption` | `remedy_route` |
  `auditpack_required` | `status`

If legal shifts are described without signals, UBC linkage, and remedy routing, the case is NON-CONFORM under CH16 fail-closed rules.
