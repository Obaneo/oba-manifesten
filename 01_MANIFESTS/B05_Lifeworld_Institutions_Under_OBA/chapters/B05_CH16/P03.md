# B05.CH16.P03 — Cap events: triggers and timing (W-MAX and I-MAX)

This paragraph specifies **cap events**: how **I-MAX** and **W-MAX** become concrete triggers in the high-income/wealth lifeworld. Cap
enforcement is not annual “tax filing theatre”; it is event-driven and timing-sensitive: vesting, carry realizations, valuation
re-marks, portfolio concentration, and property aggregation. The output is a cap-event log with explicit timing, valuation/exposure
logic, cross-cap arbitrage detection, and FAIL-CLOSED handling under opacity.

---

<a id="b05ch16p03s01"></a>
## B05.CH16.P03.S01 — I-MAX event: bonus/vesting/carry realization; deferral look-back; in-kind reclassification

I-MAX events are triggered by realizations and reclassifications that create effective income beyond the cap. The key risks are timing
games (deferral), form games (relabeling), and hidden compensation (perks).

### Standard I-MAX event types (minimum set)
- `bonus_realization_event` (cash bonus paid or credited)
- `vesting_realization_event` (equity vests, options exercised, RSUs settle)
- `carry_realization_event` (performance allocation crystallizes; waterfall triggers)
- `fee_relabel_event` (wage → fee/royalty/licensing)
- `in_kind_comp_event` (non-cash benefits reclassified or expanded)

### Required event fields (template)
For each event:
- `event_id`
- `event_type`
- `event_date` and `recognition_window` (timing rules)
- `amount_band` (gross and net if relevant)
- `payer_or_vehicle_ref` (links to entity_graph)
- `classification` ∈ {wage_like, capital_like, fee_like, royalty_like, carry_like, in_kind}
- `deferral_or_accumulation_flag` (present/absent)
- `look_back_window` (if deferral rules apply)
- `evidence_class[]` (contract terms, grant docs, fund LPA terms, payment records)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- I-MAX events **MUST** be recorded at realization time, not only at annual aggregation.
- Deferrals **MUST** be subject to a look-back/anti-bunching rule (declared as part of method_id); “not paid yet” is not sufficient.
- In-kind compensation **MUST** be classified and valued; omission is non-conform.
- Relabeling events **MUST** be tagged as shift candidates and routed to substance-over-form tests (P06/P05).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-IMAX-01 (Event logging):** at least one realization event is logged when applicable (vesting/carry/bonus).
- **DL16-IMAX-02 (Deferral handling):** deferral flags trigger look-back logic; absence → NON-CONFORM.
- **DL16-IMAX-03 (Perk valuation):** in-kind events have valuation method + band.

<a id="b05ch16p03s02"></a>
## B05.CH16.P03.S02 — W-MAX event: portfolio appreciation, concentrated positions, private valuations, property aggregation

W-MAX events occur when wealth crosses cap thresholds due to market appreciation, revaluations, concentration growth, or aggregation
effects. The primary risks are valuation discretion and hidden exposure (synthetics/leverage).

### Standard W-MAX event types (minimum set)
- `market_appreciation_event` (public positions appreciate beyond threshold)
- `private_revaluation_event` (private company/fund mark changes)
- `concentration_event` (position becomes concentrated beyond rule threshold)
- `property_aggregation_event` (multiple properties aggregate under look-through rules)
- `synthetic_exposure_event` (exposure increases via swaps/options/CFDs)
- `collateralized_borrowing_event` (debt extraction against assets without sale)

### Required event fields (template)
For each event:
- `event_id`
- `event_type`
- `event_date` and `valuation_date`
- `asset_or_vehicle_ref` (links to W_assets and entity_graph)
- `valuation_method` + `valuation_band` + `uncertainty_band`
- `liquidity_class`
- `exposure_type` (direct/synthetic/beneficial/pledged)
- `concentration_marker`
- `aggregation_basis` (for property aggregation: look-through path + rule)
- `evidence_class[]` (broker statements, appraisals, fund reports, valuation memos)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- W-MAX events **MUST** include valuation method and uncertainty; point valuations without uncertainty are non-conform.
- Concentration **MUST** be explicitly flagged and measured; concentration is a cap-event amplifier.
- Synthetic and collateralized borrowing exposures **MUST** be included; no-title exposure is still exposure.
- Property and vehicle aggregation **MUST** follow look-through rules; fragmentation is not an escape.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-WMAX-01 (Valuation completeness):** valuation_method + band + uncertainty exist for each major event.
- **DL16-WMAX-02 (Exposure coverage):** synthetic/collateral flags are explicitly handled.
- **DL16-WMAX-03 (Aggregation trace):** property aggregation events include look-through path and rule reference.

## B05.CH16.P03.S03 — Cross-cap arbitrage: I→W shifting via deferral, debt, SPV pricing (detection)

Cross-cap arbitrage occurs when actors shift across I and W to evade caps: deferring I into W, extracting cash via debt to avoid income
recognition, or using SPV pricing and related-party trades to move value off-book.

### Cross-cap arbitrage patterns (minimum taxonomy)
- `deferral_bunching` (delay income recognition to avoid I-MAX in a given window)
- `debt_cashout` (borrow against assets to fund consumption without “income”)
- `SPV_transfer_pricing` (sell/buy within controlled vehicles at engineered prices)
- `carry_relabeling` (carry disguised as fee/loan or deferred vehicle)
- `synthetic_shift` (replace title with derivative exposure to hide W)

### Detection requirements (minimum)
- `arbitrage_flags[]` with:
  - `pattern_type`
  - `signal_proxy` (e.g., rapid leverage increase, unusual internal transfer pricing, rising synthetic exposure, sudden perk expansion)
  - `graph_signature` (structure pattern: layered vehicles, circular payments, nominee layers)
  - `trigger_threshold` (coarse allowed; method_id referenced)
  - `required_auditpack` (what evidence is demanded)
- `look_back_or_recharacterization_rule` reference (method_id)
- `conservative_valuation_rule` reference for suspect pricing

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Cross-cap arbitrage detection **MUST** apply substance-over-form and economic exposure tests; labels are irrelevant.
- When flags trigger, the system **MUST** demand auditpacks and may apply provisional enforcement under opacity.
- Related-party pricing **MUST** be subject to conservative presumptions unless independently evidenced.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-ARB-01 (Flagging):** at least one arbitrage pattern is tested against the baseline architecture.
- **DL16-ARB-02 (Auditpack linkage):** each flag maps to a required auditpack.
- **DL16-ARB-03 (Recharacterization):** triggered flags route to recharacterization/look-back logic (logged).

## B05.CH16.P03.S04 — Fail-closed: opacity (no disclosure) → conservative presumption + provisional enforcement

Opacity is a primary evasion vector. CH16 therefore treats missing disclosure as actionable: enforcement does not pause because data is
withheld.

### Opacity conditions (minimum set)
- missing ownership/beneficiary disclosure for structure nodes
- missing valuation support for illiquid assets or private marks
- refusal to disclose side letters, fee/carry terms, or related-party transactions
- use of secrecy jurisdictions without sufficient evidence classes
- inability to reconcile ledgers across entities (broken lineage)

### Provisional enforcement toolkit (minimum)
- `conservative_valuation_presumption` (upper-bound valuation bands applied)
- `beneficial_ownership_presumption` (UBC attribution defaults to controlling subject)
- `escrow_or_lockbox_requirement` (disputed amounts/positions placed in escrow)
- `restriction_trigger` (rail restrictions/withholding hooks under leakage)
- `audit_escalation` (mandatory audit start within time bound)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- If opacity conditions hold, the system **MUST** apply conservative presumptions and **MUST** initiate provisional enforcement.
- Disclosure refusal **MUST NOT** suspend cap consequences; it changes the presumption and burden of proof.
- Presumptions and provisional actions **MUST** be logged, time-bounded, and contestable under dispute-flow rules (P09), but dispute does
  not automatically halt enforcement under opacity/flight risk.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-OPC-01 (Presumption trigger):** opacity conditions lead to explicit presumption selection (logged).
- **DL16-OPC-02 (Escrow boundary):** escrow/lockbox is used for disputed amounts under opacity/flight risk.
- **DL16-OPC-03 (Audit start):** audit escalation begins within declared time bound.

---

### Output format (cap event log)

At minimum, the case **MUST** emit a cap event log (table or structured list) with:

- `event_id` | `cap_type` (I/W) | `event_type` | `event_date` | `amount_or_valuation_band` | `method_ref` | `evidence_class` |
  `flags` (deferral/arbitrage/opacity) | `provisional_action` | `next_step`

If the cap event log cannot be produced with evidence linkage, the “cap enforcement” narrative is NON-CONFORM.
