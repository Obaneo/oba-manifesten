# B05.CH16.P02 — Baseline profile (pre-OBA): income and wealth architecture

This paragraph specifies the **baseline (pre-OBA)** profile for Day-in-the-Life Case III (high income/wealth). The baseline is not an
anecdote: it is the **minimum observable architecture** required to apply I-MAX/W-MAX caps, detect cross-cap arbitrage, and enforce
anti-evasion via UBC look-through and exposure tests. The baseline therefore declares income components, ownership structures, wealth
composition and valuation risk, and a UBC control map that identifies where control and block power actually sit.

---

<a id="b05ch16p02s01"></a>
## B05.CH16.P02.S01 — BRUTO (B): salary/bonus + dividends/interest + fees/royalties + carry + in-kind perks

High positions typically combine wage-like income with capital income, fee income, and contingent allocations. The baseline must separate
these streams to prevent relabeling games.

### Required fields (template)
- `B_components[]` (itemized; no lumping), with:
  - `salary_base`
  - `bonus_variable` (incl. vesting schedules and performance conditions)
  - `equity_vesting_realizations` (if applicable)
  - `dividends`
  - `interest_income`
  - `fees_consulting_management`
  - `royalties_licensing`
  - `carry_performance_allocation`
  - `rents_real_estate` (net of allowable costs; fee stacks separated)
  - `in_kind_perks` (housing, vehicles, travel, services; classification required)
- For each component:
  - `amount_band` (or value)
  - `timing_profile` (realization dates / deferral / vesting windows)
  - `payer_class` (employer, portfolio company, fund, SPV, trust, platform)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- B **MUST** be decomposed into components above; “total income” is insufficient for CH16.
- Variable and contingent income (vesting/carry/bonuses) **MUST** include timing/deferral markers (T interaction).
- In-kind perks **MUST** be explicitly declared and classified; silence is a known evasion surface.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-B-01 (Decomposition):** at least five distinct B components are present (or explicit N/A with rationale).
- **DL16-B-02 (Timing visibility):** vesting/deferral timing is declared for contingent components.
- **DL16-B-03 (Perk visibility):** in-kind perks are explicitly present/absent.

## B05.CH16.P02.S02 — Structure: holdings, trusts, SPVs, partnerships, nominee ownership, offshore exposure

The primary enforcement surface at high positions is structure. The baseline must represent the ownership graph and its opacity points.

### Required fields (template)
- `entity_graph[]` (nodes and edges), including:
  - entities: {individual, household, holding_company, operating_company, fund, GP/LP, trust, foundation, SPV, nominee, bank/custodian}
  - relationships: {equity_ownership, voting_rights, beneficiary_interest, management_control, debt_claim, option_right, contractual_control}
- `jurisdiction_map[]`:
  - entity jurisdiction, tax residency indicators, treaty exposure flags (categorical)
- `offshore_exposure`:
  - presence/absence + type: {low_tax_jurisdiction, secrecy_jurisdiction, treaty_hub, mailbox_substance}
- `nominee_or_trustee_layers`:
  - number of layers band + evidence class categories
- `fee_and_carry_contracts[]`:
  - management fee terms, carry waterfalls, side-letter privileges (existence flags)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- The case **MUST** provide a structure graph sufficient for look-through (even if anonymized); a narrative description is non-conform.
- Offshore exposure **MUST** be explicitly declared (present/absent). Silence is non-conform.
- Any nominee/trustee layer **MUST** be represented as a node with relationship type; “managed by others” is not an acceptable abstraction.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-STR-01 (Graph presence):** entity_graph exists (at least 6 nodes recommended) with relationship types.
- **DL16-STR-02 (Jurisdiction mapping):** jurisdiction_map exists for all non-domestic entities.
- **DL16-STR-03 (Offshore clarity):** offshore exposure is explicitly declared and categorized.

## B05.CH16.P02.S03 — Wealth (W): asset mix (equity, real estate, private funds, derivatives, IP claims) and valuation risks

W enforcement depends on valuation and exposure. The baseline must therefore list assets, exposure form (direct vs synthetic), and
valuation uncertainty.

### Required fields (template)
- `W_assets[]` with, per asset or position:
  - `asset_class` ∈ {public_equity, private_equity, real_estate, private_fund_interest, debt_instruments, derivatives_synthetics, cash,
    IP_claims, business_equity, collectibles_other}
  - `holding_vehicle` (which node in entity_graph holds it)
  - `exposure_type` ∈ {direct_title, beneficial_interest, synthetic_exposure, pledged_collateral, contingent_claim}
  - `concentration_marker` (position size band or “concentrated” flag)
  - `valuation_method` ∈ {market_price, appraisal, model_based, last_round, comparable, internal_mark}
  - `valuation_band` + `uncertainty_band`
  - `liquidity_class` ∈ {liquid, semi_liquid, illiquid}
  - `encumbrances` (pledges, liens, debt against asset; bands acceptable)

### Valuation risk flags (minimum)
- `private_valuation_discretion` (present/absent)
- `related_party_pricing_risk` (present/absent)
- `appraisal_frequency_band`
- `synthetic_exposure_presence` (present/absent)
- `collateralized_borrowing_presence` (present/absent)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- W **MUST** be represented with valuation methods and uncertainty bands; point valuations without uncertainty are non-conform.
- Exposure **MUST** include synthetics and collateralized borrowing; “no title” does not mean “no exposure”.
- Concentrated positions **MUST** be explicitly flagged (they are W-MAX risk amplifiers).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-W-01 (Asset mix):** at least three asset classes are represented (or explicit N/A with rationale).
- **DL16-W-02 (Uncertainty):** each non-trivial asset has a valuation_method and uncertainty_band.
- **DL16-W-03 (Exposure completeness):** synthetic/collateral flags are explicitly declared present/absent.

## B05.CH16.P02.S04 — Control map: where ultimate control and block power actually sit (UBC look-through)

CH16 requires that “who controls” is determined by **UBC** (control/benefit/block), not by labels. The control map is the enforcement
backbone: it determines attribution for caps, remedies, and anti-evasion presumptions.

### Required UBC outputs (template)
- `UBC_subjects[]` (ultimate subjects: individuals/household nodes)
- For each subject:
  - `control_vectors[]`:
    - voting control, board appointment power, management rights, veto rights, contractual chokepoints
  - `benefit_vectors[]`:
    - cashflow rights, upside participation, fee rights, carry rights, in-kind benefit routes
  - `block_vectors[]`:
    - veto power, gatekeeping roles, critical infrastructure chokepoints (platform/API, licensing)
  - `look_through_paths[]`:
    - paths through entity_graph showing how control/benefit/block propagate
  - `UBC_confidence_band` (based on evidence completeness)
  - `opacity_gaps[]` (missing disclosure points; triggers presumptions)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- UBC attribution **MUST** be based on look-through paths; “not the owner of record” is not sufficient.
- If evidence is missing, UBC **MUST** be conservative (lower confidence → stronger presumption), and opacity gaps **MUST** be recorded for
  later enforcement (P03.S04).
- Block power surfaces **MUST** be included (not only ownership/benefit), especially where platforms or gatekeeping positions exist.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-UBC-01 (Three-vector map):** control, benefit, and block vectors are all present per UBC subject.
- **DL16-UBC-02 (Look-through paths):** at least one explicit look-through path is provided per major asset/vehicle cluster.
- **DL16-UBC-03 (Opacity gaps logged):** missing disclosure points are explicitly listed and tagged.

---

### Output format (baseline architecture summary)

At minimum, the baseline case **MUST** emit:
1) **Income decomposition table**
- `component` | `amount_band` | `timing_profile` | `payer_class` | `evidence_class`

2) **Structure graph summary**
- `entity` | `jurisdiction` | `role` | `relationship_types` | `opacity_flag`

3) **Wealth inventory table**
- `asset_class` | `holding_vehicle` | `exposure_type` | `valuation_method` | `valuation_band` | `uncertainty_band` | `liquidity_class`

4) **UBC control map summary**
- `UBC_subject` | `control_vectors` | `benefit_vectors` | `block_vectors` | `confidence_band` | `opacity_gaps`

If these cannot be produced with evidence class linkage, the case is **NON-CONFORM** under CH16 fail-closed rules.
