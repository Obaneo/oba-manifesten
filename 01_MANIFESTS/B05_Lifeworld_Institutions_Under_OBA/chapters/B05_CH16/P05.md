# B05.CH16.P05 — Adaptation: legitimate adjustment versus evasion

This paragraph specifies how the high-income/wealth lifeworld adapts under caps, and how OBA distinguishes **legitimate adjustment** from
**evasion**. Caps change payoffs; they do not remove strategic behavior. The system therefore requires a **shift taxonomy** that classifies
adaptation patterns, tags pseudo-legitimate form games, and routes detection signals into auditpacks and remedies. The output is an
adaptation map with explicit classification rules, detection hooks, and links to enforcement.

---

<a id="b05ch16p05s01"></a>
## B05.CH16.P05.S01 — Legitimate: shift toward production/commons investment, less leverage, transparent structures

Legitimate adaptation is behavior that reduces concentration and rent extraction while remaining compatible with productive activity and
commons growth.

### Legitimate adaptation patterns (minimum set)
- `production_shift`:
  - reallocation from rentier cashflows to productive investment and operating value creation.
- `commons_investment_shift`:
  - increased contributions to commons capacity (C) via legitimate channels, including long-horizon infrastructure and maintenance.
- `leverage_reduction_shift`:
  - lower reliance on collateralized borrowing to fund consumption or avoid income realization.
- `structure_simplification_shift`:
  - fewer layers, clearer beneficial ownership, reduced offshore opacity.
- `fee_stack_compression_shift`:
  - reduced management fee/carry stacking and less reliance on non-optional toll layers.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Legitimate adaptation **MUST** be demonstrable via evidence classes (structure simplification, reduced leverage, transparent contracts).
- Legitimate adaptation **MUST NOT** depend on secrecy or reclassification games; transparency is a constitutive requirement.
- Claims of “productive shift” **MUST** map to measurable proxies (concentration reduction, fee stack depth reduction, commons funding flows).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-LEG-01 (Transparency):** structure simplification includes fewer opacity gaps and clearer UBC mapping.
- **DL16-LEG-02 (Leverage delta):** collateralized borrowing exposure declines or is bounded and disclosed.
- **DL16-LEG-03 (Fee stack delta):** fee/carry stacking depth declines or is bounded by rule.

<a id="b05ch16p05s02"></a>
## B05.CH16.P05.S02 — Pseudo-legitimate: relabeling into consultancy/licensing; perks; offshore SPVs; synthetic exposure

Pseudo-legitimate adaptation mimics compliance while preserving claim power through form over substance: relabeling, perks, offshore
layers, and synthetic exposure that replicates prohibited concentration.

### Pseudo-legitimate patterns (minimum set)
- `relabel_to_fee_or_royalty`:
  - wage/bonus → consultancy fee → royalty/licensing contract.
- `perk_expansion`:
  - increased in-kind benefits to bypass I-MAX reporting.
- `offshore_layering`:
  - SPVs and mailbox substance to shift profits or hide UBC.
- `synthetic_substitution`:
  - replace title with swaps/options/CFDs to keep exposure while avoiding reporting/caps.
- `debt_cashout_masking`:
  - collateralized borrowing used as “non-income” consumption funding.
- `related_party_pricing`:
  - engineered valuations via controlled transactions to manage W-MAX triggers.

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Pseudo-legitimate patterns **MUST** be treated as shift candidates and routed to substance-over-form and exposure tests.
- In-kind perks **MUST** be valued and classified; omission triggers presumptions.
- Offshore layering **MUST** trigger higher disclosure duties; missing disclosure triggers conservative presumptions and provisional enforcement.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-PSE-01 (Shift tagging):** relabeling/perk/offshore/synthetic patterns are tagged in `shift_attempts[]`.
- **DL16-PSE-02 (Exposure equivalence):** synthetics are mapped to economic exposure and included in cap computations.
- **DL16-PSE-03 (Opacity handling):** offshore layers without sufficient evidence trigger presumptions + escrow/lockbox as applicable.

## B05.CH16.P05.S03 — New status arenas: gatekeeping, reputation, network access (link to CH13)

When monetary winner-take-all is bounded, status competition can migrate into non-monetary arenas. For high positions this includes:
gatekeeping roles, institutional curation, network access, and platform reputation.

### High-risk status arenas (minimum set)
- `credential_and_board_gates` (boards, accreditation bodies, elite programmes)
- `network_access_gates` (closed conferences, grant circles, advisory boards)
- `reputation_systems` (media/awards rankings, platform verification)
- `curation_power` (control over what counts as “legitimate” knowledge/culture)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- CH16 **MUST** treat status migration as an enforcement surface when it recreates block power (E-like toll positions).
- Monitoring **MUST** be privacy-preserving and aggregated; it **MUST NOT** become behavioural scoring.
- Where status arenas become gatekeeping, the system **MUST** apply contestability and audit-of-the-auditor rails (link CH13 and P14).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-STAT-01 (Surface identification):** at least one status arena is identified if relevant to the case profile.
- **DL16-STAT-02 (Contestability):** gatekeeping surfaces include transparency and appeal/objection paths.
- **DL16-STAT-03 (Audit-on-auditor):** curation bodies are subject to auditability requirements when they control access.

<a id="b05ch16p05s04"></a>
## B05.CH16.P05.S04 — Detection: shift taxonomy applied to behavior patterns, contracts, and graph structures

Detection is the hinge: the system must classify adaptation patterns using observable features—contracts, cashflows, and structure graphs—
and then route signals into auditpacks and remedies.

### Shift taxonomy (minimum classification outputs)
For each `shift_attempt`:
- `shift_id`
- `shift_family` ∈ {legal_form_shift, technical_channel_shift, financial_exposure_shift, status_gate_shift}
- `pattern_type` (e.g., wage_to_fee, IP_box, nominee_layers, synthetic_TRS, debt_cashout, platform_take_rate)
- `signal_set[]`:
  - contract signatures (clauses, pricing anomalies),
  - cashflow signatures (loops, sudden fee growth),
  - graph signatures (layering, circularity, secrecy nodes),
  - timing signatures (bunching, deferral spikes).
- `risk_score_band` (coarse; method_id referenced)
- `required_auditpack` (what evidence is demanded)
- `default_presumption` (what applies under missing evidence)
- `remedy_route` (recharacterize, deny benefits, include in caps, escrow/lockbox, sanction)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Shift detection **MUST** be rule-based and reproducible; “investigator intuition” cannot be the primary logic.
- When signals exceed thresholds, required auditpacks **MUST** be demanded; refusal triggers presumptions and provisional enforcement.
- All classification outputs **MUST** be logged and versioned (to avoid hidden rule drift and selective enforcement).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-DET-01 (Reproducibility):** shift classification references method_id and produces consistent outputs for the same inputs.
- **DL16-DET-02 (Auditpack gating):** triggered shifts lead to auditpack demand within time bounds.
- **DL16-DET-03 (Versioning):** taxonomy and thresholds are versioned; changes are published as precedents.

---

### Output format (adaptation map)

At minimum, the case **MUST** emit an adaptation map (table or structured list) with:

- `shift_id` | `shift_family` | `pattern_type` | `signal_set` | `risk_score_band` | `auditpack_required` | `default_presumption` |
  `remedy_route` | `status` (legitimate / pseudo / under_review)

If adaptation/evasion is described without shift objects and routing logic, the case is NON-CONFORM under CH16 fail-closed rules.
