# B05.CH16.P08 — Evasion routes III: financial shifts (securitization, leverage, synthetics)

This paragraph specifies **financial evasion routes**: shifts that preserve or expand economic exposure and cash extraction while reducing
visible W or I, typically through fee stacking, leverage, securitization structures, and derivatives/synthetics. These routes are
detected and remedied by **economic exposure look-through**, conservative valuation under opacity, and explicit reporting duties.

---

<a id="b05ch16p08s01"></a>
## B05.CH16.P08.S01 — Fee stacks: management fees, carry, structuring fees in fund stacks

Fee stacks are a rent surface: layered management fees, carry waterfalls, and structuring fees across fund-of-funds, SPVs, and
intermediaries. They can preserve extraction even under caps by relocating income into opaque fee channels.

### Fee stack patterns (minimum taxonomy)
- `multi_layer_management_fees` (GP/manager fees + feeder/SPV fees + admin fees)
- `carry_waterfall_complexity` (opaque hurdles, catch-ups, side letters)
- `structuring_and_advisory_fees` (fees paid by portfolio entities back to controllers)
- `monitoring_fees` (portfolio-company fees that behave like hidden dividends)
- `pay_to_access_terms` (priority allocations, preferential liquidity via side letters)

### Detection signals (minimum)
- `fee_stack_depth` (number of fee layers; band)
- `effective_fee_rate` (all mandatory layers combined; band)
- `side_letter_privileges` present/absent + category
- `related_party_fee_routes` (fees flowing to controlled entities)
- mismatch between reported performance and extracted fees (extraction-first signature)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Fee stacks **MUST** be measured as effective fee rate and depth; headline fee disclosure is insufficient.
- Side letters **MUST** be disclosed as an evidence class; refusal triggers presumptions (opacity).
- Related-party fees **MUST** be treated as economic benefit vectors under UBC and included in cap analysis.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-FEE-01 (Depth + effective rate):** fee stack depth and effective fee rate are computed.
- **DL16-FEE-02 (Side letter handling):** side letter presence is declared; missing disclosure triggers opacity flags.
- **DL16-FEE-03 (UBC linkage):** fee routes are linked to UBC benefit vectors.

## B05.CH16.P08.S02 — Leverage and collateral: debt used to mask W or extract cashflows

Leverage can be used to extract cash (consumption funding) without “income” events and to mask W via encumbrances. Collateralized
borrowing can function as a cap bypass if not treated as exposure.

### Leverage patterns (minimum taxonomy)
- `collateralized_cashout` (borrow against appreciated assets to fund spending)
- `asset_pledge_layering` (multiple liens/pledges across vehicles)
- `recourse_shielding` (non-recourse structures engineered to hide benefit/control)
- `synthetic_leverage` (derivative leverage rather than explicit borrowing)
- `securitization_wrapping` (cashflows repackaged into vehicles to obscure ownership/exposure)

### Detection signals (minimum)
- leverage ratio spikes around cap events (timing signature)
- repeated refinancing without underlying business change
- pledge complexity (multiple collateral layers, unclear priority)
- cashflow patterns inconsistent with reported “income” (high spend with low declared I)
- inter-entity lending within controlled graph (loan loops)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Collateralized borrowing **MUST** be treated as an economic exposure/cash extraction vector and included in W/I enforcement logic as
  specified by method_id (anti-cashout bypass).
- Encumbrances **MUST** be reported with priority and amounts (bands acceptable); missing encumbrance disclosure triggers presumptions.
- Leverage structures **MUST** be mapped to UBC benefit/control vectors; “the bank owns the lien” does not remove control.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-LEV-01 (Cashout visibility):** collateralized cashout channels are declared and linked to assets.
- **DL16-LEV-02 (Pledge mapping):** pledge/lien layers are mapped with priority bands.
- **DL16-LEV-03 (Timing signal):** leverage changes are evaluated around cap events.

## B05.CH16.P08.S03 — Synthetics: total return swaps, options, contracts for difference as hidden exposure

Synthetics replicate economic exposure without title. They can hide concentration, facilitate cross-border exposure, and bypass reporting
if not included in look-through.

### Synthetic exposure patterns (minimum taxonomy)
- `total_return_swap` (TRS) on concentrated positions or indices
- `options_overlay` (deep-in-the-money or structured options replicating ownership)
- `contracts_for_difference` (CFDs) used as shadow exposure
- `synthetic_short_or_hedge` used to disguise net exposure
- `derivative_wrapping` via SPVs to obscure counterparty and beneficial exposure

### Detection signals (minimum)
- counterparty exposure without title holdings (custody mismatch)
- margin/collateral flows inconsistent with declared positions
- options strike/delta patterns consistent with ownership replication
- repeated rollovers timed around reporting windows (timing signature)
- offshore counterparties or secrecy nodes in exposure chain

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Synthetics **MUST** be included in economic exposure calculations for W-MAX and concentration tests; title is irrelevant.
- Net exposure **MUST** be computed on a look-through basis; hedges must be evidenced and cannot be asserted without contracts.
- Under opacity, conservative exposure presumptions **MUST** apply (treat as exposure unless disproven).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-SYN-01 (Exposure inclusion):** synthetic positions are mapped to equivalent exposure bands.
- **DL16-SYN-02 (Contract evidence):** derivative claims reference evidence classes (ISDA/confirmations/collateral statements).
- **DL16-SYN-03 (Opacity presumption):** missing counterparty/contract details triggers conservative exposure presumption.

## B05.CH16.P08.S04 — Remedy: look-through to economic exposure, conservative valuation, reporting duty

Financial shifts are neutralised by treating economic reality as primary: exposure, control, benefit, and block power determine cap
application.

### Remedy toolkit (minimum set)
- `economic_exposure_look_through`:
  - compute equivalent exposure for synthetics/leverage; attribute to UBC subjects.
- `conservative_valuation_rules`:
  - upper-bound valuation bands and worst-case exposure bands under missing evidence.
- `mandatory_reporting_duty`:
  - reporting of fee stacks, side letters, leverage, synthetics, collateral and counterparties.
- `recharacterization_and_inclusion`:
  - treat debt cashouts, related-party fees, and synthetic exposure as cap-relevant where rules specify.
- `escalation_under_opacity`:
  - escrow/lockbox, audit escalation, sanctions multipliers for obstruction (linked to P09/P14).

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Reporting duty **MUST** cover fee stacks, leverage, and synthetics; omission triggers presumptions.
- Remedies **MUST** be rule-bound and versioned; bespoke “structuring exceptions” are non-conform.
- Under structural opacity/obstruction, stricter presumptions **MUST** auto-activate (fail-closed escalation path).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **DL16-RMDF-01 (Look-through):** each financial shift maps to an exposure look-through computation and UBC attribution.
- **DL16-RMDF-02 (Conservative bands):** missing evidence triggers conservative valuation/exposure bands.
- **DL16-RMDF-03 (Duty + sanction):** reporting duty exists and obstruction triggers sanction multipliers or restrictions.

---

### Output format (financial shift register)

At minimum, the case **MUST** emit a financial shift register (table or structured list) with:

- `shift_id` | `pattern_type` | `instruments` | `signal_set` | `exposure_equivalent_band` | `UBC_link` | `opacity_flag` |
  `default_presumption` | `remedy_route` | `status`

If financial shifts are described without exposure equivalence and remedy routing, the case is NON-CONFORM.
