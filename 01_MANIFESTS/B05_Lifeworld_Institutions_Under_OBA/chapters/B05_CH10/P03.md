# B05.CH10.P03 — Digital baseline access (essentials)

This paragraph specifies digital baseline access under OBA: an essentials package that enables participation without surveillance rent
or platform clamp. Because identity, communication, and payment ability are now prerequisites for work, education, healthcare, and
public services, baseline digital access functions like other essentials floors (housing, energy). OBA therefore requires (i) a defined
minimum package (identity, messaging/email, basic web access, ability to pay, access to public services), (ii) affordability rails
(baseline free/low-cost, anti-regression protections), (iii) availability and incident response requirements treated as ΔG-like
infrastructure outputs, and (iv) non-discrimination across disability, language, and region with auditable exclusion criteria.
Thresholds are parameterised by jurisdiction, but the rails—measurability, contestability, and fail-closed discipline—are binding.

---

<a id="b05ch10p03s01"></a>
## B05.CH10.P03.S01 — Minimum package: identity, messaging/email, basic web access, ability to pay, access to public services (parameters)

Baseline access requires an explicit package: what is guaranteed and what cannot be withheld via business-model friction.

### Minimum package (parameters; binding requirement to set and publish)
- **Identity**: a usable identity/authentication pathway sufficient to access public and essential private services, with humane
  recovery (linked to P04).
- **Messaging/email**: a baseline communication channel (email and/or messaging) with non-discriminatory access and portability where
  feasible (linked to P05).
- **Basic web access**: affordable connectivity and basic browsing capability sufficient for information and service access.
- **Ability to pay**: access to baseline payment rails (accounts or equivalent) required for essentials transactions (linked to P09).
- **Access to public services**: ability to reach government/public-service portals without proprietary device or app gating.

### Conformance rule (binding)
- Baseline functions may not be conditional on accepting behavioural tracking, targeted advertising profiling, or non-essential data
  extraction.

---

<a id="b05ch10p03s02"></a>
## B05.CH10.P03.S02 — Affordability: baseline free/low-cost; anti-regression rails for data-poor groups

Affordability is treated as an L channel: if baseline access is expensive, digital becomes a coercive constraint.

### Affordability rails (binding)
- **Baseline free/low-cost**: jurisdictions must define a baseline affordability standard (parameters) such that typical low-income
  households can maintain baseline access without sacrificing other essentials.
- **No regressive fixed-charge loading**: fee structures that disproportionately burden low-use or low-income users are
  non-conforming.
- **Anti-regression protections**: safeguards for data-poor groups, precarious households, and rural regions where competitive
  alternatives are weak.

### Prohibited drift (binding)
- “Paid privacy” models where baseline access is free only if users accept surveillance, while privacy requires payment, are treated
  as a prohibited data toll (linked to P07).

### Publication requirement (binding)
- Publish baseline affordability indicators: coverage rates, median/p90 burden, and disconnection/termination rates (privacy-safe).

<a id="b05ch10p03s03"></a>
## B05.CH10.P03.S03 — Availability: uptime, latency, support, incident response (ΔG-like)

Baseline digital access must be reliable. Availability is treated as infrastructure performance, not as a customer-service preference.

### Availability minima (binding)
- **Uptime and latency**: set minimum service levels (parameters) for baseline identity, messaging, and payment access.
- **Support and recovery**: time-bounded support response for lockouts, fraud, and service failures; humane recovery procedures
  mandatory.
- **Incident response**: mandatory incident classification, public aggregated reporting, and post-mortems for major outages (linked to
  P11/P12 discipline).

### Conformance requirement (binding)
- Providers of baseline functions must publish aggregated reliability stats: outages, mean and tail recovery times, lockout events,
  and support response times (privacy-safe aggregation).

---

<a id="b05ch10p03s04"></a>
## B05.CH10.P03.S04 — Non-discrimination: accessibility (disability), language, region; auditable exclusion criteria

Baseline digital access must be delivered without exclusion mechanisms that convert essentials into clamp.

### Non-discrimination minima (binding)
- **Accessibility**: baseline services must meet accessibility standards (disability accommodation) and provide non-digital fallback
  where feasible for essential functions.
- **Language and region**: baseline services must not exclude minority languages or peripheral regions through design neglect or
  punitive pricing.
- **Auditable exclusion criteria**: any denial or restriction must provide reason codes, rule references, and an appeal path; “silent
  exclusion” and shadow denial are non-conforming.

### Hard stop (binding)
- Systematic exclusion via lockouts, inaccessible design, or region-based denial triggers conformance intervention and may trigger
  public option deployment (fail-closed against digital exclusion).
