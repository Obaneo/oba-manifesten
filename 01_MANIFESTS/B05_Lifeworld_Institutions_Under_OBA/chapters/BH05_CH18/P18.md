# B05.CH18.P18 — Integration: how CH18 makes the rest of Book 5 enforceable

This paragraph specifies how CH18 integrates with the rest of Book 5 to make it **enforceable** rather than aspirational. Chapters can
define templates, rails, and cases; CH18 adds the missing layer: **failure-mode checks, stop rules, and repair protocols**. It binds
sector templates (CH17), day-in-the-life regression cases (CH14–CH16), local institutions (CH12), and transition conflict scenarios
(Book 2) into a single enforcement system with measurable triggers, escalation paths, and fail-closed scaling gates.

---

<a id="b05ch18p18s01"></a>
## B05.CH18.P18.S01 — Link to sector cases (CH17): every sector case includes explicit failure-mode checks and stop rules

### Integration logic
CH17 sector cases are reusable only if they include their own early-warning and stop-rule logic. Otherwise they become “design documents”
that drift during rollout. CH18 injects a mandatory failure-mode annex into every sector case.

### Required CH18 annex in every CH17 sector case (binding)
- `failure_mode_checks`:
  - resentment proxies (friction, cohort fractures)
  - sabotage proxies (integrity/performance fractures)
  - evasion proxies (shift indicators, fee-stack deepening)
  - drift proxies (admin-L rise, data-maximalism, procedure inflation)
  - capture proxies (exception repetition, vendor dominance, audit skew)
- `stop_rules`:
  - explicit thresholds and time windows
  - scaling block scope (what stops: new contracts/procurement/expansion)
  - remediation pack requirements (audit + redesign + retest)
- `publication_requirements`:
  - aggregated dashboards and incident logs

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Every CH17 sector case **MUST** embed CH18 checks and stop rules; absence makes it NON-CONFORM for reuse.
- Sector scaling **MUST** be gated on demonstrable L reduction/tail stabilization and integrity/privacy compliance (CH17.P10 + CH18).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **INT-01 (Annex presence):** each sector case includes a CH18 failure-mode annex + stop rule registry.
- **INT-02 (Thresholds):** thresholds and actions are declared (no discretionary “review later”).

## B05.CH18.P18.S02 — Link to day-in-the-life (CH14–CH16): cases act as policy regression tests

### Integration logic
CH14–CH16 day-in-the-life cases make lifeworld claims testable. CH18 turns them into **regression tests**: if “after” cases worsen tails,
time burden, or integrity/privacy, that is a breach requiring rollback or redesign.

### Required regression-test use (binding)
- Treat each day-in-the-life case as a `scenario_test` with:
  - `before_after_dashboard` (B,T,N,L,R; tails; time friction)
  - `lock_in_map` and `commons_exit_paths`
  - `dispute_flow` and error correction visibility
- Define `regression_fail_conditions`:
  - increased arrears/lockouts/wait times
  - increased admin-L (cycle times/drop-off)
  - increased selective execution variance
  - integrity/privacy incidents

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- If regression fail conditions trigger, scaling **MUST** halt and remediation is mandatory (fail-closed).
- “Average improvement” cannot override tail worsening in day-in-the-life cases; tails are primary.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **INT-03 (Regression gates):** day-in-the-life dashboards are used as gating tests for rollout decisions.
- **INT-04 (Tail primacy):** tail worsening triggers stop rules regardless of median improvements.

## B05.CH18.P18.S03 — Link to local institutions (CH12): contestability/auditability are primary, not optional

### Integration logic
Local institutions implement lifeworld access. If contestability and auditability are weak locally, the system will drift into humiliation,
gatekeeping, and selective enforcement even if national rails are sound.

### Required local binding (binding)
- `contestability_minima`:
  - time-bounded objections/appeals, interim relief, anti-retaliation enforcement
- `auditability_minima`:
  - tamper-evident logs, incident logs, restatement discipline
- `execution_capacity_minima`:
  - ringfenced staffing/maintenance budgets for access and dispute flows
- `variance_visibility`:
  - publish local variance in outcomes and friction (aggregated) to detect postcode lotteries

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Contestability and auditability **MUST** be treated as constitutive for local institutions, not optional “service quality features”.
- Persistent local variance without transparency/remedy is a legitimacy breach and triggers escalation.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **INT-05 (Local minima):** CH12 institutional minima are referenced and enforced in local implementations.
- **INT-06 (Variance dashboards):** local variance dashboards exist (aggregated) and have escalation triggers.

## B05.CH18.P18.S04 — Link to transition conflict (Book 2): sabotage/retaliation scenarios and mitigations are predefined

### Integration logic
Transition is conflictual. Sabotage and retaliation are not surprises; they are scenario classes. CH18’s detection and mitigation logic is
linked to Book 2’s world-scenarios so that response is precommitted and not improvised under crisis pressure.

### Required linkage outputs (binding)
- `scenario_mapping`:
  - which failure modes are expected under which transition scenarios (retaliation, capital flight, labor conflict, sanctions pressure)
- `predefined_mitigations`:
  - capacity ringfencing, procurement hardening, channel controls with sunset, governance reset protocols
- `escalation_boundaries`:
  - avoid permanent emergency drift; crisis rails must sunset and remain contestable

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Scenario responses **MUST** be predeclared and versioned; improvisation under pressure is a capture vector.
- Crisis measures **MUST** be bounded and sunset; otherwise sabotage response becomes political capture.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **INT-07 (Scenario linkage):** failure-mode triggers reference Book 2 scenario categories.
- **INT-08 (Sunset discipline):** crisis mitigations include sunset/renewal and contestability.

---

### Output format (integration annex)

At minimum, CH18 **MUST** emit an integration annex (table or structured list) with:

- `linked_module` (CH17/CH14-16/CH12/Book2) | `required_CH18_artifact` | `trigger_inputs` | `stop_rule` |
  `repair_path_ref` | `publication_ref`

If integration artifacts are not defined, Book 5 remains descriptive rather than enforceable.
