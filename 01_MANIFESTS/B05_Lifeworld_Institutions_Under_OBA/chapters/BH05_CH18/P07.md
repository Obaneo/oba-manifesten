# B05.CH18.P07 — Sabotage: detection and escalation

This paragraph specifies **detection and escalation** for sabotage failure modes. Sabotage is treated as an integrity and execution
breach: the system must detect it via log integrity, performance fractures, and market/concentration signals. Escalation is standardized:
**anomaly → forensic audit → governance reset/remedies → (if needed) temporary intervention**. All steps must be logged, time-bounded, and
auditable; “investigate later” is non-conform.

---

<a id="b05ch18p07s01"></a>
## B05.CH18.P07.S01 — Integrity indicators: logging anomalies, missing lineage, signature mismatches, “silent edits”

### Core detection logic
Operational sabotage is most reliably detected through integrity signals: breaks in the evidentiary chain that make audit impossible or
hide discretionary changes.

### Required integrity indicators (minimum set)
- `logging_anomaly_rate_band`:
  - missing log entries, malformed entries, timestamp gaps, unusual volume spikes/drops
- `missing_lineage_incidents_band`:
  - broken traceability between decision → rule → evidence → outcome
- `signature_mismatch_count`:
  - mismatched hashes, unsigned changes, non-authorized actor edits
- `silent_edit_detection`:
  - rule/method/data changes without:
    - decision log entry,
    - version bump,
    - restatement reference
- `auditpack_withholding_incidents_band`:
  - refusal or failure to provide required evidence packs within time bounds

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Integrity indicators **MUST** be monitored continuously; integrity is not an annual audit topic.
- Silent edits **MUST** trigger an immediate integrity incident and route to stop rule B (rollback) logic (P20).
- Missing lineage **MUST** be treated as a fail-closed condition for claims dependent on that lineage.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SAB-DET-01 (Tamper-evidence):** logs are tamper-evident and support signature mismatch detection.
- **SAB-DET-02 (Version discipline):** rule/method/data changes require version bumps and restatement hooks.
- **SAB-DET-03 (Lineage gate):** missing lineage triggers conservative presumption or suspension of the affected claim.

<a id="b05ch18p07s02"></a>
## B05.CH18.P07.S02 — Performance fractures: sudden increases in cycle times/backlogs without an external shock

### Core detection logic
Sabotage often appears as a **fracture**: sudden degradation in execution metrics that cannot be explained by external shocks. This is
distinct from gradual capacity strain.

### Required performance fracture indicators (minimum set)
- `cycle_time_spike_band` (per process class):
  - applications, switching, disputes, service delivery
- `backlog_growth_spike_band`:
  - queue length/backlog growth beyond benchmark
- `error_rate_spike_band`:
  - reversal/restatement rates, incident rates
- `service_level_drop_band`:
  - outages, response times, missed appointments, failed deliveries
- `shock_exclusion_check`:
  - declared external shock baseline (weather, pandemic wave, major supply disruption) with time window

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Performance fractures **MUST** be evaluated relative to external-shock baselines; “things got worse” is non-actionable without the
  exclusion check.
- If fractures occur without external shocks, escalation **MUST** proceed (forensic audit start).
- Persistent fractures **MUST** block scaling until repaired (fail-closed).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SAB-DET-04 (Baseline discipline):** an external-shock exclusion check exists for each fracture event.
- **SAB-DET-05 (Time bounds):** fractures trigger audit initiation within a declared time bound.

## B05.CH18.P07.S03 — Concentration/market signals: collusion indicators, margin spikes, deeper fee stacks

### Core detection logic
Market sabotage is detected through concentration signals: unexplained margin spikes, synchronized pricing, fee stack deepening, and
intensified lock-ins. These are not “market dynamics”; they are actionable E-surface escalations.

### Required market/concentration indicators (minimum set; aggregated)
- `effective_fee_stack_depth_band` (number of mandatory fee layers)
- `effective_take_rate_band` (all mandatory layers combined)
- `margin_spike_proxy_band` (spread or margin proxies; sector-defined)
- `price_parallelism_proxy` (co-movement beyond benchmark, where measurable)
- `lock_in_intensification_proxy`:
  - penalty incidence, contract length shifts, bundling incidence
- `provider_concentration_proxy` (market share bands; HHI-like proxies allowed)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Fee stack and take-rate metrics **MUST** use effective (all-layer) measures, not headline fees.
- Unexplained spikes beyond thresholds **MUST** trigger audit and contract remedies (unbundling, prohibited clauses, bounded returns).
- If concentration signals persist, public option/commons expansion **MUST** be considered as a remedy path.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SAB-DET-06 (All-layer measurement):** effective fee/take-rate is computed for relevant markets.
- **SAB-DET-07 (Threshold triggers):** spikes beyond thresholds map to mandatory actions (audit, rewrite, escalation).

<a id="b05ch18p07s04"></a>
## B05.CH18.P07.S04 — Escalation path: anomaly → forensic audit → governance reset/remedies → (if needed) temporary intervention

### Standard escalation pipeline (binding)
1) `anomaly_detected`:
   - indicator breach recorded, time-stamped, and classified (integrity/performance/market)
2) `containment` (if needed):
   - freeze high-risk changes, halt scaling, require escrow/lockbox for disputed flows, preserve logs
3) `forensic_audit`:
   - auditpacks demanded; lineage reconstructed; root cause determined (sabotage vs capacity vs external shock)
4) `governance_reset_and_remedies`:
   - role rotation, separation enforcement, contract rewrites, procurement resets, sanctions
5) `temporary_intervention` (last resort):
   - temporary measures to restore service integrity/capacity, with sunset and contestability (avoid permanent emergency drift)
6) `restatement_and_publication`:
   - publish aggregated incident report, restate affected metrics, log redesign/version changes
7) `recertification`:
   - re-enable scaling only after indicators return within thresholds and audit passes

### Required fields (template)
- `escalation_event_id`
- `trigger_indicator_ref`
- `classification` ∈ {integrity_breach, performance_fracture, market_sabotage}
- `time_bounds`:
  - `t_ack`, `t_contain`, `t_audit_start`, `t_audit_close`, `t_remedy_execute`
- `actions_taken[]` (containment/audit/remedy/intervention)
- `decision_log_ref` + `incident_log_ref`
- `restatement_ref` + `version_change_ref`
- `recertification_status`

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Escalation steps **MUST** be time-bounded and logged; open-ended investigations are delay-as-sabotage risks.
- Temporary interventions **MUST** be sunset and contestable; otherwise they become political capture vectors.
- Scaling **MUST NOT** resume without recertification evidence.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **SAB-ESC-01 (Pipeline completeness):** escalation follows the declared pipeline stages (may skip only if documented).
- **SAB-ESC-02 (Restatement):** affected claims/metrics are restated after remediation.
- **SAB-ESC-03 (Recertification):** scaling requires audit pass + indicator normalization.

---

### Output format (sabotage escalation register)

At minimum, CH18 **MUST** emit a sabotage escalation register (table or structured list) with:

- `event_id` | `trigger_indicator` | `classification` | `containment_action` | `audit_start_time` | `audit_outcome` |
  `governance_remedy` | `temporary_intervention` | `restatement_ref` | `recertification_status`

If sabotage escalation cannot be emitted with time bounds and restatement references, the system is non-conform (no enforceable repair).
