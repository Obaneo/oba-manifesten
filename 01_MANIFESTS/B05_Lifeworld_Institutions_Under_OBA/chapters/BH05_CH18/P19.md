# B05.CH18.P19 — Measurement and publication requirements (early-warning dashboard)

This paragraph specifies the **measurement and publication** requirements for CH18’s early-warning dashboard. The dashboard is not a PR
artifact; it is an operational safety instrument. It must surface failure modes early enough to trigger stop rules and repair, while
remaining privacy-safe (aggregation, suppression, join-safety). Publication is required to prevent hidden drift and capture; privacy tiers
control granularity, not accountability.

---

<a id="b05ch18p19s01"></a>
## B05.CH18.P19.S01 — Resentment proxies: friction indices, appeal volumes, drop-off, wait times, cohort fractures

### Required resentment proxy groups (minimum set; aggregated)
- `friction_index`:
  - composite proxy (method-defined) built from cycle times, step counts, and repeated-proof incidence
- `appeal_volume_band` + `appeal_duration_band`:
  - by domain and cohort (aggregated)
- `drop_off_rate_band`:
  - started vs completed processes (by domain)
- `wait_time_exceedance_band`:
  - frequency of wait-time spikes beyond benchmarks (sector-specific)
- `cohort_fracture_table`:
  - L component deltas vs median, RB delta vs median, tail deltas vs median

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Resentment proxies **MUST** be cohort-sensitive; medians alone are non-conform.
- Proxies **MUST** be tied to repair triggers (P05) and stop rules (P20), not merely published.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **EW-RES-01 (Cohort fractures):** dashboard includes cohort fracture outputs for at least two cohort dimensions.
- **EW-RES-02 (Repair coupling):** each proxy group maps to a repair action class and time bounds.

<a id="b05ch18p19s02"></a>
## B05.CH18.P19.S02 — Sabotage proxies: integrity alerts, execution anomalies, incident rates, capacity collapses

### Required sabotage proxy groups (minimum set; aggregated)
- `integrity_alerts`:
  - signature mismatches, missing lineage incidents, silent edit detections
- `execution_anomaly_index`:
  - unusual cycle-time/backlog spikes excluding external shocks (method-defined)
- `incident_rate_band`:
  - service outages, billing incidents, lockouts, safety incidents (domain-specific)
- `capacity_collapse_proxy`:
  - staffing minima breaches, backlog runaway, audit/ombuds backlog (ΔG of governance failure)
- `selective_execution_proxy`:
  - variance in outcomes/processing times by region/cohort beyond tolerance

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Integrity alerts **MUST** be published (aggregated) with severity and time-to-containment.
- Capacity collapse **MUST** trigger scope restriction or scaling blocks (fail-closed), not just “support requests”.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **EW-SAB-01 (Integrity reporting):** integrity alerts are tracked and published with severity bands.
- **EW-SAB-02 (External shock check):** execution anomalies include external shock exclusion logic.

<a id="b05ch18p19s03"></a>
## B05.CH18.P19.S03 — Evasion proxies: shift indicators, fee-stack depth, offshore/synthetic exposure (aggregated)

### Required evasion proxy groups (minimum set; aggregated)
- `shift_indicator_counts`:
  - counts by shift family: legal/technical/financial/international (from P10 register)
- `fee_stack_depth_band` + `effective_fee_rate_band`:
  - all-layer fee measures in relevant sectors (platform take rates, servicing/collections stacks)
- `lock_in_intensification_proxy`:
  - penalty incidence, bundling incidence, portability failure rates
- `offshore_exposure_proxy`:
  - offshore node share bands, treaty hub routing incidence (UBC-graph derived, aggregated)
- `synthetic_exposure_proxy`:
  - presence rates of TRS/CFD/options-like exposure replication (aggregated)
- `leakage_test_failures`:
  - ΔM leakage breaches by domain

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Evasion proxies **MUST** include channels and structures; contract-only analysis is non-conform.
- Fee metrics **MUST** be all-layer effective measures; headline fees are evadable.
- Offshore/synthetic proxies **MUST** remain aggregated; do not publish microdata.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **EW-EVA-01 (Family coverage):** dashboard reports at least one proxy per evasion shift family.
- **EW-EVA-02 (All-layer fees):** effective fee/take-rate measures are used.

<a id="b05ch18p19s04"></a>
## B05.CH18.P19.S04 — Capture proxies: exceptions register, vendor dominance, audit coverage anomalies, revolving-door stats

### Required capture proxy groups (minimum set; aggregated)
- `exceptions_register_summary`:
  - repeat exception rates, exception duration bands, exceptions by position class
- `vendor_dominance_graph_summary`:
  - vendor share of critical rails; dependency depth; change-order dominance proxies
- `audit_coverage_anomaly_proxy`:
  - skew in audit coverage relative to risk baseline; suppressed finding discrepancy proxies
- `incident_disclosure_rate`:
  - published vs detected incident proxies (aggregated)
- `revolving_door_stats`:
  - categorical movement indicators and cooling-off violation counts (privacy-safe)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Capture proxies **MUST** be published (aggregated) because secrecy is a capture vector.
- Vendor dominance on critical rails **MUST** trigger mitigation requirements (exit assistance/tooling unbundling) when thresholds breach.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **EW-CAP-01 (Exception visibility):** exception summaries exist and are time-bounded.
- **EW-CAP-02 (Audit skew visibility):** audit coverage anomaly proxies exist and trigger meta-audit.

---

### Publication discipline (cross-cutting)

### Required publication fields (template)
- `publication_cadence` (e.g., monthly/quarterly; domain-specific)
- `privacy_tier` per table (public aggregate vs restricted audit aggregate)
- `method_binding`:
  - `definitionset_id`, `method_id`, benchmark versions, uncertainty rules
- `restatement_policy_ref`:
  - how corrected data and methods are restated and versioned
- `join_safety_statement`:
  - allowed joins and suppression rules; microdata prohibition

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Publication **MUST** include method binding and version markers; otherwise it becomes non-comparable.
- Restatements **MUST** be published when errors occur; silent edits are integrity breaches.
- Privacy tiers control granularity, not whether indicators exist.

---

### Output format (CH18 early-warning dashboard)

At minimum, CH18 **MUST** publish an early-warning dashboard bundle containing:

1) **Resentment dashboard** (fractures + friction + appeals + wait times)  
2) **Sabotage dashboard** (integrity + anomalies + incidents + capacity)  
3) **Evasion dashboard** (shift counts + fee stacks + offshore/synthetic + leakage tests)  
4) **Capture dashboard** (exceptions + vendor dominance + audit skew + revolving-door proxies)  

Each dashboard section must include:
- `time_window`
- `value_bands`
- `thresholds`
- `trigger_actions` (stop/repair links)
- `method_ref` (definitionset_id/method_id/version)

If any section cannot be emitted, the implementation is blind to that failure mode and is non-conform.
