# B05.CH18.P15 — Capture: detection, evidence, and hard stops

This paragraph specifies **detection**, **evidence requirements**, and **hard stops** for capture failure modes. Capture is treated as a
structural breach because it makes conformance non-falsifiable: rules become negotiable, enforcement becomes selective, and auditability
collapses. Detection therefore focuses on red-flag patterns (exceptions, revolving doors, vendor dominance), audit skew/suppression
signals, and missing incident disclosure. When capture is proven, discretionary powers are suspended and the relevant template/governance
regime is reset.

---

<a id="b05ch18p15s01"></a>
## B05.CH18.P15.S01 — Red flags: repeated exceptions for the same positions, revolving-door patterns, vendor dominance graphs

### Core detection logic
Capture reveals itself through repetition and concentration: the same positions repeatedly receive exceptions; the same vendors dominate
critical rails; personnel movement aligns incentives across oversight boundaries.

### Required red-flag indicators (minimum set; aggregated)
- `exceptions_register`:
  - `exception_count_by_position_band`
  - `repeat_exception_rate_band` (same position/actor class receives multiple exceptions)
  - `exception_duration_band` (temporary vs de facto permanent)
- `revolving_door_proxy`:
  - movement between regulator/auditor/executor and vendors/sector incumbents (categorical; privacy-safe)
  - cooling-off violations count
- `vendor_dominance_graph`:
  - vendor share of critical tooling (logs, identity, dispute systems, measurement tooling)
  - contract dependency depth (single points of failure)
- `standard_ownership_proxy`:
  - who steers standards/benchmarks/methods (committee concentration proxies)
- `procurement_concentration_proxy`:
  - repeated award patterns, change order dominance

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Exceptions **MUST** be logged, time-bounded, and reviewable; repeated exceptions are treated as capture signals.
- Vendor dominance on critical rails **MUST** trigger lock-in mitigation and exit assistance; otherwise the system becomes non-contestable.
- Revolving-door proxies **MUST** be monitored with privacy-safe aggregation; this is an integrity mechanism, not surveillance.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **CAP-DET-01 (Exception visibility):** an exception register exists and can be queried by position_id class (aggregated).
- **CAP-DET-02 (Vendor graph):** vendor dominance graph exists for critical rails and triggers mitigation thresholds.

<a id="b05ch18p15s02"></a>
## B05.CH18.P15.S02 — Audit signals: skewed audit coverage, suppressed findings, missing incident disclosure

### Core detection logic
Capture often expresses itself via audit distortion: audits avoid powerful actors, findings are softened or suppressed, and incidents are
not disclosed.

### Required audit distortion indicators (minimum set; aggregated)
- `audit_coverage_skew_proxy`:
  - audit rates by sector position/cohort/region vs risk baseline
- `finding_severity_distribution_shift`:
  - sudden drop in high-severity findings without corresponding risk reduction
- `suppressed_finding_proxy`:
  - mismatches between incident signals and audit outputs (method-defined discrepancy)
- `missing_incident_disclosure_rate`:
  - incidents detected internally but not published (aggregated)
- `audit_cycle_time_anomaly`:
  - unusually long audits for high-power positions (delay-as-suppression signal)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Audit coverage **MUST** be risk-based and visible in aggregated form; skew without rationale is a capture signal.
- Incident disclosure **MUST** occur with privacy tiers; systematic non-disclosure is a hard stop precursor.
- If audit outputs diverge from incident signals, a meta-audit **MUST** initiate (audit-on-the-auditor).

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **CAP-DET-03 (Coverage publication):** audit coverage stats are published (aggregated) with risk baseline reference.
- **CAP-DET-04 (Meta-audit trigger):** audit/incident divergence triggers meta-audit within time bounds.

<a id="b05ch18p15s03"></a>
## B05.CH18.P15.S03 — Public accountability: conformance downgrade + forensic audit + governance reset protocol

### Core accountability logic
Capture cannot be handled privately; secrecy is part of the mechanism. The response must include public accountability artifacts and a
governance reset protocol.

### Required accountability actions (minimum set)
- `conformance_status_downgrade`:
  - status values ∈ {CONFORM, PARTIAL, UNDER_REVIEW, NON_CONFORM}
  - downgrade triggers: repeated exceptions, audit skew, incident non-disclosure, vendor dominance breaches
- `forensic_audit`:
  - independent audit with evidence pack demands, lineage reconstruction, and published aggregated summary
- `governance_reset_protocol`:
  - role rotation and separation enforcement
  - procurement reset / tooling unbundling / open standards mandates
  - exception purge (revoke or sunset non-justified exceptions)
  - re-issue templates/procedures with versioned change log
- `restatement`:
  - restate affected decisions/metrics under corrected methods
- `recertification_gate`:
  - scaling and discretionary powers resume only after recertification evidence

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Conformance downgrades **MUST** be rule-derived and published in aggregated form.
- Governance reset **MUST** include procurement and tooling changes when those are capture surfaces; otherwise capture persists.
- Recertification **MUST** be required before resuming scaling or discretionary powers.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **CAP-ACT-01 (Downgrade publication):** conformance status changes are publicly visible (aggregated) with reason codes.
- **CAP-ACT-02 (Reset completeness):** governance reset includes role rotation + exception handling + procurement/tooling remediation.

<a id="b05ch18p15s04"></a>
## B05.CH18.P15.S04 — Hard stop: proven capture → immediate suspension of discretionary powers and template reset

### Hard stop logic (FAIL-CLOSED)
When capture is proven (evidence meets threshold), discretionary governance becomes invalid because it is now a rent surface. The system
must fail-closed: suspend discretionary powers, freeze exceptions, and reset templates.

### Proof conditions (minimum)
Capture is treated as **proven** when one or more of the following holds (method-defined thresholds):
- repeated exceptions benefiting the same positions without general rule basis
- audit suppression confirmed (blocked audits, suppressed findings, missing incident disclosure)
- procurement dominance + tool opacity prevents audit/contestability
- selective enforcement patterns confirmed without lawful rationale
- revolving-door violations coincide with procurement/exception outcomes

### Mandatory hard-stop actions (binding)
- `suspend_discretionary_powers`:
  - freeze non-essential discretion; enforce default rule paths
- `freeze_and_review_exceptions`:
  - immediate exception freeze; revoke non-justified exceptions
- `template_reset`:
  - re-issue sector or governance templates under new version; deprecate captured variants
- `forensic_audit_and_publication`:
  - publish aggregated findings, restatements, and reset rationale
- `recertification_required`:
  - no scaling, no new contracts, no new channel issuance until recertified

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Hard stop actions **MUST** be automatic under proven capture; discretion here is capture-by-definition.
- Suspension and reset **MUST** be visible (aggregated); secrecy destroys legitimacy.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **CAP-HS-01 (Auto-activation):** proof conditions map to automatic suspension + reset actions.
- **CAP-HS-02 (Template deprecation):** captured templates are deprecated with migration paths.
- **CAP-HS-03 (Recertification):** resumption requires audit pass + indicator normalization + published restatement.

---

### Output format (capture dashboard + hard-stop register)

At minimum, CH18 **MUST** emit:

1) **Capture red-flag dashboard**
- `signal` | `position_or_domain` | `value_band` | `threshold` | `trend` | `status`

2) **Audit distortion dashboard**
- `audit_coverage_skew` | `finding_severity_shift` | `incident_disclosure_rate` | `meta_audit_triggered`

3) **Hard-stop register**
- `trigger` | `proof_condition` | `action_taken` | `scope` | `audit_ref` | `template_reset_ref` | `recertification_status`

If these cannot be produced, capture detection is not operational and CH18 is non-conform.
