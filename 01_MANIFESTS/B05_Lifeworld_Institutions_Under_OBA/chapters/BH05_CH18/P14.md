# B05.CH18.P14 — Capture: forms (economic, political, epistemic, cultural)

This paragraph specifies the **forms of capture** relevant to lifeworld implementations. Capture means: decision power, benefit, or block
power concentrates into positions that can steer rules, procurement, standards, enforcement, or narratives to preserve rents and reduce
contestability. Capture is not an allegation; it is a structural failure mode with identifiable signatures. CH18 distinguishes four forms:
economic, political, epistemic, and cultural (status-gate capture).

---

<a id="b05ch18p14s01"></a>
## B05.CH18.P14.S01 — Economic capture: vendors/sectors steer procurement, standards, exceptions (carve-outs)

### Core mechanism
Economic capture occurs when vendors or sector incumbents shape procurement and standards to create dependency, then convert that
dependency into durable rents and carve-outs.

### Common economic capture forms (minimum taxonomy)
- `procurement_steering`:
  - tenders written around a specific vendor/tooling stack
- `standard_capture`:
  - “standards” encoded to match proprietary interfaces or lock-in dependencies
- `exception_insertion`:
  - repeated exemptions for specific positions or “strategic” actors
- `change_order_rent`:
  - baseline contract underpriced, profits extracted via change orders
- `consultancy_gatekeeping`:
  - proprietary certifications/methods replace public `method_id` and benchmarks

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Procurement **MUST** be auditable and portable; vendor lock-in on critical rails is non-conform unless strictly temporary and sunset.
- Exception insertion for economic actors **MUST** trigger hard stop scrutiny (carve-out breach).
- Standards **MUST** remain open and testable; captured standards are a block-power surface.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **CAP-ECO-01 (Procurement auditability):** procurement logs exist and show open criteria and conflict controls.
- **CAP-ECO-02 (Lock-in triggers):** vendor dominance triggers exit assistance and/or tooling unbundling requirements.

<a id="b05ch18p14s02"></a>
## B05.CH18.P14.S02 — Political capture: emergency regimes without sunsets, selective enforcement, maintenance budget hollowing

### Core mechanism
Political capture occurs when political actors use discretionary control to create exceptions, sustain emergencies without sunsets, apply
selective enforcement, or hollow maintenance budgets—preserving control while degrading lifeworld integrity.

### Common political capture forms (minimum taxonomy)
- `permanent_emergency`:
  - crisis measures extended without sunset/renewal criteria
- `selective_enforcement`:
  - uneven application by region, cohort, or incumbent position without rule basis
- `maintenance_hollowing`:
  - starving execution capacity to make reforms fail or appear impossible (ΔG collapse)
- `veto_on_interop`:
  - blocking standards and portability to preserve leverage
- `discretionary_exception_culture`:
  - exceptions become governance mode (opaque deals)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Emergency measures **MUST** sunset and be contestable; emergency without sunset is capture by definition.
- Selective enforcement **MUST** trigger conformance suspension and governance reset protocols (hard stop linkage).
- Maintenance budgets **MUST** be ringfenced; hollowing is sabotage/capture crossover.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **CAP-POL-01 (Sunset discipline):** every emergency measure has sunset and renewal criteria with publication.
- **CAP-POL-02 (Enforcement parity):** enforcement variance is monitored; unexplained variance triggers forensic audit.

<a id="b05ch18p14s03"></a>
## B05.CH18.P14.S03 — Epistemic capture: “experts” monopolize definitions/benchmarks; dissent becomes impossible

### Core mechanism
Epistemic capture occurs when a narrow expert class monopolizes definitions, benchmarks, and method choices, making critique and
contestability impossible. Over time, “what counts as evidence” becomes a gatekeeping tool.

### Common epistemic capture forms (minimum taxonomy)
- `definition_monopoly`:
  - definitionsets become inaccessible or change without transparent process
- `benchmark_capture`:
  - benchmarks set to favor incumbents or to hide tails/friction
- `method_black_boxing`:
  - method_id replaced by opaque models or discretionary expert judgment
- `suppressed_restated_truth`:
  - restatements avoided; errors hidden; incident logs suppressed
- `dissent_path_blocking`:
  - formal challenge exists but is practically unusable (slow, retaliatory, inaccessible)

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Definitions, methods, and benchmarks **MUST** be versioned, transparent, and contestable.
- “Expert judgment” **MUST** be bounded by reproducible methods; black boxes are non-conform.
- Dissent infrastructure **MUST** be strengthened, not managed; critique is a system input.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **CAP-EPI-01 (Method transparency):** method_id and benchmark versions are published and usable for reproduction.
- **CAP-EPI-02 (Contestability):** challenge/appeal paths are time-bounded and show non-trivial reversal/restatement rates when errors exist.

<a id="b05ch18p14s04"></a>
## B05.CH18.P14.S04 — Cultural capture: status networks replace money caps via gatekeeping (link to CH13)

### Core mechanism
Cultural capture occurs when monetary caps reduce one dominance dimension but status competition migrates into gatekeeping networks:
credentials, reputation systems, elite boards, curation institutions, and platform visibility. These can recreate block power under new
symbols.

### Common cultural capture forms (minimum taxonomy)
- `credential_inflation`:
  - expensive or exclusive credentials become required for access
- `network_gatekeeping`:
  - closed circles control grants, boards, or “in the room” access
- `reputation_platform_capture`:
  - verification, ranking, and attention become controlled tolls
- `curation_capture`:
  - control over what counts as legitimate culture/knowledge
- `status_portability_failure`:
  - reputational capital cannot be ported; locked to institutions/platforms

### Normative requirements (RFC 2119)
- Status migration **MUST** be anticipated and monitored via aggregated proxies; gatekeeping surfaces must be contestable (CH13 rails).
- Cultural capture **MUST** trigger anti-gatekeeping remedies: transparency, portability, appeal rights, audit-of-the-auditor.
- Monitoring **MUST** remain privacy-safe and must not become behavioural scoring.

### Conformance tests (minimum)
- **CAP-CUL-01 (Gate surface identification):** at least one gatekeeping surface is identified in domains where status is material.
- **CAP-CUL-02 (CH13 linkage):** contestability/audit rails are applied to gatekeeping institutions and platforms.
